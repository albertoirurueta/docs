Pyramid Cheat Sheet

This content was generated with the assistance of AI and should be verified against the official documentation before being relied on in production.

A single-page, colour-coded summary of the Pyramid essentials, written against Pyramid 2.1 (released 2026-03-11), Python 3.10+ and the companion packages pyramid_tm 2.6, pyramid_jinja2 2.10, pyramid_debugtoolbar 4.12, pyramid_retry 2.1 and WebTest 3.0 (versions verified 2026-10-07) — the single-file application and the cookiecutter project run with pserve, the Configurator with scan and include, .ini settings and the PYRAMID_* variables, add_route patterns, traversal and resources, @view_config and its predicates, renderers and JSON, the WebOb request and response, exception views, static assets and cache busting, sessions and flash, the 2.0 security policy with remember and forget and ACLs, CSRF, pyramid_tm with SQLAlchemy and Alembic, events, tweens and view derivers, internationalization, the p-scripts, pyramid.testing and WebTest, Waitress and Gunicorn deployment, and a strip of what changed since older tutorials — handy for quick memorization or as a downloadable PDF desk reference.
