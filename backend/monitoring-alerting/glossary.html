Glossary

This content was generated with the assistance of AI and should be verified against the official documentation before being relied on in production.

Checked on 2026-11-09 against Grafana 13.3.x and Kibana 9.5.x.

The two tools often name the same idea differently, and sometimes one tool has no counterpart at all. Each table below lists a term used in the section, what Grafana calls it, what Kibana calls it, and the page that explains it. Where a tool has no equivalent, the cell says so, and the explaining page describes the nearest workaround. For a full discussion of the largest differences, read Grafana vs Kibana.

Terms from the lab (the stand-in service, the load generator modes, the webhook receiver and the Collector alias) are explained on the Hands-on Lab page, and they are the same in both profiles.

Reference: Grafana fundamentals and Elastic: Explore and analyze.

In the books

The books in the bibliography use the older vocabulary: Grafana "legacy alerting" and "alert channels" (now contact points), Kibana "watches" and "visualize" (now Watcher and Lens), Elasticsearch "types" and "Beats" as the only collector. When a book term is missing from the tables above, it is probably the older name of one that is present. The publisher pages are in the bibliography.
