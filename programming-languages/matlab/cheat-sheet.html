A short worked example that combines logical indexing, the reductions sum and max, and fprintf output.

v = [3 1 4 1 5 9 2 6];
evens = v(mod(v,2) == 0);
[m, k] = max(v);
fprintf("evens: %s\n", mat2str(evens));
fprintf("sum=%d max=%d at %d\n", sum(v), m, k);
evens: [4 2 6]
sum=31 max=9 at 6
