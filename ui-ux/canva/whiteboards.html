Whiteboards

This content was generated with the assistance of AI and should be verified against the official documentation before being relied on in production.

This page was checked against the official Canva Help Center on 2026-11-07. Whiteboards are one of the document types of the Canva Visual Suite, next to designs, presentations, Docs, video and websites. A whiteboard is a design with an infinite canvas: you add sticky notes, shapes, lines, text, tables and drawings, and the canvas grows as you work. Whiteboards are built into the editor and are generally available. The Help Center does not restrict creating a whiteboard to a particular plan, but the Brand Kit is only available on Team and Enterprise accounts, and some Canva AI options for Whiteboards are described as gradually rolling out and not yet available to every user. Which plan includes what is published at https://www.canva.com/pricing/; no prices are repeated here. The AI tiers (Standard, Premium and Ultra) are explained on Plans, Pricing and AI Allowance.

The running example is Bookshelf, the book catalogue and reading-list app designed in the Figma sub-section. Its launch campaign starts with a team brainstorm on a whiteboard: what to announce, which channels to use and who owns each task. The result feeds the campaign brief and the social posts built on Social Media and Content Planner.

A whiteboard in one picture

A whiteboard uses the same editor as every other design, described on Editor Tour. The side panel opens on the Design tab for a blank board and on the Tools tab when the board already has content. Elements such as sticky notes, shapes and lines come from the Elements tab, and the drawing tools come from the Tools tab, covered on Drawing and the Tools Tab. The bottom bar holds the timer and the zoom controls.

Figure 1. The anatomy of a whiteboard, used as the map for this page

  1. The top bar shows Share and the avatars of active collaborators.

  2. The side panel offers templates, elements and tools.

  3. The canvas is infinite and expands as you drag elements toward its edge.

  4. The bottom bar holds the timer and the zoom controls.

Source: Canva Help Center: Brainstorm and collaborate visually with Canva Whiteboards

Creating a whiteboard

There are two ways to start, and both end in the same kind of canvas.

  • From scratch. On the homepage select Whiteboard. You can also choose Create a design and then Whiteboard, and start from a template or from a blank board.

  • From a presentation or brainstorm file. Open the file and right-click the page you want to expand, then select Expand to whiteboard. Alternatively select the page and choose Whiteboard on the toolbar below the editor. Expanding pages is not supported in Websites, Print, Docs and Video designs.

To go back, undo the Expand to whiteboard action. Another way is to resize the page or to create a new page that is not a whiteboard and copy the selection there. A presentation that contains a whiteboard page can look as if it does not scroll; select the Editing icon at the lower right to move around the board again.

Sticky notes

Sticky notes are the core of a brainstorm. Everyone adds one idea per note, then the team arranges them.

  • Add a note from Elements, under Sticky note, by choosing a colour, or press S.

  • Double-click a note to type. The more icon can add your name to the note.

  • Select several notes with Shift and click, then use the more icon and Copy to paste them into a spreadsheet or a text editor (Command and C, Command and V on macOS, Ctrl and C, Ctrl and V on Windows).

  • Drag across a group of notes and choose Sort in the floating toolbar. You can sort by colour, by name, by topic or by reactions.

  • Select a note and choose Add reaction to attach an emoji. Reactions appear as floating badges, and selecting a badge removes it.

Two more shortcuts speed up board building: C adds a circle and R adds a square.

Source: Canva Help Center: Add and use sticky notes

The timer

The timer keeps a session time-boxed. It is available on a computer only. Select the Timer icon at the bottom corner of the editor and set the period; the default is 5 minutes. Everyone working on the design sees the timer. In the timer you can start, reset and add one minute, and you can choose music (Focus, Dramatic, Upbeat, Energetic or Lofi) and adjust the volume. Special sounds play at the start, at the 10-second mark and at the end. When presenting, pressing a number key from 1 to 9 opens a timer of that many minutes.

Source: Canva Help Center: Setting a timer

Flowcharts with Quick Flow

Quick Flow builds flowcharts by adding connected shapes and lines for you. It aligns the elements, applies the most recent shape and line styles, and keeps shapes and lines connected as you work. It is available in every design type except Canva Docs, and it needs edit access.

Figure 2. How Quick Flow grows a flowchart from a single shape

  1. Add a shape, right-click it and select Enable Quick Flow. Arrows appear around the shape.

  2. Hover the shape until a preview appears and click a directional arrow. Canva adds the next shape, connects it and matches your styles.

  3. To connect existing shapes, drag an arrow toward another shape until it snaps into place. Use the white dots to attach the line to a side or to the centre.

  4. To change a shape without breaking its connections, open its more menu and choose a new shape. To flip a line, select Swap line ends in the toolbar.

  5. To stop, right-click the shape and select Disable Quick Flow. Existing connections stay.

If the arrows do not show, check that the shape is not locked or grouped. Quick Flow follows the last style you applied, so set your colours before adding steps.

Source: Canva Help Center: Create flowcharts fast with Quick Flow in Canva

Diagrams, maps and structured sessions

The Help Center documents several ready-made uses of the whiteboard.

Use How it works

UML diagrams

From Whiteboard, open Templates, search for UML and choose Class, Sequence or Use Case. Add shapes from Elements, connect them with lines and double-click to label. Import diagrams as PDF or SVG to keep lines and text editable, because PNG and JPG import as static images.

Concept maps

Choose Whiteboard, search Concept Map in the Design tab and add nodes. Select a node and use the add node icons to grow connected nodes. Text does not wrap automatically, so resize text boxes.

Customer journey maps

Search Customer Journey Map in the Design tab, add headings for each stage and shapes or icons for touchpoints. Use Tidy up to align elements on desktop.

Retrospectives

Search for a template such as Start, Stop and Continue, Went Well and Didn’t Go Well or the 4Ls. Gather input with sticky notes, group and prioritise, keep time with the timer and add a section for next steps.

Locking a key element keeps a framework from being moved by accident. Connectors do not sync with external tools. Select only the shape, not a group, to move it without dragging connected parts, and use Ungroup when a diagram moves as one.

Sources: Canva Help Center: Create and manage UML diagrams in Canva Whiteboard, Canva Help Center: Run a team retrospective in Canva Whiteboards and the other use-case articles listed in Canva Help Center: Whiteboards

Collaborating on the board

Select Share to invite people as Editors or Viewers, or set permission levels such as Can edit, Can comment and Can view; the details are on Sharing and Permissions and Collaboration, Comments and Version History. Use @ in a comment to tag a teammate, who is notified in Canva and by email. Version history is under File and lets you restore earlier content.

Following another person is useful in a workshop. Select a collaborator’s avatar in the toolbar, expanding the list of active collaborators if you do not see them. A coloured border matching their avatar appears around the design, and your view follows them across pages. Editors and viewers can all follow. If the person disconnects and does not return within two minutes, the follow session ends. If a presenter starts Spotlight and requires everyone to follow, you cannot stop until they select Release. To stop people following you, select Release or Release all on the notification.

Source: Canva Help Center: Navigate and collaborate in Canva whiteboards

Exporting and desktop versus mobile

Whiteboards export as PDF (Print), PNG or JPG. PDF (Print) gives the best result for large maps. An export is static, so comments and live collaboration are not included. Desktop is best for building and aligning; the mobile app is best for adding sticky notes and reviewing during a workshop.

Source: Canva Help Center: Whiteboards

Procedure: run the Bookshelf launch brainstorm

The team brainstorm decides which channels carry the launch, then turns the winners into a flow.

Figure 3. Storyboard of the launch brainstorm

  1. Open the Bookshelf launch deck, right-click the first page and select Expand to whiteboard.

  2. Press S once for each idea: a book of the week post, a story, a LinkedIn banner and a meetup invite.

  3. Drag across the notes, select Sort, choose By topic, then react to the ideas you prefer.

  4. Start the 5-minute timer so that the group votes before it ends.

  5. Select Share, invite the team as Editors and choose Download with PDF (Print) to attach the result to the campaign brief.
