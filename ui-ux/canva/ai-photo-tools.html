AI Photo Tools

This content was generated with the assistance of AI and should be verified against the official documentation before being relied on in production.

This page was checked against the official Canva Help Center on 2026-11-07. It covers the tools that change a photograph with the help of AI in the Canva editor (web, desktop app and mobile apps): Background Remover for photos and video, Magic Eraser, Pixel Eraser, Magic Grab, Grab Text, Magic Expand, Magic Edit, Magic Layers and Magic Background. Plans and AI tiers differ by tool. Background Remover is described by its Help Center article as included with paid plans; Pixel Eraser is described as available to all Canva users; Magic Layers is listed as a Premium AI tool, which needs Canva Pro, Teams, Business, Enterprise or Nonprofit; and the articles for Magic Eraser, Magic Grab, Magic Expand, Magic Edit and Magic Background only point to the AI-usage article without naming a tier, so check the comparison table on https://www.canva.com/pricing/ and the tiers (Standard, Premium and Ultra) explained on Plans, Pricing and AI Allowance. Prices are not repeated here. Another Help Center article (What’s new in Canva AI) says that Free and Education accounts include some AI features such as Background Remover with limited monthly usage, so what you see depends on your account; the safest reading is to try the tool and check the usage article if it is missing. All the tools described here are generally available. The conversational Canva AI 2.0 was launched as a research preview on 2026-04-16 and is now documented without a preview label; no general-availability date is published. It can also remove a background, as described below.

The running example is Bookshelf, the book catalogue and reading-list app designed in the Figma sub-section. Its launch campaign needs a clean "Book of the week" cover photo: the book on a desk, with a stray coffee cup removed, the frame extended to a portrait story shape and the book cut out to use as a sticker on the launch post. For the tools that change plain photographs (adjust, filters, crop and effects) see Photos and Image Editing.

Where the tools live

Select a photo in the design and the quick actions toolbar offers photo tools. BG Remover has its own button; the other tools are in the edit panel that opens from Edit, under Tools. The Help Center steps for Magic Grab, Magic Expand and Grab Text say to choose Edit, go to Tools, select See all and scroll to the bottom, which means that those three are not shown in the short list. The photo editor outside a design opens from the homepage (Projects, Images, hover a photo, More, Edit image) and offers the same tools under Tools. Some tools also have a second entry: Background Remover works on whole folders from Projects and through the Canva AI chat, Magic Layers has a tile on the homepage and Magic Background is in the Edit Page panel when you select the background layer.

Choosing a tool

The tools overlap, so it helps to ask what you want to keep. If you want to throw something away, use Magic Eraser (an object) or Pixel Eraser (precise pixels). If you want to keep the subject and drop the scene, use Background Remover. If you want to keep both but move or resize the subject, use Magic Grab, which, in the words of the Help Center, keeps the background while Background Remover leaves only the subject. If you need more picture around the subject, use Magic Expand, and if you need something that was never there, use Magic Edit. A flat graphic, such as an exported poster, that you want to rebuild piece by piece belongs to Magic Layers.

Background Remover

Background Remover removes the background of a photo or a video. The Help Center describes it as an everyday editing tool rather than a content generator: it uses AI, but it does not add Canva’s AI-generated content metadata to the design, so using it on its own should not label your post as AI content on platforms such as Instagram or TikTok. If a design is flagged anyway, the article asks you to contact Canva Support with the design link.

Photos

Select the photo, choose BG Remover on the editor toolbar and wait for the background to be processed. From the homepage, Projects, Images, hover the photo, More, Edit image and Tools, BG Remover does the same without opening a design. In Canva Sheets, select the cells with the image, choose Actions in the floating toolbar, then Background Remover, and select Apply. In the Canva AI chat on the homepage you can upload a photo and type a prompt such as "Remove background"; from the editor, select an image, choose Ask Canva on the floating toolbar and send the same prompt. Both chat routes are described as available on paid plans only, and the chat does not support video backgrounds yet.

Refine the edges with erase and restore

After the background is removed, select the image and choose BG Remover again to reopen the tool. It opens the Pixel Eraser panel with Erase and Restore brushes and a brush size slider; hold Show original image to compare, use the undo and redo icons for strokes and select Done to save. While a brush is active, zooming and undoing or redoing strokes with the usual controls do not work, so zoom in before you start. Pixel Eraser also works on its own: select the image, choose Eraser, then Pixel Eraser, drag to erase and use the Restore brush for mistakes. It works on JPG and PNG files only, not on vector graphics or Canva elements, edits one image at a time, and fully removes pixels (it has no opacity setting).

Folders in bulk

From the homepage, Projects, hover a folder, More and Remove background process every supported photo in a folder; Canva creates a new folder named after the original with "- Edited" and leaves the originals unchanged. You can also tick several images in a folder and use BG Remover in the bottom toolbar, and the edited photos are added to the same folder. The limits are the following: paid plans only, up to 50 images at a time, one folder at a time, and uploaded images only, because Canva library photos and designs are skipped. If the option is greyed out the folder has more than 50 images; images marked with an error stay in the original folder.

Video

Select the video, choose BG Remover on the editor toolbar and wait. Videos must have an original length under 10 minutes; if you trimmed the video in Canva you must download and re-upload it first, and on mobile the video must be uploaded through the Uploads tab, not the camera roll or gallery.

Limits

Background Remover works for photos under 9 MB, is not available for vector images and downscales uploaded photos above 10 megapixels to 10 megapixels after the background is removed.

Source: Canva Help Center: Remove photo and video backgrounds and Canva Help Center: Erase parts of an image with the Pixel Eraser

Cut out the Bookshelf book for a sticker

  1. Open the "Book of the week" Instagram post and select the cover photo.

  2. Choose BG Remover on the editor toolbar and wait while the background is processed.

  3. Select the image again and choose BG Remover to reopen the tool, then pick Erase or Restore and adjust the brush size.

  4. Brush along the edges, using a small brush near the spine and zooming in first; hold Show original image to compare.

  5. Select Done, then resize the cut-out and place it on the launch post as a sticker.

Magic Eraser

Magic Eraser removes unwanted objects. Select the photo (photos inside grids and frames can be selected too), choose Edit on the editor toolbar and then Magic Eraser under Tools. Choose how to select: Brush to paint over the object, Click to select objects (tap several to select several), Foreground to select the main subject and, in the editor, Text to select the text in the image. Deselect clears the selection. Then select Erase and wait. Reset Edits discards the result; in the photo editor you can also undo and redo, hold Compare to see the original, select Save (the edited photo is saved as a separate file in the Images tab and in Uploads), Download, or Create design to put it into a design. If the object is still there, try erasing a slightly larger area around it, and zoom in and move around the image for precision.

Magic Eraser and Magic Grab both take something out of a picture; Magic Eraser deletes it, while Magic Grab isolates it so you can move or reuse it.

Source: Canva Help Center: Erase or retouch photos

Magic Grab

Magic Grab makes an object in a photo selectable. Select the photo (Magic Grab does not support images inside frames or grids), choose Edit, then Tools, See all and scroll to Magic Grab. Use Click to select an object, or drag over several, or use Brush; select Grab and wait. You can then drag the subject to a new place, resize it or edit it independently of the photo background. To go back, use undo in the same session, or delete the grabbed subject and select Magic Grab again to remove its active tick, which restores the original background image.

Source: Canva Help Center: Make your photo selectable and customizable with Magic Grab

Grab Text

Grab Text turns text that is part of a photograph into editable text. It lives under Tools, See all, at the bottom of the list. The tool, its limits (it does not keep bold, italic or underline, does not read arched or non-Latin text) and a worked scan example are covered on Text and Typography, so they are not repeated here. Use it only on your own images.

Source: Canva Help Center: Selecting and changing the text in your photos with Grab Text

Magic Expand

Magic Expand extends an image in any direction, which fixes awkward framing, saves zoomed-in images or turns a vertical shot into a horizontal one. Select the photo, choose Edit, then Tools, See All and Magic Expand. Choose a size, or drag any crop handle to expand the image beyond its border, select Expand and wait. A downloaded expansion is only available as part of the design, not as a separate file. Images with faces, hands or transparent backgrounds may not expand well, so expand first and remove the background afterwards.

Source: Canva Help Center: Expand images

Magic Edit

Magic Edit adds, replaces or changes things in a photo from a prompt. Select the photo, choose Edit, then Magic Edit under Tools, and select an area with All (the whole image), Brush or Click (drag over several objects). Describe what to add or replace and select Generate. The Help Center’s tips are to brush an area roughly the shape of the thing you add (a round area for a tennis ball, an elongated one for a pencil), to brush nearby elements so that perspective and light match, to add one thing at a time, to avoid describing size in the prompt and to brush a larger area instead, and to avoid flags, text, faces, hands, celebrities and popular brands. Thin brushing can give empty results. To change a colour or material, use prompts like "change jacket to blue". Magic Edit creates new content, so unlike Background Remover it adds Canva’s AI-generated content metadata. Team admins, Teachers and brand designers can control who has access to it, and Education admins and owners can enable it for students.

Source: Canva Help Center: Use Magic Edit to add, replace, and modify photos

Magic Layers

Magic Layers uses AI to break a flat image into separate editable layers while keeping layout, hierarchy and design intent. From the homepage, select Magic Layers, choose Select Media and pick an image; or in a design, add the image from Uploads, select it, choose Edit on the toolbar and then Magic Layers. Wait while it processes, then select a layer to edit, move or delete it. You can move, resize and delete elements, apply effects to individual layers and replace elements with new images or graphics.

The supported files are single-page PNG and JPG. PDFs, vector graphics, licensed images (for example Disney or Hasbro), multi-page documents and photo-realistic imagery are not supported or give variable results, and images over 5000 pixels on the longest edge are not supported. It works best on graphic designs, illustrations and stylised visuals with distinct elements. To use it on a PDF, convert one page to PNG or JPG first. Because it is a Premium AI tool, it also stops when the monthly allowance is used up.

Figure 3. Magic Layers turns a flat poster into a stack of layers you can move, resize, delete or replace

Source: Canva Help Center: Turn images into editable layers with Magic Layers

Magic Background

Magic Background generates background options that match your text and images. It works on fixed-size designs such as presentations, Instagram posts and posters, not on Docs or Websites, and it is not available in China. Your design needs at least one element; select the background layer, choose Edit on the toolbar and Magic BG in the Edit Page panel, optionally describe the idea under Describe your background idea, and select Generate. Canva produces four options; select one to preview it, update the description and generate again for more, or choose Surprise me, then pick one to apply it. It works best with readable text, distinct foreground visuals and uncluttered designs.

Source: Canva Help Center: Generate AI backgrounds with Magic Background

Clean up and extend the Bookshelf cover photo

  1. Select the cover photo in the "Book of the week" post and choose Edit on the editor toolbar.

  2. Under Tools choose Magic Eraser, pick Click, select the coffee cup and select Erase; reset the edit if the result is wrong.

  3. Choose Magic Expand (under See all), pick a portrait size or drag a crop handle so the photo reaches story proportions, and select Expand.

  4. Choose Magic Grab, click the book, select Grab and drag the book to the lower third, leaving room for the title.

  5. Add the title and save; remember that Magic Grab does not work on images inside frames or grids.

Common pitfalls

  • Brush modes disable zoom and undo while active; zoom in before painting.

  • Expanding a photo that has faces, hands or a transparent background can look wrong.

  • Magic Grab does not work on photos placed in frames or grids, so use it on a photo that is not in one.

  • Magic Layers rejects PDFs, vectors, multi-page files and images over 5000 pixels.

  • Background Remover skips library photos and designs in a folder, stops at 50 images and downscales very large photos.

  • Magic Edit adds an AI-generated label to the design, Background Remover does not.

  • Tools missing from the list may be a plan, region, language or admin setting; check the AI-usage article and your team’s settings.

  • Check the downloaded file, not only the editor, because an expansion can only be downloaded with its design.
