Resize, Bulk Create and Translate

This content was generated with the assistance of AI and should be verified against the official documentation before being relied on in production.

This page was checked against the official Canva Help Center on 2026-11-07. It covers three features that multiply one finished design: Magic Resize, which converts a design to other sizes and formats; Bulk Create, which produces many designs from rows of data; and Translate, which converts the text of a design into another language. All three live in the standard Canva editor of the Visual Suite.

Plans differ by feature. The Resize article lists Pro, Business, Enterprise, Education, Higher education, Nonprofits and Teams, and says each use of Magic Resize counts against the monthly AI usage limit. The Bulk Create article lists Pro, Teams, Business, Enterprise, Education and Nonprofit plans, on desktop web and the Canva desktop app. The Translate article names no plan, so confirm availability in your account; it points to the AI access article for usage limits. The tier model (Standard, Premium and Ultra) is described on Plans, Pricing and AI Allowance, and prices are on https://www.canva.com/pricing/. The Help Center presents all three features without a beta label.

The running example is Bookshelf, the book catalogue and reading-list app designed in the Figma sub-section. Its launch campaign starts from one square "Book of the week" post. Magic Resize turns it into a story, a LinkedIn banner and a YouTube thumbnail; Bulk Create turns a table of twenty books into twenty cards; Translate produces a Spanish version. The campaign is scheduled as described on Social Media and the Content Planner.

Choosing the right tool

The three features solve different problems, and a fourth, Data Autofill, is often confused with Bulk Create.

Magic Resize

Magic Resize changes the format, layout or dimensions of a design. The Help Center article is titled "Resize designs and size limits" and the menu item is Resize in the editor menu bar. In the Help Center the feature is called Magic Resize; older books call the same area "Resize & Magic Switch".

Figure 1. One square master becomes a story, a banner and a thumbnail

Where it lives and every option

Select Resize in the editor menu bar to open the panel.

Figure 2. The Resize panel: suggestions and bundles, custom size, the pages to resize and the two buttons

  1. Suggestions and categories. Pick common sizes from the suggestions or use Browse by category. You can choose several sizes at once.

  2. Bundles. Under Suggested you find bundles such as vertical videos or your last selection. A bundle groups common formats, for example a set of social posts. Select it to see the formats it contains, and tick or untick each one before you create the copies.

  3. Custom size. Select Custom size to enter your own measurements. A lock beside the fields keeps or releases the aspect ratio.

  4. Pages. Choose pages under All pages; otherwise the size applies to all of them.

  5. Copy & resize creates new designs. A single copy lands in the same folder as the original; several sizes are saved in a new folder whose name appears in the confirmation message.

  6. Resize this design changes the design in place. Version history can restore the earlier size, and only when you resized directly rather than made a copy: open File, then Version history, choose the autosaved version and select Restore, or Make a copy to keep both.

The same article describes resizing one page without touching the rest: select the page background or its thumbnail, choose Edit in the floating toolbar to open the Edit Page panel, then Resize.

Source: Canva Help Center: Resize designs and size limits

If a Custom size field turns red the value is outside the limits. To get the proportions of an oversized target, such as a very wide banner, enter the target size, lock the aspect ratio, bring one value inside the limits so the other follows, and download the result as PNG at 0.5 up to 3.125 times its size. This does not create a design in the target size; it gives the same proportion.

Source: Canva Help Center: Resize designs and size limits

Resize canvas to Selection

A related command crops the page to what you selected. Select two or more elements, a group, or a single image or video, right-click the selection (or select More on mobile) and choose Resize canvas to Selection. The page fits the bounding box of the selection and the elements stay where they are; Undo reverses it. The Help Center states it is not available in Canva Websites, Docs or Sheets.

Where Magic Resize does not apply

  • Canva Docs do not support Magic Resize. The article says to use Magic Switch to transform a Doc into a presentation, which is the one place where the older name survives.

  • When you resize a presentation to video dimensions, each slide becomes its own video page instead of one long video, so no full timeline appears. Select the pages and choose Edit as video, or hold Shift, select the page thumbnails and choose Merge videos. See Video Editor, Audio and Captions.

  • From a sheet you can resize many designs by link; that route is documented on Sheets and has its own limits of up to five sizes per design.

  • Designs you cannot edit cannot be resized; Canva asks you to request access.

Desktop and mobile follow the same steps in the article, with the mobile wording differing only in where the buttons sit.

Procedure 1: resize the Book of the week post for three channels

  1. Open the square "Book of the week" master built from the brand kit on Brand Kit and Brand Voice.

  2. Select Resize in the editor menu bar.

  3. Under Suggested, open a bundle or search Browse by category, then tick Instagram story, LinkedIn banner and YouTube thumbnail. Untick formats you do not need.

  4. Leave All pages selected, because the master has one page.

  5. Select Copy & resize. Because you chose several sizes, Canva saves them in a new folder named in the confirmation message.

  6. Open each copy and check that the title still fits, that the book cover is not cropped and that the call-to-action sits clear of the platform edges.

  7. Adjust text sizes and positions by hand where Magic Resize reflowed them awkwardly.

Figure 3. Storyboard: from the square master to three resized copies

Source: Canva Help Center: Resize designs and size limits

Procedure 2: crop the page to a group of elements

  1. Select the cover image and the title text, or group them.

  2. Right-click the selection and choose Resize canvas to Selection.

  3. Check that the page now fits the group, and use Undo if it does not.

Source: Canva Help Center: Resize designs and size limits

Bulk Create

Bulk Create links data organised in rows and columns to placeholder elements on a design page. Each column is a data field, and each row creates one version of the design. You can connect up to 150 data fields. Availability is desktop web and the Canva desktop app.

Figure 4. Data columns on the left map to placeholder text boxes on the right, then four steps produce the designs

Prepare the template

Before connecting data, add the elements Canva will fill. Use a separate text box for each text field and a frame or a grid for each image. For a multi-page design, connect data fields on each page. Brand templates with locked regions are covered on Brand Templates, Controls and Approvals.

Start Bulk Create

  • From the editor: click the page background, select Edit in the toolbar, then Bulk create in the side panel. Under Choose a starting point, choose Bulk Create to make designs from data, or Labels to set fields that connected apps fill.

  • From the Apps tab: under More from Canva, select Bulk create.

  • From a Canva Sheet: select the range, open Actions and choose Bulk Create. The sheet route is detailed on Sheets, including the data rules for ranges and media.

Add the data

Source How it works

Canva Sheet

Select a range, then Actions and Bulk Create, then Continue and choose a design. You can select up to 300 rows and 150 columns.

CSV or XLSX file

In the Bulk create tab select Upload data, pick the file, choose a sheet if the workbook has several, add a range if needed and select Continue. Save from Google Sheets with File then Download, from Excel with File then Save As, or from Numbers with File then Export To.

Data apps

Choose a connected platform such as Google Analytics, HubSpot, Statista or World Bank, sign in if asked and pick a data set.

Manual table

Select Enter data manually, optionally Clear table, add Add text and Add image columns, fill the cells and add rows with Add row, then Done.

Images work with Canva Sheets and XLSX files, but image URLs from cloud photo storage are not supported and are treated as plain text. Embed each image in a single cell, not floating over several cells. A plain CSV carries text only, so covers need a Sheet, an XLSX file or the manual table.

A text-only CSV for the Bookshelf cards looks like this:

Title,Author,Tagline,Genre
Emma,Jane Austen,A comedy of matchmaking,Classic
Dracula,Bram Stoker,Told through letters and diaries,Gothic
Ulysses,James Joyce,One long day in Dublin,Modern

Source: Canva Help Center: Create designs in bulk

Connect, preview and create

Select Match and fill fields. Canva analyses the design and connects the fields it can identify; the side panel offers Auto-match fields when you start from a Sheet. To correct a match, select the element, choose Match data in the toolbar and pick the field under Connect data. If most matches are wrong, select Connect manually while the analysis runs, which cancels the AI step; if only a few are wrong, let it finish and re-map those elements by drag and drop or by selecting the element.

Select Preview to check each row. The preview is read-only, videos show a frozen first frame, some 3D elements may not render, and media replacement in the preview works only with a directly linked Canva Sheet. Then open More options (the three dots) beside Create designs and set the Output (one design with a page per row, or individual designs), how each page or design is named and the Save in folder. Select Create [number] designs; a message confirms they are saved to Projects, and View design opens the result.

Procedure 3: create twenty Book of the week cards

  1. Build a table with the columns Title, Author, Tagline, Genre and Cover, one row per book, in Canva Sheets or an XLSX file. Embed each cover in its cell.

  2. Open the Bookshelf card template and make sure there is one text box per text field and one frame for the cover.

  3. Click the page background, select Edit, then Bulk create, and under Choose a starting point choose Bulk Create.

  4. Choose Upload data and select the file, or start from the sheet range with Actions, then Bulk Create.

  5. Select Match and fill fields, then review the matches. Fix any wrong one with Match data in the toolbar.

  6. Select Preview and page through the rows, checking long titles and covers. Close the preview to adjust.

  7. Open More options beside Create designs, choose Individual designs if each card is scheduled on its own, set the naming and a Save in folder.

  8. Select Create 20 designs and open one with View design to confirm.

Translate

Translate converts the text of a design into another language. The Help Center says you can choose from 100+ languages. The default is Translate from: Automatically detect, and the most recently used language becomes the next default.

Ways to start

  • Right-click a text box and choose Translate text, then choose the language and tone and either Translate pages or Select text from current page.

  • Open the Apps tab, and under More from Canva select Translate.

  • Select the page background or thumbnail, choose Edit in the floating toolbar, then Translate in the Edit Page panel.

  • From Resize: the panel has a Magic Switch group with a Translate option. It translates one language at a time and offers Translate existing design without creating a copy; translating only selected text is not available there.

  • In Canva Sheets, select cells, choose Actions then Translate, then Translate text in selected cells, and decide whether to overwrite or Add to new column.

Options and limits

Figure 6. A Spanish translation: language and tone in the panel, then the three settings, then a review

  • Tone of voice is optional and available only for some languages: Original, Conversational, Informative, Professional, Friendly or Inspirational. The article lists English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Arabic, Indonesian, German, Italian, Hindi, Turkish, Japanese, Thai, Chinese (Simplified), Vietnamese, Russian, Chinese (Traditional), Dutch, Korean and Polish.

  • You translate one language at a time, and multiple pages within a 2000-character limit.

  • Settings: Reduce font size to fit is on by default to avoid overlap when the translation is longer; Duplicate page when translating is on by default so the original is kept and the translation is a copy; Mirror page to match text direction supports right-to-left languages.

  • Not every font supports every language. If your font is not supported, Translate asks you to choose another, and different characters in the result usually mean the font should be changed.

  • The article carries a note that AI usage limits changed; check the allowance and tier on Plans, Pricing and AI Allowance.

Translate with Smartling

For professional human translation, the Smartling app in the Apps tab connects a Smartling account. You choose the Smartling project, whole pages or single text elements, give the job a unique name with an optional due date and instructions, choose target languages (set per project inside Smartling) and select Submit. Progress shows when you reopen the app, and an email arrives when the job finishes; then select the languages and Generate translations. The service is a third party; see https://www.smartling.com/ for its own documentation.

Procedure 4: translate the Book of the week card into Spanish

  1. Open the English card, right-click its title text box and choose Translate text.

  2. Under Translate to choose Spanish. Choose a Tone of voice such as Friendly, which is offered for Spanish.

  3. Choose Translate pages and select the page, then select Translate.

  4. Open the Settings tab and keep Reduce font size to fit and Duplicate page when translating on, so the English original stays.

  5. Review the Spanish page: line breaks, text that grew past its box, and that the font shows every accent.

  6. Have a Spanish speaker check the wording before publishing.

Source: Canva Help Center: Translate your Canva designs

Procedure 5: a Spanish batch of twenty cards

Translation and Bulk Create combine in two ways. Translate the data first: in the sheet select the Tagline cells, choose Actions, Translate, a Spanish language, then Add to new column so both languages stay side by side, and run Procedure 3 with the Spanish column connected. Or generate the English cards first and translate their pages afterwards, remembering the 2000-character limit per run and one language at a time. The first route keeps one template; the second repeats the translate steps for every design.

Source: Canva Help Center: Translate your Canva designs

Common mistakes

  • Running Copy & resize on a design with several pages and forgetting the All pages choice.

  • Expecting a CSV to carry cover images; use a Sheet, an XLSX file or the manual table.

  • Using one text box for two fields, so the match is wrong or impossible.

  • Trusting the automatic matching without reading the side panel.

  • Not allowing for longer Spanish or German text: widen the text boxes before translating.

  • Resizing in place without a copy, and then needing Version history to go back.

Where older books are outdated

  • "Resize & Magic Switch" as a menu item. Older books teach this name. The Help Center today titles the article "Resize designs and size limits" and calls the feature Magic Resize; the Magic Switch name remains only as a heading in the Resize panel and in the Docs note, and has no article of its own.

  • Resize as a single step. Books describe resizing one design. Today you can resize into bundles, up to five sizes per design and up to 50 designs, and from a sheet.

  • Resize and plan. Resize now counts against the monthly AI usage limit.

  • Bulk Create from the Apps tab only. Today it also starts from the editor Edit panel and from a Sheet, with an Auto-match fields option, data apps and a manual table.

  • Translate as a Magic Switch tool. It is now a standalone app, a right-click command, an Edit Page panel option and a Sheets action, with tone of voice and settings.
