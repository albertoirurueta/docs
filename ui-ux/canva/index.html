Canva

This content was generated with the assistance of AI and should be verified against the official documentation before being relied on in production.

Canva is a browser-based visual communication platform. Its Visual Suite covers designs, presentations, docs, whiteboards, video, websites, sheets, code, forms and email, around brand tools and the Canva AI assistant. This sub-section is a practical, heavily illustrated guide to learning Canva and using it to produce on-brand visual content: the account, homepage and editor, design principles, every Visual Suite product, Canva AI, brand kits and templates, collaboration and administration, output formats and accessibility, apps and integrations, and the Canva Developers SDK. It belongs to the UI / UX section. Every page was checked against the official documentation on 2026-11-07 and describes the editor after its "glow up" redesign.

Scope

  • In scope: every area of the official Canva documentation listed in the coverage map, explained with original schematic figures, numbered procedures on one running scenario and a link to the official article each procedure derives from.

  • Also in scope: the concepts taught by two published Canva books (see the bibliography). The books were used only to decide which concepts to cover and in what order. Every procedure is written against the current official documentation, and where a book is out of date the page says so in prose with dates, collected on What Changed Since Older Guides. No book text, figure, screenshot or template is reproduced.

  • Out of scope: prices (pages name plans and AI tiers, never amounts; see Canva pricing) and any third-party tool beyond a short, labelled ecosystem mention.

  • Figures: all figures are original generic schematic drawings. They contain no Canva logo, icon, template design or help-centre image, in line with the Canva Trademark Policy.

Version baseline

Canva is continuously deployed software with no version numbers, so pages state the date the facts were checked and cite announcements by date. The table below was re-verified against the official Canva pages on 2026-11-07. Prices vary by region and are never repeated here.

Fact Value on 2026-11-07 Pages

Product family

The Visual Suite (designs, presentations, Docs, whiteboards, video, websites) introduced as Visual Suite 2.0 at Canva Create on 2025-04-11, plus Sheets, Canva Code (version 2.0 generally available to all users on 2026-07-14), Forms, Canva Email, Canva Grow, Canva Print and Affinity (free with a Canva account since 2025-10-30, part of Canva ProSuite). Leonardo.Ai, Flourish and MagicBrief are group companies, not Canva features.

Introduction and the Canva Product Family, Sheets, Canva Code, Forms and Canva Email, Canva Grow and Marketing Analytics, Affinity

Canva AI

Canva AI 2.0 launched as a research preview at Canva Create on 2026-04-16 and is now documented in the Help Center without a preview label. No general-availability date is published, so availability varies by account and plan. It is conversational and agentic, built on the Canva Design Model. The Magic Studio tool names survive for individual tools. Dream Lab arrived in 2024-10 and Canva Craft, Canva’s own image model, was announced on 2026-10-22.

Canva AI Assistant, Magic Studio Tools, AI Image and Video Generation

Plans

Free, Pro, Business and Enterprise, plus free Education (K-12) and Nonprofits programmes and Canva Campus for higher education. Canva Business replaced Canva Teams for new sign-ups on 2025-10-30; existing Teams subscribers keep their plan without the Business-only features.

Plans, Pricing and AI Allowance, Teams, Roles and Groups, Education and Nonprofits

AI allowance

A monthly allowance per member. AI tools are tiered Standard, Premium and Ultra: Standard is unmetered on paid plans, Ultra is paid only, and the AI Pass and AI top-ups add-ons raise the allowance.

Plans, Pricing and AI Allowance

Editor

The "glow up" redesign added the quick actions toolbar, the edit panel and a redesigned side panel (Design, Elements, Text, Brand, Uploads, Tools, Projects, Apps, Canva AI). Draw moved into the Tools tab. Quick actions open with the slash key or Cmd/Ctrl+E.

Editor Tour, Drawing and the Tools Tab, Keyboard Shortcuts and Quick Actions

Mobile

The tabs are Canva AI, Create (formerly Home), Templates, Your Designs (formerly Projects) and More. The apps are named Canva Classic and Canva Lite.

Desktop, Mobile and Offline, Account, Homepage and Navigation

Renamed and retired

Magic Switch no longer has a help article; the feature is Magic Resize. Smartmockups.com closed on 2024-09-27 and mockups live in the editor. The Canva Button was shut down outside China, with keys deactivated after 2025-12-30.

Resize, Bulk Create and Translate, Print and Mockups, Apps Marketplace and Integrations, What Changed Since Older Guides

Developer platform

Renamed the Canva Developers SDK (around 2026-09) with three surfaces: apps inside Canva, Canva for your platform through the REST APIs and Canva for AI assistants through the Canva MCP server (self-serve since 2026-10-27). Packages: @canva/cli 2.17.0, @canva/design 2.14.0, @canva/app-ui-kit 5.15.0 and @canva/app-scripts 1.1.2 (generally available since 2026-08-12). REST service apps arrived on 2026-11-06.

What is new

The Help Center has no release-notes page. Follow the monthly "New in Canva" pages on Design School and the launches page.

Plan availability

Data connectors in Sheets are available on Business and Enterprise only. Websites are available on all four plans. Canva AI Connector: core use needs no paid plan, while resize, autofill, brand kits and brand templates need Pro or above, and availability varies by connector and account.

Sheets, Websites, Canva in AI Assistants, The Canva MCP Server

Brand and content rules

Canva’s name and logo follow its Trademark Policy and must not be imitated. Library content follows the Content License Agreement (Free, Pro, Branded and Education content).

Accessibility

Canva states WCAG 2.1 AA support in its core experiences. The Design Accessibility checker lives under File > Accessibility, with alt text and a decorative mark, accessible PDF export, captions, reduce-motion and high-contrast settings.

Glossary

Short definitions of terms used throughout the sub-section.

Term Meaning

Visual Suite

Canva’s family of products that share one editor: designs, presentations, Docs, whiteboards, video and websites.

Canva AI

The conversational, agentic assistant introduced as Canva AI 2.0 on 2026-04-16, built on the Canva Design Model.

Magic Studio

The collective name of the individual AI tools such as Magic Write, Magic Design, Magic Resize and Magic Eraser.

Brand Kit

A saved set of colours, fonts, logos and other assets that keeps designs on brand.

Brand template

A design published for a team to reuse, with locked regions that protect the brand.

Bulk Create

A feature that fills the fields of one design from the rows of a data file to produce many designs at once.

Content Planner

The calendar where designs are scheduled for publishing to connected social accounts.

Bleed

Extra artwork beyond the trim edge of a print design so that no white edge shows after cutting.

Safe zone

The inner area of a page where important content stays clear of the trim edge.

Quick actions

The toolbar and slash menu in the editor that put the next likely action one step away.

AI allowance

The monthly amount of Premium and Ultra AI usage included per member, extended by AI Pass or top-ups.

Canva Developers SDK

The developer platform with three surfaces: apps inside Canva, the REST APIs and the Canva MCP server.

Intent

The kind of experience a Canva app provides inside the editor, such as Design Editor or Data Connector.

PKCE

Proof Key for Code Exchange, the extension of OAuth 2.0 used by the authorization code flow of the REST APIs.

MCP

The Model Context Protocol, an open standard that lets AI assistants call tools such as Canva.

Affinity

The professional vector, photo and layout app from Canva, free to use with a Canva account.

Canva or Figma?

Both tools are browser-based and collaborative, but they answer different questions. Canva is built for visual communication: presentations, social posts, documents, video, websites and print, produced quickly from templates and kept on brand with brand kits. Figma is built for product design: interface screens, components, design systems, prototypes and handoff to developers. Many teams use both, designing the product in Figma and producing its launch material in Canva, which is exactly what the Bookshelf scenario does. Canva can also import Figma files, as described on Uploads, Stock Media and Imports.

Running example

Every example produces the launch campaign of Bookshelf, the book catalogue and reading-list app designed in the Figma sub-section. It is the same domain as the Express, Node.js, NestJS, FastAPI and Next.js sections of this site, so the design side, the marketing side and the code side meet. Across the pages the campaign grows in this order:

  1. A whiteboard brainstorm and a campaign brief Doc.

  2. The Bookshelf brand kit: colours and fonts from the Figma Bookshelf Design System, logo variants and a brand voice.

  3. A social campaign: an Instagram post and story, a LinkedIn banner and a YouTube thumbnail made with Magic Resize and scheduled in the Content Planner.

  4. A "Book of the week" brand template, filled by Bulk Create from a CSV file and translated into Spanish.

  5. A launch presentation for a book-club meetup, with presenter notes, a Canva Live Q&A, a quiz and a recorded version.

  6. A 30-second promo video with Beat Sync, captions and a voiceover.

  7. A one-page launch website on a custom domain with Magic SEO and analytics.

  8. A print flyer and bookmarks ordered through Canva Print, plus a phone mockup.

  9. A reading-challenge tracker in Sheets with Magic Formulas and Magic Insights, and a Canva Code reading-streak widget that uses it.

  10. A review form and a newsletter in Canva Email.

  11. Canva AI prompts throughout, and an accessibility check before publishing.

  12. Developers: a "Bookshelf Covers" app that inserts book covers, a REST script that autofills the brand template and exports PNG files, and the Canva MCP server driven from Claude Code.
