Magic Studio Tools

This content was generated with the assistance of AI and should be verified against the official documentation before being relied on in production.

This page was checked against the official Canva Help Center, the Canva AI page and the pricing page on 2026-11-07. It maps the individual AI tools that Canva still calls its Magic tools. They are part of Canva (web, desktop app and mobile apps) and of Canva Sheets, and each one does a single job inside the editor: write words, draft or resize a design, animate pages, generate or change pictures, or turn spreadsheet cells into formulas and charts. The tools are available on different plans, so each tool’s subscription requirement and AI tier are given below. Prices are not repeated here; they are published at https://www.canva.com/pricing/.

Magic Studio was introduced as the umbrella name for these tools. Since 2026-04-16 the conversational assistant Canva AI 2.0 sits above them: it launched that day as a research preview, the Help Center now documents it without a preview label, and no general-availability date is published, so availability varies by account, language and region. The tools on this page are separate, single-prompt features that exist alongside it and that the assistant can call. Canva AI 2.0 itself is described on Canva AI Assistant. Image and video generation are covered in depth on AI Image and Video Generation, the photo tools on AI Photo Tools, and the legal side on Licensing, Responsible AI and Canva Shield. This page is the map: what each tool is for, where it lives, what it costs in AI allowance, who can switch it off, and three short procedures.

The running example is Bookshelf, the book catalogue and reading-list app designed in the Figma sub-section. Its launch campaign needs a caption for the "Book of the week" post, a reading-challenge sheet with a total and a chart, and a team whose members use AI tools on the settings the Bookshelf owner chooses.

The tools in one picture

The Magic tools fall into four groups according to the thing they work on. Words have one tool, whole designs three, photos and video the most, and data in Sheets three. The tool names are stable, but the places where Canva shows them move between releases, so the next figure and table give names and paths rather than icons.

Where the tools live in the editor

There is no single Magic menu. Each tool is reached from the place where its input already is: the quick actions launcher for text, the toolbar of a selected photo for photo tools, the Animate button for motion, the Resize entry in the menu bar, the Apps tab for generators, and the floating toolbar of a sheet for data tools. The editor itself is described on Editor Tour; the launcher is covered on Keyboard Shortcuts and Quick Actions.

If a tab such as Magic Media is not in the side panel, open Apps and search for it; the Help Center gives that route for Magic Media. A team administrator can also remove a tool from the people on a team (see the admin section below), and some tools depend on the language and region of the account.

AI tiers and the monthly allowance

Canva groups its AI tools into three tiers. Standard tools do not draw on the shared allowance on paid plans, fair-use limits aside; on Free they do, and they use the least of it. Premium tools use more of a monthly allowance that resets each month, and Ultra tools use the latest models and the most allowance. The Help Center names Image Upscaler and Magic Formulas as Standard; Magic Layers, creating an image or a design with Canva AI, Canva Code, generated elements, the music and sound-effect generator and the form generator as Premium; and conversational design with Canva AI 2.0 and the video clip as Ultra.

Figure 3. The three AI tiers and how the allowance grows with your subscription

Each member has their own allowance on Business, Nonprofits, Education and Enterprise, and it is not pooled across a team. The Free plan has limited access to Standard and Premium tools and none to Ultra. Pro, Nonprofits and Education members get 10 times the Free allowance for Premium and Ultra tools, and Business and Enterprise members get 20 times (counts as published on 2026-11-07). AI Pass is an add-on for eligible plans that adds more usage, and Business can also buy AI top-ups. The usage tracker is in Settings, under Billing, in the AI usage section: from the homepage select your profile icon, then Settings, then Billing.

Two details avoid confusion. The tier article and the pricing page disagree in places; for example, the Help Center article on Magic Formulas still calls the tool premium while the tier article lists it as Standard, and the pricing grid lists the single-prompt design tools (Magic Write, Magic Resize and the photo tools) under tools that do not draw on the shared allowance, while the Resize article says each use counts against the monthly limit. When the sources differ, the Billing page of your own account is the authority. Second, the allowance covers only Canva’s own tools: generations in third-party apps of the Apps Marketplace do not count, and their own limits apply.

The tools in detail

Magic Write

Magic Write generates text from a prompt, or changes text that already exists. In an open design, select Quick actions and choose Magic Write, or type / to open the launcher, enter the text you want, and generate. Esc, or the Stop generation button, interrupts it. More like this produces a similar result, This but…​ takes additional instructions, and the chevrons compare results; Insert places the text in the design and a copy icon copies it.

For existing text, right-click it (or use the icon), choose Magic Write, and pick Transform text, Rewrite, Fix Spelling, Continue writing, Shorten, More fun, More formal, Change voice (including a saved tone of voice), Generate quiz or Generate AI voice. Then Replace, Add below or copy the result. Shorten replaced the older Summarize, and Create Summary summarises selected shapes, lines, arrows, sticky notes and text. In a presentation, Notes then Generate notes uses the page content or a custom prompt, and the generated notes can be continued, shortened, rewritten or made more fun or formal. On the homepage, Canva AI then Draft a doc starts a document from a prompt. Writing prompts for the Bookshelf campaign are practised in the first procedure below.

Magic Design

Magic Design is reached through Canva AI on the homepage or in the editor. Describe the design, optionally pick a design type, use the microphone to speak the prompt, and use the plus icon to upload a file, add an existing design or upload, apply a Brand Kit or connect a third-party app. The result shows up to eight templates; Brand Hub incorporates the brand’s styles. The Help Center lists the design types: presentations, posters, cards, flyers, square invitations, business cards, postcards, documents and social designs (Instagram posts and stories, Facebook posts and covers, Pinterest pins, YouTube thumbnails and banners, X posts). Videos can be 1920 by 1080, 1080 by 1920 or 1080 by 1080 pixels. The templates side is described on Templates, Layouts and Magic Design.

Magic Resize

Resize in the editor’s menu bar opens a list of suggested sizes, with Browse by category, Custom size (with a lock for the aspect ratio) and a choice of pages. Copy & resize leaves the original and saves one or several copies; several sizes are saved in a new folder. Resize this design changes the open design. Resize to Selection resizes the current page to the bounding box of the selected elements. The campaign use, Instagram post to story, LinkedIn banner and YouTube thumbnail, is on Resize, Bulk Create and Translate. "Magic Switch" no longer has a Help Center article; the feature is called Magic Resize.

Magic Animate

On the toolbar choose Animate, open the Page tab and select Magic Animate. A recommended style is applied to the entire design, alternative styles can be chosen, and Remove Magic Animation clears it. It is best on a computer but is also available on mobile devices. It appears only for presentation, social media and video design types, so a missing entry usually means a different design type. Animations are limited to 10 seconds each and 50 per design, and the Reduce motion setting reduces or removes them. Details are on Animation and Transitions.

Magic Media

Magic Media has Images, Graphics, Videos and 3D tabs. Images offer the styles photography, digital art and fine art, an optional reference image and square, landscape or portrait layouts; graphics offer simple, artistic and detailed styles; a generated video is 4 seconds long; 3D elements come from the 3D tab or from the Generate button in Elements. Inspire me proposes prompts and Generate more like this repeats a result you like. The Help Center notes that this Text to Video differs from the Create a video clip tool of Canva AI. Dream Lab, Canva’s image generator on the homepage under More, with reference images, edit prompts and Boost resolution, is covered on AI Image and Video Generation.

The photo tools

Magic Edit, Magic Eraser, Magic Expand, Magic Grab, Magic Layers and Background Remover work on a selected photo and are described in full, with before and after figures, on AI Photo Tools. In short: Magic Edit changes a brushed, clicked or whole-image selection from a description; Magic Eraser removes an object using Brush, Click, Foreground or Text selection; Magic Expand extends the frame, and works poorly with faces, hands or transparent backgrounds; Magic Grab lifts an object out as its own layer and does not support images in frames or grids; Magic Layers splits PNG or JPG images up to 5000 pixels on the longest edge into layers; Background Remover works on photos and videos, in a folder for up to 50 uploaded images at a time, and does not label a design as AI content.

Sheets tools

Magic Formulas, Magic Insights and Magic Charts act on cells in Canva Sheets, described on Sheets. Magic Formulas writes a formula for a single cell at a time, so AutoFill spreads it; it handles basic math, IF logic and lookups such as VLOOKUP and HLOOKUP, needs an internet connection, and its suggest-a-fix feature only corrects syntax. Magic Insights needs a table with descriptive column headers and a selection of more than one cell; leaving the prompt blank returns a data summary, formulas and charts, and its charts do not update when the data changes, so run it again. Magic Charts is different: its charts stay linked to the sheet and sync automatically, and a Chart Source label shows the linked cells.

Admin controls

Owners and admins of Canva Teams, Canva Business, Canva Enterprise and Canva Education decide who can use Magic and AI features. Magic features are on for all members by default on Teams and Business; on Education, only administrators and teachers have access by default, and Magic Media, Dream Lab and Magic Insights are the only features enabled for students. The settings live under the profile menu, Settings, Controls and permissions, Permissions, then the Magic and AI section. The list includes Magic Write, Magic Design, Magic Activities, Magic Edit, Magic Media, Transform into Doc, Dream Lab, the Help Assistant for AI-generated design advice, Canva AI, Magic Insights, Canva Code, AI Shapes, AI Charts, using team content to generate AI responses, AI music and sound effects, Image to Video, and on-brand design generation.

On-brand generation needs two permissions at once: Magic Design, which controls the model used for generating designs, and Canva AI, which controls the entry points to AI experiences. With either off, members can still design manually with Brand Kits and Brand Templates. Changes apply to new designs; existing designs keep a Magic feature if it was already used. Availability can also depend on language and region, so some people lack a feature even when it is enabled. Canva’s pricing page describes the privacy side as granular privacy settings on Free and Pro and organisation admin controls on Business and Enterprise. Teams is no longer offered to new sign-ups; the details of roles are outside this page.

Source: Canva Help Center: Change access to Magic and AI features

Desktop and mobile differences

The Help Center writes most Magic procedures with the same steps for desktop and mobile. A few differences matter. Magic Animate is best on a computer. In brush modes of Magic Eraser and Magic Edit, zooming and undoing or redoing strokes do not work while you brush, so zoom first. Magic Video, which creates 60-second videos from 1 to 10 clips or photos, is included with all plans in several languages and is available through Canva AI on the web. Where a tool is missing on a device, update the app and check the language and region of the account.

Procedures on the Bookshelf campaign

Write the launch caption with Magic Write

The goal is a short caption for the "Book of the week" Instagram post, in a friendly voice.

  1. Open the Instagram post design and click an empty area of the page.

  2. Type / to open the quick actions launcher and choose Magic Write.

  3. Enter a prompt such as "Write a two-line caption for a book of the week post for a reading-list app, friendly and short" and generate it. Press Esc to stop if it goes the wrong way.

  4. Use More like this for a similar result, or This but…​ to add instructions such as "mention a reading challenge", and compare results with the chevrons.

  5. Select Insert to place the caption in the post. For text that already exists, right-click it, choose Magic Write and use Shorten or Change voice, then Replace.

  6. Read the result before publishing; AI text can contain mistakes.

Turn the reading-challenge sheet into a formula and a chart

The goal is the total number of books read in the Bookshelf reading-challenge sheet, plus a chart for the report page. It continues on Sheets.

  1. Open the sheet in Canva Sheets, with clear column headers such as Reader, Books read and Pages.

  2. Click the cell where the total belongs and type =; in the suggestion box choose Magic Formulas.

  3. Describe the formula in plain language, for example the prompt below, and select Insert.

  4. Adjust cell references if needed, and use AutoFill for other rows or columns; the formula covers a single cell.

  5. Select the data cells, choose Actions on the floating toolbar, then Magic Insights. Type a question or leave it blank for a summary, formulas and charts.

  6. Copy the summary or a formula, or use the chart menu to add the chart to a new presentation page. For a chart that stays synced with the sheet, use Actions, then Magic Charts instead.

Prompt:  Sum the values in column B
Typical result in the cell:  =SUM(B2:B31)

Limit Magic and AI features for the Bookshelf team

The goal is that only admins use Magic Edit while everyone keeps Magic Write. It applies to owners and admins of Business, Enterprise and Education (and existing Teams) only.

  1. From the homepage select your profile icon, then Settings.

  2. Under Controls and permissions, select Permissions.

  3. Open the Magic and AI section.

  4. Find the feature, for example Magic Edit, and choose who can access it.

  5. Review the dependencies: for on-brand generation, Magic Design and Canva AI must both stay on.

  6. Tell the team that the change applies to new designs, and that language and region can still hide features.

Pitfalls

  • Treating all Magic tools as free: Magic Resize, Magic Edit, Magic Eraser, Magic Expand, Magic Grab and Background Remover are not in Free, while Magic Write and Magic Charts are.

  • Expecting a Magic Insights chart to update with the data; it does not, whereas Magic Charts does.

  • Choosing Magic Animate on a design type without animations; it appears only for presentations, social media and video designs.

  • Using Magic Layers on a PDF, a vector file, a licensed image or an image above 5000 pixels; convert PDFs to PNG or JPG first.

  • Prompting with known brands, characters or people; the safe-use guidance asks for descriptive, generic prompts and for rights to any image you edit.

  • Assuming results are unique or protected: anyone can get similar results from similar prompts, and copyright protection for AI-created works depends on where you live.

  • Comparing a tool’s tier from one article without checking Billing: the sources differ in places.
