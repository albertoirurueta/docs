Introduction and the Canva Product Family

This content was generated with the assistance of AI and should be verified against the official documentation before being relied on in production.

This page was checked against the official Canva documentation on 2026-11-07. Canva is a browser-based visual communication platform that you also use through a desktop app and mobile apps. It is a continuously deployed service with no version numbers, so everything here is dated rather than versioned, and every launch is cited with the date of its official announcement. The products described are the Visual Suite (the editor family for designs, presentations, Docs, whiteboards, video and websites), Canva Sheets, Canva Code, Canva Forms, Canva Email, Canva Grow and Canva Print, the AI layer called Canva AI, and the related products Affinity, Leonardo.Ai, Flourish and MagicBrief.

Plans are named by tier only and never by price, because prices vary by region and change often; the current grid is on Canva Pricing. The subscriptions on that page are Free, Pro, Business and Enterprise, together with free premium access for Education (K-12) and Nonprofits, and a monthly AI Pass add-on for Pro and Business. Canva Business replaced Canva Teams for new sign-ups on 2025-10-30; existing Teams subscribers keep their plan without the Business-only features. Each product section below states the subscription it needs where the official pages say so, and says plainly where the pages disagree or say nothing. The AI tier (Standard, Premium or Ultra) is given where it matters; the tiers are explained on Plans, Pricing and the AI Allowance. Canva AI 2.0 was announced on 2026-04-16 "as a research preview"; the Help Center now documents it without a preview label, but no general-availability date is published, so what you see depends on your account and subscription.

The running example is Bookshelf, the fictitious book catalogue and reading-list app that is designed in the Figma sub-section. Here the same app gets a launch campaign: a whiteboard brainstorm, a campaign brief, an Instagram post, a launch presentation, a promo video, a one-page website, a flyer, a reading-challenge tracker and a review form. This page opens the workspace for that campaign and explains which product each piece belongs to.

What Canva is

Canva started in 2013 as a tool that lets people without design training produce professional-looking work, and Canva describes its own mission as empowering the world to design. The platform now serves more than a quarter of a billion people every month, according to the 2026-04-16 announcement of Canva AI 2.0. Three ideas explain most of how it behaves.

  • One account, many formats. A design is not tied to one output. The same account, the same Projects library, the same uploads and the same brand kit serve a social post, a slide deck, a document and a website. Since Visual Suite 2.0 (2025-04-11) those formats can even live inside one design, so a whiteboard, a doc and slides can sit in a single file.

  • Templates and elements first. Instead of an empty canvas you start from a template, a magic prompt or an AI conversation, and replace the content. Layout, hierarchy and brand come with the starting point.

  • Collaboration is built in. People edit the same design at the same time, comment, and share by link, so there is no export-and-email step between a draft and its review.

Who uses it: individuals and small businesses for social content and print, marketing teams for campaigns and brand control, teachers and students in classrooms, and nonprofits. Education and Nonprofits have their own pages in this sub-section (see Education and Nonprofits). Signing in, the profile and the homepage are covered on Account, Homepage and Navigation, and the editor itself on Editor Tour.

The Visual Suite

The Visual Suite is Canva’s name for the family of editors that share one workspace. Canva introduced Visual Suite 2.0 at Canva Create on 2025-04-11 as a single format in which presentations, videos, whiteboards, websites and documents can be combined, and extended it with Sheets and Canva Code the same day. On 2025-10-30 the Creative Operating System launch added Email Design, Forms, the Canva Design Model and Canva Grow, together with updates to video, brand tools and Affinity. Sheets, Code, Forms, Email, Grow and Print

These six products extend the suite from design into data, interactivity, collection, messaging, marketing and printing.

Canva Sheets

Canva Sheets is a spreadsheet in the Canva editor: you organise, analyse and visualise data with formulas, charts and formatting, and you can share and comment in real time. From Home you select Sheet, or Create a design and then Sheets. Three AI helpers belong to it: Magic Formulas writes formulas for you, Magic Insights finds trends and key takeaways, and Magic Charts turns raw data into charts. Sheets can be downloaded as CSV (raw data only, no formatting or formulas) or XLSX (formatting and formulas kept). The pricing grid lists Sheets on Free, Pro, Business and Enterprise. Magic Formulas is a Standard-tier tool. Sheets arrived with Visual Suite 2.0 on 2025-04-11 and gained a Sheets AI workflow with Canva AI 2.0 on 2026-04-16, where you describe a sheet such as a budget tracker or content calendar and Canva AI builds it. See Sheets.

Source: Canva Help Center: Create and edit spreadsheets in Canva

Canva Code

Canva Code creates responsive, interactive designs from a prompt without you writing code. It works in two ways: a Code design (a landing page, dashboard or portfolio that you edit in the editor and publish as a website) and Code elements (a quiz, calculator or chart that you add to a presentation, whiteboard or other design and reuse). You reach it from Home (Code) or from the Canva AI tab with the Code option. Canva Code is a Premium-tier AI feature, so each generation counts against the monthly allowance. Canva Code 2.0 was announced with Canva AI 2.0 on 2026-04-16, together with HTML import, so an HTML file or an AI-generated experience can be brought into Canva and edited there; it became generally available to all users on 2026-07-14. See Canva Code.

Source: Canva Help Center: Create interactive designs with Canva Code

Canva Forms and Canva Email

Canva Forms adds a form, with a style matching your brand, to any design, especially to a website; published responses go straight to a sheet in Canva Sheets. Canva Email Design creates branded email campaigns from an industry template or from an AI prompt, with buttons, banners and visuals dragged into place. Both were introduced with the Creative Operating System on 2025-10-30. The official pages read on 2026-11-07 do not state a plan requirement for either, so check your own account and the pricing page. See Forms and Canva Email.

Source: Canva Newsroom: Introducing Canva’s Creative Operating System

Canva Grow

Canva Grow brings three tools together: Create generates image and video ads with AI, Launch publishes ads to supported advertising platforms without leaving Canva, and Learn tracks performance and suggests improvements. You connect advertising accounts to generate ideas, publish campaigns and monitor results across Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn. It sits in the homepage side navigation (under More when it does not fit), it counts toward your AI usage as a premium feature, and the Help Center points to access restrictions by account. The performance side builds on the MagicBrief acquisition described below. See Canva Grow and Marketing Analytics.

Source: Canva Help Center: Use Canva Grow for AI-powered marketing campaigns

Canva Print

Canva Print lets you design and order printed products in the same place: flyers, business cards and other products are ordered from a design and shipped or collected. You can cancel or change an order only within a short window after placing it, and in-store pickup orders cannot be changed, so check everything before you place an order. See Print and Mockups.

Source: Canva Help Center: Learn about Canva Print

Canva AI and the Canva Design Model

Canva’s AI has two names that are easy to confuse. Canva AI is the assistant you talk to: in the editor it can see your design and make edits, update elements and styles, refine a layout and keep work on brand. Magic Studio was the 2023-10-04 umbrella for individual AI tools; the tool names survive (Magic Write, Magic Media, Magic Eraser, Magic Grab, Magic Edit, Magic Expand, Magic Animate, Magic Resize, Magic Layers, Magic Formulas and others), but Canva now markets the whole layer as Canva AI.

Canva AI 2.0 was announced at Canva Create on 2026-04-16 as "available today as a research preview", and its general availability was described as rolling out "over the coming weeks". It is built on the Canva Design Model, introduced on 2025-10-30 as a foundation model trained to understand the structure, layering, hierarchy and branding of design, so that its output arrives as separate editable objects instead of one flat image. Four capabilities define it: conversational design, agentic orchestration of Canva’s tools, layered object intelligence and a persistent Memory Library. Six workflows extend it: connectors (Slack, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Notion, Zoom, HubSpot, Microsoft, Atlassian and Linear at launch), scheduling, web research, brand intelligence, Sheets AI and Canva Code 2.0.

The Help Center states the eligibility rules on 2026-11-07. Canva AI features are available with Canva Pro, Teams, Business and Nonprofit; Free and Education include access to some AI features, such as Magic Write and Background Remover, with limited monthly usage. Memory Library requires a paid subscription. Free users can use Canva AI Connectors as they become available to their account, and availability varies by connector and account. Usage is metered per member in a monthly allowance with three tiers, shown in the figure.

Figure 2. How Canva AI, the Canva Design Model, the single-purpose models and the usage tiers relate

Two newer models serve single jobs. Dream Lab is the image generator introduced on 2024-10-22 and powered then by Leonardo.Ai’s Phoenix model. Canva Craft, announced on 2026-10-22, is Canva’s first image-generation model built and trained by Canva Research; it is available in Canva and to developers through the public API, creates images at 2K resolution in any aspect ratio, and is offered to everyone. Canva Brush, announced on 2026-10-30, is a vision-language model for design that powers supported design edits in Canva AI for Pro subscribers, with wider availability announced as coming soon. For the assistant itself see Canva AI Assistant, for the individual tools Magic Studio Tools, and for generation AI Image and Video Generation.

Desktop, browser and mobile

You can use Canva in a web browser, in the desktop app, and in the mobile apps. The editors share a design across devices, but the layout differs.

  • Homepage navigation. On desktop and Android tablets you can pin, unpin and reorder the homepage side-panel tabs, and unpinned tabs move under More, which also appears on small windows. The customisation is saved per device and is personal, so pinning a tab on your laptop does not change your phone or your teammates. On a phone, the bottom tabs are Canva AI, Create, Templates, Your Designs and More, and the mobile apps appear in the stores as Canva Classic and Canva Lite.

  • Where Grow, Content Planner, Dream Lab and the Apps tab are. On desktop they are in the side panel or under More; on a phone, Brand, Grow, Content Planner, Design School and Dream Lab are under More.

  • Offline. A design that is set to work offline opens Version history from an icon in the editor’s menu bar instead of from the File menu.

  • Printing. Placing and cancelling a Canva Print order is available on the web and in the mobile app.

See Desktop, Mobile and Offline for the details.

Procedure 1: open the Bookshelf launch workspace

This procedure starts the launch campaign: a whiteboard for ideas, a doc for the brief, and a first post drafted with Canva AI. It needs a Canva account on any plan; Canva AI usage on a Free account is limited and metered.

Figure 4. Opening the Bookshelf launch workspace in four products

  1. Sign in and open the homepage. The side panel lists Create, Projects, Brand and, under More, tools such as Apps, Grow, Content Planner, Design School and Dream Lab.

  2. Select Create, then Whiteboards. Name the file Bookshelf launch ideas and add one sticky note per idea: a cover wall, quotes, a reading list, streaks, a meetup and a giveaway. Press S to add a sticky note.

  3. Return to Create and open Docs. Name it Bookshelf campaign brief, add a heading, the goal and a checklist of deliverables.

  4. Open the Canva AI tab, or the Canva AI box on the homepage, and type a short prompt such as "Create a Book of the week Instagram post for the Bookshelf app". Canva AI builds a layered design you can edit.

  5. Open Projects. The whiteboard, the doc and the post are listed there, ready to be moved into a campaign folder.

Source: Canva Help Center: What’s new in Canva AI and Canva Help Center: Customizing your Canva navigation

Procedure 2: draft, refine and check usage with Canva AI

This procedure uses the AI layer with the brand kit. Brand kits and brand templates are taught on Brand Kit and Brand Voice; here they are used as inputs. Conversational design is an Ultra-tier feature, so it counts the most against the monthly allowance, and the exact numbers differ by subscription.

Figure 5. Draft, refine and check usage with Canva AI

  1. In the Canva AI tab, describe the output: "Book of the week post for the Bookshelf app".

  2. Select the plus button, then choose the Bookshelf brand kit and a brand template, so fonts and colours are applied from the first output.

  3. Submit the prompt. Canva AI places the result in the editor as separate objects: headline, cover images and button.

  4. Refine by chat, for example "Make the headline shorter". Only the object you describe should change; if more changes, say so and undo with Cmd Z or Ctrl Z.

  5. Read your AI usage details, as described in the AI usage article, to see how much of the monthly allowance is left before you start heavier generations such as video clips.

Source: Canva Help Center: What’s new in Canva AI and Canva Help Center: Understanding your AI usage

Common pitfalls

  • Treating Canva AI 2.0 as available to everyone. The announcement describes a research preview, no general-availability date is published, and Memory Library, connectors and Premium or Ultra features depend on your subscription.

  • Looking for Teams. New team sign-ups choose Canva Business; Teams exists only for subscribers who kept it, without Business-only features.

  • Searching for "Magic Switch". The resize feature is Magic Resize; there is no Help Center article under the older name.

  • Using the wrong product for the output. A long document is easier to maintain in Docs than across many slides, and a page for the web belongs in Websites rather than in a fixed-size design.

  • Reading the Help Center and the pricing grid as the same thing. Where they differ, such as the wording on websites eligibility, the grid is the newer source.

  • Copying the brand. Canva’s name and logo follow its Trademark Policy and must not be imitated, and library content cannot be trademarked except fonts, simple shapes and lines. Figures on these pages are original schematics for that reason.
