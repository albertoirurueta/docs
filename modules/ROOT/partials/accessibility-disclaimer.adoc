This page documents general **web accessibility** concepts as defined by the W3C
https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/[Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)]. Unlike the other
reference sections on this site, no single reference book underpins it: the content was generated with the
assistance of AI from general knowledge, and should be verified against
https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/[the W3C's own WCAG documentation] before relying on it in
production.

The <<Legal Standards and Regulations>> section in particular summarizes accessibility regulations in different
countries/regions for **general informational purposes only** -- it is not legal advice, and must be checked
against each jurisdiction's own current official text before being relied on for compliance purposes.
