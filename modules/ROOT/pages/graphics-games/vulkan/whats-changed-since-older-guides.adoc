= What Has Changed Since the Older Vulkan Guides
:description: Row-by-row corrections for the two Vulkan books this sub-section was checked against (the Vulkan Programming Guide and the Vulkan Cookbook), with what each says, what is current in 2026, the Vulkan release or date of the change and the page to read.
:keywords: outdated Vulkan tutorials, Vulkan Programming Guide, Vulkan Cookbook, VK_LAYER_KHRONOS_validation, standard_validation, dynamic rendering, synchronization2, timeline semaphores, VMA, glslc, Slang, push descriptors, descriptor heap, mesh shaders, MoltenVK, KosmicKrisp, Vulkan 1.4, corrections

include::partial$ai-disclaimer.adoc[]

The ideas in the two older Vulkan books are still sound: an instance and a device that expose queues, command buffers that record
work for a queue, pipelines built from SPIR-V shaders, resources bound through descriptors, explicit synchronisation and a
swapchain that presents the result. What has changed is almost everything that is typed: the validation layers, the way a pass is
begun, the barrier and submission structures, the semaphores, how memory and descriptors are managed, the shader toolchain and the
platforms where Vulkan runs. Both books were written for Vulkan 1.0 (2016), with the Vulkan SDK 1.0 and the shader compiler of that
time; this sub-section documents Vulkan 1.4 (December 2024), the LunarG SDK 1.4.363.0 and the 2026 roadmap. This page lists, book by
book, the statements that are out of date and what to do today, so that you can keep reading the books for their explanations and
use the pages of this sub-section for current code. The facts were checked on 1 November 2026 against the Vulkan specification and
Guide, the Khronos tutorial, the LunarG SDK documentation and the release notes of the tools. For the changes that affect all
graphics libraries, not only Vulkan, see
xref:graphics-games/whats-changed-since-older-guides.adoc[What Has Changed Since the Older Guides].

The books have the short names used in the tables: VPG (Graham Sellers with John Kessenich, _Vulkan Programming Guide_,
Addison-Wesley, 2016) and VCB (Pawel Lapinski, _Vulkan Cookbook_, Packt, 2017). Both are listed in the
xref:graphics-games/index.adoc#_bibliography[section bibliography]. Chapter numbers are those of the books.

The left column is the style of both books; the right column is the structure of
xref:graphics-games/vulkan/bookshelf-01-triangle-to-textured-book.adoc[Bookshelf 1: From a Triangle to the Textured Book]. Most of
the left column still runs on a current driver, which is why the older code does not fail on the first line: the cost of staying on it
is more code, more objects to manage, missed hardware features and, for the layers and a few calls, errors at start-up.

== The corrections, book by book

=== Both books

[cols="1,3,3,1,2",options=header]
|===
|Chapters |What the books say |What is current |Release or date |Read more

|VPG 1, VCB 1
|Enable the `VK_LAYER_LUNARG_standard_validation` layer, or the individual `VK_LAYER_LUNARG_*` and `VK_LAYER_GOOGLE_*` layers such as `GOOGLE_threading`.
|One layer, `VK_LAYER_KHRONOS_validation`. The older names no longer exist and asking for one fails with `VK_ERROR_LAYER_NOT_PRESENT`. It is configured with the Vulkan Configurator (`vkconfig`) or environment variables, and adds GPU-assisted and synchronisation validation.
|SDK 1.1.106 and later
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/validation-and-debug-utils.adoc[Validation and Debug Utils], xref:graphics-games/vulkan/instance-loader-and-layers.adoc[Instances, the Loader and Layers]

|VPG 1, VCB 1
|Features and properties are read with `vkGetPhysicalDeviceFeatures` and `vkGetPhysicalDeviceProperties`, and versions are built with `VK_MAKE_VERSION`.
|Use `vkGetPhysicalDeviceFeatures2` and `vkGetPhysicalDeviceProperties2` with `pNext` chains, the `Vulkan11` to `Vulkan14` feature structures, and `VK_MAKE_API_VERSION`.
|Vulkan 1.1 (2018)
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/physical-and-logical-devices.adoc[Physical and Logical Devices], xref:graphics-games/vulkan/versions-profiles-and-extensions.adoc[Vulkan Versions, Profiles and Extensions]

|Neither book
|Messages from the validation layers arrive on stdout, or through the old `VK_EXT_debug_report` callback.
|Create a `VK_EXT_debug_utils` messenger, name your objects and label command buffer regions so that messages and captures say which object they mean.
|`VK_EXT_debug_utils`
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/validation-and-debug-utils.adoc[Validation and Debug Utils]

|VPG 7 and 13, VCB 6
|Drawing needs a `VkRenderPass`, a `VkFramebuffer` and subpasses.
|Dynamic rendering (`vkCmdBeginRendering`) draws straight into image views, and since Vulkan 1.4 dynamic rendering local read covers what subpasses were for. Render-pass objects are legacy and are explained once, for reading older code.
|Vulkan 1.3 (January 2022), local read in 1.4 (December 2024)
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/dynamic-rendering.adoc[Dynamic Rendering in Vulkan]

|VPG 4 and 11, VCB 3 and 4
|Dependencies use `vkCmdPipelineBarrier` with `TOP_OF_PIPE` and `BOTTOM_OF_PIPE` stage masks, and work is submitted with `vkQueueSubmit`.
|synchronization2: `vkCmdPipelineBarrier2` with a stage and an access mask in every barrier, `vkQueueSubmit2`, and no use of the two catch-all stages.
|Vulkan 1.3 (January 2022)
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/synchronization-and-sync2.adoc[Synchronization and synchronization2], xref:graphics-games/vulkan/queues-and-command-buffers.adoc[Queues and Command Buffers]

|VPG 11, VCB 3
|Fences and binary semaphores are the only synchronisation primitives between the CPU and the GPU and between queues.
|Timeline semaphores carry a 64-bit counter that the host and every queue can wait on or signal. The swapchain has stricter rules for reusing the semaphores of acquire and present.
|Vulkan 1.2 (January 2020)
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/timeline-semaphores-and-frames-in-flight.adoc[Timeline Semaphores and Frames in Flight], xref:graphics-games/vulkan/window-system-integration-and-swapchain.adoc[Window System Integration and the Swapchain]

|VPG 2, VCB 4
|Each buffer and image gets its own `VkDeviceMemory` allocation.
|Allocations are limited in number, so sub-allocate with the Vulkan Memory Allocator (VMA 3.5.0), use buffer device address to pass pointers to shaders, and use resizable BAR where the device offers it.
|VMA 3.5.0 (October 2026), buffer device address core in 1.2
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/memory-heaps-types-and-vma.adoc[Memory Heaps, Types and VMA], xref:graphics-games/vulkan/bindless-descriptor-indexing-buffer-and-heap.adoc[Bindless in Vulkan]

|VPG 6, VCB 7
|Shaders are GLSL compiled to SPIR-V with `glslangValidator`.
|Compile GLSL with `glslc`, HLSL with DXC and Slang with `slangc`. Slang is hosted by Khronos and is the primary language of the Khronos Vulkan Tutorial. SPIR-V 1.6 is required by Vulkan 1.3, and inline SPIR-V arrives with maintenance5.
|Slang at Khronos (November 2024), Vulkan 1.3 and 1.4
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/shaders-spirv-and-shading-languages.adoc[Shaders, SPIR-V and Shading Languages]

|VPG 6, VCB 5
|Resources are reached through fixed descriptor sets that are written before a draw.
|Descriptor indexing and update-after-bind give bindless tables (1.2), push descriptors are core in 1.4, `VK_EXT_descriptor_buffer` stores descriptors in memory, and `VK_EXT_descriptor_heap` is the announced successor to both.
|Vulkan 1.2, 1.4 and January 2026
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/descriptors-and-push-constants.adoc[Descriptors and Push Constants in Vulkan], xref:graphics-games/vulkan/bindless-descriptor-indexing-buffer-and-heap.adoc[Bindless in Vulkan]

|VPG 9, VCB 7 and 12
|Geometry shaders make billboards, sprites and particles.
|Geometry shaders are slow on tile-based GPUs. Use compute shaders, instancing or mesh shaders (`VK_EXT_mesh_shader`).
|Mesh shaders as an extension
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/tessellation-and-geometry-shaders.adoc[Tessellation and Geometry Shaders in Vulkan], xref:graphics-games/vulkan/mesh-and-task-shaders.adoc[Mesh and Task Shaders]

|VPG and VCB overall
|Vulkan runs on Windows and Linux, and no Apple platform has it.
|MoltenVK 1.4.2 is a Vulkan 1.4 portability layer on Metal, KosmicKrisp is a Mesa-based driver on Metal 4 that is Vulkan 1.4 conformant and needs macOS 26 or later, and the LunarG SDK ships for macOS. Enumerating portability devices needs `VK_KHR_portability_enumeration`.
|2026
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/vulkan-on-apple-moltenvk-and-kosmickrisp.adoc[Vulkan on Apple: MoltenVK and KosmicKrisp]

|Neither book
|The books do not cover hardware ray tracing, mesh shaders, Vulkan Video, profiles, Vulkan SC, Vulkan-Hpp and RAII, subgroups in depth, the memory model, push descriptors or the roadmap profiles.
|These topics are covered from the Vulkan specification and Guide, and each page says so where it applies.
|1.2 to 1.4 and 2026
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/ray-tracing.adoc[Ray Tracing in Vulkan], xref:graphics-games/vulkan/vulkan-video.adoc[Vulkan Video], xref:graphics-games/vulkan/vulkan-sc-and-embedded.adoc[Vulkan SC and Embedded Vulkan], xref:graphics-games/vulkan/vulkan-hpp-raii-and-cpp-bindings.adoc[Vulkan-Hpp, RAII and {cpp} Bindings]
|===

=== VPG: Vulkan Programming Guide

[cols="1,3,3,1,2",options=header]
|===
|Chapters |What the book says |What is current |Release or date |Read more

|Whole book
|The book targets Vulkan 1.0, SDK 1.0.22, `glslangValidator` and SPIR-V 1.0.
|Vulkan 1.4, LunarG SDK 1.4.363.0 (September 2026), SPIR-V 1.6 and Vulkan-Headers 1.4.367. No Vulkan 1.5 had been announced when this page was checked.
|1 November 2026
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/setup-sdk-and-toolchain.adoc[Setting Up the Vulkan SDK and Toolchain], xref:graphics-games/vulkan/versions-profiles-and-extensions.adoc[Vulkan Versions, Profiles and Extensions]

|1
|A device can enable its own layers, and `VkDeviceCreateInfo` carries a layer list.
|Device layers are legacy. Layers are enabled on the instance, and `enabledLayerCount` of the device creation structure must be 0.
|Legacy appendix of the specification
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/instance-loader-and-layers.adoc[Instances, the Loader and Layers], xref:graphics-games/vulkan/physical-and-logical-devices.adoc[Physical and Logical Devices]

|5
|Mir is among the Linux window systems, and no Metal surface is listed.
|The current Vulkan Guide does not list a Mir surface function, and Wayland is the modern Linux compositor path. `VK_EXT_metal_surface` and portability enumeration exist for Apple platforms.
|Current Guide
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/window-system-integration-and-swapchain.adoc[Window System Integration and the Swapchain]

=== VCB: Vulkan Cookbook

[cols="1,3,3,1,2",options=header]
|===
|Chapters |What the book says |What is current |Release or date |Read more

|1
|Vulkan functions are loaded by hand with `vkGetInstanceProcAddr` and a generated list of function pointers.
|Use volk, or the Vulkan-Hpp dispatcher, which load the functions for you and can call a device function directly without the loader's trampoline.
|volk and Vulkan-Hpp
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/setup-sdk-and-toolchain.adoc[Setting Up the Vulkan SDK and Toolchain], xref:graphics-games/vulkan/vulkan-hpp-raii-and-cpp-bindings.adoc[Vulkan-Hpp, RAII and {cpp} Bindings]

|4
|Data moves with `vkCmdCopyBufferToImage` and a staging buffer.
|The `copy_commands2` variants (`vkCmdCopyBufferToImage2`) are core in 1.3 and host image copy, which copies from host memory without a staging buffer, is core in 1.4.
|Vulkan 1.3 and 1.4
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/buffers-images-and-views.adoc[Buffers, Images and Views]

|8 and 9
|A pipeline fixes almost all of its state, and each combination needs its own pipeline.
|Extended dynamic state is core in 1.3, `VK_EXT_shader_object` removes pipelines for simple cases and graphics pipeline libraries link pre-compiled parts.
|Vulkan 1.3 (January 2022)
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/dynamic-state-shader-objects-and-pipeline-libraries.adoc[Dynamic State, Shader Objects and Pipeline Libraries in Vulkan]

|Preface
|Vulkan needs Android 7 or later.
|Still true as the first release with Vulkan, but all 64-bit devices with Android 10 or later have Vulkan 1.1, Android 16 maps to Vulkan 1.4 and OpenGL ES is frozen.
|Android 10 and Android 16
|xref:graphics-games/vulkan/vulkan-on-android.adoc[Vulkan on Android]

== What to keep from the books

.What to keep from the books and what to replace
image::graphics-games-vulkan-whats-changed-keep-and-replace.svg["Two columns, the explanations to keep such as the pipeline stages, devices and queues, memory, barriers and lighting, and the typed details to replace such as render pass objects, the old barrier and submit calls, old layer names, hand-written loading and geometry shaders",width=720]

* The explanations of the pipeline stages, of the instance, device, queue and command-buffer model, of memory heaps, of why
  barriers and layout transitions exist and of descriptors remain correct. Only the names of the calls around them have changed.
* The structure of the book projects (create an instance and a device, a swapchain, a pipeline, resources, then record, submit and
  present each frame) is the structure of
  xref:graphics-games/vulkan/bookshelf-01-triangle-to-textured-book.adoc[Bookshelf 1: From a Triangle to the Textured Book].
* Where a book and the Vulkan specification or Guide disagree, follow the specification and the Guide.
* The legacy render-pass model is still worth understanding because many engines and examples use it. Read it in
  xref:graphics-games/vulkan/dynamic-rendering.adoc[Dynamic Rendering in Vulkan], which explains it once next to its replacement.
