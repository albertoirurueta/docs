A single-page, colour-coded summary of R, written against R 4.6.1 (released 2026-06-24) and checked on 2026-10-22, when R 4.6.2 was at release-candidate stage and scheduled for 2026-10-29 -- the tool map (`R`, `Rscript`, `R CMD build`/`check`/`INSTALL`, RStudio and Positron), getting help and the startup files, syntax (`+<-+`, `+<<-+`, backticks, raw strings, `+|>+` with the `_` placeholder, `+\(x)+`), the six atomic types with their literals, the coercion order and the special values (`NA`, `NULL`, `NaN`, `Inf`), vectors and recycling with `%in%`, `%notin%` and `%||%`, a subsetting grid for `[`, `[[`, `$` and `@`, factors, matrices, lists and data frames, control flow, functions (argument matching, `+...+`, `match.arg()`, `on.exit()`, closures, replacement functions), environments and lazy evaluation, the apply family, strings, regular expressions and dates, S3, S4, Reference Classes, R6 and S7 side by side, conditions and debugging, metaprogramming and formulas, I/O with files, connections, RDS, JSON, DBI and httr2, distributions, tests, models and graphics, tidyverse and data.table one-liners, packages, renv, roxygen2 and testthat, performance and parallelism, and a strip of what changed from R 4.0 to R 4.6 -- handy for quick memorization or as a xref:attachment$r-cheat-sheet.pdf[downloadable PDF] desk reference.
