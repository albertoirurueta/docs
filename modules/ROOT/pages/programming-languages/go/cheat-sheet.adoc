A single-page, colour-coded summary of the Go language and its toolchain, written against Go 1.27 (go1.27.2; checked
2026-10-18) -- the `go` command map and a `go.mod` skeleton with `toolchain`, `tool`, `godebug` and `ignore`, packages,
imports and initialization order, the table of basic types with zero values, literals and constants with `iota`,
declarations and operators, control flow with every `for` form, `range` over integers and functions and the Go 1.22
loop-variable rule, functions with `defer`, `panic` and `recover`, slices, maps, strings and the builtins, structs,
embedding and tags, methods with the method-set table, interfaces and the nil-interface pitfall, generics (including Go
1.27 generic methods) and iterators, errors with `%w`, `Is`, `As`, `AsType` and `Join`, goroutines, the channel axioms
table, `select`, `context`, `sync` and atomics, testing with `b.Loop`, fuzzing and `testing/synctest`, standard-library
one-liners, build constraints, directives and cross-compilation, diagnostics with pprof and PGO, reflection, `unsafe`,
cgo and WebAssembly, and a strip of what changed since older tutorials -- handy for quick memorization or as a
xref:attachment$go-cheat-sheet.pdf[downloadable PDF] desk reference.
