A single-page, colour-coded summary of the Rust language, written against Rust 1.99.0 and the 2024 edition (checked
2026-10-06) -- the `rustup` and `cargo` command map and a `Cargo.toml` skeleton with features, profiles, `[lints]` and a
workspace, primitive types, literals and casts, `let`/`mut`/`const`/`static` and shadowing, control flow with loop values,
labels, `if let`, `let`-`else` and let chains, *the ownership and borrowing rules in one box* with the lifetime elision
rules, structs, enums and pattern syntax, `Option`/`Result` combinators and `?` with an error-type recipe, collection,
string and iterator one-liners, generics versus `impl Trait` versus `dyn Trait`, closures and the `Fn` traits, a
smart-pointer and interior-mutability chooser, the `Send`/`Sync` table, threads, channels and async, modules and
visibility, `macro_rules!` fragment specifiers, the `unsafe` superpowers, testing and tooling flags, and a strip of what
changed since older tutorials -- handy for quick memorization or as a
xref:attachment$rust-cheat-sheet.pdf[downloadable PDF] desk reference.
