This section's material is split across *three* printable A4 sheets rather than one, because a single Apple
Platforms cheat sheet would not fit on a page: about 80% of the content applies across all five platforms
(SwiftUI, concurrency, data, system integration, security, quality and distribution), with the rest specific to
one platform or another. Each sheet groups what you look up together -- writing SwiftUI views, wiring up data and
system services, or checking a platform-specific detail -- so you can print just the one you need at your desk.
