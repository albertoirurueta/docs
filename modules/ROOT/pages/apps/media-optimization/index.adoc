= Media Optimization in Apps
:description: Loading and caching images and video in native and cross-platform apps: Coil, Glide and Media3 on Android, Kingfisher, Nuke and AVFoundation on iOS, expo-image and react-native-video in React Native, Kotlin Multiplatform and Flutter, BlurHash placeholders, and device-side compression and background uploads.
:keywords: app media optimization, Coil, Glide, Fresco, Media3, ExoPlayer, Kingfisher, Nuke, SDWebImage, AsyncImage, AVFoundation, expo-image, react-native-fast-image, react-native-video, Kotlin Multiplatform, Flutter, BlurHash, background upload, bibliography, cheat sheet

include::partial$media-optimization-apps-disclaimer.adoc[]

Native SDKs often lack a full image pipeline: SwiftUI's `AsyncImage` has no dedicated image cache, no prefetching and no
downsampling, and a plain `ImageView` or `Image` decodes whatever it is given. This sub-section documents the libraries that
fill the gap on each platform, how to show a BlurHash while an image loads, how to play adaptive video, and how to compress
and upload media from the device. It is current as of 2026-10-07. The server side is in
xref:backend/media-optimization/index.adoc[Media Optimization for the backend].
