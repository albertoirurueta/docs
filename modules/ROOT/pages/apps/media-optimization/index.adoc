= Media Optimization in Apps
:description: Loading and caching images and video in native and cross-platform apps: Coil, Glide and Media3 on Android, Kingfisher, Nuke and AVFoundation on iOS, expo-image and react-native-video in React Native, Kotlin Multiplatform and Flutter, BlurHash placeholders, and device-side compression and background uploads.
:keywords: app media optimization, Coil, Glide, Fresco, Media3, ExoPlayer, Kingfisher, Nuke, SDWebImage, AsyncImage, AVFoundation, expo-image, react-native-fast-image, react-native-video, Kotlin Multiplatform, Flutter, BlurHash, background upload, bibliography, cheat sheet

include::partial$ai-disclaimer.adoc[]

Native SDKs often lack a full image pipeline: SwiftUI's `AsyncImage` has no dedicated image cache, no prefetching and no
downsampling, and a plain `ImageView` or `Image` decodes whatever it is given. This sub-section documents the libraries that
fill the gap on each platform, how to show a BlurHash while an image loads, how to play adaptive video, and how to compress
and upload media from the device. It is current as of 2026-10-07. This is the *native and cross-platform app* home of the
topic: the server side that produces the variants is in xref:backend/media-optimization/index.adoc[Media Optimization for the backend],
and the browser side is in xref:web/media-optimization/index.adoc[Media Optimization for the web]. The pages share one
running example with the backend and web sections: the book cover `b81f` (2400 by 3600 pixels, AVIF, WebP and JPEG
variants 320, 640 and 1280 pixels wide, and a BlurHash of 28 characters) and the video `42` with its 360p to 1080p
ladder. Each page picks a variant for a 160 by 240 point tile from that JSON, and the Swift and Node programs behind
the numbers on the pages were run, while the Kotlin, Dart and TypeScript code follows the official API documentation.

*Deliberately out of scope.* This section is about getting media that already exists in the right variants onto the
screen, playing it, and sending new media back. The following topics appear in most native app books but live elsewhere on
this site, or are intentionally not covered:

* *Camera capture, pickers and photo library permissions* -- see xref:apps/apple/camera-media-and-photos.adoc[Apple: Camera, Media and Photos],
  xref:apps/android/device-features-camera-location-sensors-media.adoc[Android: Camera, Location, Sensors and Media] and
  xref:apps/react-native/device-capabilities.adoc[React Native: Device Capabilities]. Only what the picker returns is used here.
* *Photo filters, Core Image and editing* -- not covered; this section resizes and re-encodes, and does not edit content.
* *Audio playback and recording* -- not covered beyond the audio track inside a video; the platform media pages above start there.
* *Image layout and views* (`ImageView`, SwiftUI `Image`, content modes) -- see xref:apps/android/views-and-layouts.adoc[Android: Views and Layouts],
  xref:apps/apple/swiftui-controls-forms-text-and-images.adoc[SwiftUI controls, text and images] and
  xref:apps/react-native/images-and-icons.adoc[React Native: Images and Icons].
* *Concurrency* (threads, `async` and `await`, coroutines) -- see xref:apps/apple/concurrency-in-apps.adoc[Apple: Concurrency in Apps] and
  xref:apps/android/viewmodel-lifecycle-and-coroutines.adoc[Android: ViewModel, Lifecycle and Coroutines]; the pages use them without teaching them.
* *Background work and services* -- see xref:apps/apple/background-execution.adoc[Apple: Background Execution] and
  xref:apps/android/background-work-and-services.adoc[Android: Background Work and Services]; only the upload side is repeated here.
* *Networking basics and JSON* -- see xref:apps/apple/networking.adoc[Apple: Networking],
  xref:apps/android/networking-retrofit-okhttp-and-ktor.adoc[Android: Retrofit, OkHttp and Ktor] and
  xref:apps/react-native/networking-and-data-fetching.adoc[React Native: Networking and Data Fetching].
* *Persisting media and offline sync* (Core Data, Room, files, offline queues) -- see xref:apps/apple/core-data-cloudkit-and-icloud.adoc[Apple: Core Data, CloudKit and iCloud],
  xref:apps/android/local-storage-datastore-and-files.adoc[Android: Local Storage and Files] and
  xref:apps/react-native/storage-and-offline.adoc[React Native: Storage and Offline]; this section only covers HTTP caches of downloaded media.
* *Permissions and privacy prompts* -- see xref:apps/android/permissions-privacy-and-security.adoc[Android: Permissions, Privacy and Security] and
  xref:apps/apple/security-and-privacy.adoc[Apple: Security and Privacy].
* *Cross-platform framework basics* -- see xref:apps/kotlin-multiplatform/index.adoc[Kotlin Multiplatform] and
  xref:apps/react-native/index.adoc[React Native]; the hybrid frameworks are documented in xref:apps/hybrid/flutter/index.adoc[Flutter and Dart] and xref:apps/hybrid/ionic/index.adoc[Ionic and Capacitor].
* *Producing the variants* (encoding, formats, upload pipelines, thumbnails) -- see
  xref:backend/media-optimization/index.adoc[Media Optimization for the backend] and
  xref:backend/media-optimization/upload-pipelines-queues-and-jobs.adoc[upload pipelines].
* *Browsers* -- see xref:web/media-optimization/index.adoc[Media Optimization for the web].
* *DRM, live streaming and video calls* -- FairPlay, Widevine, WebRTC and live ingest are not covered; this section plays
  on-demand video only.
* *Managed image and video services* -- see xref:cloud/aws/media-optimization/index.adoc[AWS],
  xref:cloud/google-cloud/media-optimization.adoc[Google Cloud] and xref:cloud/azure/media-optimization.adoc[Azure].
