A single-page, colour-coded summary of the GWT essentials, written against GWT 2.13.1 (released 2026-06-19),
`gwt-maven-plugin` 1.3.0, JDK 21 with `-sourceLevel 17` and Jakarta Servlet 6 (versions verified 2026-10-08) -- the BOM,
archetype and development commands, module XML and properties, compiler and code-server flags, JRE emulation limits,
JsInterop and its JSNI mapping, deferred binding, widgets, panels, handlers and the scheduler, UiBinder, cell widgets and
Editors, `ClientBundle` and CSS/GSS, internationalization, History and Activities and Places, GWT-RPC, `RequestBuilder`,
JSON and RequestFactory, code splitting, linkers and caching, testing, a security strip, and a strip of what changed since
older tutorials -- handy for quick memorization or as a xref:attachment$gwt-cheat-sheet.pdf[downloadable PDF] desk
reference.
