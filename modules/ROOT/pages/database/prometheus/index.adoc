https://prometheus.io/[Prometheus] is an open-source, metrics-based monitoring and alerting toolkit: it
*pulls* numeric time series from instrumented targets on a schedule, stores them in its own local
time-series database, evaluates PromQL expressions against that data for dashboards and alerting rules,
and hands firing alerts to the *Alertmanager* for deduplication, grouping and routing. That pull model,
and its focus on numeric metrics rather than logs, distributed traces or profiles, is what sets it apart
from adjacent observability signals: metrics answer "how much/how many, over time" cheaply at scale,
where logs and traces answer "what exactly happened, in this one request". https://grafana.com/[Grafana]
complements it as the visualization and dashboarding layer, and can also evaluate its own alerting rules
against the same data.

If you are new to this section, read xref:database/prometheus/getting-started.adoc[Getting Started] first
to get a Prometheus instance scraping real targets, then
xref:database/prometheus/architecture-and-ecosystem.adoc[Architecture & Ecosystem] for how the pieces fit
together, and xref:database/prometheus/data-model-and-metric-types.adoc[Data Model & Metric Types] for the
vocabulary (time series, labels, the four metric types) every later page assumes. Everything after that
builds on those foundations.

For the application-side view of instrumenting a Spring Boot service and wiring it to Prometheus and
Grafana, see xref:backend/springboot/metrics-and-observability.adoc[Metrics & Observability]; this section
is the deeper, tool-focused reference that page links out to.
