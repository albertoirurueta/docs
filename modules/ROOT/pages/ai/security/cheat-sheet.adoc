A single-page index of quick back-links to every page in this sub-section, grouped the same way as the navigation. It was
written on 2026-10-01 against the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2026, the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications 2026,
MITRE ATLAS, NIST AI RMF 1.0 with AI 600-1, the EU AI Act as amended by the Digital Omnibus on AI, garak 0.17.0, PyRIT 1.1.0,
promptfoo 0.123.1, NeMo Guardrails 0.24.1, Guardrails AI 0.11.0, Presidio 2.2.364, LangChain 1.4.3 and Spring AI 2.0.1; the
full list is the xref:ai/security/index.adoc#_version_baseline[version baseline] on the landing page.

The xref:attachment$ai-security-cheat-sheet.pdf[downloadable PDF] is a separate, single A4 page meant for printing. It states
the version baseline and covers:

* the trust boundaries of an LLM application and the threat-model flow;
* the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2026 with its 2025 and 2023 mapping;
* the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications (ASI01 to ASI10);
* the prompt-injection defence layers;
* tool and MCP least-privilege rules;
* output-handling rules;
* sensitive information, hidden context and the RAG and vector checklist;
* supply-chain controls;
* consumption limits and the denial-of-wallet bound;
* misinformation and hallucination controls;
* the guardrail options table;
* red-teaming commands for garak, promptfoo and PyRIT;
* LLMSecOps and securing AI-assisted development;
* the NIST AI RMF function map and the AI 600-1 risks;
* the dated EU AI Act timeline, with unconfirmed dates marked;
* the RAISE and responsible-AI checklist.
