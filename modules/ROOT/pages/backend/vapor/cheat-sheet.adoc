A single-page, colour-coded summary of server-side Swift with Vapor, written against Vapor 4.122.2, Fluent 4.13 and
Leaf 4.5 on Swift 6.4 (checked 2026-10-17) -- the SwiftNIO and Vapor stack with a request's life, the Swiftly,
Toolbox and `swift run App` command map, the `Package.swift`, `entrypoint.swift` and `configure.swift` skeleton, *the
Swift 6 concurrency rules for Vapor*, routing with `RouteCollection`, `Request`, `Response`, `Content` and validation,
`Abort`, `AsyncMiddleware` and the built-ins, the Fluent property-wrapper table with migrations, queries,
transactions and raw SQL, Leaf tags with sessions and Redis, an authentication chooser with `req.auth`, JWT,
`req.password` and a security checklist, WebSocket, SSE and multipart skeletons, queues, scheduled jobs, APNs, email
and `req.client`, the OpenAPI generator workflow, VaporTesting, logging, tracing and metrics, the Dockerfile stages with
a deployment checklist, and a strip of what changed since older tutorials including the Vapor 5 beta -- handy for quick
memorization or as a xref:attachment$vapor-cheat-sheet.pdf[downloadable PDF] desk reference.
