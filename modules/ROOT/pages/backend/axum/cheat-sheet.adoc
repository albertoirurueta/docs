A single-page, colour-coded summary of web development with axum, written against axum 0.8.9, tokio 1.53 and tower-http
0.7.1 on Rust 1.99 (checked 2026-10-06) -- the tokio, hyper, tower and axum stack with a request's life, the `Cargo.toml`
and `main.rs` skeleton with `axum::serve` and graceful shutdown, routing with `+{param}+` and `+{*rest}+` captures, `nest`,
`merge` and fallbacks, *the extractor table with the body-extractor-last rule* and optional extractors, responses and an
`AppError` recipe, `State`, `FromRef` and `Extension`, middleware with `from_fn` and the layer-ordering rules, the
tower-http layers with body limits, timeouts and CORS, validation and utoipa snippets, sqlx pool, query, transaction and
migration one-liners, a sessions, passwords, JWT, OAuth2 and CSRF checklist, askama with htmx, WebSocket, SSE and
multipart skeletons, tracing, OpenTelemetry and metrics setup, testing with `oneshot` and axum-test, a deployment
checklist, and a strip of what changed since older tutorials -- handy for quick memorization or as a
xref:attachment$axum-cheat-sheet.pdf[downloadable PDF] desk reference.
