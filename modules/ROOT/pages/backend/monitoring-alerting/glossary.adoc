= Glossary
:description: A glossary of the terms used in the Monitoring & Alerting section, with the Grafana term, the Kibana term and a link to the page that explains each one.
:keywords: glossary, Grafana, Kibana, monitoring, alerting, terms, contact point, connector, notification policy, silence, snooze, maintenance window, SLO, dashboard, data source, data view

include::partial$ai-disclaimer.adoc[]

Checked on 2026-11-09 against Grafana 13.3.x and Kibana 9.5.x.

The two tools often name the same idea differently, and sometimes one tool has no counterpart at all. Each table below lists a
term used in the section, what Grafana calls it, what Kibana calls it, and the page that explains it. Where a tool has no
equivalent, the cell says so, and the explaining page describes the nearest workaround. For a full discussion of the largest
differences, read xref:backend/monitoring-alerting/grafana-vs-kibana.adoc[Grafana vs Kibana].

Terms from the lab (the stand-in service, the load generator modes, the webhook receiver and the Collector alias) are explained on
the xref:backend/monitoring-alerting/hands-on-lab.adoc[Hands-on Lab] page, and they are the same in both profiles.

Reference: https://grafana.com/docs/grafana/latest/fundamentals/[Grafana fundamentals] and
https://www.elastic.co/docs/explore-analyze[Elastic: Explore and analyze].

== In the books

The books in the bibliography use the older vocabulary: Grafana "legacy alerting" and "alert channels" (now contact points), Kibana
"watches" and "visualize" (now Watcher and Lens), Elasticsearch "types" and "Beats" as the only collector. When a book term is
missing from the tables above, it is probably the older name of one that is present. The publisher pages are in the
xref:backend/monitoring-alerting/index.adoc#_bibliography[bibliography].
