A single-page, colour-coded summary of the Tornado essentials, written against Tornado 6.5.10 (released 2026-09-15) and
Python 3.10+ (versions verified 2026-10-07; Tornado 6.6 is only an alpha, so its items are marked upcoming) -- the
`asyncio.run(main())` skeleton, `Application` settings, the `RequestHandler` lifecycle and its input and output methods,
routing and `reverse_url`, template syntax and UI modules, signed cookies, `@authenticated`, XSRF and the upcoming
`cross_origin_protection`, coroutines (`gather` and `multi`, `run_in_executor`, `PeriodicCallback`), queues and locks,
`AsyncHTTPClient`, `WebSocketHandler` and the client, `stream_request_body`, long polling and Server-Sent Events,
`tornado.options` and logging, `AsyncHTTPTestCase` and `gen_test`, multi-process deployment behind nginx, and a "legacy
to current" strip of what changed since older tutorials -- handy for quick memorization or as a
xref:attachment$tornado-cheat-sheet.pdf[downloadable PDF] desk reference.
