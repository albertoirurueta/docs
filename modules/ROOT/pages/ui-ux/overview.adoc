== The UX process at a glance

Real projects loop and skip steps, but most follow the same shape: learn about the problem, decide what to solve,
explore solutions, make them tangible, test them with people, give developers what they need and keep improving after
release.

[mermaid]
....
flowchart LR
    D1[Discover] --> D2[Define] --> D3[Ideate] --> D4[Prototype]
    D4 --> D5[Test] --> D6[Hand off] --> D7[Iterate]
    D7 -. new findings .-> D1
....

.Design is handed to developers, shipped and then iterated
image::ui-ux-design-to-code-handoff.svg["From design to shipped screen: design, review, build and release, with iteration",width=720]

. *Discover*: interviews, observation and analytics reveal who the users are and what they struggle with.
. *Define*: the findings become a clear problem statement, personas and success measures.
. *Ideate*: sketches, flows and information architecture explore several ways to solve it.
. *Prototype*: clickable mock-ups make the best ideas concrete enough to react to.
. *Test*: usability sessions show where the prototype fails real people.
. *Hand off*: specifications, assets and a design system let developers build what was designed.
. *Iterate*: metrics and feedback after release start the next round.

Accessibility belongs to every step, not only the last one. See xref:web/accessibility.adoc[Web Accessibility] for the
web, and for platform conventions the xref:apps/apple/human-interface-guidelines.adoc[Apple Human Interface Guidelines],
xref:apps/apple/liquid-glass-and-the-design-system.adoc[Liquid Glass and the Design System] and
xref:apps/android/compose-material3-and-theming.adoc[Material 3 and Theming in Compose] pages.

== Design tools landscape

Interface design tools differ in platform, collaboration model and how close they sit to code. This site documents two
of them (Figma and Canva) in depth; the others are only named so that readers know the landscape.

[cols="2,3,2",options=header]
|===
|Tool |What it is |Coverage here

|Figma
|Browser-based collaborative design, prototyping and developer handoff.
|Documented in depth: xref:ui-ux/figma/index.adoc[Figma].

|Canva
|Browser-based visual communication platform: designs, presentations, video, websites, brand tools and Canva AI.
|Documented in depth: xref:ui-ux/canva/index.adoc[Canva].

|Sketch
|macOS-only vector interface design application.
|Named only.

|Penpot
|Open-source design and prototyping tool, self-hostable.
|Named only.

|Adobe XD
|Adobe's interface design tool, now in maintenance mode with no new features; check Adobe's announcements for its current status.
|Named only.

|Framer
|Design and publishing tool that produces live websites.
|Named only.
|===

Product status changes quickly, so verify a tool's current state on its official site before choosing it.
