This page was checked against the official Canva Help Center, the Canva newsroom and the research pages on 2026-11-07. It covers the places where Canva creates new pictures and clips from a text description: Dream Lab, Canva Craft, Magic Media, the Video clip option of Canva AI and on-brand image generation in the Brand Kit. All of them work on the web; the Help Center writes the steps for the desktop and the mobile apps side by side and they match, with the differences listed in the tables below. Image generation is generally available. The conversational assistant Canva AI 2.0 was launched as a research preview on 2026-04-16 and is now documented in the Help Center without a preview label, but no general-availability date is published, so what you see depends on your account, language and country.

Plans and AI tiers differ by tool. Generating images and video draws on the shared monthly AI allowance (see xref:ui-ux/canva/plans-pricing-and-ai-allowance.adoc[Plans, Pricing and AI Allowance]). The Help Center lists "Create an image with Canva AI" among the Premium AI tools and "Create a video clip with Canva AI" among the Ultra AI tools; Premium and Ultra tools are available with Canva Pro, Teams, Business, Enterprise and Nonprofit, and Canva Free and Canva Education include some AI features with limited monthly usage. The Dream Lab and Magic Media articles do not name a plan, so try the tool and check the usage article if it is missing. On-brand image generation is stated to be a premium feature for Pro, Teams, Business, Enterprise and Nonprofit. Prices are not repeated here; see https://www.canva.com/pricing/.

The running example is *Bookshelf*, the book catalogue and reading-list app designed in the xref:ui-ux/figma/index.adoc[Figma sub-section]. Its launch campaign needs a hero picture for the "Book of the week" post and a short teaser clip. For editing photographs you already have, see xref:ui-ux/canva/ai-photo-tools.adoc[AI Photo Tools] and xref:ui-ux/canva/photos-and-image-editing.adoc[Photos and Image Editing]. For the assistant that designs through conversation see xref:ui-ux/canva/canva-ai-assistant.adoc[Canva AI Assistant], for the map of every Magic tool xref:ui-ux/canva/magic-studio-tools.adoc[Magic Studio Tools], and for ownership, labels and safe use xref:ui-ux/canva/licensing-responsible-ai-and-canva-shield.adoc[Licensing, Responsible AI and Canva Shield].

== Where generation lives

Canva has several generators that overlap, and each lives in a different place. Dream Lab is on the homepage and not in the editor; Magic Media is in the editor side panel (or under Apps if the tab is hidden); video clips come from Canva AI on the homepage, because the Help Center states that Canva AI is not yet available for video designs in the editor; and on-brand images start from the Brand Kit.

== Dream Lab

Dream Lab is Canva's AI image generator for detailed pictures from text prompts. It was introduced in October 2024 and was first powered by the Phoenix model of Leonardo.Ai, the company Canva acquired in 2024; the Help Center still names Leonardo.ai as the technology behind Dream Lab and the on-brand tool. Since 2026-10-22 it is also the place where Canva Craft is offered (see below). It is generally available.

To open it, go to the homepage, select More and then Dream Lab; the article states that Dream Lab is reachable from the homepage and not from inside the editor. Describe what you want, optionally add a reference image (this uses AI allowance) and choose from the reference options how Canva should use it; the options may vary by rollout. Select Generate and Dream Lab shows up to four images. Select the one you prefer and either describe an edit such as "make the car blue" for an updated version, or select Boost resolution (also allowance) for a higher-resolution version. Boost Resolution is not the same as Upscale: Upscale improves pixelated or low-resolution images in general, while Boost Resolution is for images that Dream Lab generated. Select History inside Dream Lab to see earlier results; the history is kept in Dream Lab, not in your uploads.

Write specific prompts: include style, colours, lighting and layout, name the composition ("top-down flat lay", "wide-angle landscape", "portrait with soft studio lighting") and generate variations before you rewrite the prompt. If nothing is generated, check your connection, try again and remove anything that might break the content guidelines. Dream Lab supports every language available in Canva except for users on canva.cn, and results depend on the quality of the prompt.

Admins, teachers and brand designers can manage access to Dream Lab for their team or class, so it may be missing from your homepage for that reason. Generated images carry a visible label saying that AI created them.

=== Generate a hero image for the Bookshelf post

. On the homepage select More and then Dream Lab.
. Type a prompt such as "An open book on a wooden desk in warm morning light, top-down, with space for a headline", and optionally add a reference image.
. Select Generate and compare the options.
. Select the best image and describe one edit, for example "remove the second cup", then review the update.
. If the image will be large, select Boost resolution once you are happy with the composition.

== Canva Craft

Canva Craft is the first image-generation model that Canva Research built and trained from scratch. It was announced on 2026-10-22 and, according to the announcement, is available in Canva to all users globally and to developers through the public API. To use it, open Dream Lab. The announcement says Craft plans the composition first (which elements appear, their positions, scale and any text) and then renders the image, creates images at up to 2K resolution in more than 30 aspect ratios or one you enter yourself, accepts prompts in any language and can create text inside images in 55 languages. Its outputs are described as well suited to Magic Layers, which turns a generated image into editable elements (see xref:ui-ux/canva/ai-photo-tools.adoc[AI Photo Tools]). Two faster variants, Craft-fast and Craft-ultrafast, were announced as upcoming, and availability through the Canva MCP server was announced as coming soon.

The Dream Lab Help Center article does not yet mention Craft and still describes a limit of four images per prompt, so treat the newsroom text as the source for Craft and the Help Center article as the source for the Dream Lab workflow. The announcement states that Craft is backed by Canva Shield, with input and output moderation and reporting tools.

Source: https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-craft/[Canva Newsroom: Introducing Canva Craft] and https://www.canva.com/ai-research/[Canva Research]

== Magic Media in the editor

Magic Media creates images, graphics, videos and 3D elements while you are inside a design, so the result lands directly in the design. In the editor side panel select Magic Media; if you cannot see it, go to Apps and search for Magic Media.

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Tab | How it works

| Images | Describe the image or select Inspire me for sample prompts. Select Styles and choose photography, digital art or fine art; you can also upload a reference image. Choose a square, landscape or portrait layout and select Generate images.
| Graphics | Describe the graphic or select Inspire me, select Styles (simple, artistic or detailed) and select Generate graphics.
| Videos | Describe the video or select Inspire me and select Generate video. Every generated video is 4 seconds long.
| 3D | Describe the 3D object or select Inspire me and select Generate 3D and add to your design. You can also open Elements, enter what to generate and use the Generate button dropdown, then 3D.
|===

After a result appears, select Generate again for a new set, or open More on the image you prefer and select Generate more like this. Select the image to add it to the design. 3D elements work in Presentations, Videos, Whiteboards, Social Media and Websites, and are currently not supported in Docs, Sheets and Email. If a 3D result looks distorted, simplify the prompt first and then add detail gradually.

Magic Media only uses the text you enter, so the more specific you are, the better the result. The Help Center warns that Magic Media's Text to Video is different from the Video clip option of Canva AI. The Help Center articles on Magic Media and the image apps still appear side by side with Dream Lab and no official article says that one replaced the other, so use whichever entry point matches where you are working.

=== Add a generated photo to the launch post

. Open the "Book of the week" post and select Magic Media in the editor side panel (or search for it under Apps).
. In the Images tab type "An open book on a sunlit wooden desk, shallow depth of field", choose a style and a portrait layout.
. Select Generate images; select Generate again for a new set if none fits.
. Open More on the image you prefer and select Generate more like this to explore close variants.
. Select the image to add it to the design, then resize it in the layout.

== Video clips with Canva AI

Canva AI can generate a short clip from a text prompt. From the homepage select Canva AI and then Video clip, describe what you want to see (for example "A foggy forest at dawn") and optionally attach a reference image. The clip is 8 seconds long with audio or 6 seconds without, and Canva saves it automatically. Generation can take up to 2 minutes. When it is ready you can preview it, download it with the download icon, or select Use Canva Editor to trim it, add text or overlay music in the video editor. The Help Center lists these limits: prompts are supported in English only, inappropriate prompts are blocked and moderated, and you cannot manually change the audio in videos that have sound. Canva AI is not yet available for video designs in the editor, which is why the option starts on the homepage.

The video prompt tips say to put the main subject or action first, describe camera movement clearly, call out important details with brackets or parentheses and mention emotions early. Opening Video clip from the homepage also lets you pick preferences such as style, aspect ratio, framing and lighting. If the clip does not appear, it may still be generating.

=== Create a teaser clip for the launch

. On the homepage select Canva AI and then Video clip.
. Type "Pages of a book turning slowly in soft morning light, camera moves closer", and attach a reference image if you have one.
. Choose the optional style, aspect ratio, framing and lighting preferences.
. Wait up to 2 minutes and preview the clip.
. Select the download icon, or select Use Canva Editor to add the Bookshelf title text and trim it.

== On-brand images

If your Brand Kit contains photos, Canva can use one as a style guide to generate new images that match it. In the homepage side panel select Brand, open the Brand Kit tab (choose the kit if you have several), hover over an image under Assets, select the More options icon and then Generate from photo. Pick the dimensions (for example 1:1, 16:9 or 9:16), select Generate, review the results, pick the ones you like and select Add to Brand Kit. Only owners, administrators and brand designers can use a Brand Kit image as a style reference. The output is at most four images per use, it follows only the description you enter, and it supports every Canva language except for users on canva.cn. Unlike Dream Lab, which makes general-purpose images, this tool aims to match your brand's style, and you can edit the results further afterwards.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/create-onbrand-images-with-ai/[Canva Help Center: Create on-brand images with AI]

== Choosing a quality tier

For photos and videos generated with Canva AI you can choose a quality tier. Standard gives good quality and uses less allowance; on paid plans it does not count toward your allowance and on Canva Free it does. Premium is the default and balances quality and efficiency. Ultra gives the highest quality, uses the allowance fastest and is for paid plans only. To choose a tier from the editor open the Elements tab, select the plus icon in the search bar to open the Attachments menu and select AI quality; from the homepage select Canva AI, then the plus icon and AI quality. Free accounts see Ultra with a Pro badge.

.Which quality tiers each kind of content supports
image::ui-ux-canva-ai-image-and-video-generation-quality-tiers.svg["Matrix of content types against Standard, Premium and Ultra: photos support all three, videos support Premium and Ultra, and graphics, audio, 3D, forms, code, charts and Sheets support Premium only",width=720]

If you choose a tier that a content type does not support, Canva switches to the supported one and tells you, for example Standard video becomes Premium. Your Ultra choice still applies to photos and videos. When options are greyed out you have probably reached your monthly limit: on Free all tiers wait until the allowance resets, and on paid plans Premium may be briefly unavailable while Standard stays available within fair use limits.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/canva-ai-quality-tiers-standard-premium-ultra/[Canva Help Center: Set the quality of Canva AI-generated content]

== Third-party image apps

The Apps Marketplace lists image-generation apps from other companies; the Help Center names DALL·E by OpenAI and Imagen by Google Cloud. They are part of the ecosystem and are not Canva's own models. The article states that Canva makes no copyright claim over the images you create with them, that you own them as between you and Canva subject to the Terms, that this does not mean you hold the copyright or exclusive rights, and that recognizable characters or brands may be protected by copyright or trademarks. Prompts are reviewed automatically for terms that could produce inappropriate imagery, and you can report unsafe images with the Report button in the app. Check each app's listing for its current availability and limits, because apps change independently of Canva.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/ai-image-generation-apps/[Canva Help Center: Use AI image generation apps]

== Ownership, labels and safe use

For Dream Lab and Magic Media the Help Center says you own what you create as long as you follow the Terms, but copyright law for AI-generated content varies by country and you may not have exclusive rights; if a design shows recognizable people, characters or brands you need permission from the rights holder, which Canva cannot grant. Prompts must follow the Acceptable Use Policy and harmful, illegal or inappropriate requests are blocked. Dream Lab images carry a visible label showing that AI created them, and unsafe images can be reported with the Report button. The licence rules are on xref:ui-ux/canva/licensing-responsible-ai-and-canva-shield.adoc[Licensing, Responsible AI and Canva Shield].

== Pitfalls

* Dream Lab is not in the editor. If you look for it there you will not find it; start from the homepage.
* Magic Media videos are 4 seconds, while Canva AI video clips are 6 or 8 seconds. They are different features.
* Video clip prompts are supported in English only, and the audio of a clip cannot be changed manually.
* A vague prompt wastes allowance on regenerations. Include subject, style, colours, lighting and layout, and use Inspire me when you are stuck.
* Reference images and Boost resolution use allowance on top of the generation itself.
* A tier that a content type does not support is silently replaced by Premium, with a message.

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Date | Change

| 2024-10 | Dream Lab introduced, first powered by Leonardo.Ai Phoenix
| 2026-04-16 | Canva AI 2.0 launched as a research preview; no general-availability date is published
| 2026-10-22 | Canva Craft announced, offered through Dream Lab to all users

Older books describe Dream Lab as powered by Leonardo and treat Magic Media as the main generator, and they show third-party apps such as DALL·E as the way to add AI images. Today Dream Lab also serves Canva's own Craft model, Canva AI creates video clips, quality tiers and the shared allowance decide how much you can generate, and the apps are listed as ecosystem. Magic Studio is the older umbrella name; the assistant is now Canva AI 2.0.
