This page was checked against the official Canva Help Center on 2026-11-07. It covers three features that multiply one finished design: *Magic Resize*, which converts a design to other sizes and formats; *Bulk Create*, which produces many designs from rows of data; and *Translate*, which converts the text of a design into another language. All three live in the standard Canva editor of the Visual Suite.

Plans differ by feature. The Resize article lists Pro, Business, Enterprise, Education, Higher education, Nonprofits and Teams, and says each use of Magic Resize counts against the monthly AI usage limit. The Bulk Create article lists Pro, Teams, Business, Enterprise, Education and Nonprofit plans, on desktop web and the Canva desktop app. The Translate article names no plan, so confirm availability in your account; it points to the AI access article for usage limits. The tier model (Standard, Premium and Ultra) is described on xref:ui-ux/canva/plans-pricing-and-ai-allowance.adoc[Plans, Pricing and AI Allowance], and prices are on https://www.canva.com/pricing/. The Help Center presents all three features without a beta label.

The running example is *Bookshelf*, the book catalogue and reading-list app designed in the xref:ui-ux/figma/index.adoc[Figma sub-section]. Its launch campaign starts from one square "Book of the week" post. Magic Resize turns it into a story, a LinkedIn banner and a YouTube thumbnail; Bulk Create turns a table of twenty books into twenty cards; Translate produces a Spanish version. The campaign is scheduled as described on xref:ui-ux/canva/social-media-and-content-planner.adoc[Social Media and the Content Planner].

== Magic Resize

Magic Resize changes the format, layout or dimensions of a design. The Help Center article is titled "Resize designs and size limits" and the menu item is *Resize* in the editor menu bar. In the Help Center the feature is called Magic Resize; older books call the same area "Resize & Magic Switch".

.One square master becomes a story, a banner and a thumbnail
image::ui-ux-canva-resize-bulk-create-and-translate-resize-fan-out.svg["Diagram of a square Book of the week post going through the Resize panel into a tall story, a wide LinkedIn banner and a thumbnail, with the limits of five sizes per design and 250 outputs per action",width=720]

=== Where it lives and every option

Select *Resize* in the editor menu bar to open the panel.

.The Resize panel: suggestions and bundles, custom size, the pages to resize and the two buttons
image::ui-ux-canva-resize-bulk-create-and-translate-resize-panel.svg["Schematic editor with the Resize panel open, listing suggested bundles with checkboxes, a custom size field with an aspect-ratio lock, an Apply to pages choice, a Copy and resize button and a Resize this design button, with five numbered callouts",width=720]

. *Suggestions and categories.* Pick common sizes from the suggestions or use *Browse by category*. You can choose several sizes at once.
. *Bundles.* Under *Suggested* you find bundles such as vertical videos or your last selection. A bundle groups common formats, for example a set of social posts. Select it to see the formats it contains, and tick or untick each one before you create the copies.
. *Custom size.* Select *Custom size* to enter your own measurements. A lock beside the fields keeps or releases the aspect ratio.
. *Pages.* Choose pages under *All pages*; otherwise the size applies to all of them.
. *Copy & resize* creates new designs. A single copy lands in the same folder as the original; several sizes are saved in a new folder whose name appears in the confirmation message.
. *Resize this design* changes the design in place. Version history can restore the earlier size, and only when you resized directly rather than made a copy: open *File*, then *Version history*, choose the autosaved version and select *Restore*, or *Make a copy* to keep both.

The same article describes resizing one page without touching the rest: select the page background or its thumbnail, choose *Edit* in the floating toolbar to open the Edit Page panel, then *Resize*.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/resize/[Canva Help Center: Resize designs and size limits]

=== Limits

[cols="2,3"]
|===
| Limit | Value stated by the Help Center

| Designs per bulk action | Up to 50
| New sizes per design | Up to 5
| Total outputs per bulk action | 250; run a second batch if you hit the limit
| Minimum dimensions | 40 x 40 px
| Maximum dimensions | 8000 x 3125 px
|===

If a Custom size field turns red the value is outside the limits. To get the proportions of an oversized target, such as a very wide banner, enter the target size, lock the aspect ratio, bring one value inside the limits so the other follows, and download the result as PNG at 0.5 up to 3.125 times its size. This does not create a design in the target size; it gives the same proportion.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/resize/[Canva Help Center: Resize designs and size limits]

=== Resize canvas to Selection

A related command crops the page to what you selected. Select two or more elements, a group, or a single image or video, right-click the selection (or select *More* on mobile) and choose *Resize canvas to Selection*. The page fits the bounding box of the selection and the elements stay where they are; *Undo* reverses it. The Help Center states it is not available in Canva Websites, Docs or Sheets.

=== Where Magic Resize does not apply

* Canva Docs do not support Magic Resize. The article says to use Magic Switch to transform a Doc into a presentation, which is the one place where the older name survives.
* When you resize a presentation to video dimensions, each slide becomes its own video page instead of one long video, so no full timeline appears. Select the pages and choose *Edit as video*, or hold Shift, select the page thumbnails and choose *Merge videos*. See xref:ui-ux/canva/video-editor-audio-and-captions.adoc[Video Editor, Audio and Captions].
* From a sheet you can resize many designs by link; that route is documented on xref:ui-ux/canva/sheets.adoc[Sheets] and has its own limits of up to five sizes per design.
* Designs you cannot edit cannot be resized; Canva asks you to request access.

Desktop and mobile follow the same steps in the article, with the mobile wording differing only in where the buttons sit.

==== Procedure 1: resize the Book of the week post for three channels

. Open the square "Book of the week" master built from the brand kit on xref:ui-ux/canva/brand-kit-and-brand-voice.adoc[Brand Kit and Brand Voice].
. Select *Resize* in the editor menu bar.
. Under *Suggested*, open a bundle or search *Browse by category*, then tick Instagram story, LinkedIn banner and YouTube thumbnail. Untick formats you do not need.
. Leave *All pages* selected, because the master has one page.
. Select *Copy & resize*. Because you chose several sizes, Canva saves them in a new folder named in the confirmation message.
. Open each copy and check that the title still fits, that the book cover is not cropped and that the call-to-action sits clear of the platform edges.
. Adjust text sizes and positions by hand where Magic Resize reflowed them awkwardly.

.Storyboard: from the square master to three resized copies
image::ui-ux-canva-resize-bulk-create-and-translate-storyboard-magic-resize.svg["Five-panel storyboard: open the square master, select Resize, tick the story banner and thumbnail sizes, select Copy and resize, then review each copy",width=720]

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/resize/[Canva Help Center: Resize designs and size limits]

==== Procedure 2: crop the page to a group of elements

. Select the cover image and the title text, or group them.
. Right-click the selection and choose *Resize canvas to Selection*.
. Check that the page now fits the group, and use *Undo* if it does not.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/resize/[Canva Help Center: Resize designs and size limits]

== Bulk Create

Bulk Create links data organised in rows and columns to placeholder elements on a design page. Each column is a data field, and each row creates one version of the design. You can connect up to 150 data fields. Availability is desktop web and the Canva desktop app.

.Data columns on the left map to placeholder text boxes on the right, then four steps produce the designs
image::ui-ux-canva-resize-bulk-create-and-translate-bulk-create-data-mapping.svg["Diagram of a data table with Title, Author, Tagline and Genre columns mapped to four placeholder text boxes of a template, followed by the four steps match and fill, review, preview and create",width=720]

=== Prepare the template

Before connecting data, add the elements Canva will fill. Use a separate text box for each text field and a frame or a grid for each image. For a multi-page design, connect data fields on each page. Brand templates with locked regions are covered on xref:ui-ux/canva/brand-templates-controls-and-approvals.adoc[Brand Templates, Controls and Approvals].

=== Start Bulk Create

* From the editor: click the page background, select *Edit* in the toolbar, then *Bulk create* in the side panel. Under *Choose a starting point*, choose *Bulk Create* to make designs from data, or *Labels* to set fields that connected apps fill.
* From the *Apps* tab: under *More from Canva*, select *Bulk create*.
* From a Canva Sheet: select the range, open *Actions* and choose *Bulk Create*. The sheet route is detailed on xref:ui-ux/canva/sheets.adoc[Sheets], including the data rules for ranges and media.

=== Add the data

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Source | How it works

| Canva Sheet | Select a range, then *Actions* and *Bulk Create*, then *Continue* and choose a design. You can select up to 300 rows and 150 columns.
| CSV or XLSX file | In the Bulk create tab select *Upload data*, pick the file, choose a sheet if the workbook has several, add a range if needed and select *Continue*. Save from Google Sheets with File then Download, from Excel with File then Save As, or from Numbers with File then Export To.
| Data apps | Choose a connected platform such as Google Analytics, HubSpot, Statista or World Bank, sign in if asked and pick a data set.
| Manual table | Select *Enter data manually*, optionally *Clear table*, add *Add text* and *Add image* columns, fill the cells and add rows with *Add row*, then *Done*.
|===

Images work with Canva Sheets and XLSX files, but image URLs from cloud photo storage are not supported and are treated as plain text. Embed each image in a single cell, not floating over several cells. A plain CSV carries text only, so covers need a Sheet, an XLSX file or the manual table.

A text-only CSV for the Bookshelf cards looks like this:

[source,text]
----
Title,Author,Tagline,Genre
Emma,Jane Austen,A comedy of matchmaking,Classic
Dracula,Bram Stoker,Told through letters and diaries,Gothic
Ulysses,James Joyce,One long day in Dublin,Modern
----

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/bulk-create/[Canva Help Center: Create designs in bulk]

=== Connect, preview and create

Select *Match and fill fields*. Canva analyses the design and connects the fields it can identify; the side panel offers *Auto-match fields* when you start from a Sheet. To correct a match, select the element, choose *Match data* in the toolbar and pick the field under *Connect data*. If most matches are wrong, select *Connect manually* while the analysis runs, which cancels the AI step; if only a few are wrong, let it finish and re-map those elements by drag and drop or by selecting the element.

Select *Preview* to check each row. The preview is read-only, videos show a frozen first frame, some 3D elements may not render, and media replacement in the preview works only with a directly linked Canva Sheet. Then open *More options* (the three dots) beside *Create designs* and set the *Output* (one design with a page per row, or individual designs), how each page or design is named and the *Save in* folder. Select *Create [number] designs*; a message confirms they are saved to Projects, and *View design* opens the result.

=== Bulk Create or Data Autofill

[cols="1,2,2"]
|===
| | Bulk Create | Data Autofill

| Output | Static designs generated in advance | One editable design that updates with live data
| Volume | Many designs at once | One design filled repeatedly
| Sync | One time, when the data is uploaded | Live, from connected sources
| Source | Canva Sheets, CSV, Excel or supported apps | Salesforce or custom apps through an API
| Limits | 300 rows and 150 columns; one media item per cell | 150 data fields
|===

For the API side of autofill see xref:ui-ux/canva/rest-apis.adoc[REST APIs].

==== Procedure 3: create twenty Book of the week cards

. Build a table with the columns Title, Author, Tagline, Genre and Cover, one row per book, in Canva Sheets or an XLSX file. Embed each cover in its cell.
. Open the Bookshelf card template and make sure there is one text box per text field and one frame for the cover.
. Click the page background, select *Edit*, then *Bulk create*, and under *Choose a starting point* choose *Bulk Create*.
. Choose *Upload data* and select the file, or start from the sheet range with *Actions*, then *Bulk Create*.
. Select *Match and fill fields*, then review the matches. Fix any wrong one with *Match data* in the toolbar.
. Select *Preview* and page through the rows, checking long titles and covers. Close the preview to adjust.
. Open *More options* beside *Create designs*, choose *Individual designs* if each card is scheduled on its own, set the naming and a *Save in* folder.
. Select *Create 20 designs* and open one with *View design* to confirm.

== Translate

Translate converts the text of a design into another language. The Help Center says you can choose from 100+ languages. The default is *Translate from: Automatically detect*, and the most recently used language becomes the next default.

=== Ways to start

* Right-click a text box and choose *Translate text*, then choose the language and tone and either *Translate pages* or *Select text from current page*.
* Open the *Apps* tab, and under *More from Canva* select *Translate*.
* Select the page background or thumbnail, choose *Edit* in the floating toolbar, then *Translate* in the Edit Page panel.
* From *Resize*: the panel has a Magic Switch group with a *Translate* option. It translates one language at a time and offers *Translate existing design without creating a copy*; translating only selected text is not available there.
* In Canva Sheets, select cells, choose *Actions* then *Translate*, then *Translate text in selected cells*, and decide whether to overwrite or *Add to new column*.

=== Options and limits

.A Spanish translation: language and tone in the panel, then the three settings, then a review
image::ui-ux-canva-resize-bulk-create-and-translate-storyboard-translate-to-spanish.svg["Four-panel storyboard: open the English card, pick Spanish and a friendly tone, check the three settings, then review the Spanish copy",width=720]

* *Tone of voice* is optional and available only for some languages: Original, Conversational, Informative, Professional, Friendly or Inspirational. The article lists English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Arabic, Indonesian, German, Italian, Hindi, Turkish, Japanese, Thai, Chinese (Simplified), Vietnamese, Russian, Chinese (Traditional), Dutch, Korean and Polish.
* You translate one language at a time, and multiple pages within a 2000-character limit.
* Settings: *Reduce font size to fit* is on by default to avoid overlap when the translation is longer; *Duplicate page when translating* is on by default so the original is kept and the translation is a copy; *Mirror page to match text direction* supports right-to-left languages.
* Not every font supports every language. If your font is not supported, Translate asks you to choose another, and different characters in the result usually mean the font should be changed.
* The article carries a note that AI usage limits changed; check the allowance and tier on xref:ui-ux/canva/plans-pricing-and-ai-allowance.adoc[Plans, Pricing and AI Allowance].

=== Translate with Smartling

For professional human translation, the Smartling app in the Apps tab connects a Smartling account. You choose the Smartling project, whole pages or single text elements, give the job a unique name with an optional due date and instructions, choose target languages (set per project inside Smartling) and select *Submit*. Progress shows when you reopen the app, and an email arrives when the job finishes; then select the languages and *Generate translations*. The service is a third party; see https://www.smartling.com/ for its own documentation.

==== Procedure 4: translate the Book of the week card into Spanish

. Open the English card, right-click its title text box and choose *Translate text*.
. Under *Translate to* choose Spanish. Choose a *Tone of voice* such as Friendly, which is offered for Spanish.
. Choose *Translate pages* and select the page, then select *Translate*.
. Open the *Settings* tab and keep *Reduce font size to fit* and *Duplicate page when translating* on, so the English original stays.
. Review the Spanish page: line breaks, text that grew past its box, and that the font shows every accent.
. Have a Spanish speaker check the wording before publishing.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/translate-canva-designs/[Canva Help Center: Translate your Canva designs]

==== Procedure 5: a Spanish batch of twenty cards

Translation and Bulk Create combine in two ways. Translate the data first: in the sheet select the Tagline cells, choose *Actions*, *Translate*, a Spanish language, then *Add to new column* so both languages stay side by side, and run Procedure 3 with the Spanish column connected. Or generate the English cards first and translate their pages afterwards, remembering the 2000-character limit per run and one language at a time. The first route keeps one template; the second repeats the translate steps for every design.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/translate-canva-designs/[Canva Help Center: Translate your Canva designs]

== Common mistakes

* Running Copy & resize on a design with several pages and forgetting the *All pages* choice.
* Expecting a CSV to carry cover images; use a Sheet, an XLSX file or the manual table.
* Using one text box for two fields, so the match is wrong or impossible.
* Trusting the automatic matching without reading the side panel.
* Not allowing for longer Spanish or German text: widen the text boxes before translating.
* Resizing in place without a copy, and then needing Version history to go back.

== Where older books are outdated

* *"Resize & Magic Switch" as a menu item.* Older books teach this name. The Help Center today titles the article "Resize designs and size limits" and calls the feature Magic Resize; the Magic Switch name remains only as a heading in the Resize panel and in the Docs note, and has no article of its own.
* *Resize as a single step.* Books describe resizing one design. Today you can resize into bundles, up to five sizes per design and up to 50 designs, and from a sheet.
* *Resize and plan.* Resize now counts against the monthly AI usage limit.
* *Bulk Create from the Apps tab only.* Today it also starts from the editor Edit panel and from a Sheet, with an Auto-match fields option, data apps and a manual table.
* *Translate as a Magic Switch tool.* It is now a standalone app, a right-click command, an Edit Page panel option and a Sheets action, with tone of voice and settings.

Compare more changes on xref:ui-ux/canva/whats-changed-since-older-guides.adoc[What Has Changed Since Older Guides] and the wider tool set on xref:ui-ux/canva/magic-studio-tools.adoc[Magic Studio Tools].
