This page was checked against the official Affinity and Canva Help Center pages on 2026-11-07. The product is *Affinity* (also called Affinity Studio and Affinity by Canva), the professional design app that joined the Canva family and was relaunched on 2025-10-30 as one application that unites vector design, photo editing and page layout. Its official site is https://www.affinity.studio. It is generally available, runs as a separate app on macOS, Windows and iPad, and is free for individuals with a verified Canva account, including a Canva Free account. Paid Canva plans do not unlock a different Affinity; they unlock extras that connect the two products, namely the Brand Kits panel and the Canva AI tools inside Affinity. Prices are not repeated here; they are published at https://www.canva.com/pricing/.

Affinity is not part of the Canva editor. It is a desktop-class app for work that needs precise vector paths, photo retouching or print-grade layout, and it exchanges files with Canva instead of replacing it. The editor itself is described on xref:ui-ux/canva/editor-tour.adoc[Editor Tour]; the product map is on xref:ui-ux/canva/introduction-and-product-family.adoc[Introduction and Product Family].

The running example is *Bookshelf*, the book catalogue and reading-list app designed in the xref:ui-ux/figma/index.adoc[Figma sub-section]. In this page you sign in to Affinity with the account that owns the Bookshelf launch designs, use the Bookshelf brand kit while you polish the logo and a cover illustration, and send the finished cover art back to Canva for the launch campaign.

== What Affinity is

Earlier Affinity releases were three separate apps (Photo, Designer and Publisher) that you bought once and switched between. The app launched on 2025-10-30 replaces that split with a single application in which the three kinds of work live side by side. The official announcement describes vector, photo and layout tools in one fast, responsive platform, and the Affinity site describes designing, editing and laying out without leaving the same `.af` file.

.Affinity as one app with Vector, Pixel and Layout studios, the Canva sign-in and the optional Canva AI Studio
image::ui-ux-canva-affinity-unified-app.svg["Schematic of one Affinity app containing Vector, Pixel and Layout studios, with a Canva account sign-in box and an optional Canva AI Studio box",width=720]

The main ideas are the following.

* *Studios.* A studio is a saved arrangement of tools and panels. The app offers Vector, Pixel and Layout studios and lets you mix and match tools from all three, rearrange panels, save several set-ups for different projects and share or download custom studios.
* *Vector tools* draw shapes, curves and logos with real-time editing, which suits the Bookshelf logo variants.
* *Photo (Pixel) tools* work non-destructively, so adjustments, RAW development, retouching and compositing can be refined without losing the original, which suits cleaning up a book-cover photograph.
* *Layout tools* provide multi-page documents with master pages, shared text styles and typographic controls, which suit the print bookmark sheet.
* *File compatibility.* The Affinity site lists import of PSD, AI, IDML and DWG files, among other formats, keeping layers and structure.
* *Optional Canva AI.* With a Canva premium plan, Canva AI tools appear in Affinity inside the Canva AI Studio, for example Generative Fill, Generative Expand, Remove Background and image generation. The AI allowance and tiers are explained on xref:ui-ux/canva/plans-pricing-and-ai-allowance.adoc[Plans, Pricing and AI Allowance].
* *Animation hand-off.* The Affinity site also promotes importing finished designs into Cavalry, a separate animation tool, which is outside the scope of this guide.

Source: https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/all-new-affinity/[Canva Newsroom: Introducing the all-new Affinity]

== Installing Affinity

The download links for macOS, Windows and iPad are on https://www.affinity.studio. The Help Center documents these installation paths.

* *macOS* (10.15 Catalina or higher): double-click the downloaded `.dmg` file in Downloads and drag the app icon to the Applications folder.
* *Windows* (Windows 10 May 2020 Update, build 19041, or higher): double-click the downloaded EXE or MSIX file, or search the Microsoft Store for "Affinity by Canva" and check that the publisher is Canva before selecting Get.
* *iPad.* Affinity for iPad was announced on the Affinity site as a full creative experience designed for touch and Apple Pencil. Install it from the App Store link on https://www.affinity.studio.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/install-affinity/[Canva Help Center: Install Affinity to your device]

== Sign in with Canva

Affinity asks you to sign in on first launch. Choosing *Sign in with Canva* sends you to a browser page where you authorize Affinity to access your Canva account. When you do, you agree to the Affinity Additional Terms and to Canva's Privacy Policy. If your organization uses single sign-on, you still start with *Sign in with Canva* and then follow the single sign-on steps of the Help Center. Because the sign-in is tied to the Canva account, the same account decides whether the Brand Kits panel and Canva AI tools are available.

=== Procedure: sign in with the Bookshelf account

. Install the Affinity app on your Mac, Windows PC or iPad.
. When the app prompts you to sign in, select *Sign in with Canva*.
. Select *Sign in with Canva* again on the page that opens. A browser page asks you to authorize Affinity to access your Canva account.
. Select *Allow*.
. If your web browser asks, select *Allow* or *Open* to return to the app. A welcome message appears in Affinity.
. Select *Launch*.

image::ui-ux-canva-affinity-storyboard-sign-in.svg["Storyboard of five panels: install the app, choose Sign in with Canva, allow access in the browser, launch from the welcome message, and Affinity open with the Canva account",width=720]

If you cannot sign in, the Help Center has articles for login issues, resetting a password and the case where Affinity cannot be accessed at all, listed on the Affinity category page.

== Brand Kits inside Affinity

A Brand Kit created in Canva can be used directly in Affinity. The Canva Brand Kits panel shows brand fonts, text styles (Title, Subtitle and Body), colour palettes and image assets such as logos, photos and graphics. Brand Kits are created and edited only in Canva; changes sync automatically to Affinity. How a kit is built is explained on xref:ui-ux/canva/brand-kit-and-brand-voice.adoc[Brand Kit and Brand Voice].

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Where | How to reach it

| Open the panel | Window menu, then Canva, then Canva Brand Kits, or the Toggle Brand Kits Panel button on the status bar. The panel can be docked, moved and resized, and shown in any default or custom studio
| Fonts | With a text tool selected, the Font Family pop-up menu on the context toolbar
| Text styles | With a text tool selected, the Paragraph pop-up menu on the context toolbar
| Colours | Display the Swatches panel and use its Palettes pop-up menu
| Switch kit | The Brand Kit pop-up menu on the Canva Brand Kits panel
| Add an asset | Drag a selected object onto an Assets category (then choose a format in the Export dialog), or use Quick Export with the destination set to To Canva and an Assets category chosen
|===

Several elements of a Canva kit do not appear in Affinity: charts, linked folders, category guidelines, the guidelines doc, components and Brand Templates added to the kit. A small number of fonts, including the Morisawa family, are not supported because of font licensing. Each asset category has a limit of 2,000 assets, and only people with edit permission, such as a Team Admin or Brand Designer, can add assets; other people see a message that Brand Kits cannot be edited. Only the kit currently loaded in the panel can receive exports.

=== Procedure: polish the Bookshelf logo with the brand kit

. In Affinity, open the Window menu, select *Canva*, then *Canva Brand Kits*. The panel opens; dock it beside your document.
. In the panel's Brand Kit pop-up menu, select the *Bookshelf* kit.
. Select the text tool, click the tagline of the logo and choose the brand font from the Font Family pop-up menu on the context toolbar.
. Apply a brand colour from the Palettes pop-up menu of the Swatches panel.
. Select the finished logo and drag it onto the *Logos* category in the Canva Brand Kits panel.
. In the Export dialog that appears, choose a format (typically PNG, or JPG, SVG or WebP) and select *Export*. The logo is added to the Bookshelf brand kit.

== Moving work between Affinity and Canva

The two products exchange files; they do not edit the same file together. The official behaviour is summarised below.

.The round trip between Canva and Affinity
image::ui-ux-canva-affinity-round-trip.svg["Diagram of a round trip: Canva downloads an SVG, PDF, PNG or JPG that Affinity opens, and Affinity exports to Canva, where the work arrives as a static image",width=720]

[mermaid]
....
flowchart LR
    A[Canva design] -->|Share, Download as SVG, PDF, PNG or JPG| B[File on disk]
    B -->|Open in Affinity| C[Edit in Affinity]
    C -->|Export or Quick Export, To Canva| D[Uploads or Projects folder in Canva]
    D -->|Static image or graphic| A
....

* *From Canva to Affinity.* Canva files cannot be opened directly in Affinity. Download the design from Canva (Share, then Download) as PDF for multi-page layout and print, SVG for editable vector elements, or PNG or JPG for raster edits, and open the file in Affinity. SVG is the choice that keeps things editable; PDF and PNG can flatten layers.
* *From Affinity to Canva.* Export the document, or a selected area, with *To Canva* as the export location and pick your Uploads folder or a Projects folder. Creating dedicated folders in Canva before exporting keeps work organised.
* *What arrives.* The exported file shows up in Canva as a static image or graphic. You can move it, resize it and add Canva elements around it, but you cannot change the original Affinity layers there. Keep the `.af` file as the source of truth.

The Canva-side download options are described on xref:ui-ux/canva/downloading-and-file-formats.adoc[Downloading and File Formats], and imports on xref:ui-ux/canva/uploads-stock-media-and-imports.adoc[Uploads, Stock Media and Imports].

=== Procedure: send the Bookshelf cover art to Canva

. In Affinity, select the layer in the Layers panel that holds the finished cover illustration.
. On the toolbar, select the arrow next to the Quick Export option (its label reads Export PNG or similar).
. In the dialog, set the file name, dimensions, preset and scale, and choose what to export: *Whole Document* or *Selection Only*.
. Set the export location to *To Canva* and select your Uploads folder or a Projects folder, for example the Bookshelf campaign folder.
. Optionally enable *Show in Browser* to view the file in your Canva account after export, then select *Export*. Name the file and save it if the app asks.
. In Canva, open the Bookshelf launch design and add the uploaded cover art to the page, then place text and elements around it.

.Storyboard: send Bookshelf cover art from Affinity to Canva
image::ui-ux-canva-affinity-storyboard-export-to-canva.svg["Storyboard of five panels: finish the cover art, open the Quick Export arrow, set the location to To Canva, tick Show in Browser and export, and the cover art arriving in Canva as a static image",width=720]

The full Export dialog does the same job: on the File menu select Export, then Export, choose a preset, set To Canva as the export location and pick the folder.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/sharing-export-to-canva/[Canva Help Center: Export Affinity files to Canva]

== Desktop and mobile

Affinity is a separate app, not a mode of the Canva mobile apps. The Help Center's Affinity articles give separate instructions for the desktop app and for mobile, and the Affinity site lists Mac, Windows and iPad downloads. Interface labels such as the Window menu path to the Canva Brand Kits panel apply to the desktop app; on iPad, follow the Mobile tab of each article. In Canva itself, downloads and uploads work the same on web, desktop and phone, so the round trip does not depend on which Canva app you use. See xref:ui-ux/canva/desktop-mobile-and-offline.adoc[Desktop, Mobile and Offline] for the Canva apps.

== Common pitfalls

* *Expecting live editing.* An exported Affinity file arrives in Canva as a static image. Edit the `.af` file in Affinity and export again to update it.
* *Flattened layers.* Downloading a Canva design as PDF or PNG can flatten layers. Prefer SVG when you want to keep vectors editable.
* *Brand Kit panel is empty or locked.* The panel needs an internet connection and a paid Canva plan, and the account must be allowed to edit the kit before assets can be added.
* *Unsupported kit items.* Components, Brand Templates and charts of a Canva kit do not appear in Affinity.
* *Wrong app from a store.* In the Microsoft Store, install only the app whose developer is Canva.
* *AI tools missing.* Canva AI tools in Affinity need a Canva premium plan; for Education accounts only administrators and teachers currently have them.
* *Expecting an Enterprise or school account to work immediately.* Access is turned on by the organization; contact the admin.
