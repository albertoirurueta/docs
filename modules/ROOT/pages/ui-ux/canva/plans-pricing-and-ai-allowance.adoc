This page was checked against the official Canva pages on 2026-11-07. It covers the subscription side of Canva as a whole, not one editor feature: which plan a feature needs, how the monthly AI allowance works, and how to start, change, pause or cancel a subscription. These plans apply to the whole Visual Suite described in xref:ui-ux/canva/introduction-and-product-family.adoc[Introduction and Product Family]; every other page in this sub-section states which plan its feature needs in its own introduction and points back here for the details.

The paid plans are *Pro*, *Business* and *Enterprise*. *Free* is available to anyone. *Education* (for eligible K-12 teachers, students and schools) and *Nonprofits* (for eligible organisations) give most premium features at no cost, and *Canva Campus* is a paid offer for higher education. *Canva Teams* is a legacy plan that is closed to new sign-ups. The AI tools are gated by an *AI tier* (Standard, Premium or Ultra) and share a monthly *AI allowance* that grows with each higher plan. Canva AI 2.0 launched as a research preview on 2026-04-16 and is now documented in the Help Center without a preview label; no general-availability date has been published, and availability varies by account and plan, so treat it as rolling out. This page does not quote prices: they differ by region and currency, change often, and are listed only on https://www.canva.com/pricing/[the Canva pricing page].

The running example is *Bookshelf*, the book catalogue and reading-list app that appears throughout this sub-section. Its launch campaign needs a brand kit, brand templates, Bulk Create, Magic Resize, a Content Planner schedule and a print flyer. The procedures below work out which plan that campaign needs, try it with a trial, keep an eye on the AI allowance and manage the subscription afterwards.

== Canva plans at a glance

Each plan includes what the previous one includes, and more. The pricing page states this as a rule for the main columns, and the Help Center repeats it for Business, which "includes everything in Canva Pro".

[cols="1,3,3", options="header"]
|===
| Plan | Who it is for | Notes on 2026-11-07

| Free | Anyone | One brand kit, 5 GB of cloud storage and a limited AI allowance. Most editor basics, Sheets, Websites, Docs, whiteboards and video are included in the grid.
| Pro | Solo creators | More stock content, five brand kits, 100 GB of storage, brand templates, Bulk Create, print-ready CMYK export and ten times Free's AI allowance for Premium and Ultra tools.
| Business | Individuals who want brand management, and growing teams | Everything in Pro, plus Brand Controls, approvals, team features, reports, 100 brand kits, 500 GB of storage and twenty times Free's AI allowance. It is also required to create a team.
| Enterprise | Large organisations | Everything in Business, plus multi-team organisations, SSO, SCIM, audit logs, organisation-wide brand kits and templates, 1 TB of storage and sales-led pricing.
| Education | Eligible K-12 teachers, students and schools | Premium features free, with ten times Free's AI allowance. Each member has their own allowance.
| Nonprofits | Eligible nonprofit organisations | Pro features free for one team of up to 50 members; extra seats are offered at a discount on Enterprise.
| Canva Campus | Higher education institutions | Paid. Separate staff and student teams, SSO and LMS integrations. Institutions can start on Business and move to Campus at a licence threshold.
| Canva Teams | Existing subscribers only | Closed to new sign-ups and upgrades since 2025-10-30. Existing subscribers keep their price and features, and receive new Pro features but not Business-only ones.
|===

=== Business replaced Teams

Canva introduced Canva Business on 2025-10-30. From that date Teams could no longer be bought or upgraded to. A Teams subscriber who cancels cannot resubscribe to Teams and must choose Pro (for solo use) or Business (for teams) instead. A Teams subscriber who stays keeps the current price, including when adding members, and keeps its features, but does not get features that are exclusive to Business. Designs created inside a Teams workspace belong to that workspace: members who lose access to the team lose access to those designs, so download or share what you need before changing plans.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/about-canva-teams/[Canva Help Center: About Canva Teams]

=== Enterprise, Education, Nonprofits and Campus

Enterprise adds an *organisation* above teams, with organisation admins who handle billing, brand controls, user provisioning and team creation. It is covered in xref:ui-ux/canva/administration-enterprise-and-security.adoc[Administration, Enterprise and Security]. According to the pricing grid, legal protection for content created with AI (Canva Shield indemnity) and control over unprotected content need a minimum number of Enterprise licences, a dedicated customer success manager and creative services need a larger minimum, and Connectors in Canva AI are marked as coming soon for Enterprise. Ask Sales for the current thresholds.

Education is free for eligible, currently active K-12 teachers, librarians and learning-support staff at accredited schools, and for eligible schools, districts and departments of education. The details are in xref:ui-ux/canva/education-and-nonprofits.adoc[Education and Nonprofits]. Nonprofit status is verified through an application, and the free Pro features cover one team of up to 50 members. Campus is the paid higher-education offer: it gives students a free student team and staff a separate team, and an institution can start on Business and move to Campus once it reaches the licence threshold for its full-time student body.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/about-canva-for-education/[Canva Help Center: About Canva Education]

=== Version history and other plan-limited details

Some limits live in Help Center articles rather than in the grid. Version history, for example, is available to Pro, Teams, Business, Education and Nonprofits, keeps up to 1,000 versions with no time limit, and is visible only to the design owner or people with edit access. See xref:ui-ux/canva/collaboration-comments-and-version-history.adoc[Collaboration, Comments and Version History]. Pro-only elements in a design appear watermarked if you fall back to Free, and your designs are never deleted when a plan lapses.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/version-history/[Canva Help Center: Review and restore older versions of designs]

== The AI tiers and the monthly allowance

Canva sorts its AI tools into three tiers. A tier is a cost class, not a quality promise: it tells you how much of your monthly allowance a use draws.

.The three AI tiers share one monthly allowance
image::ui-ux-canva-plans-pricing-and-ai-allowance-ai-tiers.svg["Three panels for the Standard, Premium and Ultra AI tiers with example tools, how each tier draws from the shared monthly allowance and a bar showing heavier use per tier",width=720]

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/ai-access/[Canva Help Center: Understanding your AI usage]

[cols="1,3,3", options="header"]
|===
| Tier | Example tools named in the Help Center | How it uses the allowance

| Standard | Image Upscaler, Magic Formulas | On paid plans these do not count towards the shared allowance (fair use limits apply). On Free they draw from it, and use the least.
| Premium | Magic Layers, generating a design or an image with Canva AI, Canva Code, generating elements (shapes, 3D, charts, forms), the Music and Sound Effect generator, the Form generator, and premium AI tools in Affinity | Part of the shared allowance. Free has limited access, and Affinity's premium AI tools are not available on Free.
| Ultra | Conversational design with Canva AI 2.0, creating a video clip with Canva AI | Part of the shared allowance and the heaviest draw. Paid plans only; Free has no access.
|===

The pricing grid labels the same tiers in its AI section. One tool can carry a different tier label in the grid than in the Help Center article, so treat the Help Center article as the tier reference and check the tool itself.

=== How big the allowance is

The allowance is monthly, belongs to one member and resets on the billing date. On Business, Nonprofits, Education and Enterprise each member has their own allowance: nothing is pooled across a team or organisation. The Help Center states the size as a multiple of Free's allowance.

[cols="2,4", options="header"]
|===
| Plan | AI access on 2026-11-07

| Free | Limited access to Standard and Premium tools; no Ultra and no premium Affinity AI.
| Pro and Nonprofits | Standard tools as much as you like (fair use), plus ten times Free's allowance for Premium and Ultra tools.
| Business | Standard tools as much as you like (fair use), plus twenty times Free's allowance for Premium and Ultra tools.
| Enterprise | Standard tools as much as you like (fair use), plus twenty times Free's allowance for Premium and Ultra tools. Ask Sales for details; the pricing grid points there.
| Education | Standard tools as much as you like (fair use), plus ten times Free's allowance for Premium and Ultra tools.
| Pro or Business trial | The same size as Free: limited Standard and Premium, no Ultra, no premium Affinity AI.
|===

The ranges quoted in the Help Center are best cases: a simple prompt, one kind of tool, the lightest tool in a tier. Heavier tools and more complex prompts use more allowance, so real use usually falls toward the low end.

=== Canva AI 2.0 and the allowance

Canva AI 2.0 is a conversational design partner that can take several editing actions on its own. It draws from the shared allowance, and it draws more than a single-prompt tool, depending on four things: how large the designs you reference are (a 40-slide deck costs more than a 5-slide deck), how complex the task is, how many tools the task uses and which models it needs. Canva AI features are listed as available with Pro, Teams, Business and Nonprofit; Free and Education include some AI features, such as Magic Write and Background Remover, with limited monthly usage, and an Education school admin has to enable Canva AI. A request that creates a campaign across several document types, with files and connected tools, is a complex task. A single Instagram post from a brand kit is a simple one. Canva AI 2.0 itself is covered in xref:ui-ux/canva/canva-ai-assistant.adoc[Canva AI Assistant].

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/new-canva-ai/[Canva Help Center: What's new in Canva AI]

=== Canva AI in other assistants and third-party apps

The same allowance pays for generating designs with Canva AI inside AI assistants connected to Canva, such as ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot (see xref:ui-ux/canva/canva-in-ai-assistants.adoc[Canva in AI Assistants]). Generations made through third-party AI apps from the Apps Marketplace do not draw from it. They have their own subscriptions or limits, and Canva Support cannot grant credits for them.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/ai-access/[Canva Help Center: Understanding your AI usage]

=== AI quality tiers when you generate media

When you generate photos or videos, you can pick the quality level of the result. Standard is available to everyone, Premium is the default and Ultra is the highest quality and available on paid plans only. A higher level draws allowance faster. See xref:ui-ux/canva/ai-image-and-video-generation.adoc[AI Image and Video Generation].

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/canva-ai-quality-tiers-standard-premium-ultra/[Canva Help Center: Set the quality of Canva AI-generated content]

== Getting more allowance

When you near your limit, Canva shows a notification. What happens at the limit depends on your subscription.

* *Free:* short pauses appear between generations for Standard and Premium tools. Ultra tools are not available until the allowance resets.
* *Pro, Teams and Nonprofits:* upgrade, or add AI Pass. Without it you can keep using AI tools with short pauses between tasks.
* *Business:* add AI Pass or an AI top-up.
* *Enterprise and Education:* you can keep using Premium tools with short pauses between tasks.

=== AI Pass

AI Pass is a monthly, recurring add-on that sits beside Pro, Teams, Business or Nonprofits. It gives forty times the AI of the base plan on Pro, Teams and Nonprofits, and twenty times on Business, and it works for the tools that share the allowance, including Ultra. It also works in Affinity for Pro and Business subscribers. It is not available on Free, Education or Enterprise, during a free trial, on prepaid plans (1-day, 1-week, 1-month or 1-year), or on a subscription managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play. On Teams and Business only owners and admins can buy it, per member. Its price is on the Canva pricing and AI Pass pages and is not repeated here.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/ai-pass/[Canva Help Center: Understanding AI Pass]

=== AI top-ups

An AI top-up is a one-off block of extra allowance equal to your monthly allowance on Business, usable within 30 days and then expiring. It is available to Business users only. It does not unlock features; it only adds allowance for tools your subscription already includes. You can buy one manually on web or mobile, or turn on *auto top-ups*, which buy a new block when the allowance runs out up to a spend cap you set for each billing period. Auto top-ups work for web-managed subscriptions; a subscription managed through Apple or Google Play can buy top-ups manually. The AI usage tracker shows the monthly allowance and the top-up allowance separately.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/ai-top-ups/[Canva Help Center: Understanding AI top-ups]

[cols="2,3,3", options="header"]
|===
| | AI Pass | AI top-up

| Plans | Pro, Teams, Business, Nonprofits (web-managed) | Business only
| Shape | Recurring add-on | One-off block, or auto top-ups with a spend cap
| Size | 40x Pro, 20x Business | Same as the monthly Business allowance
| Lifetime | Renews with the add-on | 30 days, no roll-over
| Tools | All Standard, Premium and Ultra tools | All Standard, Premium and Ultra tools
|===

== Trials, billing and changing plan

Free trials of Pro and Business are offered on the upgrade screens. A trial is available once per Canva account and once per payment method, so someone who has tried one before does not see the option. A trial carries the same AI allowance as Free and is excluded from AI Pass; the ten or twenty times allowance applies when you buy a paid plan. A plan renews automatically unless cancelled. You can check the trial end date on the Billing page.

You can switch Pro or Teams to Business at any time. The new plan takes effect immediately and the Business rate applies from your next billing date. If you later cancel Business, your earlier Pro plan is not reinstated. A prepaid Pro plan has to run out first, or be cancelled and resubscribed, before moving to Business. You can pause a monthly or annual plan for up to three months instead of cancelling (an annual plan can be paused when less than two months remain before renewal), and you can cancel from the Billing page, or in the store if you subscribed through Apple or Google. Uninstalling the mobile app does not cancel a subscription.

=== Regional prices and billing channel

Pricing is shown in your local currency and differs by country. The pricing page opened in a European country on 2026-11-07 showed euro prices for annual billing; the same page for another country shows different amounts and currencies. This is the reason this sub-section hard-codes no price. For the same reason, an AI Pass price and a Business seat cost depend on where you buy and whether you pay Canva directly or through an app store.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/upgrade-to-canva-pro-or-business/[Canva Help Center: Upgrade to Canva Pro or Canva Business]

== Desktop and mobile

Billing and plan changes are available in the browser and the desktop app from the profile menu, then Settings, then Billing. The desktop app gives the same features as the browser. Some behaviours depend on where the subscription is managed.

* A subscription bought in the iOS or Android app is managed by Apple or Google. AI Pass is not available on it, auto top-ups are not available on it yet, a plan cannot be paused from the iOS App Store, and cancellation happens in the store.
* Canva Business mobile subscriptions are available to solo users only; creating a team needs a web subscription to Business.
* The AI usage tracker has a mobile view as well as a desktop view.

See xref:ui-ux/canva/desktop-mobile-and-offline.adoc[Desktop, Mobile and Offline] for the apps themselves.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/about-canva-business/[Canva Help Center: About Canva Business]

== Procedures on the Bookshelf campaign

=== Work out which plan the campaign needs

. Write down what the Bookshelf campaign uses: a brand kit, a Book of the week brand template, Bulk Create from a CSV, Magic Resize, the Content Planner and a CMYK flyer.
. Open https://www.canva.com/pricing/ and choose *Compare features*.
. Find each row in the grid. In the grid above, Bulk Create, brand templates, Magic Resize, the Content Planner and CMYK export are all ticked from Pro upwards.
. Check the rows that are not ticked for Pro: Brand Controls and approvals need Business, and inviting people to a team needs Business.
. Decide: a solo launch needs Pro; if the campaign needs reviewer approvals or a shared team, plan for Business.

.Storyboard: choosing a plan for the Bookshelf campaign
image::ui-ux-canva-plans-pricing-and-ai-allowance-storyboard-choose-a-plan.svg["Five-panel storyboard: list campaign needs, find the rows on the pricing grid, try Pro free, open Billing to watch AI usage and pick the lowest plan that fits",width=720]

Source: https://www.canva.com/pricing/[Canva pricing]

=== Try Pro with a free trial

. From the homepage, select *Upgrade your plan* or *Start your free trial*.
. Select *Canva Pro* and then *Upgrade*. If you are eligible, the button reads *Try it free*.
. Choose a monthly or yearly plan, then *Next*.
. Choose a payment method and enter the details to confirm.
. Build the brand template and run a Bulk Create test. Remember that the trial has Free's AI allowance, with no Ultra tools.
. Open *Settings*, then *Billing* to see the trial end date before it converts to a paid plan.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/upgrade-to-canva-pro-or-business/[Canva Help Center: Upgrade to Canva Pro or Canva Business]

=== Track the AI allowance and add more

. Run the Canva AI task you need, for example a prompt that makes a series of Book of the week posts.
. When the limit notification appears, open your profile menu, then *Settings*, then *Billing*.
. Under *AI usage*, read the usage and the remaining balance.
. Select *Get more* in the AI usage section.
. On an annual plan, choose *Monthly* or *Yearly* under billing option.
. Select *Purchase AI Pass*, or upgrade instead. On Business you can also buy an AI top-up.

.Storyboard: running low on the AI allowance
image::ui-ux-canva-plans-pricing-and-ai-allowance-storyboard-ai-limit.svg["Five-panel storyboard: run an AI task, read the limit notification, check usage in Billing, choose Get more with AI Pass or an upgrade, then keep working or wait for the reset",width=720]

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/ai-pass/[Canva Help Center: Understanding AI Pass]

=== Give AI Pass to a team member

. Sign in as a team owner or admin on a web-managed Business or Teams plan.
. Open your profile menu, then *Settings*, then *People*.
. Next to the member who produces the video clips, select *Get more AI*.
. Choose the billing option if you are on an annual plan, then *Purchase AI Pass*.
. Check the new line item on the *Billing* page, where you can manage or cancel it.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/ai-pass/[Canva Help Center: Understanding AI Pass]

=== Switch, pause or cancel

. To move from Pro to Business, open the pricing page and upgrade, or add your first team member and accept the prompt. The new plan applies immediately.
. To pause for up to three months, open *Settings*, then *Billing*, select *Cancel plan* and follow the pause option offered in that flow. Your subscription resumes automatically after an email reminder.
. To cancel, follow the same path and select *Cancel subscription*. If you subscribed through Apple or Google, cancel in the store.
. Download or share the designs you need before removing team members or changing plan.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/cancel-canva-plan/[Canva Help Center: Cancel a Canva plan]

=== Apply for Canva for Nonprofits

If a reading charity runs the book-club meetup that Bookshelf supports, it can apply for the free programme.

. Make sure your Canva account has a valid email address.
. Check the eligibility guidelines for your country on https://www.canva.com/nonprofits/[the Canva for Nonprofits page].
. In *Settings*, open the *Billing* tab and select *Upgrade to Canva for Nonprofits*, then fill out and submit the form.
. Complete the verification process. Canva reviews applications and replies by email, normally within 72 hours; verifying the affiliation can take longer.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/canva-for-nonprofits/[Canva Help Center: Canva for Nonprofits]

== Common mistakes

* *Assuming a feature is Free because a template shows it.* Premium elements in a design appear watermarked if you fall back to Free, so check the elements before a subscription lapses.
* *Planning around an old plan list.* Teams cannot be bought any more. Create a team on Business.
* *Expecting a trial to include Ultra AI or AI Pass.* Trials carry Free's allowance.
* *Buying AI Pass for an app-store subscription.* It needs a web-managed subscription.
* *Expecting the allowance to be pooled.* Each member has their own, even on Business and Enterprise.
* *Blaming Canva for a third-party app running out of credits.* Apps from the Marketplace have their own limits.
* *Using a price from a blog or a book.* Prices differ by region and change; read https://www.canva.com/pricing/[the pricing page] for your country.
