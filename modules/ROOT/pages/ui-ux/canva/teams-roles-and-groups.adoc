This page was checked against the official Canva Help Center on 2026-11-07. It explains how Canva organizes the people who work together: the organization, the team and the group, and the roles a person can hold in each. Creating a team and managing roles needs a paid team plan: *Canva Business* (one team per account), *Canva Enterprise* (several teams under one organization) or *Canva for Nonprofits* (one free team). The Canva Teams plan is no longer sold to new customers, see <<Teams plan and Business>>. Prices are not listed here, see https://www.canva.com/pricing/[Canva pricing]. Inviting members from the mobile app is limited to accounts that subscribed before October 2025.

The running example is *Bookshelf*, the book catalogue and reading-list app designed in the xref:ui-ux/figma/index.adoc[Figma sub-section]. The launch campaign needs several people: a marketing lead who owns the team, a brand designer who maintains the xref:ui-ux/canva/brand-kit-and-brand-voice.adoc[brand kit] and the xref:ui-ux/canva/brand-templates-controls-and-approvals.adoc[brand templates], social creators who build posts, and editorial reviewers who approve them. This page sets up that team, invites those people, gives each a role and a group, and limits what the team may spend and automate. Sharing individual designs is covered on xref:ui-ux/canva/sharing-and-permissions.adoc[Sharing and Permissions], and comments and version history on xref:ui-ux/canva/collaboration-comments-and-version-history.adoc[Collaboration, Comments and Version History].

== Organization, team and group

Canva describes three layers of people, from the widest to the narrowest.

* The *organization* is the top layer. It holds every team, the global security settings such as single sign-on, and central billing. Organization-level roles exist only on plans that support several teams.
* A *team* is a workspace for a department, brand or region. A team has its own members, its own roles, its own brand kit and its own settings. Collaboration between different teams is limited, so a second team is the right answer for a separate business unit and the wrong answer for a working group.
* A *group* is a set of people inside one team, organized by function, for example reviewers or social creators. Groups exist to share folders and designs with many people at once, to act as approvers and to receive permissions.

.One organization with two teams, the groups inside them and the three layers explained
image::ui-ux-canva-teams-roles-and-groups-org-hierarchy.svg["Schematic of an organization containing a Marketing team and an Editorial team, each with groups, plus numbered notes on the organization, team and group layers",width=720]

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/organize-people-projects/[Canva Help Center: How Canva is organized: People and projects]

A Canva Business subscription allows one team per account and up to 100 people through online seat purchases; a larger team needs the sales team. Only Canva Enterprise and Canva for District support multiple teams. Canva for Nonprofits provides one team of up to 50 members at no cost. Teams are not available on 1-day or 1-week plans. Each person added to a paid team uses one seat, and adding someone from outside the team to a group can add a seat too.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/create-teams/[Canva Help Center: Create a team]

A person can belong to several teams. The profile menu on the homepage lists them under Teams, and choosing another one switches the workspace. After a switch you see only the designs, plans and purchases that belong to that team, which is the usual reason for "my designs disappeared" or "my Pro features are gone".

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/switch-team/[Canva Help Center: Switch between multiple teams]

== Roles

A role defines what a person can see and manage. Roles are assigned by team owners, team admins or organization admins, they can be changed at any time, and everybody's role is visible on the People page in Settings.

[cols="1,1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Level | Role | What the role is for

| Organization | Organization admin | Oversees all teams, users and settings, configures single sign-on, SCIM and roster sync, and assigns other organization-level roles
| Organization | Organization brand designer | Creates and manages brand kits and brand templates in any team, can share them with every team, and sees usage reports across all teams
| Team | Team owner | Creates the team and has full control over settings, members and deletion
| Team | Team admin | Manages members, content and permissions, including publishing settings and design approvals, and can manage brand kits, brand templates and brand controls
| Team | Team brand designer | Creates and manages brand kits and brand templates for the team and reads brand template usage reports
| Team | Member | Creates designs and collaborates on shared content with limited access to team settings
|===

On Canva Business the matrix below applies. The owner is the only role that creates and deletes the team, and the owner and admin together manage members, team discovery, brand controls and design approvals. The brand designer role is deliberately narrow: it can build and publish brand assets and read their usage reports but cannot manage people or approvals. Members use everything the team publishes.

.The role matrix for a Business team
image::ui-ux-canva-teams-roles-and-groups-roles-matrix.svg["Schematic matrix of eight team responsibilities against the roles of owner, admin, brand designer and member, marked Yes or No",width=720]

In Canva Enterprise the table has five columns. The organization admin additionally configures SSO, SCIM and roster sync and assigns the other organization-level roles. Team admins manage members, settings, discovery, brand controls and approvals for their own team. Organization brand designers and team brand designers can set up and publish brand kits and templates, and only organization-level roles can share brand kits and brand templates across the whole organization. Usage reports are available to organization admins and organization brand designers for every team, and to team admins and team brand designers for their own team only. Education adds tiers of its own, which are covered on xref:ui-ux/canva/education-and-nonprofits.adoc[Education and Nonprofits].

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/roles-and-permissions/[Canva Help Center: Canva Teams, Canva Business, and Canva Enterprise roles and access]

Auto-synced groups follow the same roles. Canva creates a group per team role and keeps it up to date, so someone given the team admin role joins the matching group and leaves it when the role changes. These groups carry an Auto-synced label and cannot be renamed, edited or deleted. There is no auto-synced group for the Member role: to share with everyone, share with the team itself.

== Invite people and set their roles

The marketing lead, who owns the Bookshelf team, invites the brand designer, two social creators and two reviewers. People need a Canva account that uses the email address the invitation goes to, and each new member uses one paid seat.

. Open the homepage, select your profile, then choose *Settings*.
. In the side menu choose *People*.
. Select *Invite people* (or *Review and invite* when Canva suggests collaborators).
. Enter the email addresses. To invite many people at once, paste a list of addresses into the first field.
. Choose a role for each person. People join as members by default and you can change the role later.
. Select *Invite people to my team*. To send a follow-up link, copy it and select *Done*.

.Invite people, choose roles, create a group and share the folder
image::ui-ux-canva-teams-roles-and-groups-storyboard-invite-and-group.svg["Five-panel storyboard: invite people by email, choose roles, create a Reviewers group, share the Book of the week folder with the group, and reviewers can edit",width=720]

Each invitation is an email link that works for 30 days and only once, so send a new invitation if it expires. Where an invite link is shown, copy it to share it elsewhere and select the deactivate option to stop it working. Join requests appear under invite requests on the People page when discovery allows them, and a team admin approves or declines each one.

To change a role later, open the Team role dropdown next to the person and choose another role. To remove someone, choose Remove from team in the same dropdown and decide what happens to their designs: transfer them and remove the person, which needs ownership transfer to be enabled in the team controls, or remove the person only. Several people can be removed in one step with the checkboxes on the People page.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/manage-members/[Canva Help Center: Add or remove people in a team or class]

== Groups

Groups sit inside a team. Everyone must belong to the team before they can join a group, and group members can collaborate only once they have accepted the team invitation. Two kinds exist: groups you create and manage by hand, and auto-synced groups described above. A group has two roles, *group admin* and *group member*. Group admins rename the group, change its description, add and remove members, change roles and delete the group; the creator is an admin by default and a group can have several. Team owners, organization admins and team admins can manage any group in the team, even without belonging to it.

. Open *Settings*, then *Groups*.
. Select *Create a group* and enter a name such as Reviewers. A description is optional.
. Add people by typing names or emails, picking from the team list, or sharing the group invite link.
. Select *Done*.
. To use the group, share a folder with it: in Projects open a folder's More menu, choose Share and pick the group. A group can also be created from this dialog.

For the Bookshelf campaign the Reviewers group receives edit access to the Book of the week folder, and the same group can be set as the approvers for brand template changes, as described on xref:ui-ux/canva/brand-templates-controls-and-approvals.adoc[Brand Templates, Controls and Approvals].

Team administrators decide in the Permissions tab who is allowed to create groups. When a permission has been assigned to a group, group admins automatically become group members, the group role column disappears and only team admins can change that group. This is the usual reason why someone who created a group suddenly cannot edit it.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/groups/[Canva Help Center: Create groups]

== Team settings and controls

Team owners and admins set rules for the whole team from Settings.

.Where the team settings live
image::ui-ux-canva-teams-roles-and-groups-permissions-map.svg["Schematic of a Settings menu with People, Groups, Permissions, Controls, Billing and Apps and integrations, opening a Permissions page with Share and publish, Controls and Magic and AI sections",width=720]

=== Team discovery

Discovery decides whether other people with the same email domain can find the team. In Settings, open Controls, and under Team access choose who can join: only invited people (private), anyone with the domain email can request to join (the default, with admin approval), or anyone with the domain email can join without approval. Anyone who joins uses a seat on a paid plan. Discovery is not offered for educational domains.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/team-discovery/[Canva Help Center: Team discovery and settings]

=== Share, publish and upload permissions

Under Permissions the admin chooses who can use Canva Print, who can download designs and who can schedule posts in the xref:ui-ux/canva/social-media-and-content-planner.adoc[Content Planner]. The same area limits publishing to selected apps, and the Controls tab limits who uploads media from a device to everyone or only to admins and brand designers, and chooses which third-party apps members can import from. The ownership-transfer switch in Controls lets admins keep a departing member's designs. In an organization, organization admins can set defaults for every team and decide whether team admins may override them, which is why a team admin sometimes finds a setting locked.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/setting-up-team-permissions/[Canva Help Center: Setting up permissions for your team]

=== Purchasing permission

Under Settings, Billing, the Purchasing permission dropdown says who may buy with the team's stored payment method. Owners and billing contacts receive an email for every team purchase, and members who are not allowed to use the team payment method can still add their own.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/purchasing-permission/[Canva Help Center: Set purchasing permissions for your team]

=== Canva AI access

Admins of Business, Enterprise and Education teams, and of existing Teams subscriptions, can decide which AI features members may use. Magic features are on for everyone by default on Business and Teams. Under Settings, Permissions, Magic and AI there is one switch each for the following features.

* Magic Write, Magic Design, Magic Activities, Magic Edit and Magic Media
* Transform into Doc and Dream Lab
* The Help Assistant, Canva AI and Magic Insights
* Canva Code, AI Shapes and AI Charts
* Using team content to generate AI responses
* Canva AI music and sound effects and Image to Video

Changes apply to new designs. On-brand generation depends on two switches, Magic Design (which model creates designs) and Canva AI (the entry points): if either is off, members can still design by hand with the brand kit and brand templates but may not be able to generate new on-brand designs. Availability also depends on language and region, so a switched-on feature may still be missing for some people. How the allowance behind these features is metered is covered on xref:ui-ux/canva/plans-pricing-and-ai-allowance.adoc[Plans, Pricing and the AI Allowance].

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/magic-features-admin-controls/[Canva Help Center: Change access to Magic and AI features]

=== Admin tidy-up of shared content

Owners and admins can move and delete folders and move items such as designs, brand templates, images and videos that they can at least view, including content from former members. A deleted item owned by someone else stays in its owner's trash, and the owner is notified.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/manage-content-as-team-admin-or-owner/[Canva Help Center: Manage your team's content as a team admin or owner]

== Transfer ownership and add teams

Only the team owner can transfer ownership, and only to a member with an active account. The owner chooses Change owner under Team profile in Settings, nominates a member and confirms with a code sent by email. The nominee has 30 days to accept, and until then the nominator stays owner and can withdraw the nomination. On Business, update the team payment method before transferring, because it cannot be changed afterwards.

An Enterprise organization admin adds a team under Settings, Teams, Create a team, where the name and an optional SAML team ID are entered. The first extra team turns a single-team organization into a multi-team one, after which content, admin roles and brand kits may need to be moved or set up again.

Sources: https://www.canva.com/help/transfer-and-take-over-teams/[Canva Help Center: Transfer and take over teams] and https://www.canva.com/help/enterprise-manage-teams-organization-structure/[Canva Help Center: Manage teams and organization structure in Canva Enterprise]

== Common pitfalls

* Creating a second team when a group would do. Teams are separate workspaces with limited collaboration.
* Giving brand designer to someone who must manage people. The role cannot manage members, settings or approvals.
* Forgetting that auto-synced groups cannot be edited: change the person's role instead.
* Expecting a Member auto-synced group. Share with the team instead.
* Letting a payment method belong to a personal card before transferring ownership.
* Leaving the discovery setting on the default when the team should be private.
* Inviting an address that differs from the person's Canva account email.

== Teams plan and Business

Older guides describe a Canva Teams plan and show how to create a team from it. Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups or upgrades; Canva Business, which includes everything in Canva Pro plus higher AI usage limits, brand management tools and marketing features, replaced it for new customers on 2025-10-30. Existing Teams subscribers keep their pricing and features and continue to receive the premium features that are added to Pro, but not the features exclusive to Business. If a Teams subscription is cancelled it cannot be resumed, and designs created inside the team workspace stay with that workspace. Role names such as owner, admin and member are still used, but the current plans, the Business limit of one team per account and the Business-only brand management tools are not in those guides. For the full list of corrections see xref:ui-ux/canva/whats-changed-since-older-guides.adoc[What Changed Since Older Guides].
