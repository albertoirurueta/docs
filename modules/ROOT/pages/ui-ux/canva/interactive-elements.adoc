This page was checked against the official Canva Help Center on 2026-11-07. It covers the elements that make a design do something when someone reads it: forms, polls and quizzes, dropdowns, dates, hyperlinks, QR codes and equations. They are available in Canva on the web, the desktop app and the mobile apps, with the limits noted per element. Forms, polls and quizzes, hyperlinks and the Static QR codes app are not tied to a paid plan, whereas several Dynamic QR code features (Insights, expiry dates, collaborator editing, custom logos) need Canva Business, Canva Enterprise or Canva for Nonprofit. Prices are not repeated here; they are published at https://www.canva.com/pricing/.

Two items use Canva AI. Adding a form from the Elements tab counts as a premium feature toward the AI allowance, and creating a form with Canva AI draws on the same allowance (tiers Standard, Premium and Ultra are explained on xref:ui-ux/canva/plans-pricing-and-ai-allowance.adoc[Plans, Pricing and AI Allowance]). Generating a quiz from selected text is powered by Magic Write and is included in all plans except in China. Canva AI 2.0 launched as a research preview on 2026-04-16; no general-availability date is published, and availability varies by account, language and region.

The running example is *Bookshelf*, the book catalogue and reading-list app designed in the xref:ui-ux/figma/index.adoc[Figma sub-section]. Here the launch campaign gets a reader poll, a linked flyer and a QR code.

== Where interactive elements live

The Help Center groups them under Editing and designing, Elements, Interactive elements: forms and quizzes, dates, dropdowns, Hello QArt and Equations. In the editor most are reached from the side panel (Elements for forms, Apps for QR codes and equations) or from the floating toolbar and the quick actions menu opened with the slash key. Dates and dropdowns are special: they exist only in Canva Docs and Canva Sheets.

== Forms, polls and quizzes

A form collects answers inside a design. Canva calls the simplest versions polls and quizzes; a quiz is a form whose options include a correct answer. The Help Center gives these limits: up to 10 questions per form, up to 10 options per question, up to 10 forms per page and 20 per design. They can be added to all design types except Docs and Email. To collect responses a form has to be shared through a Canva link; a design downloaded as an image, video or PDF does not collect anything.

=== Three ways to create one

* From the library: in the editor side panel select Elements, then under Browse categories select Forms, use See all to view the types of a category and select a form to add it.
* With Canva AI: open Canva AI from the homepage or the editor side panel and describe the form, for example a post-event poll or a quiz about land animals. The plus icon adds context (uploads, files from Canva, brand and connected apps), and the result is added as a new page.
* From your content: select two or three full sentences of text (or sticky notes, shapes or lines with text), choose Magic Write on the floating toolbar and then Generate quiz. If there is not enough content, select more text and retry; failed attempts do not count toward your AI usage.

=== Customise and read the answers

Select the form and choose Edit from the quick-access menu. In the Questions tab you add or remove questions, change question types, set required fields, add helper text and mark correct answers. In the Settings tab you show or hide the header field, change the submit button text and decide whether respondent names are collected. Appearance options (colours, border style, corner rounding, transparency, animation, position) sit in the same quick-access menu, and the text toolbar formats the text.

To read results, select the form, choose Responses and, from More, Download as CSV. If you change options after entries exist, the data is relabelled: a deleted option marks earlier submissions Inactive, changing the correct answer of a quiz re-scores earlier submissions, and edited labels apply to all earlier submissions. The form owner gets an email when someone submits, up to five notification emails a day with batching; you cannot add other recipients. Notifications are switched in the Settings tab of the form, or in Data, Settings of the connected sheet for Canva Code forms.

Name collection decides anonymity: with it off, responses are anonymous even from signed-in users, and on Canva Websites responses are always anonymous. Pro accounts see data insights.

=== Add a reader poll to the Bookshelf post

. Open the *Book of the week* Instagram post design.
. In the side panel select Elements and, under Browse categories, Forms.
. Select See all for the poll type you want and select a form to place it on the page.
. Select the form, choose Edit and enter the question "Which genre next?" with the options Fantasy, Mystery and Poetry.
. In the Questions tab mark a correct answer only if you are building a quiz; in Settings leave name collection off.
. Share the design with a Canva link, then select the form and open Responses to read the votes.

== Dropdowns

Dropdowns create selectable menus in Docs and Sheets, useful for status labels or categories. On the floating toolbar select Actions, or click a cell and type / to open the quick actions, then search for Dropdown and choose Create dropdown or a preset. Hover an option to rename it, change its colour or delete it, and use Add new option for more. To turn existing entries into options, highlight the cells or whole column, choose Actions, Dropdown, Create dropdown, and Canva converts the values. When the same dropdown appears elsewhere in a sheet, an edit prompts you to apply the change to all similar dropdowns. Delete a dropdown by selecting the cell and pressing the delete key.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/dropdowns/[Canva Help Center: Add dropdowns in Canva]

== Dates

Dates are also limited to Docs and Sheets. Select the cell or the spot on the page, choose the number format (the 123 icon) or Actions and then Date, or type /date. Click the date pill to pick a day from the calendar; /today, /tomorrow and /yesterday insert relative dates. In Sheets type dates as MM/DD/YYYY. To restyle, choose number format, More formats, Date formats and pick Month Day, Year, Month/Day/Year or Month Year. If a date shows as text, check the format, stray spaces and that the cell format is Date rather than Text; Canva follows the device and browser regional settings.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/add-dates/[Canva Help Center: Add and customize dates in your design]

== Hyperlinks and interactive PDFs

You can link text, elements, images and videos. Select the element (or double-click a text box and highlight the words), choose More on the floating toolbar, then Link. Enter a web address, or choose a page of the same design from Pages in this document, or one of your recent designs from Recent; make sure readers have permission to open a linked Canva design. To change a link select it and choose Edit link, where you can alter Text, Link and Display mode, copy or delete the link.

The important rule is where links keep working. Hyperlinks stay clickable when you download the design as a PDF (flattened PDFs excluded) or present it. For video, links are clickable only in the Presenter window in Presenter view. They do not work in PNG, JPG or MP4 files, nor when you post to social media platforms, including PDF uploads there. In Sheets select the text inside the cell, not the whole cell, before adding a link. If a link on text with effects does not work, the Help Center has a dedicated troubleshooting article.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/hyperlinks/[Canva Help Center: Add hyperlinks]

== QR codes

Canva offers two tools. Static QR codes give a quick fixed link with no setup. Dynamic QR codes let you change the destination, customise patterns and corners and, on Business, Enterprise and Nonprofit, track scans, set an expiry date, allow collaborators to edit and use a custom logo. A static code can never be edited and neither type converts into the other. Dynamic codes work on web and mobile.

.Static and dynamic QR codes compared
image::ui-ux-canva-interactive-elements-qr-static-vs-dynamic.svg["Two panels comparing a static QR code with a fixed link to a dynamic QR code with an editable destination, analytics and expiry",width=720]

=== Create and manage a code

From the editor choose Apps and search QR code, or go to Elements and search QR code, where a banner offers Create static and Create dynamic. For a static code enter the URL, optionally set background colour, foreground colour and margin, then Save. For a dynamic code select a pattern, enter the URL, optionally name it, then Save. To edit, select the code and choose Edit: the URL can be updated with no reprint, whereas visual changes mean reprinting if the code is already printed. By default only the creator can edit a dynamic code, even if you can edit the design. Deleted dynamic codes created on or after 2026-10-01 can be restored by Canva Support within 60 days; earlier ones cannot.

Best practice from the Help Center: keep codes at least 2 by 2 cm, shorten the URL, use strong contrast and no transparency, keep other content away from the code and leave a quiet zone of at least four times the width of one module.

=== Hello QArt

Hello QArt is an app that merges a picture described in words with a QR code. Open Apps, search Hello QArt, enter the URL, describe the imagery and select Generate. Scan the result to test it, and generate a new one if it fails. It is not the app for editing a code later; only Dynamic QR codes allows that.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/hello-qart/[Canva Help Center: Create artistic QR codes with Hello QArt]

=== Link the Bookshelf flyer and add a QR code

. Open the *Bookshelf Launch Flyer* and select the "Read the catalogue" button element.
. Select More on the floating toolbar, then Link, and choose the catalogue page of the design or enter the web address.
. In the side panel open Apps and search QR code; select the dynamic app if the printed flyer's destination may change.
. Choose a pattern, enter the link, name the code and select Save.
. Test the code with a phone camera before printing, and keep the quiet zone clear.
. Download the flyer as a PDF so the button stays clickable; the QR code carries the link into print.

== Equations

The Equations (beta) app inserts typeset math. Open Apps, search Equations (beta), type the equation using the quick buttons and symbol sets, choose a colour with the picker or a hex code, and select Insert equation. Resize with the corner handles and animate it from the menu. The app carries a beta label in the Help Center as of the check date.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/math-equations/[Canva Help Center: Use the Equations (beta) app]

== Desktop and mobile

The Help Center documents the form, dropdown, date, hyperlink and equation steps for both the desktop browser and the mobile app, and dynamic QR codes are supported on mobile. Menus are shorter on a phone, so the quick actions and More menus are where options hide. Keyboard shortcuts are not published for these elements beyond the slash-key quick actions; see xref:ui-ux/canva/keyboard-shortcuts-and-quick-actions.adoc[Keyboard Shortcuts and Quick Actions].

== Common pitfalls

* A form exported as an image, video or PDF stops collecting answers; share a Canva link.
* Hyperlinks die in PNG, JPG and MP4 exports and on social platforms; use PDF, present mode or a QR code.
* Dates and dropdowns are not available in ordinary designs, only in Docs and Sheets.
* Changing a quiz answer after submissions silently re-scores earlier responses.
* A printed static QR code cannot be redirected; choose dynamic when the destination may change.
* Changing a dynamic code's look after printing requires a reprint.
