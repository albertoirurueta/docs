This page was checked against the official Canva Help Center on 2026-11-07. Downloading belongs to the Canva editor on the web, the desktop app and the mobile apps, and it is generally available (no beta or research preview is involved). Every plan can download a design, and most formats are available to everyone. A group of options is limited to paid plans: transparent backgrounds, SVG, quality and file size controls, compressed PDFs, the CMYK colour profile and bulk download. The Help Center names these plans as Pro, Teams, Business, Education and Nonprofits, and its articles are not uniform about which tiers are named, so check the Download panel of your own account; see xref:ui-ux/canva/plans-pricing-and-ai-allowance.adoc[Plans, Pricing and AI Allowance] for the tiers and https://www.canva.com/pricing/ for current prices, which this page does not repeat. No AI tier applies to downloading.

The running example is *Bookshelf*, the book catalogue and reading-list app designed in the xref:ui-ux/figma/index.adoc[Figma sub-section]. Its launch campaign needs several outputs from one account: a "Book of the week" Instagram post as an image, a five-page carousel, a print flyer, a launch deck that must open in PowerPoint and a 30-second promo video. This page shows how to pick the format for each, which settings matter, and what to check when the file does not look as expected.

== Where downloading lives

Everything starts in the Share menu of the top bar. Choose Share, then Download, and the Download panel opens with a file type list and the options that apply to the chosen type. The same panel exists on mobile, where it appears after Share. Packaging for other destinations (a link, a social account, a website, print ordering) is on the same Share menu and is covered on xref:ui-ux/canva/sharing-and-permissions.adoc[Sharing and Permissions] and xref:ui-ux/canva/print-and-mockups.adoc[Print and Mockups].

.A numbered walk through the download panel of a Bookshelf post
image::ui-ux-canva-downloading-and-file-formats-storyboard-download.svg["Five-panel storyboard: open Share then Download, pick PNG, set size and quality, choose the pages, download the file",width=720]

The panel is context sensitive. The options below the file type change with the type, and some entries are missing in some design types: WebP is not available for Canva Docs, PPTX is only for presentations and DOCX is only for Canva Docs. Sheets offers CSV and XLSX. When something needs attention, the Download button can read Fix followed by a number (for example because an upload has not finished or a daily download limit was reached), and a Tip or Warning appears at the bottom of the panel when Canva spots something that may affect quality or layout. Review it, change the design or choose Download to continue unchanged.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/download-file-types/[Canva Help Center: Choose the right download file type for your Canva design]

== Choosing a format

The first decision is the purpose of the file, not the format. The chooser below groups the formats by what you will do with them.

.Choosing a download format by purpose
image::ui-ux-canva-downloading-and-file-formats-format-chooser.svg["Decision chart grouping formats into pictures on screen, documents and print, and motion and data",width=720]

[cols="1,3,3", options="header"]
|===
| Format | Best for | Notes from the Help Center

| JPG | Photographs and anything that must stay small | Quality Low, Medium, High or Limit size; no transparency
| PNG | Graphics, text and logos | Quality Compress, High or Limit size; supports a transparent background
| WebP | Websites | Much smaller than JPG or PNG, supports transparency, High means lossless; not for Canva Docs
| SVG | Web graphics that must stay sharp at any size | Keeps quality at any size and supports transparency
| PDF Standard | Illustrations, text and graphics for screens, 96 dpi | The only PDF type that supports a password; keeps links
| PDF Print | Printing, 300 dpi | Bleed, crop marks and an RGB or CMYK colour profile
| PPTX | Opening a presentation in Microsoft PowerPoint | Presentations only
| DOCX | Opening a Canva Doc in Microsoft Word | Canva Docs only
| GIF | Short animated designs | Size from 0.2x to 1x or a typed size, optional transparent background
| MP4 | Designs with video and music | Quality 480p, 720p, 1080p or 4K (desktop only)
| CSV | Raw Sheets data for spreadsheet or analysis tools | Sheets only
| XLSX | Structured spreadsheets for Microsoft Excel | Sheets only; keeps formatting and formulas where possible
|===

Two rules of thumb follow from the table. Use PNG for anything with flat colour or text, such as the Bookshelf logo or a quote card, and JPG for photographic content where a smaller file matters more than perfect edges. Use a PDF whenever the recipient needs a document rather than a picture, and PDF Print whenever a printer will receive it.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/download-file-types/[Canva Help Center: Choose the right download file type for your Canva design]

== Size, quality and limits

For JPG, WebP and PNG, the Size control scales the export. Drag the slider or type a value, and the pixel size follows the page size multiplied by the factor. The Quality control trades detail for weight. JPG offers Low, Medium and High, WebP offers Low, Medium and High (lossless), and PNG offers Compress and High. Each of these also has Limit size, where you type a ceiling in KB.

.A size multiplier on a Bookshelf post and the export limits for JPG and PNG
image::ui-ux-canva-downloading-and-file-formats-size-and-limits.svg["Three page rectangles at 0.5x, 1x and 1.5x of a 1080 by 1350 post next to a panel with the JPG and PNG limits",width=720]

The published limits for JPG and PNG are a maximum file size of 1,000,000 KB, minimum dimensions of 40 x 40 px and maximum dimensions of 8000 x 3125 px. A design can have up to 350 pages. If a design is larger than these limits at the chosen multiplier, lower the multiplier.

=== Limit size

Limit size is useful when a platform rejects large uploads. It is available only for PNG, JPG and WebP, and only when you download a single page, so choose This Page or Custom under Select pages first. After you choose Limit size under Quality and type the number of KB, the Quality options may become unavailable. If Canva cannot reach the exact value, for example because the content is complex, it exports the smallest file it can within its limits, and the file may be larger than the value you entered so that image quality is retained.

=== Premium content and the Download button

If a design contains paid stock elements and you are on Free, the Download button changes to Pay and Download. You can pay for a Pro Content License, upgrade, or download a watermarked draft. On paid plans the licences come with your subscription and you are not asked to pay. Licensing in more detail is on xref:ui-ux/canva/licensing-responsible-ai-and-canva-shield.adoc[Licensing, Responsible AI and Canva Shield].

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/download-file-types/[Canva Help Center: Choose the right download file type for your Canva design]

== Pages and multi-page downloads

If a design has several pages, choose All for the entire design, This Page for the page in view, or Custom for specific pages. Image formats of several pages are delivered as a single ZIP file that is named after the design, and the files inside are named after page numbers or page titles. For example, pages 1, 2 and "3 - FB Format" of a design named "My Design" produce a ZIP named My Design.zip containing 1.jpg, 2.jpg and FB Format.jpg. You can rename the ZIP before saving but not the files inside it. The page selection is kept only while the design stays open; closing or refreshing it resets the selection to all pages.

To export part of a page rather than a whole page, the Help Center describes a separate selection download that is not covered here. Bulk download of several designs from Projects is described on xref:ui-ux/canva/projects-folders-and-file-management.adoc[Projects, Folders and File Management]. Its limits per the Help Center: formats PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP, MP4 and GIF; only one file type per download; up to five top-level folders (subfolders are not included); up to 200 designs at once, or 25 when the file type is MP4 or GIF; and the result is one flattened ZIP file.

== Transparent backgrounds

A transparent export keeps the page background empty so the file can sit on top of other artwork, which is what a logo needs. It is a paid option that is offered for PNG and WebP (and GIF), and the Help Center states that it is not possible for Canva Docs. The toggle is called Transparent background.

The background itself must be empty. The article advises setting the page colour to white (the default of a blank design) or deleting background colours and images, because a coloured or photographic background is exported as it is. If an image sits on the page, remove its own background first with the tools described on xref:ui-ux/canva/ai-photo-tools.adoc[AI Photo Tools]. If the toggle is missing, the usual causes are a plan without the option, a file type other than PNG or WebP, or a Canva Doc.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/transparent-background/[Canva Help Center: Download design with transparent background]

== PDF: Standard, Print and the Advanced Settings

PDFs have two presets. PDF Standard is described by Canva as the choice for illustrations, text and graphics at a resolution of 96 dpi, and it is the only type that can be password protected, which needs a PDF viewer that supports protected files. PDF Print targets printing at 300 dpi with bleed, crop marks and a colour profile (RGB or CMYK) option. A Compress PDF option reduces the file size at the cost of image quality and is for paid plans. The Advanced Settings section appears only for PDFs.

.PDF Standard compared with PDF Print
image::ui-ux-canva-downloading-and-file-formats-pdf-standard-vs-print.svg["Two pages side by side: PDF Standard at 96 dpi and PDF Print at 300 dpi with crop marks and an amber bleed margin",width=720]

=== Bleed and crop marks

Bleed is extra artwork that extends past the trim edge so no white strip appears when the sheet is cut; crop marks show the printer where to cut. A background colour or photo that must touch the edge should run into the bleed. The editor can show a Margins guide and rulers from File, Settings (see xref:ui-ux/canva/editor-tour.adoc[Editor Tour]), and the layout side of print is on xref:ui-ux/canva/frames-grids-guides-and-print-layout.adoc[Frames, Grids, Guides and Print Layout].

=== CMYK

Screens mix light (RGB) and printers layer inks (CMYK), so very bright colours such as electric blues or hot pinks can look softer in print. The Help Center says that when a design is sent to Canva Print the colours are converted automatically to the closest CMYK match, that a CMYK PDF is among the options of paid plans, and that Canva support does not review designs before printing, so proof the file yourself. For colour planning it suggests choosing print-safe colours from the start and ordering one test copy; the editor itself works with hex codes.

=== Flatten PDF

Flatten PDF, in Advanced Settings, converts the layers into one so that all elements print and fonts display exactly as designed. The article says the result cannot be edited in software like Adobe Acrobat and that the file is larger, especially with high resolution images.

=== Cropped images and passwords

A PDF keeps the original of every cropped picture. Someone with a PDF editor could therefore recover the uncropped image. The Help Center advises deleting what you do not want seen, or recreating the image inside the design, before downloading. Use the Standard preset when a password is needed, and share the password separately from the file.

=== Presenter notes

To print notes, download as a PDF, choose the Digital or Print preset, select the pages and switch on Include notes under Advanced Settings. The procedure is on xref:ui-ux/canva/presentations.adoc[Presentations] and in the Editor Tour.

Sources: https://www.canva.com/help/download-file-types/[Canva Help Center: Choose the right download file type for your Canva design]; https://www.canva.com/help/download-flattened-pdf/[Canva Help Center: Download designs as flattened PDF]; https://www.canva.com/help/cmyk-for-print/[Canva Help Center: Design with print colors]

== PowerPoint, Word, spreadsheet and data formats

PPTX is for presentations only and DOCX is for Canva Docs only; other design types cannot be downloaded that way. Neither format is a perfect copy. The Help Center warns that PPTX and DOCX downloads may look different when opened in Microsoft PowerPoint or Word, that you may need to install the fonts you used in Canva, and that animations and embedded videos are not yet supported there. If the exact look matters, send a PDF and keep the Canva link for editing. See xref:ui-ux/canva/presentations.adoc[Presentations] and xref:ui-ux/canva/docs.adoc[Docs].

Canva Sheets downloads as CSV, for raw data that goes to spreadsheet or analysis tools, and as XLSX, which keeps formatting and formulas where possible. See xref:ui-ux/canva/sheets.adoc[Sheets] for charts, Magic Formulas and the data connectors.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/download-file-types/[Canva Help Center: Choose the right download file type for your Canva design]

== Video and GIF

MP4 is for designs with video and music. The Quality list has 480p, 720p, 1080p and 4K, and 4K is available on desktop only. On Free, changing the quality or resolution from the default shows an upgrade prompt that is not an error; keep the default to download without upgrading. A GIF offers a Size choice of 0.2x, 0.4x, 0.6x, 0.8x or 1x, or a typed value, plus a transparent background toggle.

.The MP4 and GIF settings with the published limits
image::ui-ux-canva-downloading-and-file-formats-video-and-gif.svg["Two panels: MP4 quality levels from 480p to 4K and GIF size multipliers, each with the free and paid duration and resolution limits",width=720]

The published limits are, on Free, MP4 files of up to 30 minutes at a maximum of 1920 x 1080 px and GIFs of up to 1 minute at a maximum of 1280 x 720 px; on the paid plans named in the article, MP4 files of up to 2 hours at 3840 x 2160 px and GIFs of up to 2 minutes at 1920 x 1080 px. If premium or paid audio is in the video and you will post it on social platforms, verify the licence when downloading to avoid copyright claims. When the video shows an error icon, the playback troubleshooting article of the Help Center applies. Editing videos is on xref:ui-ux/canva/video-editor-audio-and-captions.adoc[Video Editor, Audio and Captions] and animation on xref:ui-ux/canva/animation-and-transitions.adoc[Animation and Transitions].

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/download-as-video/[Canva Help Center: Downloading designs as video or GIF]

== Saved download settings, file names and devices

Turn on Save download settings to make your file type and its options the default for new designs of the same type on the current device. The settings are stored locally and do not sync across devices. They are not saved for page selections after you close the design, across devices or across different design types, and the option does not appear in files that mix several design formats, such as a sheet, a presentation and a Doc in one project. Settings can reset if you switch browsers or devices, clear cookies and history, or stay signed out until the session expires. For a one-off export, leave the toggle off.

By default the downloaded file takes the name of the design, such as My Design.jpg, and a second download gets a number from your device, such as My Design(1).jpg. The desktop app asks for a name and location every time. In a browser the behaviour follows the browser setting (the Help Center explains how to turn on the Save As prompt in Chrome, Safari and Edge). On iOS and Android, files saved to the photo library use default names, whereas files saved to storage can be renamed.

Sources: https://www.canva.com/help/save-download-settings/[Canva Help Center: Save download settings for your designs]; https://www.canva.com/help/download-file-types/[Canva Help Center: Choose the right download file type for your Canva design]

== Desktop and mobile

The Download panel works the same way on the web, in the desktop app and on mobile, with a few differences:

* The desktop app prompts for a location every time and is where the Help Center says MP4 4K is available ("desktop only").
* The mobile apps save to the photo library or to a file location, and only the file location lets you rename the file.
* Shortcuts for downloading are not documented in the Help Center articles checked here, so use Share, then Download. Other shortcuts are listed on xref:ui-ux/canva/keyboard-shortcuts-and-quick-actions.adoc[Keyboard Shortcuts and Quick Actions].

== Procedures on the Bookshelf campaign

=== Download the Book of the week post as a PNG

. Open the Book of the week design and select Share, then Download.
. Choose PNG as the file type.
. Set Size to 1x and Quality to High. Leave Transparent background off, because the post has a coloured tint.
. Under Select pages, choose All to get the whole carousel as a ZIP, or This Page for the post in view.
. Select Download. If paid elements are in the design and you are on Free, the button reads Pay and Download.

The storyboard figure at the top of this page shows the same five steps.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/download-file-types/[Canva Help Center: Choose the right download file type for your Canva design]

=== Export the Bookshelf logo with a transparent background

. Open the logo design. Remove any background colour or image from the page, or set the page colour to white.
. Select Share, then Download and choose PNG (or WebP).
. Switch on Transparent background and choose a Size that covers the largest use you expect.
. Select Download and test the file on a dark background to confirm that the edges are clean.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/transparent-background/[Canva Help Center: Download design with transparent background]

=== Prepare the print flyer for the printer

.A numbered walk through the PDF Print settings for the Bookshelf flyer
image::ui-ux-canva-downloading-and-file-formats-storyboard-print-pdf.svg["Five-panel storyboard: open Share then Download, choose PDF with the Print preset, set crop marks bleed and colour profile, flatten, send the file",width=720]

. Select Share, then Download and choose PDF.
. Under Presets, choose Print.
. Choose All pages, or the pages that belong in the print job.
. Open Advanced Settings and choose the options that your printer asks for: crop marks, bleed and, on a paid plan, the CMYK colour profile.
. Switch on Flatten PDF if the printer must see a single layer with fonts exactly as designed. Switch on Save download settings if you will repeat the export.
. Select Download. Open the file and check the margins before you send it. If you order from Canva Print instead, follow xref:ui-ux/canva/print-and-mockups.adoc[Print and Mockups].

Sources: https://www.canva.com/help/download-file-types/[Canva Help Center: Choose the right download file type for your Canva design]; https://www.canva.com/help/download-flattened-pdf/[Canva Help Center: Download designs as flattened PDF]

=== Download the 30-second promo as MP4 and GIF

. Optionally preview the design above the video editor, then select Share, then Download.
. Choose MP4 Video and leave Quality at 1080p. Select All pages.
. Select Download. Then repeat with GIF, Size 0.6x, for a short loop on a website.
. If the video has licensed audio, verify the licence on download.

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/download-as-video/[Canva Help Center: Downloading designs as video or GIF]

== Pitfalls and troubleshooting

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Symptom | What to check

| The image looks blurry or soft | Raise Size above 1x, use PNG or High quality instead of Low, and avoid Limit size on photographs. For print, choose PDF Print (300 dpi) rather than PDF Standard (96 dpi).
| The file is too big | Use JPG or WebP, lower Quality or use Limit size on a single page; for PDFs use Compress PDF if your plan has it
| The background is not transparent | Delete the background colour or image or set it to white, choose PNG or WebP and switch Transparent background on; it is not available for Docs
| Fonts changed in PowerPoint or Word | Install the fonts you used, or send a PDF; animations and videos do not play in PPTX or DOCX
| Colours look dull in print | Convert to CMYK early, choose print-safe colours and order a test copy
| The Download button says Fix | Open it and resolve the item, for example wait for uploads to finish, then select Download again
| A premium element asks for payment | Buy the licence, replace the element, upgrade or download a watermarked draft
| Saved settings disappeared | They are per device and per design type; they reset when cookies are cleared or the session expires
| A multi-page PDF leaks a cropped photo | Delete the hidden part or recreate the image before downloading
|===

Source: https://www.canva.com/help/download-file-types/[Canva Help Center: Choose the right download file type for your Canva design]
