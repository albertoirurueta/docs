SAP is a family of business software, and SAP S/4HANA is its current core ERP system: the one place where a company keeps its accounts, sales, purchasing, production and people data. This subsection teaches SAP from zero. It explains what the products are and how a company is set up in them, how a user works with them, and how a developer builds applications and services around them with SAP BTP, the Cloud Application Programming Model (CAP), SAPUI5 and SAP Fiori elements, OData and the integration tools. Two larger areas have their own indexes: xref:erp/sap/finance/index.adoc[Finance] and xref:erp/sap/human-resources/index.adoc[Human Resources]. The ABAP language has its own section, xref:programming-languages/abap/index.adoc[ABAP]. A complete beginner starts with xref:erp/sap/start-here.adoc[Start Here: SAP from Zero].

== Version baseline

[cols="1,3",options=header]
|===
|Product |Baseline used on the pages

|SAP S/4HANA on premise and Private Edition
|2025 FPS02 (the Product Assistance label reads "2025 FPS02 (Oct 2026)").

|SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition
|2608. Public Edition upgrades arrive twice a year.

|CAP
|`@sap/cds` 10.2 on Node.js 22 or newer, and CAP Java 5.2 on Java 21 or newer (Java 25 is the recommended version).

|SAPUI5
|1.154 (the 2611 release) is the latest version; 1.148 (the 2605 release) is the long-term maintenance version.

|SAP SuccessFactors
|2H 2026 (the 2611 release).

|SAP HCM for SAP S/4HANA (H4S4)
|A separate license is needed. From SAP S/4HANA 2025 its business function is activated by default.

|Product name
|The SAP Help Portal still names the platform SAP Business Technology Platform (SAP BTP). SAP's product page now presents SAP BTP capabilities as part of SAP Business AI Platform, so both names appear in current material.
|===

SAP's announced maintenance dates frame how long a system landscape is likely to stay as it is:

[cols="2,3",options=header]
|===
|Product |Announced maintenance

|SAP ERP 6.0 (SAP Business Suite 7)
|Mainstream maintenance until the end of 2027, with optional extended maintenance until the end of 2030.

|SAP S/4HANA
|Maintenance commitment until the end of 2040.

|SAP ERP, private edition, transition option
|Available for purchase from 2028, with use from 2031 to 2033.
|===

SAP's announcement names SAP S/4HANA for the 2040 date. For SAP HCM for SAP S/4HANA, confirm the date with SAP's current statements before planning around it. Where a page and this baseline ever disagree after a later update, the baseline is the one to trust.

== Running example

Every business page uses one invented company, *Example Trading Co.*, with company code `EX01`. All codes, numbers and names are examples made for this site and are not values delivered by SAP. The Finance and Human Resources indexes extend the company with ledgers, accounts and employees.

The development pages add a small CAP application for the same company, a partner dashboard that shows business partners read from the company's SAP S/4HANA system. xref:erp/sap/btp-account-model.adoc[SAP BTP Account Model] plans its subaccounts, xref:erp/sap/btp-connectivity-and-destinations.adoc[Connectivity, Destinations and Cloud Connector] connects it to the back end, and xref:erp/sap/guide-consume-an-sap-api-from-cap.adoc[Consume an SAP API Specification in CAP] builds the consuming part against a mocked SAP API. The other guides use small teaching models in the lab, such as expense claims for CAP, SAPUI5 and OData.
