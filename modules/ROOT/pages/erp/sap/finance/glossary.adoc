= Finance Glossary
:description: Alphabetical glossary of the SAP Finance terms used in the Finance part, from account determination and asset accounting to universal journal and zero balance clearing, each in plain words with a link to the page that explains it, for SAP S/4HANA 2025 and Cloud Public Edition.
:keywords: SAP Finance glossary, FI, CO, general ledger, universal journal, cost center, asset accounting, profit center, posting period, clearing, definitions

include::partial$ai-disclaimer.adoc[]

This glossary explains, in plain words, the finance and accounting terms used across the Finance part of the SAP section, such as general ledger, cost center, posting period and clearing, and links each one to the page that teaches it. Use it when a Finance page uses a word you have not met. General SAP terms are in the SAP Glossary. Example values on the Finance pages, such as the profit centers EX-RETAIL and EX-WHOLE, are made up. Terms are listed alphabetically.

== A

[[account-assignment-object]]Account assignment object::
The thing a cost is charged to. Every cost posting in Controlling names one, such as a cost center or an internal order, so the cost can later be reported against it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/controlling-overview-and-cost-elements.adoc[Controlling Overview and Cost Elements].

[[account-determination]]Account determination::
The set of rules that lets the system choose the G/L account for a line it creates by itself, so that nobody has to enter the account by hand each time. The rules read data such as the material, the kind of line and the chart of accounts. See xref:erp/sap/finance/account-determination-and-automatic-postings.adoc[Account Determination and Automatic Postings].

[[account-group]]Account group::
A label on a customer, supplier or G/L account record that decides how the record is created: which number interval it takes and which fields appear on its screens. Records of the same kind therefore look and number the same way. See xref:erp/sap/finance/charts-of-accounts-and-gl-accounts.adoc[Charts of Accounts and G/L Accounts].

[[account-key]]Account key::
A short key attached to a pricing condition type, such as a discount or a freight charge, so that its amount is posted to its own revenue or deduction account instead of the general sales account. See xref:erp/sap/finance/account-determination-and-automatic-postings.adoc[Account Determination and Automatic Postings].

[[abti]]Accounting Business Transaction Interface (ABTI)::
The interface in Accounting that takes business data handed over by other applications, such as a sales or purchasing document, and turns it into journal entries. It is the path that substitution and validation rules for those postings run on. See xref:erp/sap/finance/substitutions-validations-and-badis.adoc[Substitutions, Validations and BAdIs].

[[accounting-document]]Accounting document::
The stored record of one posting. It has a header with data for the whole document and at least two line items that balance, and it keeps its own number so the posting can always be found again. See xref:erp/sap/finance/journal-entries-document-types-and-posting-keys.adoc[Journal Entries, Document Types and Posting Keys].

[[accounting-principle]]Accounting principle::
A set of valuation rules for the books, such as IFRS, US GAAP or a local standard. The same business event can be valued differently under each principle, so each one is kept in its own ledger. See xref:erp/sap/finance/ledgers-and-currencies.adoc[Ledgers, Currencies and Exchange Rates].

[[accounts-payable]]Accounts payable::
The part of accounting that records what the company owes its suppliers. Each supplier invoice creates a payable that stays open until a payment settles it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-payable.adoc[Accounts Payable].

[[accounts-receivable]]Accounts Receivable (FI-AR)::
The part of accounting that records what customers owe the company. Each customer invoice becomes an open item until the customer's payment clears it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-receivable-dunning-and-credit.adoc[Accounts Receivable, Dunning and Credit Management].

[[accrual]]Accrual::
Recording a cost or a revenue in the period it belongs to, even when the invoice or the money arrives later. It keeps each period's result from being distorted by the timing of paperwork. See xref:erp/sap/finance/period-end-closing.adoc[Period-End Closing].

[[apc]]Acquisition and production cost (APC)::
The cost at which an asset is first recorded, including what it took to buy or build it, before any depreciation is taken off. Subtracting the accumulated depreciation gives the net book value. See xref:erp/sap/finance/asset-accounting.adoc[Asset Accounting].

[[activity-type]]Activity type::
A measurable service that a cost center provides, such as machine hours or repair hours. Giving it a price per unit lets the cost of the service be charged to the objects that use it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/allocations.adoc[Allocations: Assessment, Distribution and Activity Allocation].

[[actual-costing]]Actual costing::
An optional calculation at the end of a period that replaces the preliminary standard price of a material with a price based on what the material really cost. It is run by the Material Ledger. See xref:erp/sap/finance/product-costing-and-material-ledger.adoc[Product Costing and the Material Ledger].

[[advanced-financial-closing]]Advanced Financial Closing::
SAP's separately licensed solution for the closing process. It runs task lists for the period-end close and can coordinate tasks across several systems. See xref:erp/sap/finance/period-end-closing.adoc[Period-End Closing].

[[allocation]]Allocation::
The period-end passing of costs, or of activity quantities, from sender objects to receiver objects, for example sharing the cost of a service department among the departments that use it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/allocations.adoc[Allocations: Assessment, Distribution and Activity Allocation].

[[allocation-cycle]]Allocation cycle::
The definition of one allocation run. It has a header with its validity and settings and one or more segments that hold the actual senders, receivers and rules. See xref:erp/sap/finance/allocations.adoc[Allocations: Assessment, Distribution and Activity Allocation].

[[allocation-segment]]Allocation segment::
The part of an allocation cycle that holds the senders, the receivers and the rules for one allocation. A cycle can contain several segments, each passing on a different set of costs. See xref:erp/sap/finance/allocations.adoc[Allocations: Assessment, Distribution and Activity Allocation].

[[app-id]]App ID::
The identifier of an app in the SAP Fiori apps reference library, such as F0718. It identifies the app unambiguously even when its name differs between releases or languages. See xref:erp/sap/finance/transactions-and-fiori-apps.adoc[Finance Transactions and Fiori Apps].

[[application-interface-framework]]Application Interface Framework (AIF)::
An SAP component whose monitor shows the messages that finance services process asynchronously, so you can see which ones arrived, failed or still wait. It helps you follow a message that was accepted but not yet posted. See xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-apis.adoc[Finance APIs].

[[asset]]Asset::
Something the company owns or controls that is expected to bring future benefit, such as cash, stock or equipment. Assets are listed on one side of the balance sheet. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounting-basics.adoc[Accounting Basics].

[[asset-retirement]]Asset retirement::
Removing an asset, or a part of it, from the asset portfolio, for example by selling or scrapping it. The system takes the asset's value off the books and posts any gain or loss. See xref:erp/sap/finance/asset-accounting.adoc[Asset Accounting].

[[assigned-value]]Assigned value::
The amount counted against a budget. It includes commitments, such as open purchase orders, as well as costs already posted, so the budget is used up before the invoices arrive. See xref:erp/sap/finance/planning-and-budgeting.adoc[Planning and Budgeting].

[[attributed-account-assignment]]Attributed account assignment::
A profitability segment that the system works out for a line item from the item's other account assignments when the journal entry is created. It shows up in margin analysis reports only. See xref:erp/sap/finance/margin-analysis.adoc[Margin Analysis (Profitability Analysis)].

[[automatic-payment-program]]Automatic payment program::
The program that pays due invoices in bulk. It selects the open items that are due, proposes the payments, creates the payment documents and prepares the output for the bank. See xref:erp/sap/finance/automatic-payment-program.adoc[Automatic Payment Program].

[[automatic-posting]]Automatic posting::
A line that the system adds to a document by itself, for example for tax, a cash discount or an exchange rate difference. Account determination decides which account it uses. See xref:erp/sap/finance/account-determination-and-automatic-postings.adoc[Account Determination and Automatic Postings].

[[availability-control]]Availability control::
A check that compares the funds already used with the budget of an object and reacts when a limit is reached, either with a warning or by stopping the posting. See xref:erp/sap/finance/planning-and-budgeting.adoc[Planning and Budgeting].

== B

[[balance-sheet]]Balance sheet::
The statement that shows what the company owns (assets), what it owes (liabilities) and what is left for the owners (equity) at one date. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounting-basics.adoc[Accounting Basics].

[[bank-clearing-account]]Bank clearing account::
An interim account that a two-step posting rule uses between the bank account and the customer or supplier account. It collects the bank amounts until the matching open items are cleared. See xref:erp/sap/finance/bank-accounting-and-cash-management.adoc[Bank Accounting and Cash Management].

[[bank-statement]]Bank statement::
The bank's list of the movements and the balance of one account for a period. Matching it against your own postings shows which payments have really gone through. See xref:erp/sap/finance/bank-accounting-and-cash-management.adoc[Bank Accounting and Cash Management].

[[bapi]]BAPI::
A documented function module that other programs call to read or change business data without touching the tables directly. BAPI_ACC_DOCUMENT_POST, which posts an accounting document, is one example. See xref:erp/sap/finance/bapi-acc-document-post.adoc[Posting from ABAP: BAPI_ACC_DOCUMENT_POST].

[[baseline-date]]Baseline date::
The date from which the days of the payment terms are counted. It is often the invoice date or the posting date, and it fixes when the cash discount periods and the due date end. See xref:erp/sap/finance/payment-terms-and-cash-discount.adoc[Payment Terms, Cash Discount and Installment Plans].

[[budget]]Budget::
The approved, binding cost limit for an object such as an internal order or a cost center. Unlike a plan, which is an expectation, a budget can be enforced by availability control. See xref:erp/sap/finance/planning-and-budgeting.adoc[Planning and Budgeting].

[[business-area]]Business area::
An older, optional unit of external accounting for a business segment or area of responsibility. In current releases the segment and the profit center cover this need. See xref:erp/sap/finance/organizational-units.adoc[Organizational Units in Finance].

[[business-context]]Business context::
The circumstances a substitution or validation rule is defined for, such as Journal Entry Item or Coding Block. It decides which fields are available inside the rule. See xref:erp/sap/finance/substitutions-validations-and-badis.adoc[Substitutions, Validations and BAdIs].

[[business-function]]Business function::
A switch in the Switch Framework that turns on a group of features. Some business functions cannot be turned off again once they are active. See xref:erp/sap/finance/universal-parallel-accounting.adoc[Universal Parallel Accounting].

[[business-partner]]Business partner::
The single master record for a customer, a supplier or a contact. One partner can take several roles, so its address and bank data are kept once. See xref:erp/sap/finance/business-partners-customers-and-suppliers.adoc[Business Partners as Customers and Suppliers].

[[bp-grouping]]Business partner grouping::
A classification of business partners that decides which number range the partner's number is taken from. See xref:erp/sap/finance/business-partners-customers-and-suppliers.adoc[Business Partners as Customers and Suppliers].

[[business-partner-role]]Business partner role::
The view of a business partner for one business function, such as customer or supplier in financial accounting. Each role holds the data that function needs. See xref:erp/sap/finance/business-partners-customers-and-suppliers.adoc[Business Partners as Customers and Suppliers].

[[business-transaction]]Business transaction::
A predefined kind of business event, such as a vendor invoice, a payment or a cash receipt. In document splitting it tells the rules what kind of document they see, and in the cash journal it fixes the accounts of a receipt or payment. See xref:erp/sap/finance/document-splitting.adoc[Document Splitting].

== C

[[callup-point]]Callup point::
In the classic tools, the position in the processing of a posting at which a validation or substitution is called, for example at the document header, at a line item or at the complete document. See xref:erp/sap/finance/substitutions-validations-and-badis.adoc[Substitutions, Validations and BAdIs].

[[cardinality]]Cardinality::
How many units of one kind can belong to one unit of another kind, written n : 1 or 1 : n. For example, many company codes can belong to one controlling area. See xref:erp/sap/finance/organizational-units.adoc[Organizational Units in Finance].

[[cash-discount]]Cash discount::
A percentage taken off an invoice when it is paid within an agreed period. It rewards quick payment and is defined in the payment terms. See xref:erp/sap/finance/payment-terms-and-cash-discount.adoc[Payment Terms, Cash Discount and Installment Plans].

[[cash-journal]]Cash journal::
A Bank Accounting subledger for the cash a business keeps on hand. It records each receipt and payment as a cash journal entry and keeps its own balances. See xref:erp/sap/finance/cash-journal.adoc[Cash Journal].

[[cash-position]]Cash position::
An overview of how much money the company has, shown by bank, company, country and currency. It is built from the bank balances and is used to decide where cash is available. See xref:erp/sap/finance/bank-accounting-and-cash-management.adoc[Bank Accounting and Cash Management].

[[characteristic]]Characteristic::
A level at which you analyze profitability, such as sales organization, region, product or customer. A combination of characteristic values forms a profitability segment. See xref:erp/sap/finance/margin-analysis.adoc[Margin Analysis (Profitability Analysis)].

[[chart-of-accounts]]Chart of accounts::
The list of G/L accounts that one or more company codes use. Each account has a number and a name, and sharing one chart makes the books of the company codes comparable. See xref:erp/sap/finance/charts-of-accounts-and-gl-accounts.adoc[Charts of Accounts and G/L Accounts].

[[chart-of-depreciation]]Chart of depreciation::
A country-specific directory of depreciation areas and depreciation keys, assigned to company codes. It decides how assets are depreciated in those company codes. See xref:erp/sap/finance/asset-accounting.adoc[Asset Accounting].

[[check-indicator]]Check indicator::
A setting on a tax code that decides whether the system gives a warning or an error when a manually entered tax amount differs from the calculated one. See xref:erp/sap/finance/tax.adoc[Tax Codes and Tax Calculation].

[[clean-core]]Clean core::
The rule of extending SAP only through released interfaces and extension points, never by changing standard code or tables. It keeps the system easy to upgrade. See xref:erp/sap/finance/bapi-acc-document-post.adoc[Posting from ABAP: BAPI_ACC_DOCUMENT_POST].

[[clearing]]Clearing::
Marking open items that add up to zero as settled, for example an invoice and the payment that pays it. Cleared items no longer count as open. See xref:erp/sap/finance/open-items-and-clearing.adoc[Open Item Management and Clearing].

[[clearing-document]]Clearing document::
The document the system creates when items are cleared. Its number is entered in every cleared item so you can see which items were settled together. See xref:erp/sap/finance/open-items-and-clearing.adoc[Open Item Management and Clearing].

[[co-pa-derivation]]CO-PA derivation::
The tool that fills further characteristics of a profitability segment from the data that is already available, for example finding the product group from the product. See xref:erp/sap/finance/margin-analysis.adoc[Margin Analysis (Profitability Analysis)].

[[coding-block]]Coding block::
The account assignment fields of a line, such as cost center, project and internal order. They say which objects the amount is charged to. See xref:erp/sap/finance/universal-journal.adoc[The Universal Journal (ACDOCA)].

[[commitment]]Commitment::
A purchase requisition or purchase order that will cause a cost later but has not been posted as a cost yet. Counting commitments lets a budget reflect spending that is already decided. See xref:erp/sap/finance/planning-and-budgeting.adoc[Planning and Budgeting].

[[communication-arrangement]]Communication arrangement::
The Public Edition setting that switches a communication scenario on for one communication system and one user. Without an arrangement, the scenario's services cannot be called. See xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-apis.adoc[Finance APIs].

[[communication-scenario]]Communication scenario::
SAP's design-time description of which inbound and outbound services two partners use and how they authenticate. It is the template from which a communication arrangement is created. See xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-apis.adoc[Finance APIs].

[[company-code]]Company code::
The smallest organizational unit for which a complete, independent set of accounts can be kept. It is the legal unit that produces the annual accounts. See xref:erp/sap/finance/organizational-units.adoc[Organizational Units in Finance].

[[company-code-data]]Company code data::
The part of a customer or supplier record that is kept separately for each company code, such as the reconciliation account and the payment terms. See xref:erp/sap/finance/business-partners-customers-and-suppliers.adoc[Business Partners as Customers and Suppliers].

[[condition-technique]]Condition technique::
The search method that tries condition tables from the most specific to the most general until it finds a matching record. It is used wherever the system must pick a value depending on several conditions, such as an account for a pricing condition. See xref:erp/sap/finance/account-determination-and-automatic-postings.adoc[Account Determination and Automatic Postings].

[[condition-type]]Condition type::
One line in a calculation procedure. In tax calculation it is a tax type with its base amount and account key. See xref:erp/sap/finance/tax.adoc[Tax Codes and Tax Calculation].

[[consolidation-unit]]Consolidation unit::
The entity whose reported data is consolidated in Group Reporting. In the usual case it is one company. See xref:erp/sap/finance/group-reporting.adoc[Group Reporting and Consolidation].

[[contra-node]]Contra node::
A node of a financial statement version paired with another node. It holds the same accounts with the opposite debit or credit setting, so that each account shows on the side that matches its balance. See xref:erp/sap/finance/financial-statement-versions.adoc[Financial Statement Versions].

[[contribution-margin]]Contribution margin::
What remains of revenue after subtracting the costs you choose at one level of the margin structure. It shows how much a product or customer contributes toward covering the other costs. See xref:erp/sap/finance/margin-analysis.adoc[Margin Analysis (Profitability Analysis)].

[[control-level]]Control level::
The strictness of a validation rule: Error stops the user, Warning lets the user continue. See xref:erp/sap/finance/substitutions-validations-and-badis.adoc[Substitutions, Validations and BAdIs].

[[controlling]]Controlling::
Controlling (CO)::
The application area for management accounting. It shows where costs and revenues arise inside the company and why, to support planning and decisions. See xref:erp/sap/finance/controlling-overview-and-cost-elements.adoc[Controlling Overview and Cost Elements].

[[controlling-area]]Controlling area::
The organizational unit within which a complete cost accounting is done in a closed system. Cost centers, orders and other Controlling objects belong to one controlling area. See xref:erp/sap/finance/controlling-overview-and-cost-elements.adoc[Controlling Overview and Cost Elements].

[[controlling-area-currency]]Controlling area currency::
The currency in which cost accounting is done. It is also the default currency of the Controlling objects of the controlling area. See xref:erp/sap/finance/controlling-overview-and-cost-elements.adoc[Controlling Overview and Cost Elements].

[[cost-center]]Cost center::
A clearly delimited place in the organization where costs occur, such as a department. It belongs to one controlling area and is where costs are collected and monitored. See xref:erp/sap/finance/cost-centers-and-internal-orders.adoc[Cost Centers and Internal Orders].

[[cost-center-group]]Cost center group::
A collection of cost centers that reflects part of the structure of the organization. Groups make it easy to report on or process several cost centers at once. See xref:erp/sap/finance/cost-centers-and-internal-orders.adoc[Cost Centers and Internal Orders].

[[cost-component-split]]Cost component split::
The breakdown of a cost estimate into groups such as raw materials, production and overhead. It shows what a product's cost is made of. See xref:erp/sap/finance/product-costing-and-material-ledger.adoc[Product Costing and the Material Ledger].

[[cost-element]]Cost element::
A classification of a cost or revenue for management accounting. In SAP S/4HANA a cost element is a type of G/L account. See xref:erp/sap/finance/controlling-overview-and-cost-elements.adoc[Controlling Overview and Cost Elements].

[[cost-element-category]]Cost element category::
The setting on a cost element that decides which business transactions may use it, for example whether it can be used for postings from financial accounting or for allocations. See xref:erp/sap/finance/controlling-overview-and-cost-elements.adoc[Controlling Overview and Cost Elements].

[[cost-element-group]]Cost element group::
A hierarchy of cost elements used to build report rows and to process several cost elements in one step. See xref:erp/sap/finance/controlling-overview-and-cost-elements.adoc[Controlling Overview and Cost Elements].

[[cost-estimate]]Cost estimate::
A calculation of what it costs to make a product, built from the quantities of materials and activities and their prices. See xref:erp/sap/finance/product-costing-and-material-ledger.adoc[Product Costing and the Material Ledger].

[[costing-based-profitability-analysis]]Costing-based profitability analysis::
The older form of profitability analysis that stores values in value fields and needs reconciliation with Financial Accounting. Margin analysis is the account-based form that replaced it in SAP S/4HANA. See xref:erp/sap/finance/margin-analysis.adoc[Margin Analysis (Profitability Analysis)].

[[country-chart-of-accounts]]Country chart of accounts::
An optional chart of accounts that holds the accounts a country's legal reporting needs. The Public Edition calls it the alternative or local chart of accounts. See xref:erp/sap/finance/charts-of-accounts-and-gl-accounts.adoc[Charts of Accounts and G/L Accounts].

[[credit]]Credit::
The right side of an account in double-entry bookkeeping. A credit increases liabilities, equity and revenue and decreases assets and expenses. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounting-basics.adoc[Accounting Basics].

[[credit-exposure]]Credit exposure::
The amount of a customer's credit limit that has already been used up, for example by open invoices and orders not yet invoiced. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-receivable-dunning-and-credit.adoc[Accounts Receivable, Dunning and Credit Management].

[[credit-limit]]Credit limit::
The amount a customer may owe before the credit checks react. It is the ceiling against which the credit exposure is compared. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-receivable-dunning-and-credit.adoc[Accounts Receivable, Dunning and Credit Management].

[[credit-management]]Credit management::
Monitoring how much each customer owes against an agreed limit, so that sales to a customer can be warned about or stopped when the limit is exceeded. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-receivable-dunning-and-credit.adoc[Accounts Receivable, Dunning and Credit Management].

[[currency-type]]Currency type::
The purpose of a currency in a ledger, such as company code currency or group currency, together with its translation settings. See xref:erp/sap/finance/ledgers-and-currencies.adoc[Ledgers, Currencies and Exchange Rates].

[[customer-invoice]]Customer invoice::
The document that makes a customer owe money. It creates an open item on the customer account that stays open until the customer pays. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-receivable-dunning-and-credit.adoc[Accounts Receivable, Dunning and Credit Management].

[[customer-supplier-integration]]Customer/Supplier Integration (CVI)::
The synchronization that keeps the business partner record in step with the customer or supplier record. It lets older customer and supplier data live together with the business partner. See xref:erp/sap/finance/business-partners-customers-and-suppliers.adoc[Business Partners as Customers and Suppliers].

== D

[[data-monitor]]Data monitor::
The app in which the tasks that bring and check unit data are run for each consolidation unit in Group Reporting. See xref:erp/sap/finance/group-reporting.adoc[Group Reporting and Consolidation].

[[days-in-arrears]]Days in arrears::
How many days an item is past its due date. The dunning run uses it, after any grace days, to decide whether to remind the customer. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-receivable-dunning-and-credit.adoc[Accounts Receivable, Dunning and Credit Management].

[[debit]]Debit::
The left side of an account in double-entry bookkeeping. A debit increases assets and expenses and decreases liabilities, equity and revenue. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounting-basics.adoc[Accounting Basics].

[[default-profit-center]]Default profit center::
A normal profit center used to collect postings until they are moved to the right profit centers. It is also called a clearing profit center. See xref:erp/sap/finance/profit-center-accounting.adoc[Profit Center Accounting].

[[depreciation-area]]Depreciation area::
A set of values and rules kept in parallel for each asset, for example one for the balance sheet and one for taxes. Each area depreciates the same asset under its own rules. See xref:erp/sap/finance/asset-accounting.adoc[Asset Accounting].

[[depreciation-key]]Depreciation key::
The combination of calculation rules, such as the method and the useful life, that the system uses to compute depreciation automatically. See xref:erp/sap/finance/asset-accounting.adoc[Asset Accounting].

[[direct-quotation]]Direct quotation::
An exchange rate written as units of the To currency for one unit of the From currency. For example, 0.85 GBP for 1 EUR is a direct quotation of EUR to GBP. See xref:erp/sap/finance/ledgers-and-currencies.adoc[Ledgers, Currencies and Exchange Rates].

[[discount-base]]Discount base::
The amount that the cash discount percentage is applied to, for example the invoice amount with or without tax. See xref:erp/sap/finance/payment-terms-and-cash-discount.adoc[Payment Terms, Cash Discount and Installment Plans].

[[distribution-rule]]Distribution rule::
The part of a settlement rule that names one receiver, the share it receives and the period the rule applies to. See xref:erp/sap/finance/cost-centers-and-internal-orders.adoc[Cost Centers and Internal Orders].

[[document-header]]Document header::
The part of a document with the data that holds for the whole document, such as the dates and the document type. See xref:erp/sap/finance/journal-entries-document-types-and-posting-keys.adoc[Journal Entries, Document Types and Posting Keys].

[[document-number]]Document number::
The number that identifies one accounting document within a company code and a fiscal year. It is taken from the number range of the document type. See xref:erp/sap/finance/number-ranges-field-status-and-tolerance-groups.adoc[Number Ranges, Field Status Variants and Tolerance Groups].

[[document-principle]]Document principle::
The rule that every posting leaves a numbered document behind instead of changing a balance directly. It lets any balance be traced back to the documents behind it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/journal-entries-document-types-and-posting-keys.adoc[Journal Entries, Document Types and Posting Keys].

[[document-splitting]]Document splitting::
The function that divides the lines of a document by chosen dimensions, such as profit center or segment, when it is posted. It adds clearing lines where a dimension would otherwise not balance. See xref:erp/sap/finance/document-splitting.adoc[Document Splitting].

[[document-splitting-characteristic]]Document splitting characteristic::
A dimension, such as profit center or segment, for which documents are split so that each value of it balances on its own. See xref:erp/sap/finance/document-splitting.adoc[Document Splitting].

[[document-splitting-rule]]Document splitting rule::
The setting that says, for one business transaction, which item categories are split and from which lines the account assignments are taken. See xref:erp/sap/finance/document-splitting.adoc[Document Splitting].

[[document-type]]Document type::
A two-character key in the document header that classifies the document, limits the account types it may post to and selects its number range. See xref:erp/sap/finance/journal-entries-document-types-and-posting-keys.adoc[Journal Entries, Document Types and Posting Keys].

[[double-entry-bookkeeping]]Double-entry bookkeeping::
Recording every business event in at least two accounts, with equal debits and credits, so that the books always balance. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounting-basics.adoc[Accounting Basics].

[[down-payment]]Down payment::
Money paid or received before the goods or services are delivered. It is shown separately from normal open items until the final invoice settles it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/special-gl-transactions.adoc[Special G/L Transactions].

[[down-payment-request]]Down payment request::
A reminder document that asks for a down payment, or triggers its automatic payment. It does not change any balance by itself. See xref:erp/sap/finance/special-gl-transactions.adoc[Special G/L Transactions].

[[dummy-profit-center]]Dummy profit center::
A default value of a controlling area that receives postings to objects without a profit center, in the classic design. See xref:erp/sap/finance/profit-center-accounting.adoc[Profit Center Accounting].

[[dunning]]Dunning::
The automatic reminder process for overdue open items. It groups the overdue items of a customer and sends a reminder whose tone depends on how far behind the payment is. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-receivable-dunning-and-credit.adoc[Accounts Receivable, Dunning and Credit Management].

[[dunning-block]]Dunning block::
A key on an account or an item that keeps it out of the dunning run, for example while a dispute is being sorted out. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-receivable-dunning-and-credit.adoc[Accounts Receivable, Dunning and Credit Management].

[[dunning-level]]Dunning level::
The step of the dunning procedure that an item has reached. It decides how firm the reminder text is. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-receivable-dunning-and-credit.adoc[Accounts Receivable, Dunning and Credit Management].

[[dunning-procedure]]Dunning procedure::
The setting that says how a business partner is dunned: how often, after how many days and how many times. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-receivable-dunning-and-credit.adoc[Accounts Receivable, Dunning and Credit Management].

== E

[[electronic-bank-statement]]Electronic bank statement::
A bank statement that enters the system digitally, as a file or through a bank connection, and cannot be edited. The system posts its items by rules instead of by hand. See xref:erp/sap/finance/bank-accounting-and-cash-management.adoc[Bank Accounting and Cash Management].

[[embedded-analytics]]Embedded analytics::
Reporting tools that run inside SAP S/4HANA on its own data, without copying the data to another system. See xref:erp/sap/finance/financial-reporting-and-analytics.adoc[Financial Reporting and Embedded Analytics].

[[entry-view]]Entry view::
The document as it was typed or handed over by the source application, before document splitting. See xref:erp/sap/finance/document-splitting.adoc[Document Splitting].

[[equity]]Equity::
What is left for the owners after the liabilities are subtracted from the assets. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounting-basics.adoc[Accounting Basics].

[[event]]Event::
The exact moment and place in a business process where a substitution or validation rule runs, for example when a journal entry item is checked. See xref:erp/sap/finance/substitutions-validations-and-badis.adoc[Substitutions, Validations and BAdIs].

[[exception-list]]Exception list::
The list of open items that the automatic payment program did not propose, with the reason for each one. See xref:erp/sap/finance/automatic-payment-program.adoc[Automatic Payment Program].

[[exchange-rate-difference]]Exchange rate difference::
The gain or loss that arises when an amount in a foreign currency is booked or settled at a different rate than before. It is posted automatically to its own account. See xref:erp/sap/finance/open-items-and-clearing.adoc[Open Item Management and Clearing].

[[exchange-rate-type]]Exchange rate type::
A key that separates the rates of the same currency pair by purpose, for example one for average rates and another for bank rates. See xref:erp/sap/finance/ledgers-and-currencies.adoc[Ledgers, Currencies and Exchange Rates].

[[expense]]Expense::
The cost used up to earn the revenue of a period. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounting-basics.adoc[Accounting Basics].

[[extension-ledger]]Extension ledger::
A ledger that reads through to an underlying ledger and stores only its own postings or adjustments. It avoids copying all the data of the underlying ledger. See xref:erp/sap/finance/ledgers-and-currencies.adoc[Ledgers, Currencies and Exchange Rates].

[[external-number-assignment]]External number assignment::
A way of numbering documents in which the user, or a feeder system, supplies the number, which must be unique within its number range. See xref:erp/sap/finance/number-ranges-field-status-and-tolerance-groups.adoc[Number Ranges, Field Status Variants and Tolerance Groups].

== F

[[feature-comparison]]Feature comparison::
A table in the SAP help that lists transactions and apps side by side, with what each of them can do, so you can see what replaces an old transaction. See xref:erp/sap/finance/transactions-and-fiori-apps.adoc[Finance Transactions and Fiori Apps].

[[field-status-group]]Field status group::
A named set of field settings that decides, for each field of a posting screen, whether it is hidden, required or optional. A G/L account points to one. See xref:erp/sap/finance/number-ranges-field-status-and-tolerance-groups.adoc[Number Ranges, Field Status Variants and Tolerance Groups].

[[field-status-variant]]Field status variant::
A container for field status groups, assigned to one or more company codes. The G/L accounts of those company codes pick their field status group from it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/number-ranges-field-status-and-tolerance-groups.adoc[Number Ranges, Field Status Variants and Tolerance Groups].

[[finance-api]]Finance API::
A published, supported interface of SAP S/4HANA Finance, either an OData service or a SOAP service, that outside programs use to read or post finance data. See xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-apis.adoc[Finance APIs].

[[financial-accounting]]Financial Accounting (FI)::
The application area that keeps the legal books of each company code and reports to the outside world. See xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-overview.adoc[Finance Overview].

[[financial-closing-cockpit]]Financial Closing cockpit::
The task-list tool delivered with SAP S/4HANA that plans, runs and monitors the tasks of a period-end close. See xref:erp/sap/finance/period-end-closing.adoc[Period-End Closing].

[[financial-statement-version]]Financial statement version::
Financial statement version (FSV)::
A hierarchy that groups G/L accounts into the lines of a balance sheet and a profit and loss statement. It lets the same accounts be reported in a layout you choose. See xref:erp/sap/finance/financial-statement-versions.adoc[Financial Statement Versions].

[[fiscal-year]]Fiscal year::
The period, usually 12 months, for which a company produces its balance sheet and statements. It need not match the calendar year. See xref:erp/sap/finance/fiscal-year-and-posting-periods.adoc[Fiscal Year Variants and Posting Periods].

[[fiscal-year-variant]]Fiscal year variant::
The setting that defines how many posting periods and special periods a fiscal year has and how a posting date maps to a period. See xref:erp/sap/finance/fiscal-year-and-posting-periods.adoc[Fiscal Year Variants and Posting Periods].

[[fit-to-standard]]Fit-to-standard::
A workshop approach in which the business sees the standard process first and states only what does not fit. It replaces writing every requirement in advance. See xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-implementation-migration-and-testing.adoc[Finance Implementation, Migration and Testing].

[[fixed-asset]]Fixed asset::
A long-lived item the company owns and uses, such as a machine or a building. It is recorded in Asset Accounting with its own master record. See xref:erp/sap/finance/asset-accounting.adoc[Asset Accounting].

[[foreign-currency-valuation]]Foreign currency valuation::
Revaluing foreign currency balances and open items at the exchange rate of the key date at period end, so the books show their current value. See xref:erp/sap/finance/period-end-closing.adoc[Period-End Closing].

[[fs-item]]FS item::
A financial statement item: the account of the consolidation chart of accounts to which G/L accounts are mapped in Group Reporting. See xref:erp/sap/finance/group-reporting.adoc[Group Reporting and Consolidation].

[[fsv-node]]FSV node::
One element of a financial statement version, also called a financial statement item, that holds a group of G/L accounts. See xref:erp/sap/finance/financial-statement-versions.adoc[Financial Statement Versions].

== G

[[gl-account]]G/L account::
One account of the general ledger, kept as a master record, such as a bank or a revenue account. Postings add up on it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/charts-of-accounts-and-gl-accounts.adoc[Charts of Accounts and G/L Accounts].

[[gl-account-type]]G/L account type::
The classification of a G/L account as balance sheet, nonoperating expense or income, primary costs or revenue, or secondary costs or cash. It decides how the account is used. See xref:erp/sap/finance/charts-of-accounts-and-gl-accounts.adoc[Charts of Accounts and G/L Accounts].

[[general-ledger]]General ledger::
General ledger (FI-GL)::
The central record of all accounts of a company code. The totals of the subledgers, such as accounts payable, feed it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-overview.adoc[Finance Overview].

[[general-ledger-view]]General ledger view::
A document can look different in each ledger, because split lines and clearing lines are added. The general ledger view is the document as one ledger stores it, including those extra lines. See xref:erp/sap/finance/document-splitting.adoc[Document Splitting].

[[global-hierarchy]]Global hierarchy::
A hierarchy is a tree that groups items for reporting. A global hierarchy is one that is maintained in the app Manage Global Hierarchies and shared by many reports; the financial statement version is one type of such hierarchy. See xref:erp/sap/finance/financial-statement-versions.adoc[Financial Statement Versions].

[[goods-receipt]]Goods receipt::
The posting that records that ordered goods have arrived at the company. It is the first step of a purchase and is later matched with the supplier invoice. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-payable.adoc[Accounts Payable].

[[gr-ir-clearing-account]]GR/IR clearing account::
A holding account between the arrival of goods and the invoice for them. It carries the value of goods received but not yet invoiced, or invoiced but not yet received, and it should return to zero once both have been posted. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-payable.adoc[Accounts Payable].

[[group-chart-of-accounts]]Group chart of accounts::
A chart of accounts whose accounts are shared by the whole corporate group. Because every company can map its own accounts to it, the results of all companies can be reported together. See xref:erp/sap/finance/charts-of-accounts-and-gl-accounts.adoc[Charts of Accounts and G/L Accounts].

[[group-reporting]]Group Reporting::
The SAP S/4HANA application for financial consolidation, which combines the figures of several companies of a group into the results of the group as a whole. See xref:erp/sap/finance/group-reporting.adoc[Group Reporting and Consolidation].

[[group-reporting-preparation-ledger]]Group reporting preparation ledger::
An accounting ledger that you configure so that the data needed for group reporting is kept in General Ledger Accounting, ready to be passed to the consolidation. See xref:erp/sap/finance/group-reporting.adoc[Group Reporting and Consolidation].

[[group-valuation]]Group valuation::
Valuing assets and materials from the point of view of the whole group, where profits made between group companies are eliminated. With Universal Parallel Accounting it has its own ledger. See xref:erp/sap/finance/universal-parallel-accounting.adoc[Universal Parallel Accounting].

[[guarantee]]Guarantee::
A promise to stand in for someone else's debt, either made by the company to a customer or received from a supplier. It is recorded as a special posting that does not change the normal balances. See xref:erp/sap/finance/special-gl-transactions.adoc[Special G/L Transactions].

== H

[[held-document]]Held document::
A document you save temporarily while you are still entering it. It has no document number and changes no account balance until you complete and post it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/journal-entries-document-types-and-posting-keys.adoc[Journal Entries, Document Types and Posting Keys].

[[house-bank]]House bank::
A bank at which your company code keeps an account. It is set up once and identified by a bank ID, so that the system knows where payments are made from. See xref:erp/sap/finance/bank-accounting-and-cash-management.adoc[Bank Accounting and Cash Management].

[[house-bank-account]]House bank account::
The representation of one real bank account of the company in Financial Accounting. It is identified by an account ID under its house bank and is linked to a G/L account. See xref:erp/sap/finance/bank-accounting-and-cash-management.adoc[Bank Accounting and Cash Management].

== I

[[incoming-payment]]Incoming payment::
Money received from a customer. It is posted to the bank and used to clear the open invoices that it pays. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-receivable-dunning-and-credit.adoc[Accounts Receivable, Dunning and Credit Management].

[[indirect-quotation]]Indirect quotation::
A way of writing an exchange rate. Instead of saying how much of the second currency one unit of the first currency buys, it states how many units of the first currency make one unit of the second. See xref:erp/sap/finance/ledgers-and-currencies.adoc[Ledgers, Currencies and Exchange Rates].

[[input-tax]]Input tax::
Tax that a company pays on its purchases and expenses. It can normally claim this tax back from the tax office. See xref:erp/sap/finance/tax.adoc[Tax Codes and Tax Calculation].

[[installment-payment]]Installment payment::
An invoice that is paid in several parts instead of one amount. Each part has its own payment terms and due date. See xref:erp/sap/finance/payment-terms-and-cash-discount.adoc[Payment Terms, Cash Discount and Installment Plans].

[[integrated-planning]]Integrated planning::
A way of planning in which the plan values are also written as plan line items and passed on, so that other applications can use them. See xref:erp/sap/finance/planning-and-budgeting.adoc[Planning and Budgeting].

[[internal-number-assignment]]Internal number assignment::
A way of numbering documents in which the system picks the next free number of the number range by itself, so that nobody has to supply or check it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/number-ranges-field-status-and-tolerance-groups.adoc[Number Ranges, Field Status Variants and Tolerance Groups].

[[internal-order]]Internal order::
A Controlling object for a specific job or task, such as a trade fair or a repair. It is used to plan, collect and finally settle the costs of that task. See xref:erp/sap/finance/cost-centers-and-internal-orders.adoc[Cost Centers and Internal Orders].

[[item-category]]Item category::
A predefined class, such as vendor, expense or tax, to which every account is assigned. The splitting rules use it to know what kind of line they are looking at. See xref:erp/sap/finance/document-splitting.adoc[Document Splitting].

== J

[[journal-entry]]Journal entry::
One posting that records one business event. It has a header and at least two lines, and the debits equal the credits. In SAP S/4HANA this is the name for what used to be called an accounting document. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounting-basics.adoc[Accounting Basics].

== K

[[key-date]]Key date::
The date on which a closing run evaluates the data. It is usually the last day of the period being closed. See xref:erp/sap/finance/period-end-closing.adoc[Period-End Closing].

== L

[[leading-ledger]]Leading ledger::
The main ledger, which is updated in every company code and feeds Controlling. In the standard system it is the ledger with the key `0L`. See xref:erp/sap/finance/ledgers-and-currencies.adoc[Ledgers, Currencies and Exchange Rates].

[[ledger]]Ledger::
A complete set of books in General Ledger Accounting, kept for one accounting principle, for example the rules of one country. See xref:erp/sap/finance/ledgers-and-currencies.adoc[Ledgers, Currencies and Exchange Rates].

[[ledger-group]]Ledger group::
A named combination of ledgers. A posting or a closing step can address all ledgers of the group together instead of one at a time. See xref:erp/sap/finance/ledgers-and-currencies.adoc[Ledgers, Currencies and Exchange Rates].

[[ledger-specific-valuation]]Ledger-specific valuation::
Calculating a value, such as depreciation or a material price, separately for each ledger, so that every ledger follows its own accounting principle. See xref:erp/sap/finance/universal-parallel-accounting.adoc[Universal Parallel Accounting].

[[liability]]Liability::
Something the company owes to others, such as a loan or an unpaid supplier invoice. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounting-basics.adoc[Accounting Basics].

[[line-item]]Line item::
One line of a journal entry, with one account, one amount and a debit or credit side. A journal entry is made of at least two line items that balance. See xref:erp/sap/finance/journal-entries-document-types-and-posting-keys.adoc[Journal Entries, Document Types and Posting Keys].

[[line-item-display]]Line item display::
A list of the individual postings on an account, as opposed to only its balance. It lets you see which items are open and which are cleared. See xref:erp/sap/finance/open-items-and-clearing.adoc[Open Item Management and Clearing].

[[liquidity-forecast]]Liquidity forecast::
A forecast of the cash that is expected to come in and go out in the coming days, together with the expected closing balance of the bank accounts. See xref:erp/sap/finance/bank-accounting-and-cash-management.adoc[Bank Accounting and Cash Management].

== M

[[margin-analysis]]Margin analysis::
The form of Profitability Analysis that stores costs and revenues in accounts, so that it is permanently reconciled with Financial Accounting. It shows how profitable products, customers or markets are. See xref:erp/sap/finance/margin-analysis.adoc[Margin Analysis (Profitability Analysis)].

[[material-ledger]]Material Ledger::
Material ledger::
The component that values material stock and can keep those values in several currencies and valuations. In SAP S/4HANA it is also called Material Subledger. See xref:erp/sap/finance/product-costing-and-material-ledger.adoc[Product Costing and the Material Ledger].

[[migration-cockpit]]Migration cockpit::
The SAP Fiori app Migrate Your Data - Migration Cockpit, which loads business data from another system into SAP S/4HANA following predefined migration objects. See xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-implementation-migration-and-testing.adoc[Finance Implementation, Migration and Testing].

[[migration-key-date]]Migration key date::
The date, set per company code, up to which the loaded balances and open items are valid. Postings after it are made in the new system. See xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-implementation-migration-and-testing.adoc[Finance Implementation, Migration and Testing].

[[migration-object]]Migration object::
The description of how the data of one kind of business object, such as G/L account balances, is moved into the new system. See xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-implementation-migration-and-testing.adoc[Finance Implementation, Migration and Testing].

[[movement-type]]Movement type::
The three-digit key that says what kind of goods movement is posted, for example 261 for a material issue to a production order. It helps decide which accounts are posted automatically. See xref:erp/sap/finance/account-determination-and-automatic-postings.adoc[Account Determination and Automatic Postings].

== N

[[negative-posting]]Negative posting::
A posting entered with an amount whose sign is opposite to the usual sign of its debit or credit side. It reduces the balance instead of increasing it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-apis.adoc[Finance APIs].

[[net-book-value]]Net book value (NBV)::
The value an asset still has in the books: its acquisition and production cost (APC) minus the depreciation accumulated so far. See xref:erp/sap/finance/asset-accounting.adoc[Asset Accounting].

[[non-deductible-input-tax]]Non-deductible input tax::
The part of an input tax that the company cannot claim back from the tax office. It therefore becomes a cost. See xref:erp/sap/finance/tax.adoc[Tax Codes and Tax Calculation].

[[non-leading-ledger]]Non-leading ledger::
A parallel ledger that you activate for the company codes that need to follow another accounting principle in addition to the leading ledger. See xref:erp/sap/finance/ledgers-and-currencies.adoc[Ledgers, Currencies and Exchange Rates].

[[noted-item]]Noted item::
An entry that is kept only as a reminder, for example for a down payment that is still requested. It does not update any account balance. See xref:erp/sap/finance/special-gl-transactions.adoc[Special G/L Transactions].

[[number-range]]Number range::
A named series of numbers, with its limits, from which the documents of one or more document types take their numbers. See xref:erp/sap/finance/number-ranges-field-status-and-tolerance-groups.adoc[Number Ranges, Field Status Variants and Tolerance Groups].

[[number-range-interval]]Number range interval::
The from and to numbers, and the fiscal year they apply to, that make up a number range for one company code. See xref:erp/sap/finance/number-ranges-field-status-and-tolerance-groups.adoc[Number Ranges, Field Status Variants and Tolerance Groups].

== O

[[object-currency]]Object currency::
The currency kept in the master data of one Controlling object, such as a cost center. See xref:erp/sap/finance/controlling-overview-and-cost-elements.adoc[Controlling Overview and Cost Elements].

[[open-item]]Open item::
A line on an account that still has to be cleared by another line, for example an invoice that has not been paid yet. See xref:erp/sap/finance/open-items-and-clearing.adoc[Open Item Management and Clearing].

[[open-item-management]]Open item management::
A setting that makes an account hold items that must be cleared. The balance of the account is then always the total of its open items. See xref:erp/sap/finance/open-items-and-clearing.adoc[Open Item Management and Clearing].

[[operating-chart-of-accounts]]Operating chart of accounts::
The chart of accounts used for the daily postings in a company code and in the controlling area. Every company code has exactly one. See xref:erp/sap/finance/charts-of-accounts-and-gl-accounts.adoc[Charts of Accounts and G/L Accounts].

[[operating-concern]]Operating concern::
The organizational unit for which the sales market has a uniform structure. It defines the characteristics, such as product or customer, that profitability analysis can use. See xref:erp/sap/finance/margin-analysis.adoc[Margin Analysis (Profitability Analysis)].

[[order-status]]Order status::
The processing state of an order, such as created or released. It decides which business transactions are allowed on the order. See xref:erp/sap/finance/cost-centers-and-internal-orders.adoc[Cost Centers and Internal Orders].

[[outgoing-payment]]Outgoing payment::
The posting that pays a supplier. It clears the open invoices that it settles. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-payable.adoc[Accounts Payable].

[[output-tax]]Output tax::
Tax that a company charges on its sales. The company passes it on to the tax office. See xref:erp/sap/finance/tax.adoc[Tax Codes and Tax Calculation].

== P

[[parallel-accounting]]Parallel accounting::
Keeping the books under more than one accounting principle at the same time, for example for the group and for local law. See xref:erp/sap/finance/ledgers-and-currencies.adoc[Ledgers, Currencies and Exchange Rates].

[[parked-document]]Parked document::
A saved but incomplete document that already has a document number. It changes no balance until it is completed and posted. See xref:erp/sap/finance/journal-entries-document-types-and-posting-keys.adoc[Journal Entries, Document Types and Posting Keys].

[[payment-block]]Payment block::
A mark on an invoice or on a supplier account that keeps the payment program from paying it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-payable.adoc[Accounts Payable].

[[payment-medium]]Payment medium::
The output of a payment run that goes to the bank or the payee, such as a payment file, a check or a payment advice. See xref:erp/sap/finance/automatic-payment-program.adoc[Automatic Payment Program].

[[payment-method]]Payment method::
How a payment is made, for example by check, bank transfer or bank direct debit. See xref:erp/sap/finance/automatic-payment-program.adoc[Automatic Payment Program].

[[payment-proposal]]Payment proposal::
The list of payments that the payment program would make. You can check and change it before anything is posted. See xref:erp/sap/finance/automatic-payment-program.adoc[Automatic Payment Program].

[[payment-run]]Payment run::
One execution of the automatic payment program, identified by a run date and an identification. See xref:erp/sap/finance/automatic-payment-program.adoc[Automatic Payment Program].

[[period-end-closing]]Period-end closing::
The ordered set of periodic activities that correct and complete the books of a period before the period is closed to further postings. See xref:erp/sap/finance/period-end-closing.adoc[Period-End Closing].

[[periodic-unit-price]]Periodic unit price::
The actual price that actual costing calculates for a material for one closed period, based on what the material really cost in that period. See xref:erp/sap/finance/product-costing-and-material-ledger.adoc[Product Costing and the Material Ledger].

[[plan-version]]Plan version::
A separate copy of plan data, so that alternative plans can be kept side by side and compared. See xref:erp/sap/finance/planning-and-budgeting.adoc[Planning and Budgeting].

[[plan-actual-variance]]Plan/actual variance::
The difference between the actual costs and the planned costs of an object such as a cost center. See xref:erp/sap/finance/cost-centers-and-internal-orders.adoc[Cost Centers and Internal Orders].

[[planning]]Planning::
Entering the expected costs, activities, prices or statistical key figures for a period, so that actual values can be compared with them later. See xref:erp/sap/finance/planning-and-budgeting.adoc[Planning and Budgeting].

[[posting-key]]Posting key::
A two-digit key on each line of a document. It fixes the account type, the debit or credit side and the layout of the entry screen. See xref:erp/sap/finance/journal-entries-document-types-and-posting-keys.adoc[Journal Entries, Document Types and Posting Keys].

[[posting-period]]Posting period::
One slice of the fiscal year, usually a month, with a start date and an end date. A posting is made into the period of its posting date, and only open periods accept postings. See xref:erp/sap/finance/fiscal-year-and-posting-periods.adoc[Fiscal Year Variants and Posting Periods].

[[posting-period-variant]]Posting period variant::
A named set of rules that says which posting periods are open. One or more company codes can share it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/fiscal-year-and-posting-periods.adoc[Fiscal Year Variants and Posting Periods].

[[posting-rule]]Posting rule::
The older rule for processing a bank statement. It says exactly which accounts an item posts to and whether it clears open items. See xref:erp/sap/finance/bank-accounting-and-cash-management.adoc[Bank Accounting and Cash Management].

[[prediction-ledger]]Prediction ledger::
The extension ledger in which the predicted entries are written. It is structured like the ledger of actual data. See xref:erp/sap/finance/predictive-accounting.adoc[Predictive Accounting].

[[predictive-accounting]]Predictive accounting::
Using the newest data from outside Finance, such as Sales, to predict accounting results before the actual postings exist. See xref:erp/sap/finance/predictive-accounting.adoc[Predictive Accounting].

[[predictive-journal-entry]]Predictive journal entry::
An entry in the prediction ledger that shows what a future actual entry is expected to look like. See xref:erp/sap/finance/predictive-accounting.adoc[Predictive Accounting].

[[price-control]]Price control::
The setting in the material master that says whether a material is valued at a standard price (S) or at a moving average price (V). See xref:erp/sap/finance/product-costing-and-material-ledger.adoc[Product Costing and the Material Ledger].

[[price-difference]]Price difference::
The part of a posting that exceeds or falls short of the standard price of a material. See xref:erp/sap/finance/product-costing-and-material-ledger.adoc[Product Costing and the Material Ledger].

[[processing-rule]]Processing rule::
The newer rule, with conditions and actions, for processing bank statement items. SAP recommends it instead of posting rules. See xref:erp/sap/finance/bank-accounting-and-cash-management.adoc[Bank Accounting and Cash Management].

[[profit-and-loss-statement]]Profit and loss statement::
The statement of revenue and expenses over a period, also called the income statement. It shows whether the company made a profit or a loss. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounting-basics.adoc[Accounting Basics].

[[profit-center]]Profit center::
An organizational unit that reflects a management-oriented structure of the company for internal control. Its result can be determined and analyzed on its own. See xref:erp/sap/finance/profit-center-accounting.adoc[Profit Center Accounting].

[[profit-center-accounting]]Profit Center Accounting::
The part of accounting that determines profit and loss by profit center, either by period accounting or by the cost-of-sales approach. See xref:erp/sap/finance/profit-center-accounting.adoc[Profit Center Accounting].

[[profit-center-derivation]]Profit center derivation::
The rules by which a line item receives a profit center from the object it is posted to. See xref:erp/sap/finance/profit-center-accounting.adoc[Profit Center Accounting].

[[profit-center-group]]Profit center group::
A tree of profit centers, used for reporting, allocations and planning. See xref:erp/sap/finance/profit-center-accounting.adoc[Profit Center Accounting].

[[profit-center-valuation]]Profit center valuation::
A valuation approach in which goods that move between profit centers are valued at transfer prices. See xref:erp/sap/finance/profit-center-accounting.adoc[Profit Center Accounting].

[[profitability-segment]]Profitability segment::
One combination of values of the characteristics of an operating concern, for example one product sold to one customer group. It corresponds to a market segment. See xref:erp/sap/finance/margin-analysis.adoc[Margin Analysis (Profitability Analysis)].

== R

[[receiver]]Receiver::
In an allocation, the object that is charged with the allocated value, for example a cost center. See xref:erp/sap/finance/allocations.adoc[Allocations: Assessment, Distribution and Activity Allocation].

[[reconciliation]]Reconciliation::
Comparing two sources of the same figure, here the old and the new system, and explaining every difference. See xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-implementation-migration-and-testing.adoc[Finance Implementation, Migration and Testing].

[[reconciliation-account]]Reconciliation account::
A general ledger account that the system posts to automatically whenever you post to a customer or supplier account. The ledger therefore always shows the total of the subledger. See xref:erp/sap/finance/business-partners-customers-and-suppliers.adoc[Business Partners as Customers and Suppliers].

[[reduction-posting]]Reduction posting::
A predictive entry that cancels the amount of an earlier prediction once the actual entry exists. See xref:erp/sap/finance/predictive-accounting.adoc[Predictive Accounting].

[[reference-key]]Reference key::
The object key that a posting coming from another application carries, so that the source document can be found again from the accounting entry. See xref:erp/sap/finance/bapi-acc-document-post.adoc[Posting from ABAP: BAPI_ACC_DOCUMENT_POST].

[[release-universal-journal]]Release Universal Journal::
The older task that copies data from accounting to group reporting. It is the classic way of integrating the two. See xref:erp/sap/finance/group-reporting.adoc[Group Reporting and Consolidation].

[[retained-earnings-account]]Retained earnings account::
The equity account that receives the balance of the profit and loss accounts at year end, so that the result of the year is carried forward. See xref:erp/sap/finance/charts-of-accounts-and-gl-accounts.adoc[Charts of Accounts and G/L Accounts].

[[revenue]]Revenue::
The value of the goods and services sold in a period. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounting-basics.adoc[Accounting Basics].

== S

[[sap-fiori-app]]SAP Fiori app::
A task-focused web application that you start from a tile on the launchpad. See xref:erp/sap/finance/transactions-and-fiori-apps.adoc[Finance Transactions and Fiori Apps].

[[sap-fiori-apps-reference-library]]SAP Fiori apps reference library::
SAP's public catalog of apps, with their name, ID, release availability and technical data. See xref:erp/sap/finance/transactions-and-fiori-apps.adoc[Finance Transactions and Fiori Apps].

[[sap-gui]]SAP GUI::
The older desktop-style user interface of SAP systems, in which you open screens by typing transaction codes. See xref:erp/sap/finance/transactions-and-fiori-apps.adoc[Finance Transactions and Fiori Apps].

[[segment]]Segment::
A division of a company for which you can create financial statements for external reporting. See xref:erp/sap/finance/organizational-units.adoc[Organizational Units in Finance].

[[sender]]Sender::
In an allocation, the object that holds the value to be passed on, for example a cost center together with an account. See xref:erp/sap/finance/allocations.adoc[Allocations: Assessment, Distribution and Activity Allocation].

[[settlement]]Settlement::
The transfer of the costs collected on a sender object, such as an order, to its receivers. See xref:erp/sap/finance/cost-centers-and-internal-orders.adoc[Cost Centers and Internal Orders].

[[settlement-profile]]Settlement profile::
The settings that control settlement for a kind of sender, such as which receivers are allowed. See xref:erp/sap/finance/cost-centers-and-internal-orders.adoc[Cost Centers and Internal Orders].

[[settlement-rule]]Settlement rule::
The definition of where the costs of a sender are settled. It is made up of one or more distribution rules. See xref:erp/sap/finance/cost-centers-and-internal-orders.adoc[Cost Centers and Internal Orders].

[[simplification-list]]Simplification List::
SAP's list of what changed between SAP ERP and SAP S/4HANA, including transactions that are redirected or obsolete. See xref:erp/sap/finance/transactions-and-fiori-apps.adoc[Finance Transactions and Fiori Apps].

[[source-document]]Source document::
The business document, here a sales order, from which predictions are derived. See xref:erp/sap/finance/predictive-accounting.adoc[Predictive Accounting].

[[special-gl-indicator]]Special G/L indicator::
The one-letter code that marks a line item as a special G/L transaction and says which kind it is. See xref:erp/sap/finance/special-gl-transactions.adoc[Special G/L Transactions].

[[special-gl-transaction]]Special G/L transaction::
A posting to a customer or supplier account that is shown separately in the general ledger and in the subledger, for example a down payment. It is not handled like an ordinary invoice. See xref:erp/sap/finance/special-gl-transactions.adoc[Special G/L Transactions].

[[special-node]]Special node::
One of seven nodes that the system creates in every financial statement version. Each carries a fixed meaning in reporting. See xref:erp/sap/finance/financial-statement-versions.adoc[Financial Statement Versions].

[[special-period]]Special period::
An extra period, also called an adjustment period, that subdivides the last regular period so that year-end closing postings can be made separately. See xref:erp/sap/finance/fiscal-year-and-posting-periods.adoc[Fiscal Year Variants and Posting Periods].

[[staging-table]]Staging table::
A table that the migration cockpit creates for each migration object. You fill it from a template file before the data is loaded. See xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-implementation-migration-and-testing.adoc[Finance Implementation, Migration and Testing].

[[standard-cost-estimate]]Standard cost estimate::
The cost estimate, normally made once a year, whose result becomes the standard price of a material. See xref:erp/sap/finance/product-costing-and-material-ledger.adoc[Product Costing and the Material Ledger].

[[standard-hierarchy]]Standard hierarchy::
The one tree of cost centers (and, separately, of profit centers) that every such object must belong to. See xref:erp/sap/finance/organizational-units.adoc[Organizational Units in Finance].

[[statistical-key-figure]]Statistical key figure::
A number kept on a Controlling object that is not an amount, for example the number of employees or the floor area. It is often used as a tracing factor. See xref:erp/sap/finance/allocations.adoc[Allocations: Assessment, Distribution and Activity Allocation].

[[statistical-posting]]Statistical posting::
A posting whose offsetting entry always goes to the same preset account, such as a guarantee. See xref:erp/sap/finance/special-gl-transactions.adoc[Special G/L Transactions].

[[subledger]]Subledger::
A detailed ledger, such as accounts payable or asset accounting, whose totals feed the general ledger. See xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-overview.adoc[Finance Overview].

[[substitution]]Substitution::
A rule that derives, replaces or clears the value of a field while data is being entered. See xref:erp/sap/finance/substitutions-validations-and-badis.adoc[Substitutions, Validations and BAdIs].

[[supplier-invoice]]Supplier invoice::
The bill from a supplier, entered in the system so that it becomes a payable. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-payable.adoc[Accounts Payable].

== T

[[t-account]]T-account::
A drawing of one account as a letter T, with the debits on the left and the credits on the right. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounting-basics.adoc[Accounting Basics].

[[target-tax-code]]Target tax code::
The tax code that a deferred tax is moved to when the tax is recognized. See xref:erp/sap/finance/tax.adoc[Tax Codes and Tax Calculation].

[[tax-calculation-procedure]]Tax calculation procedure::
The country-specific list of tax types (condition types) and the rules that calculate and post them. See xref:erp/sap/finance/tax.adoc[Tax Codes and Tax Calculation].

[[tax-code]]Tax code::
A short key that stands for a tax rate and its rules. It is entered on a line item so that the system can check, calculate and post the tax. See xref:erp/sap/finance/tax.adoc[Tax Codes and Tax Calculation].

[[tax-jurisdiction-code]]Tax jurisdiction code::
A multi-level key for the taxing authorities of a location. Together with the tax code it is used to find the tax amount (United States and Brazil). See xref:erp/sap/finance/tax.adoc[Tax Codes and Tax Calculation].

[[tax-type]]Tax type::
The kind of tax that a tax code carries, such as input tax, output tax or non-deductible input tax. It decides how the amount is calculated and posted. See xref:erp/sap/finance/tax.adoc[Tax Codes and Tax Calculation].

[[technical-clearing-account]]Technical clearing account for integrated asset acquisition::
The account that sits between the supplier invoice and the asset posting in an integrated asset acquisition. It always ends at zero. See xref:erp/sap/finance/asset-accounting.adoc[Asset Accounting].

[[terms-of-payment]]Terms of payment::
Payment terms::
The agreement on when an invoice is due and with which cash discount it can be paid early. In the system it is a key that holds the days, the cash discount percentages and the baseline date rule. See xref:erp/sap/finance/payment-terms-and-cash-discount.adoc[Payment Terms, Cash Discount and Installment Plans].

[[tolerance-group]]Tolerance group::
A named set of limits, kept for a company code, for the amounts a user may post or the differences the system may accept. See xref:erp/sap/finance/number-ranges-field-status-and-tolerance-groups.adoc[Number Ranges, Field Status Variants and Tolerance Groups].

[[tolerance-limit]]Tolerance limit::
The usage level or absolute amount at which availability control issues a warning or an error. See xref:erp/sap/finance/planning-and-budgeting.adoc[Planning and Budgeting].

[[tracing-factor]]Tracing factor::
The number that decides what share of the sender value each receiver gets, for example floor area or hours used. See xref:erp/sap/finance/allocations.adoc[Allocations: Assessment, Distribution and Activity Allocation].

[[transaction-code]]Transaction code::
A short name, such as `FB50`, that opens one screen of the SAP GUI. See xref:erp/sap/finance/transactions-and-fiori-apps.adoc[Finance Transactions and Fiori Apps].

[[transaction-key]]Transaction key::
A three-character key, such as `BSX`, that names the kind of line in a goods movement or invoice, so that Customizing can assign an account to it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/account-determination-and-automatic-postings.adoc[Account Determination and Automatic Postings].

[[trial-balance]]Trial balance::
A list of all account balances, used to check that the total of the debits equals the total of the credits. See xref:erp/sap/finance/accounting-basics.adoc[Accounting Basics].

[[true-account-assignment]]True account assignment::
A line item whose profitability segment was determined by the sending application or entered by hand. It is available to follow-on processes. See xref:erp/sap/finance/margin-analysis.adoc[Margin Analysis (Profitability Analysis)].

== U

[[universal-allocation]]Universal Allocation::
The group of apps in current releases for defining, running and checking allocations. See xref:erp/sap/finance/allocations.adoc[Allocations: Assessment, Distribution and Activity Allocation].

[[universal-journal]]Universal Journal::
The book of original entry for Financial Accounting and Controlling in SAP S/4HANA. Its line items are stored once, in the table ACDOCA, instead of in separate tables for each area. See xref:erp/sap/finance/universal-journal.adoc[The Universal Journal (ACDOCA)].

[[universal-parallel-accounting]]Universal Parallel Accounting::
The capability, switched on by one business function, that makes the subledger processes of Asset Accounting, Controlling and the Material Ledger work per ledger. See xref:erp/sap/finance/universal-parallel-accounting.adoc[Universal Parallel Accounting].

== V

[[validation]]Validation::
A rule that checks the values entered and raises a warning or an error when they break the rule. See xref:erp/sap/finance/substitutions-validations-and-badis.adoc[Substitutions, Validations and BAdIs].

[[validity-period]]Validity period::
A date range for which one version of a cost center's master data holds. See xref:erp/sap/finance/cost-centers-and-internal-orders.adoc[Cost Centers and Internal Orders].

[[valuation-class]]Valuation class::
A setting in the accounting data of a material that groups materials which post to the same accounts. See xref:erp/sap/finance/account-determination-and-automatic-postings.adoc[Account Determination and Automatic Postings].

[[variance]]Variance::
The difference between the costs a production order should have had (target costs) and the costs charged to it. See xref:erp/sap/finance/product-costing-and-material-ledger.adoc[Product Costing and the Material Ledger].

== Y

[[year-shift]]Year shift::
The entry (-1, 0 or +1) that tells the system how to turn the calendar year of a posting date into the fiscal year. See xref:erp/sap/finance/fiscal-year-and-posting-periods.adoc[Fiscal Year Variants and Posting Periods].

== Z

[[zero-balance-clearing]]Zero balance clearing::
The extra lines the system adds so that each balancing dimension of a document nets to zero. See xref:erp/sap/finance/document-splitting.adoc[Document Splitting].
