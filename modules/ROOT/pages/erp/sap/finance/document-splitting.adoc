= Document Splitting
:description: How SAP document splitting divides the lines of a journal entry by profit center or segment so each one balances on its own, with a worked vendor invoice split across two profit centers, for SAP S/4HANA 2025 FPS02 on premise.
:keywords: SAP, S/4HANA, finance, document splitting, splitting characteristic, profit center, segment, item category, business transaction, document splitting rule, zero balance clearing, general ledger view, entry view, passive splitting, active splitting, FAGL_SHOW_SPLIT
:stem: latexmath

include::partial$ai-disclaimer.adoc[]

A company that reports by area, for example one balance sheet per profit center, has a problem the moment an invoice is
shared between two areas. The vendor owes one amount for the whole invoice, yet each area wants to see only its own part
of the cost and its own part of the debt. Document splitting is the SAP function that solves this: when a document is
posted, the system divides selected lines by the chosen dimensions, such as profit center or segment, and where the
parts still do not balance it adds clearing lines so that every dimension balances to zero on its own. After reading this
page you can explain what the function does, name its building blocks (splitting characteristics, item categories,
business transactions, rules and methods), follow a worked vendor invoice from the lines a clerk types to the lines the
ledger stores, and know which Customizing activities and which simulation tool to open. It is written against the SAP
S/4HANA 2025 FPS02 on-premise documentation. The Customizing paths below are read from that documentation. This page
does not describe the Cloud Public Edition settings for document splitting.

== Key terms

* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#document-splitting[Document splitting]: the function that divides document lines by
  selected dimensions at posting time and adds clearing lines where a dimension would not balance.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#document-splitting-characteristic[Document splitting characteristic]: a dimension,
  such as profit center or segment, for which documents are split.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#item-category[Item category]: a predefined class such as vendor, expense or tax that
  every account is assigned to, so the rules know what kind of line they see.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#business-transaction[Business transaction]: the kind of business event a document
  represents for splitting, such as vendor invoice or payment.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#document-splitting-rule[Document splitting rule]: the setting that says, for one
  business transaction, which item categories are split and from which lines the account assignments are taken.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#zero-balance-clearing[Zero balance clearing]: the extra lines the system adds so
  that each balancing dimension of a document nets to zero.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#entry-view[Entry view]: the document as it was typed or handed over by the source
  application, before splitting.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#general-ledger-view[General ledger view]: the document as one ledger stores it,
  including split lines and clearing lines.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#profit-center[Profit center]: an area of the organization whose profit and loss,
  and possibly balance sheet, is reported on its own.

== Why documents are split

SAP describes the purpose in one sentence: with document splitting you automatically split line items for selected
dimensions, for example receivable lines by profit center, or bring selected dimensions to a zero balance in the
document, and in doing so the system creates additional clearing lines. The dimensions SAP lists are segments, profit
centers, business areas, funds, receivables and a customer-defined field. The reason is reporting: with the split in
place you can draw up a complete balance sheet, or a partial one, for each of those dimensions at any time, for example
a segment balance sheet that follows a legal requirement or a profit center balance sheet that follows areas of
responsibility.

Without splitting, a balance sheet account carries no profit center, because the clerk who posts a vendor invoice
enters the profit center only on the cost line. The payable would then belong to nobody. Splitting copies the cost
line's account assignment to the payable, so the payable is also reported by profit center.

SAP adds two more uses. At the time of posting, additional costs such as realized or valuated exchange rate differences
can be assigned to the Controlling account assignment they relate to, and fixed assets can be capitalized afterwards.

Source: https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/651d8af3ea974ad1a4d74449122c620e/4911c9cc2a934a18e10000000a42189b.html[Document Splitting (SAP Help Portal, on premise)]

== Entry view and general ledger view

A posted document is looked at in two ways. In the entry view it contains the lines as they were originally entered or
transferred from the source component. In the general ledger view it contains those lines plus the split lines and any
clearing lines that document splitting generated. A general ledger view always belongs to one ledger, and SAP notes that
a characteristic such as profit center or segment is shown there only if it belongs to the scenarios assigned to that
ledger. Display shows the leading ledger first, and a button chooses another ledger.

To see the general ledger view, display the document and choose General Ledger View, or choose Other Ledger for a ledger
that is not the leading one. SAP Easy Access reaches this through Accounting, Financial Accounting, General Ledger,
Document, then either Display (entry view) or Display General Ledger View.

Source: https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/651d8af3ea974ad1a4d74449122c620e/e950d7531a4d424de10000000a174cb4.html[Document Display (SAP Help Portal, on premise)]

== The building blocks

=== Splitting characteristics

A document splitting characteristic is the account assignment object for which splitting happens, for example profit
center, segment, business area or a customer field. SAP separates two groups. Characteristics for General Ledger
Accounting can be marked relevant for the balance sheet, which is what triggers the zero balance clearing described
below, and can be marked as a required entry field, so a posting that lacks the account assignment on any line is
rejected. Additional characteristics that matter for other components, Controlling for example, are split in the entry
view and are transferred together with the line item to that component. A characteristic can be used only if at least
one ledger updates it, and you decide which ones a ledger carries by assigning a scenario to it or by adding a customer
field. The activity is named Define Document Splitting Characteristics for General Ledger Accounting, under Financial
Accounting, General Ledger Accounting, Business Transactions, Document Splitting.

Source: https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/651d8af3ea974ad1a4d74449122c620e/49121ad4cbff280ee10000000a42189c.html[Document Splitting Characteristic (SAP Help Portal, on premise)]

=== Item categories

The system cannot guess what an account is for, so every account is assigned an item category, a predefined class.
SAP lists the categories and says you cannot add your own. A few that appear on this page:

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Category |Meaning in the SAP documentation

|`01000` |Balance sheet account
|`01001` |Zero balance posting (free balancing units)
|`03000` |Vendor
|`04000` |Cash account
|`05100` |Tax on sales and purchases
|`20000` |Expense
|`30000` |Revenue
|===

You must assign balance sheet accounts, bank accounts (cash fund), expense accounts and revenue accounts yourself in the
activity Classify G/L Accounts for Document Splitting. SAP states that the assignment of all other accounts is
predefined in table `T8G16`, and that an indicator allows you to override it, for example to treat special general
ledger accounts of customers or vendors as the detailed categories `02100` or `03100`.

Source: https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/651d8af3ea974ad1a4d74449122c620e/ee79c5c00ca249b783bc74fff0e484aa.html[Item Category (SAP Help Portal, on premise)]

=== Business transactions

Splitting must know what kind of event a document records, because a vendor invoice is split differently from a payment.
SAP predefines the business transactions and you cannot create your own. Examples from the list are `0200` customer
invoice, `0300` vendor invoice and `1000` payment. Each has a business transaction variant, and the delivered variant
`0001` (Standard) contains all item categories available for that transaction. A customer invoice, for instance, cannot
contain the vendor category. You link a document type to a business transaction in Classify Document Types for Document
Splitting. SAP says that mixing different business processes under one document type is not supported, so each
business transaction needs its own document type, and that a document containing several business processes at once,
for example a payment and an invoice, cannot be split.

Source: https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/651d8af3ea974ad1a4d74449122c620e/4912875f879c6b41e10000000a42189c.html[Business Transaction (SAP Help Portal, on premise)]

=== Rules and methods

A document splitting rule is defined for one business transaction and one variant. It lists the item categories to be
split and, for each, the basis item categories from which the account assignments are taken. The system works through
both lists from bottom to top and stops for an item category as soon as one basis category yields the account
assignment. A method is just the collection of the rules that share its key. SAP delivers the standard method
`0000000012` (follow-up costs online), which can be used unchanged in most cases, and two methods for US Fund
Accounting. The method in use is set in Assign Document Splitting Method under Extended Document Splitting, with the
standard method as default. SAP recommends copying a delivered method before changing it and never changing the
delivered one.

Source: https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/651d8af3ea974ad1a4d74449122c620e/324b59076895417c9d2ccb0cfb3f06c0.html[Document Splitting Method (SAP Help Portal, on premise)],
https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/651d8af3ea974ad1a4d74449122c620e/2a38fede2515438d9f8487de64a46a7d.html[Document Splitting Rule (SAP Help Portal, on premise)]

=== Active and passive splitting

SAP divides the work in two. Active splitting covers everything done without reference to an earlier document, such as
the invoice in the example below, and you control it through Customizing. Passive splitting covers the processes the
system performs internally and that Customizing cannot control, such as clearing, reversal and invoices referenced in a
payment: the system copies the account assignments already stored on the original lines.

Sources:
https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/651d8af3ea974ad1a4d74449122c620e/4913c36f26586b41e10000000a42189c.html[Active Document Splitting (SAP Help Portal, on premise)],
https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/651d8af3ea974ad1a4d74449122c620e/4913726726586b41e10000000a42189c.html[Passive Document Splitting (SAP Help Portal, on premise)]

=== Missing assignments: inheritance and standard account assignment

When active splitting leaves a line without the characteristics, two options, both switched on when you activate document
splitting, can fill the gap. Inheritance projects the characteristics of the document onto lines without any, provided
the combination in the document is unique, and it also produces a zero balance. A standard account assignment writes a
constant you define into lines still lacking one. Both can be deactivated for single item categories in a rule. The
activity is Activate Document Splitting.

Source: https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/651d8af3ea974ad1a4d74449122c620e/de2ae8621a304b88b94d90a90afa14f0.html[Enhancement Logic (SAP Help Portal, on premise)]

== How the system splits a document

The documented process, in order, is shown below.

[mermaid]
----
flowchart TD
    A["Posting: document type, accounts, amounts"] --> B["Document type gives the business transaction"]
    B --> C["Accounts give the item categories; check they are allowed for the transaction"]
    C --> D["Passive splitting: copy account assignments from preceding documents"]
    D --> E["Active splitting: apply the document splitting rule"]
    E --> F["Missing assignments: inheritance or standard account assignment"]
    F --> G["Zero balance setting: add clearing lines where a dimension does not net to zero"]
    G --> H["General ledger view stored per ledger"]
----

After posting, the split lines are available to later processes. SAP names the realized exchange rate difference, which
can use the Controlling account assignments on clearing or partial payment, and closing operations such as foreign
currency valuation of open items, which can be run for the splitting characteristics.

Source: https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/651d8af3ea974ad1a4d74449122c620e/4911c9cc2a934a18e10000000a42189b.html[Document Splitting (SAP Help Portal, on premise)]

== A worked example: one vendor invoice, two profit centers

Example Trading Co. (company code `EX01`, leading ledger `0L`) receives one supplier invoice of EUR 1,000.00 for
services that two departments use. The clerk assigns EUR 600.00 of the cost to profit center `EX-RETAIL` and EUR 400.00 to
profit center `EX-WHOLE`. Account names, profit center names and the numbers are illustrative values for this site, not
delivered content. Tax is left out to keep the arithmetic readable. How the tax line is treated is set in the delivered
rule for the vendor invoice and is not described here. The group currency is GBP and, for this example only, one euro
is worth 0.85 pound, so every line also carries the amount in GBP. Splitting characteristic: profit center.

=== What the clerk enters (entry view)

The vendor line, as in the invoice entry, has no profit center. Debit is positive, credit is negative.

[cols="1,2,1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Item category |Account |Profit center |EUR (company code currency) |GBP (group currency)

|20000 expense |Consulting expense |EX-RETAIL |600.00 |510.00
|20000 expense |Consulting expense |EX-WHOLE |400.00 |340.00
|03000 vendor |Supplier payable | |-1,000.00 |-850.00
|===

=== What the ledger stores (general ledger view, ledger `0L`)

For the vendor invoice the payable is split by the cost lines it pays for, so the one payable line becomes two.

[cols="1,2,1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Item category |Account |Profit center |EUR |GBP

|20000 expense |Consulting expense |EX-RETAIL |600.00 |510.00
|20000 expense |Consulting expense |EX-WHOLE |400.00 |340.00
|03000 vendor |Supplier payable |EX-RETAIL |-600.00 |-510.00
|03000 vendor |Supplier payable |EX-WHOLE |-400.00 |-340.00
|===

The entry view still shows the single payable of EUR 1,000.00. The split lines exist so that each profit center is
complete in the general ledger view.

=== The figure: T accounts by profit center

The same four lines as T accounts, grouped by profit center, with the debit side and the credit side of each. Within a profit center the debit and the credit are equal, so each profit center balances on its own.

[cols="2,2,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Profit center |T account |Debit (EUR) |Credit (EUR)

.3+|EX-RETAIL |Consulting expense |600.00 |
|Supplier payable | |600.00
|*Total* |*600.00* |*600.00*

.3+|EX-WHOLE |Consulting expense |400.00 |
|Supplier payable | |400.00
|*Total* |*400.00* |*400.00*
|===

The check is one equation per profit center. For a profit center stem:[p] with debits stem:[D_p] and credits
stem:[C_p] the balance is stem:[D_p - C_p], and it is zero for both:

[stem]
++++
\text{EX-RETAIL: } 600.00 - 600.00 = 0 \qquad\qquad \text{EX-WHOLE: } 400.00 - 400.00 = 0
++++

The same holds in GBP, stem:[510.00 - 510.00 = 0] and stem:[340.00 - 340.00 = 0]. The document as a whole stays
balanced too: stem:[600 + 400 - 600 - 400 = 0]. A simple way to read this is that the invoice produced one balanced
document for each profit center inside the one posted document.

Source for the mechanism, with an equivalent vendor invoice example split by segment: https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/651d8af3ea974ad1a4d74449122c620e/4911c9cc2a934a18e10000000a42189b.html[Document Splitting (SAP Help Portal, on premise)]. This worked example requires an SAP system to reproduce; not run here.

=== Paying the invoice (passive splitting)

When Example Trading Co. pays the whole invoice, the payment refers to it. The payment clears the payable and the system
splits it passively: the payable lines are reversed per profit center, and the bank line is split in the same
proportion. The result in the general ledger view is 600.00 and 400.00 on the payable lines and 600.00 and 400.00 on
the bank line, so each profit center again nets to zero. SAP's own payment example, a payment of 95 with cash discounts for an
invoice of 100 split between two segments, shows the bank and discount lines split the same way.

Source: https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/651d8af3ea974ad1a4d74449122c620e/4911c9cc2a934a18e10000000a42189b.html[Document Splitting (SAP Help Portal, on premise)]

== Zero balance clearing lines

The invoice above balanced by itself in each profit center. That is not always so. SAP describes the case in which the
dimensions of a document do not net to zero: the system then creates extra clearing lines so that they do. This is
switched on by setting the Zero Balance indicator on the characteristic (the characteristic is then relevant for the
balance sheet). In the document splitting rule you can name one of the account keys that you created under Define
Zero-Balance Clearing Account, and the clearing lines are posted to the account behind that key. If the document lines
already carry partner characteristics (the sender and receiver of an assignment), the clearing follows those. If they do
not, the system builds two-sided clearing lines with an amount procedure and fills the partner assignment from the
matching line.

A constructed illustration of the mechanism, not taken from SAP: suppose the payment of the example invoice reaches
the system with the bank line already carrying a third, illustrative profit center, `EX-CENTRAL`, while the payable lines it clears carry
`EX-RETAIL` (debit 600.00) and `EX-WHOLE` (debit 400.00).

[cols="2,1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Line |Profit center |EUR |Item category

|Supplier payable |EX-RETAIL |600.00 |03000
|Supplier payable |EX-WHOLE |400.00 |03000
|Bank |EX-CENTRAL |-1,000.00 |04000
|===

Profit center `EX-RETAIL` now nets to 600.00, `EX-WHOLE` to 400.00 and `EX-CENTRAL` to -1,000.00, so none of the three
balances on its own. Two-sided clearing lines, with each amount shown on both sides of the partner pair, bring each to
zero:

[cols="2,1,1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Clearing line (item category 01001) |Profit center |Partner |EUR |Effect

|On the zero balance clearing account |EX-RETAIL |EX-CENTRAL |-600.00 |EX-RETAIL nets 600.00 - 600.00 = 0
|On the zero balance clearing account |EX-CENTRAL |EX-RETAIL |600.00 |EX-CENTRAL so far -1,000.00 + 600.00
|On the zero balance clearing account |EX-WHOLE |EX-CENTRAL |-400.00 |EX-WHOLE nets 400.00 - 400.00 = 0
|On the zero balance clearing account |EX-CENTRAL |EX-WHOLE |400.00 |EX-CENTRAL now -1,000.00 + 600.00 + 400.00 = 0
|===

The four clearing lines sum to stem:[-600 + 600 - 400 + 400 = 0], so the document stays balanced, and each profit
center nets to zero. A real system's lines depend on the rule and the Customizing in force, so confirm them by
simulating the document. This illustration requires an SAP system to reproduce; not run here.

== Testing a split without posting

During document entry you can simulate the general ledger view. In General Posting choose Document, then Simulate
General Ledger View, and the screen shows the lines of the leading ledger. With document splitting active, the Expert
Mode button opens a screen that shows the Customizing for the business transaction and details of the split document.
For a document already posted, report `FAGL_SHOW_SPLIT` calls the same expert mode. SAP says this is particularly useful
when you test the Customizing for document splitting.

Source: https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/651d8af3ea974ad1a4d74449122c620e/704fd7531a4d424de10000000a174cb4.html[Simulating Documents in the General Ledger View (SAP Help Portal, on premise)]

== Limits and traps

* Splitting needs each document to belong to one business process. A document that mixes a payment and an invoice cannot
  be split.
* Splitting is driven by the document type. Give each business transaction its own document type.
* A characteristic appears in the general ledger view of a ledger only if that ledger carries it.
* SAP recommends copying a delivered splitting method and changing the copy, never the delivered method itself.
* Material written for SAP ERP describes document splitting as a feature of "new General Ledger Accounting" next to the
  classic general ledger. SAP's current help pages still use that wording, but the Universal Journal of SAP S/4HANA
  stores the lines, so read that older material for the concepts and check each setting against the current
  documentation.

== Summary

* Document splitting divides selected lines of a document by dimensions such as profit center or segment, so that
  reports and balance sheets can be drawn for each.
* An account's item category and a document type's business transaction decide how a line is treated; a rule and a
  method collect the settings, and the delivered standard method is usually enough.
* Active splitting follows Customizing, and passive splitting copies assignments from earlier documents.
* A vendor invoice of EUR 1,000.00 with cost lines of 600.00 and 400.00 on two profit centers produces two payable
  lines of the same amounts, so each profit center balances alone.
* Where a dimension does not net to zero, zero balance clearing lines are added.
* Simulating the general ledger view, with the expert mode, shows the rule that was applied.
