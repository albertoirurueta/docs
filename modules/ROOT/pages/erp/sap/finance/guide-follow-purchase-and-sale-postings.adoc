Checked on 2026-11-13 against CAP Node.js 10.2 (`@sap/cds` 10.2.2, `@sap/cds-dk` 10.2.1, `@cap-js/cds-test` 1.0.2), the Fiori elements preview of `cds watch` (SAPUI5 1.145.0), curl 8.7 and Node.js 24.21.

== What you build

This is a teaching model built with CAP. It is not SAP S/4HANA or SAP SuccessFactors and does not reproduce their screens.

You build a small ledger for the company code `EX01` of Example Trading Co. (euro, ledger `0L`) with six actions, one for each business event of a purchase and a sale: a goods receipt, a supplier invoice, a payment to the supplier, a sale (the goods issue and the billing document), a payment from the customer, and the clearing of open items. No action receives a G/L account. Each one builds its lines from a *transaction key* such as `BSX` (stock) or `WRX` (GR/IR clearing), and a table of account determination turns the key into the account, the way xref:erp/sap/finance/account-determination-and-automatic-postings.adoc[Account Determination and Automatic Postings] describes. You buy 50 pieces, receive them, get the invoice and pay it, sell 40 of them, get paid, clear the open items that add up to zero, and read the ledger as T-accounts.

image::sap-finance-guide-follow-purchase-and-sale-postings-11-result.svg["Ten T-accounts of company code EX01 after six documents: bank debit 107.10, customers zero, stock of trading goods debit 75.00, price differences debit 35.00, input tax debit 77.90, suppliers zero, GR/IR clearing zero, output tax credit 95.00, cost of goods sold debit 300.00 and sales revenue credit 500.00",width=900]

== Before you start

* No account is needed: the CAP tools and curl install without signing in. Installing `@sap/cds-dk` means accepting the SAP Developer License Agreement, version 3.2, which the lab README names.
* Do the guides xref:erp/sap/guide-first-cap-service.adoc[Your First CAP Service] (it explains `cds init`, CDS files, CSV data and `cds watch`) and xref:erp/sap/finance/guide-post-balanced-journal-entries.adoc[Post Balanced Journal Entries in a Teaching Ledger] first. This guide uses the same sign convention: a debit is a positive amount and a credit a negative one.
* Read the pages that the model copies: xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-payable.adoc[Accounts Payable] (goods receipt, invoice, GR/IR clearing and payment), xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-receivable-dunning-and-credit.adoc[Accounts Receivable, Dunning and Credit Management] (order to cash), xref:erp/sap/finance/open-items-and-clearing.adoc[Open Item Management and Clearing] and xref:erp/sap/finance/account-determination-and-automatic-postings.adoc[Account Determination and Automatic Postings].
* You need curl 7.82 or newer, which has the option `--json`. `curl --version` prints the version.
* The names. `CompanyCode`, `Ledger`, `GLAccount` and `AmountInCompanyCodeCurrency` are element names of the SAP view `I_JournalEntryItemBasic`, as in the first finance guide. The transaction keys `BSX`, `PRD`, `WRX` and `GBB` and the account key `ERL` are the ones that the account determination page names from SAP pages. The keys `INTAX`, `OUTTAX`, `VENDOR`, `CUSTOMER` and `BANK`, the document type codes `WE`, `RE`, `WA` and `RV` (`KZ` and `DZ` are listed on xref:erp/sap/finance/journal-entries-document-types-and-posting-keys.adoc[Journal Entries, Document Types and Posting Keys]), the entity names, the account numbers and the purchase order, material, supplier and customer codes are this model's own. All codes and amounts are made up, and the tax rate of 19 percent is illustrative.
* The figures are drawings of the editor, the terminal, the browser and the Fiori page, not screenshots. A highlighted box with a number marks the file, the command or the page part of the step with the same number.

The sums on this page are the amounts that the service answered and `npm test` asserts. The goods receipt values 50 pieces at the standard price of 7.50 and at the order price of 8.00:

[stem]
++++
50 \times 7.50 = 375.00, \qquad 50 \times 8.00 = 400.00, \qquad 400.00 - 375.00 = 25.00
++++

The invoice is for stem:[50 \times 8.20 = 410.00] net, the tax is stem:[410.00 \times 0.19 = 77.90] and the supplier is credited with stem:[410.00 + 77.90 = 487.90]. GR/IR is cleared at the order value of 400.00, and the extra stem:[410.00 - 400.00 = 10.00] goes to the price difference line:

[stem]
++++
400.00 + 10.00 + 77.90 = 487.90
++++

The sale of 40 pieces costs stem:[40 \times 7.50 = 300.00] and bills stem:[40 \times 12.50 = 500.00] net, with output tax stem:[500.00 \times 0.19 = 95.00], so the customer owes stem:[500.00 + 95.00 = 595.00]. Then:

* GR/IR clearing has debits and credits of 400.00 each: the goods were received and invoiced, nothing is left.
* The suppliers and customers accounts balance at zero, and `OpenItems` is empty: every open item was cleared.
* The stock account is stem:[375.00 - 300.00 = 75.00], which is the 10 pieces left at the standard price of 7.50, and `curl -s $BASE/Materials` shows `StockQuantity` 10.
* The price difference account holds stem:[25.00 + 10.00 = 35.00], which is the invoice net minus the stock value: stem:[410.00 - 375.00 = 35.00].
* The bank account is stem:[595.00 - 487.90 = 107.10].
* The whole ledger balances, as every posting does: all debits are stem:[595.00 + 595.00 + 375.00 + 35.00 + 77.90 + 487.90 + 400.00 + 300.00 = 2\,865.80] and all credits are stem:[487.90 + 595.00 + 300.00 + 487.90 + 400.00 + 95.00 + 500.00 = 2\,865.80].

The goods receipt function is also checked against the worked example of the SAP Learning page *Describing Automatic Account Determination*. For 10 pieces with a standard price of 10 and an order price of 12, that page posts 100 to stock, 120 to GR/IR clearing and the difference of 20 to a price difference account, and the test `sap-learning-example.test.js` expects the same three lines. For an invoice of 110 against the goods receipt value of 120, the page clears the full 120 and posts the gap of 10 on another account. This model puts the gap on the price difference line too, so its account differs from the page's example but the amount is the same.

Run the tests with `npm test` in a copy of the lab project `ledger-postings`. They post the story of this guide and the cases of the next sections, and they pass.

== Variations

* Invoice only part of the goods. Restart `cds watch` (the database is in memory), post the goods receipt again, and post an invoice for 30 pieces:
+
[source,bash]
----
curl -sw "\n" --json '{"PurchaseOrder":"PO-1","Quantity":30,"InvoicePrice":8.00,"PostingDate":"2026-03-10"}' $BASE/postInvoiceReceipt
----
+
The invoice clears only stem:[30 \times 8.00 = 240.00] of GR/IR. Clearing `PO-1` answers `Items do not add up to zero: debits 240.00, credits 400.00` with HTTP 400: the credit balance of 160.00 is goods received and not yet invoiced, 20 pieces at 8.00, as the accounts payable page explains.
* Sell for the other profit center. After the goods receipt, post a sale of 10 pieces with `ProfitCenter` set:
+
[source,bash]
----
curl -sw "\n" --json '{"Material":"M-100","Quantity":10,"NetPrice":11.00,"Customer":"C-3000","PostingDate":"2026-03-26","ProfitCenter":"EX-WHOLE"}' $BASE/postSale
----
+
The lines with the profit center `EX-WHOLE` are the cost of goods sold of 75.00 on `600000` and the revenue of -110.00 on `800000`.
* Take a key out of the determination table. In `db/data/sap.guide.ledgerpostings-AccountDetermination.csv` delete the line of `WRX`, save, and post the goods receipt. The answer is `No account is determined for key WRX` with HTTP 400, and nothing is stored: the received quantity of `PO-1` and the stock of `M-100` are still 0, because the request is rolled back. Put the line back to continue.

== What can go wrong

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
|Symptom
|Cause
|Fix

|`PO-1 has only 50 pieces open` and HTTP 400
|The goods receipt asks for more pieces than the purchase order has open (60 instead of 50).
|Lower `Quantity` in the JSON file.

|`PO-1 has only 0 pieces open`
|The goods receipt was posted before in this run, so nothing is open.
|Skip the step, or restart `cds watch` to get the initial data back.

|`Stock of M-100 is 10, the sale needs 40` and HTTP 400
|The sale was posted a second time, or asks for more pieces than the stock.
|Sell at most the stock, or post a goods receipt first.

|`Items do not add up to zero: debits 240.00, credits 400.00`
|The open items selected for clearing do not add up to zero, as after a partial invoice.
|Post the missing document first, or select items that add up to zero.

|`Items do not add up to zero: debits 0.00, credits 0.00`
|Nothing is open for the account, the partner or the reference: the items are cleared already, or the document is not posted yet.
|Check `OpenItems`, then post the missing document.

|`No account is determined for key WRX`
|The determination table has no row for the key and the valuation class of the material.
|Add the row to the CSV file.
|===

== Clean up

Press `Ctrl+C` in the terminal that runs `cds watch`. The database lives only in memory, so the six documents are gone at the next start; the CSV files bring back the accounts, the material and the purchase order.
