= Universal Parallel Accounting
:description: What Universal Parallel Accounting (UPA) changes in SAP S/4HANA, how ledger-specific valuation reaches Asset Accounting, Controlling and the Material Ledger, which business functions and prerequisites apply, and how to size a migration, for SAP S/4HANA 2025 FPS02 on premise with notes on Cloud Public Edition 2608.

:stem: latexmath

A company that keeps its books under two accounting principles, for example a group principle and a local one, needs
more than two sets of journal entries. The value of a machine, of the stock in a warehouse or of a production order can
differ between the two principles, and so can every cost that is spread over them. Universal Parallel Accounting (UPA)
is the SAP S/4HANA capability that lets each of those processes calculate and post its own result for every ledger,
instead of calculating once in the leading ledger and copying the result to the others. This page explains what changes
for Asset Accounting, Controlling and the Material Ledger, how UPA is switched on, what it needs and what it cannot be
undone from, and what a migration involves. It is written against SAP S/4HANA 2025 FPS02 on premise (and Private
Edition), where the business function is documented. For SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition 2608 the page names only what
the Public Edition help states for ledgers.

== Key terms

* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#universal-parallel-accounting[Universal Parallel Accounting]: the capability, switched
  on by one business function, that makes the subledger processes of Asset Accounting, Controlling and the Material
  Ledger work per ledger.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#business-function[Business function]: a switch in the Switch Framework that turns on
  a group of features; some cannot be turned off again.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#ledger-specific-valuation[Ledger-specific valuation]: calculating a value, such as
  depreciation or a material price, separately for each ledger and its accounting principle.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#group-valuation[Group valuation]: valuation at group level, where profits between
  group companies are eliminated; with UPA it has its own ledger.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#profit-center-valuation[Profit center valuation]: valuation based on the transfer
  prices used for trading between profit centers, also kept in its own ledger.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#material-ledger[Material ledger]: the component that keeps material prices and
  inventory values, in S/4HANA also called Material Subledger.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#actual-costing[Actual costing]: the period-end run that replaces the standard price
  of materials with prices computed from actual costs.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#depreciation-area[Depreciation area]: the Asset Accounting entity in which an asset
  is depreciated under one set of rules.

== The problem UPA solves

The xref:erp/sap/finance/ledgers-and-currencies.adoc[ledger page] showed that a posting can reach the leading ledger
`0L` and a non-leading ledger `2L` at the same time, and that a manual posting can be sent to one of them only. That
covers the general ledger. Beneath it, three components do their own calculations, and each has a history of a single
answer:

* Asset Accounting calculates depreciation per depreciation area.
* Controlling distributes costs (overhead calculation, settlement, allocation) and works out what products cost.
* The Material Ledger holds material prices and the value of stock.

Stoil Jotev's _Configuring SAP S/4HANA Finance_ (3rd edition, 2025) describes the effect without UPA: in cost
distribution, assessment and settlement, the values of the leading ledger were also posted to the non-leading ledgers,
which needed manual workarounds. With UPA, these processes run per ledger and the result is correct for each accounting
principle. SAP's own description of the feature says the same: you can post different data for each ledger and display
or select data from different ledgers in many Finance apps, and you can define up to ten currencies that are used across
all ledgers and accounting processes.

== What UPA changes

The business function is called Universal Parallel Accounting, technical name `FINS_PARALLEL_ACCOUNTING_BF`. It is an
enterprise business function, available from SAP S/4HANA 2022, and it touches General Ledger Accounting, Asset
Accounting, Overhead Cost Controlling, Profitability Analysis, Product Cost Controlling, Actual Costing, the Material
Subledger, Inventory Balance Sheet Valuation and Investment Management. Once it is active, all ledgers and currencies
configured for the Universal Journal become relevant for the Material Ledger, Controlling and Asset Accounting. SAP
also states that it is incompatible with the business function `FIN_CO_COGM`, and that the Cost of Goods Manufactured
solution is not needed and not supported with parallel accounting. Activating UPA also activates the features for an
enhanced procedure of setting up alternative fiscal year variants.

The general ledger side is a matter of ledgers. You use separate ledgers and assign the required accounting principle to
each. You can assign the same principle to all company codes or assign principles company code by company code. Group
valuation is a single-valuation-ledger approach: the legal valuation and the group valuation are stored in separate
ledgers. From SAP S/4HANA 2023, profit center valuation is available, also in a separate ledger and based on transfer
prices.

----

    UJ["Universal Journal (ACDOCA)<br/>ledgers 0L, 2L and further valuation ledgers"]
    UJ --> FAA["Asset Accounting<br/>depreciation and postings per ledger"]
    UJ --> CO["Controlling<br/>overhead, settlement, cost rates per ledger"]
    UJ --> ML["Material Ledger<br/>prices, actual costing and balance sheet valuation per ledger"]

    ML --> R
----

The figure shows the idea of the whole page. The Universal Journal remains the single store; the three subledger
components now each write per ledger into it, and reporting filters by ledger.

=== Asset Accounting

SAP describes the Asset Accounting that comes with the business function as one that fully supports parallel
accounting. It differs from the previous Asset Accounting in several ways, each of which a newcomer should know about.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Area |What changes with UPA

|Data model
|The data structures for asset master data and configuration were simplified and no longer repeat structures of General
Ledger. All journal entries, even statistical postings, go to the Universal Journal, and all postings are
ledger-specific.

|Customizing
|The Implementation Guide Asset Accounting (Parallel Accounting) replaces the previous guide. The accounting principle is
now the main valuation entity. A valuation view is assigned to the accounting principle, and the chart of depreciation
only bundles the depreciation keys.

|Master data
|The Manage Fixed Assets app (F3425) combines the asset worklist, master data maintenance and value display. Quantities
are no longer stored in the asset master record; they are posted with journal entries, per ledger.

|Posting and depreciation
|The posting apps accept a ledger on the selection screen, journal entries are shown per ledger and depreciation area,
and the depreciation run takes a ledger.

|Currencies
|All currencies defined in the ledger settings are supported, and one depreciation area manages the values in all of
them. Parallel currency areas and foreign currency areas are no longer used.

|Forecasting
|Depreciation can be forecast for fiscal years that are not yet open, up to 5 years ahead; the forecast values are
stored in table `ACDOCP`.

|Group valuation
|A specific ledger and a depreciation area are available for it.

|Interfaces
|SOAP-based services create and change asset master data (for example `FAA_FIXED_ASSET_CREATE_MAIN_WEBI`), and
`API_FIXEDASSETACQUISITION_G4BA`, `API_FIXEDASSETRETIREMENT_G4BA` and `API_FIXEDASSETREVALUATION_G4BA` post
transactions. The previous master data BAPIs are replaced.
|===

The rule from the help page that matters most when you set it up is that every ledger created in General Ledger needs at
least one depreciation area in Asset Accounting, so a ledger is never "just" a General Ledger setting once UPA is on.

=== Controlling

Overhead calculations, actual cost rate calculations and settlements run per ledger, and cost rates are maintained per
ledger. A few consequences follow from the help page.

* If an overhead calculation names no ledger, the overheads are calculated in the leading ledger only and the values are
  copied to all ledgers. To get ledger-specific results you run it once for each ledger.
* A settlement run also needs a ledger. If you do not run it for a ledger, the values posted to that ledger stay on the
  settled object, which matters for archiving and for closing a project.
* Cost rates are stored in table `ACCOSTRATE`, which replaced table `COST`. They are maintained with Manage Cost Rates -
  Plan (F3162), Manage Cost Rates - Services (F3161) and Manage Cost Rates - Actual (F3422), and shown with Display Cost
  Rates (F5150). When no ledger-specific rate exists, the ledger-independent rate is used. Intercompany cost rates are
  always ledger-independent, because they act as prices between legal entities.
* The periodic actual cost rate (Periodic Price) pairs with the actual costing run type Single Period Run, and the
  cumulative one (Cumulative Price) with Year-to-Date Run. For each ledger and company code you choose one of the two.
* Classic planning transactions are not supported with parallel accounting; SAP Analytics Cloud is the planning tool.
* Only margin analysis is available in Profitability Analysis; costing-based and combined profitability analysis are not
  supported.
* For Investment Management, only projects and internal orders can be investment measures, and for internal orders
  restrictions apply.

=== Material Ledger and product costing

For Product Cost Controlling, Product Cost Planning, Actual Costing, the Material Subledger and Inventory Balance Sheet
Valuation, SAP lists what you can now do per ledger.

* Maintain material prices per ledger, with Manage Material Valuations (F2680) or the material master apps, and change
  prices in bulk with Upload Material Inventory Prices (F4006).
* Calculate the cost of goods manufactured per ledger. Ledger-specific costing variants (`P00L`, `P02L` and `P03L` for
  material cost estimates) and valuation variants (`00L`, `02L`, `03L`) are delivered for the ledgers `0L`, `2L` and `3L`.
* Run actual costing per ledger (a ledger run), after creating a run template per ledger in Edit Actual Costing Runs
  (transaction `CKMLCP`).
* Determine inventory balance sheet values and lowest values per ledger with the apps Determine Inventory Balance Sheet
  Value (`MRN9N`), Determine Lowest Value - Movement Rate (`MRN2N`) and Determine Lowest Value - Range of Coverage
  (`MRN1N`), and post the result with `MRND` and `MRNP`. If you name no ledger, the leading ledger is used.

SAP spells out two cautions here. After the business function is active, you maintain material prices for each ledger
individually, because a price change is not updated in the additional ledgers automatically; this applies to existing
and to new materials. And FIFO valuation and lowest value determination based on market prices can currently be
processed only for the leading ledger. Where Segmentation Valuation is activated, a valuation level between group and
profit center valuation is available through segmentation views, which needs Profit Center Accounting in the Universal
Journal and one Controlling area per group.

=== Reporting

Only the analytical apps listed by SAP are enabled for ledgers and up to ten currencies. They filter by ledger, but
since the ledger filter takes a single value you cannot select and compare two ledgers in one run. Examples are Cost
Centers - Actuals (F0963), Internal Orders - Actuals (F0941), Projects - Actuals (F0942), P&L - Actuals (F0958) and
Allocation Flow (F4022).

== A worked example: one asset, two ledgers

Example Trading Co. (company code `EX01`, company code currency EUR, group currency GBP, ledgers `0L` and `2L`, as on the
ledgers page) buys a machine for EUR 12,000. The group principle in `0L` depreciates it straight line over 5 years. The
local principle in `2L` allows 4 years. With UPA the depreciation run is executed for each ledger, and each ledger
receives its own amount. The values are illustrative.

[stem]
++++
\text{monthly depreciation}_{0L} = \frac{12\,000}{5 \times 12} = 200.00 \qquad
\text{monthly depreciation}_{2L} = \frac{12\,000}{4 \times 12} = 250.00
++++

The group currency is held in every ledger at posting time. At the illustrative EUR to GBP rate of 0.85 for the month,
each amount is translated as stem:[200.00 \times 0.85 = 170.00] and stem:[250.00 \times 0.85 = 212.50].

[cols="1,1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Ledger |Depreciation (EUR, type 10) |Depreciation (GBP, type 30) |Accumulated after 12 months (EUR)

|`0L` |200.00 |170.00 |2,400.00
|`2L` |250.00 |212.50 |3,000.00
|Difference `2L` minus `0L` |50.00 |42.50 |600.00
|===

The checks: stem:[12 \times 200.00 = 2\,400.00], stem:[12 \times 250.00 = 3\,000.00], stem:[250.00 - 200.00 = 50.00] and
stem:[212.50 - 170.00 = 42.50]. The monthly gap in GBP equals the monthly gap in EUR times the rate:
stem:[50.00 \times 0.85 = 42.50]. Because the run is ledger-specific, no manual valuation posting is needed to bring
`2L` to the local figure. Without UPA, the values of the leading ledger could be copied to the other ledgers in Controlling processes, which Jotev's book describes as needing manual workarounds.

The Material Ledger works the same way. A material priced at EUR 10.00 per unit in `0L` and at EUR 9.00 in `2L`, with 100
units in stock, shows stock of stem:[100 \times 10.00 = 1\,000.00] in `0L` and stem:[100 \times 9.00 = 900.00] in `2L`,
because the price is maintained per ledger.

== Activating UPA

UPA is not a setting that you try and revert. SAP's help states that the activation has major implications for multiple
business processes, that the business function cannot be deactivated afterward, and that a team must plan well ahead.
The business functions are only available to authorized users, so the first step is a request to SAP.

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
|Question |Start with an empty system |Start with a system that has postings

|Prerequisite
|No postings in any company code, no asset master data or asset postings, and no material master data or material prices.
|A migration project; the business function can only be activated together with it. A fully automated migration from
feeder systems is not supported.

|Business functions
|`FINS_PARALLEL_ACCOUNTING_BF` (Universal Parallel Accounting).
|`FINS_UPA_MIGRATION` (Universal Parallel Accounting Migration) for the prechecks, then `FINS_PARALLEL_ACCOUNTING_BF`;
no separate permission is needed for the second.

|Request
|A case for component FI-GL-GL with the subject "Activation of Universal Parallel Accounting".
|A case for component FI-GL-GL with the subject "Migration to Universal Parallel Accounting".

|After approval
|Implement the correction instruction of SAP Note 3207221, then activate with the Switch Framework (transaction `SFW5`).
|Implement the correction instruction of SAP Note 3325920, then activate with `SFW5`.
|===

Before activation, the scenario `FIN_PARL_ACCTG_LEDGER` has to be activated in the switchable authorization check
framework (SACF), as described in SAP Note 1922808. Availability, scope and main restrictions are described in SAP Note
3191636. The notes are accessible only to authorized customers, so this page does not repeat their content.

The business function lists localization for the USA, Germany, France, Japan and Romania. Check that list and SAP Note
3191636 for your own country before planning a project; this page makes no statement about other countries.

Requires an SAP system; not run here.

== Migrating an existing system

If Finance applications are already in use and master, transaction and Customizing data exist, you run a migration
project first. SAP divides it into five phases.

. Information and preparation: learn the prerequisites, scope and process flow, then activate `FINS_UPA_MIGRATION`,
  which shows the IMG node with the precheck activities.
. Precheck: run the prechecks. When they finish without errors you can activate `FINS_PARALLEL_ACCOUNTING_BF`.
. Pre-migration: create the migration project in the system and migrate the Asset Accounting Customizing.
. Migration: the data is migrated automatically with a migration cockpit.
. Post-migration: run checks, make the Controlling Customizing settings and complete the project.

SAP gives four warnings. Only the current fiscal year, the migration year, may be open, so the previous year has to be
closed at the latest before the project is created. No postings can be made during the migration phase. After the
migration, the fiscal year before the migration year can no longer be reopened in Asset Accounting. The migration
creates additional data volume. SAP recommends testing it first in a sandbox that is a copy of production, ideally on
the same hardware.

== Setting up ledgers and valuation under UPA

A reference setup from the Private Edition administration guide (version 2023, so older than the baseline of this page)
shows the shape of a configuration, with company code `1010` of SAP's Best Practices content. The pieces are the same
for `EX01`. The table gives the on-premise path.

[cols="2,4",options="header"]
|===
|Step |On premise (SPRO)

|Assign an accounting principle to a ledger and company code, including the group valuation ledger `4G` or the profit
center valuation ledger `5P`, which start without one
|Financial Accounting, Financial Accounting Global Settings, Ledgers, Ledger, Define Settings for Ledgers and Currency
Types. SAP recommends the same corporate principle for the leading ledger `0L` and for `4G` or `5P`, and the local
principle for the non-leading ledger `2L`.

|Create at least one depreciation area per ledger
|Financial Accounting, Asset Accounting (Parallel Accounting), Basic Settings for Valuation, Define Depreciation Areas in
Company Code.

|Clearing between company codes for group valuation
|Controlling, General Controlling, Manage Multiple Valuation Approaches/Transfer Prices, Level of Detail, Define Valuation
Clearing Account.

|Controlling: use event-based result analysis
|Change the result analysis key and costing variant of the order type (transaction `S_ALR_87008022`). The guide changes
key `MBMF01` to `RSEBW` and the costing variants `PYG1` and `PYG2` to `PYR1` and `PYR2`.

|Material Ledger: move to the new price table
|Run the report `FML_CREATE_ENH_IMPL_FOR_UPA` (transaction `SA38`). The tables `CKMLPR` and `CKMLCR` are obsolete for UPA
and replaced by `FMLT_PRICE`.

|Ledger-specific valuation variants
|Controlling, Product Cost Controlling, Product Cost Planning, Material Cost Estimates with Quantity Structure, Costing
Variant: Components, Define Valuation Variants.
|===

== Availability by release and edition

[cols="2,4",options="header"]
|===
|Release or edition |What the SAP Help Portal states

|SAP S/4HANA 2022
|The business function `FINS_PARALLEL_ACCOUNTING_BF` is available from this release.

|SAP S/4HANA 2023
|Profit center valuation, in its own ledger, is available as of this release.

|SAP S/4HANA 2025 FPS02, on premise and Private Edition
|The business function is documented as above: requested from SAP, activated with `SFW5`, irreversible. Neither it nor

|SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition 2608
|The Public Edition help does not document the business function. It documents the ledger combinations that you choose

for every ledger that is activated.

ledger. For a Public Edition project, rely on the Public Edition pages, not on the on-premise business function page.

== What older books show, and what has changed

Stoil Jotev's _Configuring SAP S/4HANA Finance_ (3rd edition, 2025), section 4.4, introduces UPA as new in SAP S/4HANA
2022, shows the activation of `FINS_PARALLEL_ACCOUNTING_BF` in `SFW5` with its warning, and names four benefits: up to
ten currencies posted in parallel in Financial Accounting and Controlling, parallel legal valuation per ledger,
group valuation in a dedicated ledger with real-time elimination of intercompany profits, and a dedicated fiscal year
variant for each ledger and process. The book describes the activation as possible only in a system without material or
asset master data and transactions, or as part of a migration. It does not describe the request to SAP, the SAP Notes
and the SACF scenario that the 2025 help page requires before activation, the per-ledger rules of overhead calculation
and settlement, or the five-phase migration. Where the book and the help page differ, the help page wins.

== Summary

* UPA makes asset depreciation, Controlling distributions and Material Ledger valuation run per ledger, so each
  accounting principle receives its own result instead of a copy of the leading ledger.
* The switch is the business function `FINS_PARALLEL_ACCOUNTING_BF`, available from SAP S/4HANA 2022, activated with
  `SFW5` after SAP approves a request. It cannot be deactivated.
* A system with no postings and no asset or material master data can activate it directly. A system with postings goes
  through a five-phase migration that needs `FINS_UPA_MIGRATION` and an open migration year only.
* Run overhead, settlement, actual costing and balance sheet valuation for each ledger, and maintain material prices per
  ledger, because nothing is copied automatically.
* Group valuation and profit center valuation get their own ledgers (`4G`, `5P`), and every ledger needs a depreciation
  area in Asset Accounting.
* The Public Edition help documents the ledger combinations and per-ledger closing, not the business function.
