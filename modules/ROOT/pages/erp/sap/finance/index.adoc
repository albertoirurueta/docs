= Finance
:description: Landing page for the SAP Finance reference -- financial accounting (FI) and controlling (CO) in SAP S/4HANA 2025 FPS02 and Cloud Public Edition 2608, from accounting basics and the Universal Journal through the general ledger, payables, receivables, assets, cost accounting and closing to the developer side with APIs, BAPIs and substitutions, plus step-by-step guides on a teaching ledger.
:keywords: SAP Finance, SAP S/4HANA Finance, FI, CO, Universal Journal, ACDOCA, general ledger, G/L account, chart of accounts, accounts payable, accounts receivable, asset accounting, cost center, profit center, allocations, period-end closing, group reporting, BAPI_ACC_DOCUMENT_POST, journal entry, ledger, document splitting, SAP Fiori

include::partial$ai-disclaimer.adoc[]

Finance in SAP covers two connected parts. Financial Accounting (FI) keeps the legal books of a company: the general ledger, payables, receivables and fixed assets. Controlling (CO) keeps the internal view: costs by cost center, orders and profit center, and the allocations between them. In SAP S/4HANA both write into one table of line items, the Universal Journal, so the two views always agree. This subsection explains the concepts in plain words for readers with no accounting background (it starts with xref:erp/sap/finance/accounting-basics.adoc[Accounting Basics]), walks through each area with the same example company, and ends with the developer side: the Finance APIs, posting from ABAP and the places where custom logic can be added. Code for the developer topics links to the xref:programming-languages/abap/index.adoc[ABAP] section instead of repeating it.

== Running example

The pages follow *Example Trading Co.* (company code `EX01`), which keeps its books in euro (EUR) in the leading ledger `0L` and uses an operating chart of accounts called `YCOA`. All codes, accounts and amounts are examples made for this site and are not values delivered by SAP. One purchase, one sale, one asset and one allocation run through the pages: the purchase and the sale show how documents reach the ledger and the open items, the asset shows depreciation, and the allocation moves costs between cost centers. Profit centers `EX-RETAIL` and `EX-WHOLE` split the results.
