= SAP Finance Guide: Allocate Costs and Read a Trial Balance
:description: Step-by-step guide to a small CAP teaching model (CAP Node.js 10.2): collect the office rent of company code EX01 on a cost center, assess it to three cost centers by floor area, and read a trial balance that proves debits equal credits.
:keywords: SAP, finance, controlling, cost center, allocation, assessment, overhead allocation, distribution, tracing factor, statistical key figure, trial balance, debit, credit, company code, CAP, Fiori elements, step by step, guide
:stem: latexmath

include::partial$ai-disclaimer.adoc[]

Checked on 2026-11-13 against CAP Node.js 10.2 (`@sap/cds` 10.2.2, `@sap/cds-dk` 10.2.1), the Fiori elements preview of `cds watch` (SAPUI5 1.145.0), curl 8.7 and Node.js 24.21.

== What you build

This is a teaching model built with CAP. It is not SAP S/4HANA or SAP SuccessFactors and does not reproduce their screens.

You build a small model of the period-end allocation of Example Trading Co. (company code `EX01`, euro). The office rent of 1,000.00 EUR was paid once and sits on a collector cost center, `EX-RENT`. Administration, sales and production use the building, so at the end of March the rent has to move to the cost centers that caused it. You describe that move as an *allocation cycle*: the sender, the share of its balance that it releases, and the receivers with a *tracing factor*, here the floor area of each cost center. Your service runs the cycle and posts the result as journal lines. Then you read a *trial balance*, the list of every account with its starting balance, its debits, its credits and its ending balance, and see that the total of the debits equals the total of the credits, before and after the allocation.

image::sap-finance-guide-allocate-costs-and-read-a-trial-balance-08-result.svg["Browser with the trial balance of March 2026 as JSON: the accounts 113100 Bank, 300000 Share capital, 455000 Rent and 940000 Overhead allocation with starting balance, debits, credits and ending balance, then TotalDebits 2000.00, TotalCredits 2000.00, TotalEndingBalance 0.00 and Balanced true",width=720]

== Before you start

* No account is needed: the CAP tools and curl install without signing in. Installing `@sap/cds-dk` means accepting the SAP Developer License Agreement, version 3.2, which the lab README names.
* Do the guides xref:erp/sap/guide-first-cap-service.adoc[Your First CAP Service] and xref:erp/sap/guide-cap-domain-model-and-custom-logic.adoc[Model a Domain and Add Custom Logic in CAP] first: they explain `cds init`, CDS files, CSV data, `cds watch` and handlers. The guide xref:erp/sap/finance/guide-post-balanced-journal-entries.adoc[Post Balanced Journal Entries in a Teaching Ledger] builds the idea of a balanced entry that this guide relies on.
* Read xref:erp/sap/finance/allocations.adoc[Allocations: Assessment, Distribution and Activity Allocation] for senders, receivers, rules and tracing factors, xref:erp/sap/finance/cost-centers-and-internal-orders.adoc[Cost Centers and Internal Orders] for the cost center, xref:erp/sap/finance/financial-reporting-and-analytics.adoc[Financial Reporting and Analytics] for the trial balance, and xref:erp/sap/finance/controlling-overview-and-cost-elements.adoc[Controlling Overview and Cost Elements] for the secondary cost element of category 42 that an assessment posts on.
* You need curl 7.82 or newer, which has the option `--json`. `curl --version` prints the version.
* The names. `CostCenter`, `GLAccount`, `CompanyCode`, `FiscalPeriod` and `AmountInCompanyCodeCurrency` are element names that the page of the SAP view `I_JournalEntryItemBasic` prints. The parameters `FromPostingDate` and `ToPostingDate` of the trial balance and its four measures (starting, debit, credit and ending balance) follow the page of the SAP view `C_Trialbalanceq0001`, which has the input parameters `P_FromPostingDate` and `P_ToPostingDate`. The words sender share, tracing factor, Factor Per, receiver weighting factor and overhead allocation account come from the Manage Allocations help pages. The other names (`AllocationCycles`, `KeyFigures`, `Document`, `Source` and so on) and the sign convention (a debit is a positive amount, a credit a negative one) are this model's own. Codes and amounts are made up: the cost centers `EX-RENT`, `EX-ADM`, `EX-SALES` and `EX-PROD` and the floor areas 120, 180 and 300 square meters are the illustrative values of the reference page on allocations, the account numbers `113100` (bank), `300000` (share capital) and `455000` (rent) are invented example numbers, and `940000` is the illustrative overhead allocation account of the controlling page. SAP does not deliver any of them.
* The figures are drawings of the editor, the terminal, the browser and the Fiori pages, not screenshots. A highlighted box with a number marks the file, the command or the page part of the step with the same number.

=== Write the allocation rules

[start=4]
. Create `srv/allocation-service.cds`. It exposes the entities as an OData service and adds three things. `CostCenterBalances` is a view that adds up the lines per cost center and account. `runAllocation` is an action that runs a cycle for one fiscal year and period. `trialBalance` is a function that takes a time frame and returns the trial balance.
+
[source,cds]
----
include::example$sap-lab/cost-allocation/srv/allocation-service.cds[]
----
+
Source: https://cap.cloud.sap/docs/guides/services/custom-actions[CAP: Custom Actions and Functions]
. Create `srv/allocation-service.js`. CAP loads it next to the service of the same name. The function `split` shares the released cents out; it works in whole cents and thousandths, so every sum is exact. The handler of `runAllocation` follows the steps of an allocation: it removes the lines of an earlier run of the same cycle and period (SAP's help says that a repeated period-based allocation reverses and overwrites the line items), takes the sender's balance and its share, reads the tracing factor of each receiver, splits the amount, and posts the lines with the posting date of the last day of the month, as the help says for an actual allocation. An assessment posts on the overhead account, a distribution on the original account. The handler of `trialBalance` adds up the lines per account: the lines before the start date make the starting balance, the later debits and credits within the time frame make the movement.
+
[source,javascript]
----

=== Start the service and run the allocation

[start=6]
. In the terminal, in the project folder, start the server. The log shows the six CSV files loaded and `AllocationService` served at `/odata/v4/allocation`; the line `+impl: 'srv/allocation-service.js'+` shows that CAP found your rules. Your log may show one more line about bindings, which you can ignore.
+
[source,bash]
----
cds watch
----
+
image::sap-finance-guide-allocate-costs-and-read-a-trial-balance-03-cds-watch.svg["Terminal after cds watch in the cost-allocation project: the log lists the model files, loads the CSV files for KeyFigures, JournalLines, GLAccounts, CostCenters, AllocationReceivers and AllocationCycles into an in-memory SQLite database, serves AllocationService at /odata/v4/allocation with the implementation srv/allocation-service.js and ends with server listening on http://localhost:4004; callout 6 marks the command",width=900]
. Open a second terminal in the project folder and read the balance of every cost center. Only `EX-RENT` has a balance: the rent of 1,000.00 EUR, on account `455000`. Administration, sales and production carry nothing yet, so they look free of rent.
+
[source,bash]
----
curl -s http://localhost:4004/odata/v4/allocation/CostCenterBalances
----
+
image::sap-finance-guide-allocate-costs-and-read-a-trial-balance-04-balances-before.svg["Terminal with the curl command that reads the entity CostCenterBalances and the answer: only the cost center EX-RENT has a balance, 1000.00 on the G/L account 455000; callout 7 marks the command and the answer",width=900]
. Create the folder `http` and in it the file `run-allocation.json`. It names the cycle and the period that the action runs for.
+
[source,json]
----
include::example$sap-lab/cost-allocation/http/run-allocation.json[]
----
+
Post it. The option `--json` sets the content type, and `-w` prints the status after the answer:
+
[source,bash]
----
curl -s -w "\nHTTP %{http_code}\n" --json @http/run-allocation.json http://localhost:4004/odata/v4/allocation/runAllocation
----
+
The answer is `HTTP 200`. The cycle released 1,000.00, and the answer lists four lines on account `940000`: a credit of -1,000.00 for `EX-RENT` and debits of 200.00 for `EX-ADM`, 300.00 for `EX-SALES` and 500.00 for `EX-PROD`. The posting date is `2026-03-31`, the last day of the month.
+
Check the split by hand. The tracing factors are the floor areas, 120 + 180 + 300 = 600 square meters, so `EX-ADM` gets 1,000.00 x 120 / 600 = 200.00, `EX-SALES` gets 1,000.00 x 180 / 600 = 300.00 and `EX-PROD` gets 1,000.00 x 300 / 600 = 500.00. With stem:[C_S] for the amount that the sender releases and stem:[f_i] for the tracing factor of receiver stem:[i], the rule is
+
[stem]
++++
C_i = C_S \cdot \frac{f_i}{\sum_j f_j}, \qquad 200.00 + 300.00 + 500.00 = 1\,000.00\ \text{EUR}
++++
+
The receivers are debited with exactly what the sender is credited with, so the allocation changes where the cost sits, not how much cost there is.
+
image::sap-finance-guide-allocate-costs-and-read-a-trial-balance-05-allocation-run.svg["Terminal with the curl command that posts http/run-allocation.json to runAllocation and the answer: the cycle RENT released 1000.00 with the posting date 2026-03-31, EX-RENT is credited -1000.00 and EX-ADM, EX-SALES and EX-PROD are debited 200.00, 300.00 and 500.00, all on the account 940000, followed by HTTP 200; the long answer wraps onto several lines; callout 8 marks the command",width=900]

=== Read the balances and the trial balance

[start=9]
. Create `app/annotations.cds`. The first part gives the fields their labels, the second builds the list report: its title and its three columns.
+
[source,cds]
----
include::example$sap-lab/cost-allocation/app/annotations.cds[]
----
. Open http://localhost:4004/$fiori-preview/AllocationService/CostCenterBalances?sap-language=EN#preview-app. The list report *Cost Center Balances (5)* shows what the allocation did. `EX-RENT` still has the rent of 1,000.00 on `455000` and now also -1,000.00 on `940000`, so the sender nets to zero overall. Administration, sales and production carry 200.00, 500.00 and 300.00 on `940000`, and `455000` no longer shows the rent on them: with an assessment the receivers see only the overhead account. The preview needs an internet connection, because it loads SAPUI5 from the SAP content network.
+
image::sap-finance-guide-allocate-costs-and-read-a-trial-balance-06-balances-list.svg["Fiori elements list report Cost Center Balances (5) with the columns Cost Center, G/L Account and Balance in Company Code Currency: EX-ADM 940000 200.00, EX-PROD 940000 500.00, EX-RENT 455000 1,000.00, EX-RENT 940000 -1,000.00 and EX-SALES 940000 300.00; callout 10 marks the table",width=900]
. Read the trial balance of March. The function takes a time frame, as the SAP view does with its two date parameters. The answer is one row per G/L account that has a balance or a movement, with `StartingBalance`, `Debits`, `Credits` and `EndingBalance`, then the totals and `Balanced`. The quotes keep the shell from reading the brackets and the commas.
+
[source,bash]
----
curl -s "http://localhost:4004/odata/v4/allocation/trialBalance(FromPostingDate=2026-03-01,ToPostingDate=2026-03-31)"
----
+
image::sap-finance-guide-allocate-costs-and-read-a-trial-balance-07-trial-balance.svg["Terminal with the curl command that reads the trial balance of March 2026 and the compact JSON answer: the rows for the accounts 113100, 300000, 455000 and 940000 with starting balance, debits, credits and ending balance, then TotalStartingBalance 0.00, TotalDebits 2000.00, TotalCredits 2000.00, TotalEndingBalance 0.00 and Balanced true; the long answer wraps onto several lines; callout 11 marks the command",width=900]
. Open http://localhost:4004/odata/v4/allocation/trialBalance(FromPostingDate=2026-03-01,ToPostingDate=2026-03-31) in the browser to read the same answer as text with one value per line, as the figure under What you build shows it; a browser's pretty-print option gives you the indentation. The bank starts the month with 20,000.00, pays the rent (credits 1,000.00) and ends with 19,000.00. The share capital stays at -20,000.00, and the rent account has debits of 1,000.00 and ends with 1,000.00. The overhead allocation account `940000` has debits of 1,000.00 (the three receivers) and credits of 1,000.00 (the sender) and ends with 0.00: the assessment moved cost between cost centers without adding any. `TotalDebits` and `TotalCredits` are both 2,000.00, `TotalEndingBalance` is 0.00 and `Balanced` is `true`.
+
Check it by hand. Each ending balance is the starting balance plus the debits minus the credits, for the bank 20,000.00 + 0.00 - 1,000.00 = 19,000.00. Adding the four accounts in the order of the answer, the debits and the credits of the month are
+
[stem]
++++
0.00 + 0.00 + 1\,000.00 + 1\,000.00 = 2\,000.00 \qquad 1\,000.00 + 0.00 + 0.00 + 1\,000.00 = 2\,000.00
++++
+
so the debits equal the credits, and the ending balances add up to zero:
+
[stem]
++++
19\,000.00 - 20\,000.00 + 1\,000.00 + 0.00 = 0.00
++++
+
The trial balance is the proof that no line was posted without its counter line. This model adds up the lines of the journal when you ask, as the SAP view does: a trial balance is not stored.

== Check the result

* The run of step 8 answers `HTTP 200` with `Released` 1000.00 and the lines 200.00, 300.00 and 500.00.
* The list report shows five rows, and `EX-RENT` has 1,000.00 on `455000` and -1,000.00 on `940000`.
* The trial balance of March has `TotalDebits` and `TotalCredits` of 2000.00 and `Balanced` `true`. The trial balance of April (`FromPostingDate=2026-04-01`, `ToPostingDate=2026-04-30`) has no debits and no credits, and its starting balances are the ending balances of March.

== Variations

The database lives in memory. Stop `cds watch` with `Ctrl+C` and start it again between the variations, so that each one starts from the CSV files.

* Release only half of the rent. Run the cycle once, as in step 8. Then create `http/share-50.json` with the content `+{ "SenderShare": 50 }+`, change the cycle, and run it again:
+
[source,bash]
----
curl -s -X PATCH --json @http/share-50.json "http://localhost:4004/odata/v4/allocation/AllocationCycles('RENT')"
curl -s --json @http/run-allocation.json http://localhost:4004/odata/v4/allocation/runAllocation
----
+
The second run replaces the lines of the first. It releases 500.00 and credits `EX-RENT` with -500.00, and the receivers get 100.00, 150.00 and 250.00, since 500.00 x 120 / 600 = 100.00. The other 500.00 stays on the sender. The trial balance of March now shows debits and credits of 500.00 on `940000` (not 1,500.00, because the first lines were replaced), `TotalDebits` and `TotalCredits` of 1500.00, and `Balanced` `true`.
* Distribute instead of assess. Create `http/distribution.json` with `+{ "AllocationType": "DISTRIBUTION" }+` and send it with the same `curl -X PATCH` command, then run the cycle. The four lines now carry account `455000`. In the list report `EX-ADM` shows 200.00 on `455000`, so each receiver still sees that the cost is rent, and `EX-RENT` ends with 0.00 on `455000`. This is the difference that the reference page describes: distribution keeps the original account, assessment hides it behind the overhead account.
* Change a tracing factor so that the shares no longer divide evenly. Create `http/key-figure-area.json` with `+{ "Value": 125 }+` and change the floor area of `EX-ADM`, then run the cycle:
+
[source,bash]
----
curl -s -X PATCH --json @http/key-figure-area.json "http://localhost:4004/odata/v4/allocation/KeyFigures(CostCenter='EX-ADM',KeyFigure='AREA',FiscalYear='2026')"
----
+
The areas now add up to 125 + 180 + 300 = 605 square meters, and 1,000.00 x 125 / 605 = 206.61157 EUR, 1,000.00 x 180 / 605 = 297.52066 EUR and 1,000.00 x 300 / 605 = 495.86777 EUR. In whole cents these are 20,661.157, 29,752.066 and 49,586.777, which round down to 20,661, 29,752 and 49,586 and add up to 99,999 cents, one cent short. The model gives the left-over cent to the receiver with the largest remainder, `EX-PROD`, and the answer shows 206.61, 297.52 and 495.87, which add up to 1,000.00. That rule is this model's own choice; the SAP help pages used here do not say how SAP rounds.
* Weight a receiver. The Receiver Weighting Factors page of the SAP help has an example: 500 from one sender, tracing factors of 200, 200 and 300 employees, weighting factors 20, 30 and 50 and a Factor Per of 100. The weighted reference of a receiver is stem:[w_i = (F_i / F_{per}) \cdot f_i], with weighting factor stem:[F_i], divisor stem:[F_{per}] and tracing factor stem:[f_i], and the amount is stem:[C_S \cdot w_i / \sum_j w_j]. With numbers, (20 / 100) x 200 = 40, (30 / 100) x 200 = 60 and (50 / 100) x 300 = 150, which add up to 250, so the receivers get 500 x 40 / 250 = 80, 500 x 60 / 250 = 120 and 500 x 150 / 250 = 300. The model implements the same two calculations through the field `Factor` of a receiver and the field `FactorPer` of the cycle, and the test `the SAP help example of receiver weighting factors` in `test/allocation.test.js` reproduces those three amounts. The SAP page also says that a receiver is excluded by setting its factor to 0; the test `a weighting factor of 0 excludes a receiver` shows the model giving 400.00 and 600.00 when `EX-PROD` has the factor 0.
* Break the books on purpose. Create `http/lone-line.json` with a single line (a debit of 100.00 on `455000` for `EX-ADM`, dated 2026-03-20, without a counter line) and post it to `JournalLines`:
+
[source,json]
----
include::example$sap-lab/cost-allocation/http/lone-line.json[]
----
+
[source,bash]
----
curl -s -o /dev/null -w "HTTP %{http_code}\n" --json @http/lone-line.json http://localhost:4004/odata/v4/allocation/JournalLines
----
+
The answer is `HTTP 201`: the model does not check balance when a line is posted, unlike the ledger of the first finance guide. Read the trial balance of March again. `TotalDebits` is 1100.00, `TotalCredits` is 1000.00, `TotalEndingBalance` is 100.00 and `Balanced` is `false`. The difference of 100.00 is exactly the lone line, and the account with the odd balance, `455000`, shows where to look.

== What can go wrong

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
|Symptom
|Cause
|Fix

|`Nothing to allocate: cost center EX-RENT has a balance of 0.00 on account 455000 in 2026/004` and HTTP 400
|The model allocates what was posted on the sender in the fiscal period of the run, and nothing was posted on `EX-RENT` in April. The body is the one of step 8 with `FiscalPeriod` set to `004` (the lab file `http/run-april.json`).
|Run the cycle for a period that has a posting on the sender, here `003`.

|`The tracing factors of cycle RENT add up to 0` and HTTP 400
|No receiver has a tracing factor: the key figure of the cycle has no row in `KeyFigures` for that fiscal year, or every weighting factor is 0.
|Make `KeyFigure` of the cycle match the `KeyFigure` column of the CSV file, and check `FiscalYear`.

|`Fiscal period 013 must be 001 to 012` and HTTP 400
|The model uses the calendar year with 12 monthly periods.
|Use a period from `001` to `012`.

|`Allocation cycle HEAT does not exist` and HTTP 404
|The `Cycle` in the body is not a key of `AllocationCycles`.
|Use `RENT` or create the cycle first.

|`Give FromPostingDate and ToPostingDate, from first` and HTTP 400
|A date is missing, or the start date is later than the end date.
|Write the time frame as `FromPostingDate=2026-03-01,ToPostingDate=2026-03-31`.

|`Balanced` is `false` in the trial balance
|A line was posted without its counter line, so debits and credits differ.
|Find the account with the odd ending balance, then post the missing line or remove the wrong one.
|===

== Clean up

Press `Ctrl+C` in the terminal that runs `cds watch`. The database lives only in memory, so everything you posted is gone at the next start; the CSV files bring back the opening balance, the rent and the cycle.
