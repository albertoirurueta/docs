= Group Reporting and Consolidation

:keywords: SAP, S/4HANA, finance, Group Reporting, consolidation, consolidation unit, preparation ledger, Release Universal Journal, group journal, FS item, data monitor, consolidation of investments, Universal Journal
:stem: latexmath

include::partial$ai-disclaimer.adoc[]

A company that owns other companies has a problem: each company keeps its own books, but the owners, the banks and the
tax and securities authorities want one set of figures for the whole group, as if it were a single company. Making that
single set of figures is called consolidation, and the SAP product for it inside SAP S/4HANA is called Group Reporting.
This page is a brief introduction: it explains what consolidation is, which master data it needs, and how the data of
the ordinary books (the xref:erp/sap/finance/universal-journal.adoc[Universal Journal]) is handed over to Group
Reporting. It is written against the SAP S/4HANA 2025 FPS02 help for on premise and Private Edition. Group Reporting is
a separate area of the product with its own documentation (called FIN-CS), and Public Edition has its own pages for it,
which this page does not describe.

== Key terms

* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#group-reporting[Group Reporting]: the SAP S/4HANA application for the financial
  consolidation of a group of companies.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#consolidation-unit[Consolidation unit]: the entity whose reported data is
  consolidated; in the usual case it is one company.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#fs-item[FS item]: a financial statement item, the account of the consolidation
  chart of accounts to which G/L accounts are mapped.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#group-reporting-preparation-ledger[Group reporting preparation ledger]: an
  accounting ledger that you configure so that group reporting data is kept in General Ledger accounting.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#release-universal-journal[Release Universal Journal]: the older task that copies
  data from accounting to group reporting; the classic integration.
* xref:erp/sap/finance/glossary.adoc#data-monitor[Data monitor]: the app in which the tasks that bring and check unit
  data are run for each consolidation unit.

== What consolidation is

Imagine company EX01 of Example Trading Co. sells goods for 2,000.00 EUR to a sister company EX02 that it owns. In the
books of EX01 this is revenue and a receivable. In the books of EX02 it is a purchase and a payable. For the group as a
whole nothing has been sold: the goods only moved from one pocket to another. A group balance sheet that simply added the
two sets of books would show 2,000.00 EUR of receivable and 2,000.00 EUR of payable that the group owes to itself, and
revenue that never left the group. Consolidation removes such double counting. The sum for the group is, in the
simplest case,

[stem]
++++
\text{group figure} = \sum_{\text{units}} \text{reported figure} - \text{intercompany items (eliminated)}
++++

so that in the example the receivable and the payable of 2,000.00 EUR are both eliminated, and the figures of the group
equal what an outside observer would see. The same idea applies to the investment of the parent in a subsidiary, which
is removed against the equity of the subsidiary; SAP calls that part consolidation of investments. How the eliminations
are defined and posted is a large topic of its own and is not covered by this brief page.

Group Reporting also changes the currency. Each company reports in its local currency, and the group reports in its
group currency. With the running example's rate of 0.85 GBP for 1 EUR, a reported item of 10,000.00 EUR is shown in a group that
reports in GBP as stem:[10000.00 \times 0.85 = 8500.00] GBP. The rates and methods that Group Reporting really uses are
configured in the system; the figures here only show the idea.

== Consolidation units and the consolidation chart of accounts

SAP's help describes the Define Consolidation Units app as the way to display, change, create or delete consolidation
unit master data. The master data of a unit includes attributes that depend on time and on the version, and SAP says
you can add custom fields to it. The help gives the following facts that matter for a beginner.

* *The unit is the thing that is consolidated.* For data that comes from accounting, a company is assigned to the
  consolidation unit, and a company code is assigned to that company. In the classic integration the company that has
  the same key as the consolidation unit is assigned automatically.
* *Group Reporting has its own accounts.* Postings are not consolidated by their G/L account. Each G/L account is
  mapped to an FS item of the consolidation chart of accounts. The help names the apps Map FS Items with G/L Accounts and
  Assign FS Item Mappings for that. The help also states that a posting on a G/L account that is not mapped to an FS
  item is not transferred.
* *Versions.* Data is kept in a consolidation version, and the ledger that supplies the data is assigned to the version.
  A version may have extension versions on top of it.
* *Fiscal year variant.* A unit can have its own fiscal year variant; if it has none, the one of the version applies.

== Two ways to get accounting data into Group Reporting

SAP's help for 2025 FPS02 documents two integrations, and it says that as of the From Year for the group reporting
preparation ledger, the preparation ledger integration is the only process to integrate accounting with group reporting.
So a new system uses the preparation ledger, and the classic task matters for older fiscal years and for reading older
documentation.

[mermaid]
----
flowchart LR
    UJ["Universal Journal<br/>(ledger postings of company code EX01)"]
    CL["Classic integration:<br/>Release Universal Journal task<br/>copies data to the group journal"]
    PL["Preparation ledger integration:<br/>group fields live in<br/>General Ledger accounting"]
    GR["Group Reporting<br/>(consolidation units, FS items, versions)"]
    EXT["Data loaded from<br/>outside the system"]
    UJ --> CL --> GR
    UJ --> PL --> GR
    EXT --> GR
----

=== The preparation ledger integration

The help says that integrating General Ledger accounting and group reporting data directly, without first fetching the
data, brings these benefits: early access to finance data at a high level of detail, access to the most recent data at
any time (including the latest predictions based on logistics documents), the option to release only changed data (a
delta release), and the option to release data to special periods so that it is not added to the last period.

You set the integration up in the configuration activity Define Group Reporting Preparation Ledgers, which defines an
accounting ledger as a preparation ledger. Fields that Group Reporting needs, such as the consolidation group and the
posting level, are added to General Ledger accounting. Their values are derived during the release task by predefined
substitution rules, with the data replicated to accounting. The help adds that data loaded from outside the system
directly into Group Reporting is still supported, because some companies have no accounting configuration in the system.
The configuration is written as a sequence of steps in SAP's pages (a seven-step setup is described), and it is done by
a finance configuration consultant in an SAP system (requires an SAP system; not run here).

=== The classic integration: Release Universal Journal

In the classic integration, the Release Universal Journal task copies data from accounting into the group journal of
Group Reporting. The help describes it as follows.

* *Prerequisites.* The period is open in Group Reporting; the unit's universal journal integration option is Transfer
  from Universal Journal; and G/L accounts that carry postings are assigned to FS items.
* *Where it runs.* In the Data Monitor app, or by the Schedule Jobs for Consolidation Tasks app.
* *Run types.* An update run releases the data; a test run simulates it; and a test with the original list ignores the
  status of blocked units.
* *Modes.* In Current Period - Complete Data mode, all records of the period are processed and only the differences from
  what was already transferred are sent. In Current Period - Delta mode, only data posted after the last run is released;
  the help says there is a delay of 20 seconds, so the update contains only data created at least 20 seconds before the
  run. The two prior-period modes exist only in the preparation ledger integration.
* *Derived fields.* In the classic integration, the company, the consolidation unit, the partner consolidation unit, the
  consolidation chart of accounts, the FS item and the subitem are derived during the run and kept in the group journal;
  they are not available in the Universal Journal on the accounting side.
* *Final run.* SAP recommends a final run after the period is locked in accounting, so that all accounting data has
  been transferred.

== Common mistakes

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Mistake |What happens, as far as SAP's pages say
|A G/L account is not mapped to an FS item
|Postings on it are not transferred to Group Reporting, so the consolidated figures miss them.
|Quantity amounts are transferred without a unit of measure
|Validation errors occur in Group Reporting and the data must be corrected.
|The final release is run before the accounting period is locked
|Postings created later are not in Group Reporting unless the task is run again.
|A document is missed in Delta mode
|The data validation tasks may find an imbalance; a run in Complete Data mode transfers the missing document.
|===

== Where this fits with the rest of Finance

Group Reporting reads what General Ledger accounting has posted, so everything on the pages about the
xref:erp/sap/finance/universal-journal.adoc[Universal Journal] and
xref:erp/sap/finance/ledgers-and-currencies.adoc[ledgers and currencies] still applies. Consolidation is done after the
period-end work of each company is finished; the Period-End Closing page describes that work. The ledger that feeds
Group Reporting is one of the ledgers of the company code, and the
xref:erp/sap/finance/charts-of-accounts-and-gl-accounts.adoc[chart of accounts page] explains why a separate
consolidation chart of accounts is needed.

== Summary

* Consolidation produces one set of figures for a group by adding the reported figures of the consolidation units and
  eliminating what the units owe or sold to each other.
* A consolidation unit is usually one company, whose G/L accounts are mapped to FS items of a consolidation chart of
  accounts; unmapped accounts are not transferred.
* In SAP S/4HANA 2025 FPS02 on premise, accounting data reaches Group Reporting by the preparation ledger integration,
  which SAP's help names the only integration process from its From Year onward, or by the classic Release Universal
  Journal task.
* The release task can run in complete or delta mode, and SAP recommends a final run after the accounting period is
  locked.
* Setup is done in an SAP system by a finance consultant and is not run on this site.
