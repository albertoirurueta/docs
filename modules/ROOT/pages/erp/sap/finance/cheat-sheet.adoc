= SAP Finance Cheat Sheet
:description: A compact quick reference for SAP Finance in SAP S/4HANA: organizational units, charts of accounts, the Universal Journal ACDOCA, ledgers and currencies, document types and posting keys, payables and receivables, clearing, assets, bank and cash, controlling and allocations, closing, tax, transaction codes, Fiori app IDs, journal entry APIs and BAPI_ACC_DOCUMENT_POST.

include::partial$ai-disclaimer.adoc[]

A one-page reference for everyday SAP Finance work, written against SAP S/4HANA 2025 FPS02 (on premise and Private Edition) and SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition 2608. It condenses the Finance pages of this site and follows the running example Example Trading Co. (company code `EX01`): every code, account and amount of the example is made up for this site and is not a value delivered by SAP. Each section links to the page that explains it in depth, and the full list of pages is at the end.

== Organizational units

The units that own the books: company code, controlling area, cost center, profit center and segment, with the keys of the running example.

*Running example*

[source,text]
----
Company code     EX01 (EUR, group GBP)
Ledgers          0L leading, 2L local
Controlling area EX00
Cost center      EX-ADM
Profit centers   EX-RETAIL, EX-WHOLE
Segments         RETAIL, WHOLESALE
----

*Rules*

n company codes : 1 controlling area (same operational chart of accounts). n profit centers : 1 controlling area. n profit centers : 1 segment. Business area is legacy.

Explained in xref:erp/sap/finance/organizational-units.adoc[Organizational Units in Finance].

== Ledgers and currencies

Currency types, the example exchange rate and the kinds of ledger.

*Currency types*

[source,text]
----
00 document   10 company code
20 controlling area   30 group
40 hard   50 index-based
----

*Rate 0.85 GBP for 1 EUR*

[source,text]
----
10,000.00 EUR x 0.85 =  8,500.00 GBP
   240.00 EUR x 0.85 =    204.00 GBP
----

*Ledger kinds*

Leading 0L (feeds Controlling), non-leading, extension and day ledger. A ledger group addresses several ledgers at once.

Explained in xref:erp/sap/finance/ledgers-and-currencies.adoc[Ledgers, Currencies and Exchange Rates].

== Payables, receivables and payments

Transactions and apps for supplier and customer documents, and the payment run example.

*Transactions and apps*

[source,text]
----
Supplier invoice  FB60, MIRO; F0859
Customer invoice  FB70
Pay supplier      F-53, F-48; F1612
Line items  FBL1N F0712, FBL5N F0711
Payment run  F110; F0770, F0771, F1868
Blocks F0593A, dunning F2435, credit F4596
----

*Payment run example*

[source,text]
----
I1 4,000.00, 2% discount = 80.00
I2 6,000.00; I3 blocked
3,920.00 + 6,000.00 = 9,920.00 EUR
9,920.00 x 0.85 = 8,432.00 GBP
----

Explained in xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-payable.adoc[Accounts Payable] and xref:erp/sap/finance/accounts-receivable-dunning-and-credit.adoc[Accounts Receivable, Dunning and Credit] and xref:erp/sap/finance/automatic-payment-program.adoc[Automatic Payment Program].

== Open items and clearing

How to look at open items and how items that total zero are cleared.

*Line items*

[source,text]
----
G/L items  FBL3N, FAGLL03; F2217, F2218
----

*Clearing*

[source,text]
----
Manual     F-03, FB1S; F1579
With post  F-04, F-30, FB05
Automatic  F.13; job app F1927
Reset      FBRA
----

*Rule*

Only accounts with open item management are cleared; the items of one clearing total zero.

Explained in xref:erp/sap/finance/open-items-and-clearing.adoc[Open Items and Clearing].

== Profit centers and margin

Document splitting and the margin example of the product groups.

*Document splitting*

Invoice 1,000.00 EUR for profit centers PC-SALES 600.00 and PC-ADMIN 400.00: the payable is split by the cost lines (table T8G16, report FAGL_SHOW_SPLIT).

*Margin, EX01, 0.85 GBP for 1 EUR*

[source,text]
----
Revenue PG-A 8,000 + PG-B 4,000 = 12,000
After COGS            5,000.00 EUR
After packing, freight 3,500.00 EUR
3,500.00 x 0.85 = 2,975.00 GBP
----

Explained in xref:erp/sap/finance/document-splitting.adoc[Document Splitting] and xref:erp/sap/finance/profit-center-accounting.adoc[Profit Center Accounting] and xref:erp/sap/finance/margin-analysis.adoc[Margin Analysis].

== APIs and BAPIs

Interfaces that post to and read from the Universal Journal.

*SOAP post*

[source,text]
----
JournalEntryCreateRequestConfirmation_In
JournalEntryBulkCreationRequest_In (bulk)
----

*OData read*

[source,text]
----
API_JOURNALENTRYITEMBASIC_SRV
API_COSTCENTER_SRV, API_PROFITCENTER_SRV
/A_JournalEntryItemBasic/?$top=2
----

*ABAP BAPI*

[source,text]
----
BAPI_ACC_DOCUMENT_CHECK, then _POST
DOCUMENTHEADER, ACCOUNTGL, CURRENCYAMOUNT
ITEMNO_ACC links line and amount, RETURN
BAPI_TRANSACTION_COMMIT or _ROLLBACK
----

Explained in xref:erp/sap/finance/finance-apis.adoc[Finance APIs] and xref:erp/sap/finance/bapi-acc-document-post.adoc[Posting from ABAP: BAPI_ACC_DOCUMENT_POST].
