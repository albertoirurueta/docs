= Hire to Retire, End to End
:description: How one employee moves through the SAP human resources landscape from job opening to destruction of the personnel data, with recruiting, onboarding and Employee Central in SAP SuccessFactors (2H 2026), replication to SAP HCM for SAP S/4HANA 2025 FPS02, payroll, posting to accounting, leaving and data privacy.
:keywords: SAP, HR, hire to retire, recruit to retire, SuccessFactors, Recruiting, Onboarding, Employee Central, ECS4HCM, core hybrid, H4S4, payroll, posting to accounting, termination, blocking, destruction

include::partial$ai-disclaimer.adoc[]

A company does not experience human resources as a list of programs. It experiences a person: someone applies, joins,
works, is paid every month, and one day leaves, and the company still has to keep and finally erase some of the data
about that person. SAP calls this chain hire to retire. This page follows one made-up employee through the whole chain
and says, at each step, which system does the work and which neighboring system receives the result. It shows the
chain as a swimlane diagram, with one lane for each system or role. After reading it you can tell where a new hire's
data is created, how it reaches payroll, what the payroll posts to finance, what happens at the end of the employment
and why the data is not erased the same day. It is written for the setup that SAP calls core hybrid: SAP SuccessFactors
(documentation version 2H 2026) for recruiting, onboarding and core HR, and SAP HCM for SAP S/4HANA (H4S4) 2025 FPS02
on premise or Private Edition for payroll. Other setups are named at the end. The page explains the flow and does not
configure anything: every SuccessFactors step needs a tenant and an account, so none of it was run for this page.

== Key terms

* xref:erp/sap/human-resources/glossary.adoc#hire-to-retire[Hire to retire]: the chain of steps from a job opening to the end of the employment and the erasure of the data.
* xref:erp/sap/human-resources/glossary.adoc#employee-central[Employee Central]: the SAP SuccessFactors application that holds the core HR record of an employee.
* xref:erp/sap/human-resources/glossary.adoc#core-hybrid[Core hybrid]: core HR in Employee Central combined with payroll or time management in SAP HCM for SAP S/4HANA.
* xref:erp/sap/human-resources/glossary.adoc#replication[Replication]: copying employee data from one system to another on a schedule or on a trigger, so that the second system can work with it.
* xref:erp/sap/human-resources/glossary.adoc#event-reason[Event reason]: the reason Employee Central records for a change such as a hire or a termination.
* xref:erp/sap/human-resources/glossary.adoc#personnel-number[Personnel number]: the number under which the SAP S/4HANA system keeps one employment.
* xref:erp/sap/human-resources/glossary.adoc#blocking[Blocking]: taking away access to data that is no longer needed day to day, without deleting it.

== The example used on this page

Every page of this section uses one made-up business, Example Trading Co. All keys, names, numbers and dates are
invented example values for this site, not values delivered by SAP. Dates are written DD.MM.YYYY.

* `00001234` Ana Torres, Sales Manager, hired 01.03.2024, monthly basic pay 4,000.00 EUR. Her payslip for March 2026 is
  the one used across this section: gross 4,000.00; employee social contributions 20.00% = 800.00; income tax 15.00% of
  (4,000.00 - 800.00) = 3,200.00 x 15% = 480.00; net 2,720.00; employer contributions 21.00% of gross = 840.00, so the
  employer's total cost is 4,840.00. Those rates are generic flat example rates and are not the rules of any country.
  The arithmetic was checked by hand and with a small script, not by running an SAP system.
* `00001235` Ben Okoye, Sales Clerk, hired 01.09.2024, monthly basic pay 2,400.00 EUR. This page extends the example for
  him with one fact that the other pages do not need: he leaves the company with his last day on 31.08.2026, two years
  after he joined. That date is an invention of this page, chosen as a month end so that no part-month calculation is
  needed.
* Payroll area `E1` "Monthly EX01", a monthly period from the first to the last day of the month, as in the payroll
  pages. Company code `EX01`.

Ana is the featured employee for the middle of the chain, the monthly payroll. Ben is used for the two ends, joining
and leaving, because his hire date is the one the personnel actions page already uses.

== Step 1: Recruiting

SAP Learning describes SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting as the solution that helps companies source, engage and hire talent:
positions are posted online, applications are accepted and interviews are scheduled. The public OData API reference
shows what the product keeps for this: a job requisition entity holds the core application data of an opening, and
candidate entities hold the data about applicants. For Example Trading Co. the opening is Ben's future position, the
Sales Clerk position `51000002` of the example organizational structure, and the hiring manager is the holder of the
Sales Manager position, Ana.

Nothing about Ben exists in the payroll system yet. A candidate is not an employee: he has no personnel number, no
payroll area and no pay.

== Step 2: Onboarding

Once a candidate is hired, SAP Learning says that SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding takes over: new hires can complete their
paperwork online before they start, and managers get a consolidated view of their new hires. SAP states that the
Onboarding solution is integrated with Recruiting and with Employee Central, so information gathered in the earlier
steps can be passed on to Employee Central instead of being typed again.

The API reference adds how the integration looks for a company that uses another applicant tracking system. It
describes a function import, `createOnboardee`, that creates an onboarding record for an external user from the data of
that system, and another, `initiateOnboardingForUser`, that starts the onboarding process once the Employee Central data
exists. The reference says all Employee Central entities of a new hire (person, job and employment data) must be written
before the second call. Both pages are only needed when the recruiting system is not SuccessFactors Recruiting.

Source: https://help.sap.com/docs/successfactors-platform/sap-successfactors-api-reference-guide-odata-v2/createonboardee[createOnboardee (SAP SuccessFactors API Reference Guide, OData V2)], https://help.sap.com/docs/successfactors-platform/sap-successfactors-api-reference-guide-odata-v2/initiateonboardingforuser[initiateOnboardingForUser (SAP SuccessFactors API Reference Guide, OData V2)] and https://help.sap.com/docs/successfactors-platform/sap-successfactors-api-reference-guide-odata-v2/jobrequisition[JobRequisition (SAP SuccessFactors API Reference Guide, OData V2)]

Requires an SAP SuccessFactors system; not run here.

== Step 3: The hire in Employee Central

In Employee Central the hire is an event. The reference page on Employee Central OData APIs explains that employee
objects come in two families: personal objects exposed with names starting with `Per`, and employment objects exposed
with names starting with `Emp`. The employment entity holds employment information such as the termination date, the
possibility of rehire and payroll information, and the job information entity holds the job data that People Profile
shows as the Job Information block. A hire therefore creates a person, an employment and a first job record, all with an
effective date: Ben's records start on 01.09.2024.

The integration guide for the core hybrid option states that Employee Central events such as Hire and Rehire trigger
the standard processes, and that the events are replicated together with the data. Each event comes with an event
reason, the reason that Employee Central records. A customer defines the event reasons. SAP's own example in the
integration guide is an event reason "New Hire" for the Hire event.

From this step on Employee Central is the system of record. SAP's overview of the core hybrid option says that employee
master data and organizational data, including organizational assignments, are maintained there.

Source: https://help.sap.com/docs/successfactors-platform/sap-successfactors-api-reference-guide-odata-v2/about-employee-central-odata-apis[About Employee Central OData APIs (SAP SuccessFactors API Reference Guide, OData V2)], https://help.sap.com/docs/successfactors-platform/sap-successfactors-api-reference-guide-odata-v2/empemployment[EmpEmployment] and https://help.sap.com/docs/successfactors-ec-s4-hcm-integration/implementing-data-replication-from-employee-central/overview-of-core-hybrid-deployment-option[Overview of the Core Hybrid Deployment Option (SAP Help Portal)]

== Step 4: Replication into the payroll system

Payroll does not run in Employee Central in the core hybrid model, so the data has to arrive in the SAP S/4HANA system.
The integration guide describes the path in three systems. A query program in the SAP S/4HANA system asks the
middleware, SAP Cloud Integration, for the changed data. The middleware requests it from Employee Central through the
Compound Employee API and hands it over in packages. The SAP S/4HANA system stores each package first in a staging
area as replication requests, and a processing program then creates or updates the infotype records in Personnel
Administration and Organizational Management. The program also reports back whether each employee was replicated
successfully, with errors or is still pending. The details of this flow belong to the page xref:erp/sap/human-resources/ec-to-s4hana-integration.adoc[Employee Central to SAP S/4HANA Integration]; here three consequences matter for the employee's life cycle.

* *One employment, one personnel number.* SAP states that a new employment in Employee Central results in a new
  personnel number when the employee is replicated. Ben's hire creates the personnel number `00001235` in the example.
  An international transfer is best modeled as a new employment, and SAP recommends terminating and rehiring when the
  legal entity changes.
* *The event reason becomes an action.* The event is stored in the Actions infotype (0000) as a personnel action. The
  customer maps each event reason to a personnel action type; SAP's example maps the event reason "New Hire" to the
  action type Hire with key `01`. An event reason that is not mapped is not recorded as an action: the system extends
  the previous infotype record instead.
* *A correction is a two-system job.* If the hire date was wrong and payroll has already run, replication stops with the
  message that the personnel number was already accounted. SAP describes the one exception to "change data in Employee
  Central only": the date is moved in Employee Central, then in the SAP S/4HANA system, and Employee Central is
  synchronized afterwards. The page in the SAP S/4HANA documentation points to SAP Notes for the steps, which need an SAP
  login and were not read for this page.

Source: https://help.sap.com/docs/successfactors-ec-s4-hcm-integration/implementing-data-replication-from-employee-central/process-flow-of-employee-data-replication-from-employee-central[Process Flow of Employee Data Replication from Employee Central], https://help.sap.com/docs/successfactors-ec-s4-hcm-integration/implementing-data-replication-from-employee-central/employment-in-employee-central-compared-to-personnel-number-in-sap-s-4hana[Employment in Employee Central Compared to Personnel Number in SAP S/4HANA], https://help.sap.com/docs/successfactors-ec-s4-hcm-integration/implementing-data-replication-from-employee-central/mapping-event-reasons-from-employee-central-to-action-types-in-sap-s-4hana[Mapping Event Reasons from Employee Central to Action Types in SAP S/4HANA] and https://help.sap.com/docs/successfactors-ec-s4-hcm-integration/implementing-data-replication-from-employee-central/changing-hire-date-of-employee-after-payroll-has-run-in-sap-s-4hana[Changing the Hire Date of an Employee After Payroll Has Run in SAP S/4HANA] (all SAP Help Portal)

Where no SuccessFactors system is involved, an administrator creates the same records directly with a personnel action
in transaction PA40; the page xref:erp/sap/human-resources/personnel-actions.adoc[Personnel Actions and Master Data Maintenance] follows exactly that path for Ben's hire. The two paths end with the same thing in the payroll system: a personnel number with an Actions record, an organizational assignment and basic pay in the xref:erp/sap/human-resources/infotypes.adoc[infotypes].

== Step 5: Working life and monthly payroll

For the months between the hire and the leaving, the lane that matters is the payroll lane. Changes keep arriving from
Employee Central, for example a raise or a move to another position. SAP states that such data changes are usually
stored directly in the matching infotype and not recorded as an action. Each month the payroll administrator runs
payroll for the payroll area, here `E1`, with the steps the page xref:erp/sap/human-resources/payroll-process.adoc[The Payroll Process] describes: release, run, check, correct, exit.

Ana's payslip for March 2026 is the example of one such month:

[cols="3,2",options="header"]
|===
|Payslip line |Amount in EUR

|Gross pay (monthly basic pay) |4,000.00
|Employee social contributions, 20.00% of gross |800.00
|Income tax, 15.00% of (4,000.00 - 800.00) |480.00
|*Net pay* |*2,720.00*
|Employer contributions, 21.00% of gross |840.00
|*Total employer cost* |*4,840.00*
|===

The check: 4,000.00 - 800.00 - 480.00 = 2,720.00 for the net pay, and 4,000.00 + 840.00 = 4,840.00 for the employer
cost. The rates are the generic flat example rates of this section.

== Step 6: Posting to accounting

A payroll result is also a cost, and this is where the people chain meets the finance chain. After the payroll run the
posting to accounting turns the payroll results into accounting documents, with the expense
on one side and what the company owes the employee, the social institutions and the tax office on the other. For Ana's
March 2026 payslip the posting page shows debits of 4,000.00 + 840.00 = 4,840.00 and credits of 2,720.00 + 1,640.00 +
480.00 = 4,840.00, so the document balances. The accounting page is xref:erp/sap/human-resources/posting-payroll-to-accounting.adoc[Posting Payroll to Accounting]; the Universal Journal that receives the document is explained in xref:erp/sap/finance/universal-journal.adoc[The Universal Journal].

== Step 7: The employment ends

Back in Employee Central the end of the employment is a termination event. The API reference describes a termination
entity for it, with an operation that terminates the employment, and with the termination date as part of its business
key. When such a record is written, an event reason is required. The entity carries the facts that the later steps
need: the last date worked, whether the employee is eligible for rehire, and the date until which benefits are granted.
SAP lists termination among the processes that the standard core hybrid integration covers, next to hire, rehire,
transfer, global assignment and concurrent employment.

For Ben, the termination is entered with the end date 31.08.2026. Replication then brings it to the SAP S/4HANA system,
where the event reason is again mapped to a personnel action type. That is the same Leaving action that a
payroll administrator would enter directly in a system without SuccessFactors. Because the last day is a month end, August 2026 is the last full period in this example, and payroll for `E1`
calculates it like any other month and posts it to accounting like every earlier month. That the final period is
handled this way is the example's assumption, not a statement taken from SAP documentation.

Source: https://help.sap.com/docs/successfactors-platform/sap-successfactors-api-reference-guide-odata-v2/empemploymenttermination[EmpEmploymentTermination (SAP SuccessFactors API Reference Guide, OData V2)] and https://help.sap.com/docs/successfactors-ec-s4-hcm-integration/implementing-data-replication-from-employee-central/business-process-examples-covered-by-core-hybrid-deployment-option[Business Process Examples Covered by the Core Hybrid Deployment Option (SAP Help Portal)]

== Step 8: Blocking and destruction

Leaving does not mean erasing. The payroll results of Ben's last year may still be needed for a tax audit, so
the data stays, and nobody can read it casually. SAP HR therefore separates blocking, which only changes who may read
the data, from destruction, which removes it for good once the retention period has ended and nothing, such as a legal
case, requires keeping it. The page xref:erp/sap/human-resources/data-privacy-blocking-and-destruction.adoc[Data Privacy: Blocking and Destruction] gives the switches, reports and transactions for the SAP S/4HANA system. This page adds only the placement in the chain: blocking comes first and can be done soon after the final payroll, destruction comes last and happens only after the
retention periods that the company defined and the law demands. Which periods apply is a decision of the company and
its legal advisers, and this page states none. SAP SuccessFactors has its own data protection tools for the data in
the cloud; this page does not describe them, so a complete plan for Ben has to cover both systems.

The data is kept in two places in the core hybrid model: the cloud system of record and the payroll system. A process
that protects only one of them leaves personal data of a former employee in the other.

== Which system is the owner

The table collects, for one employee, which system leads at each step in the core hybrid model of this page.

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
|Step |System that does the work |What it hands on

|Recruiting |SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting |The hired candidate, to Onboarding
|Onboarding |SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding |New-hire data, to Employee Central
|Hire and changes |Employee Central (system of record) |Events and data, to the middleware
|Replication |SAP Cloud Integration and the SAP S/4HANA staging area |Infotype records and actions, to payroll
|Payroll |SAP HCM for SAP S/4HANA |Payroll results, to accounting
|Posting |Posting to accounting, SAP S/4HANA Finance |Accounting documents in the Universal Journal
|Termination |Employee Central, then replication |The Leaving action, to payroll
|Final payroll and exit |SAP HCM for SAP S/4HANA |Final results, to accounting
|Blocking and destruction |Both systems, each with its own tools |Nothing: the data is gone at the end
|===

== Other setups

The swimlane changes lanes, but not the idea, when the setup changes.

* *Everything in the SAP S/4HANA system (H4S4 only):* the first lanes disappear. The administrator starts at the personnel
  action Hiring, and no replication is needed. This is the path of the other pages of this section.
* *Payroll in Employee Central Payroll:* the payroll lane moves to the cloud. The landscape page lists it as an option and
  states no details of its processes.
* *SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition:* SAP states that the ECS4HCM add-on is not intended for it and that SAP Master Data
  Integration replicates only the workforce person, which is not enough to support HR processes such as payroll.
* *No compatibility mode:* from SAP S/4HANA 2025 the business function for SAP HCM for SAP S/4HANA is active by default,
  so a system on that release or later runs H4S4 and cannot fall back to the old compatibility mode.

== What older books show, and what has changed

The book SAP ERP HCM: Technical Principles and Programming, 1st edition (2013), by Dirk Liepold and Steve Ritter,
shows the front of the chain as SAP's own on-premise modules. It describes the original R/3 recruitment module, with
applicant entry in the style of an employee hire and applicant tables that mirror the personnel tables, and SAP
E-Recruiting, available since 2003 with a web interface built on business server pages. Its note on the 2012 purchase of
SuccessFactors says that SuccessFactors Recruiting and Succession Development were then becoming an alternative to the
on-premise solutions. Its last chapter explains the hybrid models of its time, with an integration add-on between SAP ERP
HCM and the SuccessFactors Business Execution Suite (BizX). What has changed: Recruiting, Onboarding and Employee Central
are today SAP's cloud solutions for the front of the chain, the add-on for the current core hybrid is ECS4HCM, which
works with SAP Cloud Integration and the Compound Employee API, and the SAP S/4HANA side is SAP HCM for SAP S/4HANA, a
separately licensed component, active by default from SAP S/4HANA 2025. The book's menu paths and add-on names are from the 2013 era and must not be used as steps for a current project.

== Summary

* Hire to retire is one employee's path through several systems: Recruiting, Onboarding and Employee Central in the cloud, then replication, payroll and posting in SAP S/4HANA, then leaving, blocking and destruction.
* In the core hybrid setup Employee Central is the system of record; the SAP S/4HANA system receives a copy through SAP Cloud Integration so that it can run payroll.
* Each event in Employee Central has an event reason, which the SAP S/4HANA system maps to a personnel action type; one employment in Employee Central becomes one personnel number.
* Payroll results are posted to accounting every month, and the final period of a leaver is posted like any other.
* Leaving ends the payroll, not the obligations: data is blocked first and destroyed only when the retention period is over, in both systems.
* The example employees are made up, the payslip rates are generic, and Ben's leaving date is an invention of this page.
