= Human Resources
:description: Landing page for the SAP Human Resources reference -- SAP HCM, SAP HCM for SAP S/4HANA (H4S4) and SAP SuccessFactors, from infotypes, organizational management, time management and payroll to the HR ABAP development topics and the SuccessFactors OData API, plus step-by-step guides on teaching models.
:keywords: SAP HR, SAP HCM, SAP HCM for SAP S/4HANA, H4S4, SAP SuccessFactors, Employee Central, infotype, organizational management, personnel administration, time management, time evaluation, payroll, wage type, payroll schema, personnel calculation rule, PNP, logical database, OData, Metadata Framework, hire to retire, HR authorizations, data privacy

include::partial$ai-disclaimer.adoc[]

Human resources software keeps the records of the people who work for a company and uses them to schedule work, track time and pay salaries. SAP offers this in several forms: the classic SAP ERP HCM, SAP HCM for SAP S/4HANA (H4S4), and the cloud suite SAP SuccessFactors. This subsection explains the ideas in plain words for readers with no human resources background (it starts with xref:erp/sap/human-resources/hr-and-payroll-basics.adoc[HR and Payroll Basics]), follows one example company through personnel administration, organizational management, time and payroll, and then covers the developer side: the HR ABAP programming model and the SuccessFactors OData APIs. Code for the ABAP topics links to the xref:programming-languages/abap/index.adoc[ABAP] section instead of repeating it.

== Version baseline

[cols="1,3",options=header]
|===
|Product |Baseline used on the pages

|SAP HCM for SAP S/4HANA (H4S4) on SAP S/4HANA 2025 FPS02
|A separate license is needed. From SAP S/4HANA 2025 the business function of H4S4 is activated by default and the compatibility mode is no longer possible.

|SAP ERP HCM (SAP ERP 6.0)
|Mainstream maintenance until the end of 2027, with optional extended maintenance until the end of 2030.

|SAP SuccessFactors
|2H 2026 (the 2611 release).

|Teaching models
|Small CAP projects of the lab (`@sap/cds` 10.2 on Node.js 22.12 or newer). They are teaching models, not SAP software.
|===

SAP's announcement of maintenance until the end of 2040 names SAP S/4HANA. For H4S4 itself, confirm the date with SAP's current statements before planning around it. Where a page and this baseline ever disagree after a later update, the baseline is the one to trust.

== Running example

The pages follow *Example Trading Co.* (company code and personnel area `EX01`), which pays in euro (EUR). Three invented employees appear throughout: Ana Torres (personnel number `00001234`, Sales Manager, the featured employee), Ben Okoye (`00001235`, Sales Clerk) and Chloe Martin (`00001236`, Warehouse Clerk, paid by the hour). All codes, names and amounts are examples made for this site and are not values delivered by SAP. The salary figures on the payroll pages use generic flat rates, not the rules of any country.
