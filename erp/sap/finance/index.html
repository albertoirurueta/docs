Finance

This content was generated with the assistance of AI and should be verified against the official documentation before being relied on in production.

Finance in SAP covers two connected parts. Financial Accounting (FI) keeps the legal books of a company: the general ledger, payables, receivables and fixed assets. Controlling (CO) keeps the internal view: costs by cost center, orders and profit center, and the allocations between them. In SAP S/4HANA both write into one table of line items, the Universal Journal, so the two views always agree. This subsection explains the concepts in plain words for readers with no accounting background (it starts with Accounting Basics), walks through each area with the same example company, and ends with the developer side: the Finance APIs, posting from ABAP and the places where custom logic can be added. Code for the developer topics links to the ABAP section instead of repeating it.

Running example

The pages follow Example Trading Co. (company code EX01), which keeps its books in euro (EUR) in the leading ledger 0L and uses an operating chart of accounts called YCOA. All codes, accounts and amounts are examples made for this site and are not values delivered by SAP. One purchase, one sale, one asset and one allocation run through the pages: the purchase and the sale show how documents reach the ledger and the open items, the asset shows depreciation, and the allocation moves costs between cost centers. Profit centers EX-RETAIL and EX-WHOLE split the results.
