Universal Parallel Accounting

This content was generated with the assistance of AI and should be verified against the official documentation before being relied on in production.

A company that keeps its books under two accounting principles, for example a group principle and a local one, needs more than two sets of journal entries. The value of a machine, of the stock in a warehouse or of a production order can differ between the two principles, and so can every cost that is spread over them. Universal Parallel Accounting (UPA) is the SAP S/4HANA capability that lets each of those processes calculate and post its own result for every ledger, instead of calculating once in the leading ledger and copying the result to the others. This page explains what changes for Asset Accounting, Controlling and the Material Ledger, how UPA is switched on, what it needs and what it cannot be undone from, and what a migration involves. It is written against SAP S/4HANA 2025 FPS02 on premise (and Private Edition), where the business function is documented. For SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition 2608 the page names only what the Public Edition help states for ledgers.

Before you read

  • Ledgers, Currencies and Exchange Rates: what a leading and a non-leading ledger, an accounting principle and a currency type are. UPA builds directly on them.

  • Organizational Structures: what a company code is.

  • Customizing and the Implementation Guide: where the SPRO paths on this page are found.

  • The sibling page on the fiscal year variants and posting periods (Fiscal Year Variants and Posting Periods) matters because UPA allows a separate fiscal year variant for each ledger.

Key terms

  • Universal Parallel Accounting: the capability, switched on by one business function, that makes the subledger processes of Asset Accounting, Controlling and the Material Ledger work per ledger.

  • Business function: a switch in the Switch Framework that turns on a group of features; some cannot be turned off again.

  • Ledger-specific valuation: calculating a value, such as depreciation or a material price, separately for each ledger and its accounting principle.

  • Group valuation: valuation at group level, where profits between group companies are eliminated; with UPA it has its own ledger.

  • Profit center valuation: valuation based on the transfer prices used for trading between profit centers, also kept in its own ledger.

  • Material ledger: the component that keeps material prices and inventory values, in S/4HANA also called Material Subledger.

  • Actual costing: the period-end run that replaces the standard price of materials with prices computed from actual costs.

  • Depreciation area: the Asset Accounting entity in which an asset is depreciated under one set of rules.

The problem UPA solves

The ledger page showed that a posting can reach the leading ledger 0L and a non-leading ledger 2L at the same time, and that a manual posting can be sent to one of them only. That covers the general ledger. Beneath it, three components do their own calculations, and each has a history of a single answer:

  • Asset Accounting calculates depreciation per depreciation area.

  • Controlling distributes costs (overhead calculation, settlement, allocation) and works out what products cost.

  • The Material Ledger holds material prices and the value of stock.

Stoil Jotev’s Configuring SAP S/4HANA Finance (3rd edition, 2025) describes the effect without UPA: in cost distribution, assessment and settlement, the values of the leading ledger were also posted to the non-leading ledgers, which needed manual workarounds. With UPA, these processes run per ledger and the result is correct for each accounting principle. SAP’s own description of the feature says the same: you can post different data for each ledger and display or select data from different ledgers in many Finance apps, and you can define up to ten currencies that are used across all ledgers and accounting processes.

What UPA changes

The business function is called Universal Parallel Accounting, technical name FINS_PARALLEL_ACCOUNTING_BF. It is an enterprise business function, available from SAP S/4HANA 2022, and it touches General Ledger Accounting, Asset Accounting, Overhead Cost Controlling, Profitability Analysis, Product Cost Controlling, Actual Costing, the Material Subledger, Inventory Balance Sheet Valuation and Investment Management. Once it is active, all ledgers and currencies configured for the Universal Journal become relevant for the Material Ledger, Controlling and Asset Accounting. SAP also states that it is incompatible with the business function FIN_CO_COGM, and that the Cost of Goods Manufactured solution is not needed and not supported with parallel accounting. Activating UPA also activates the features for an enhanced procedure of setting up alternative fiscal year variants.

The general ledger side is a matter of ledgers. You use separate ledgers and assign the required accounting principle to each. You can assign the same principle to all company codes or assign principles company code by company code. Group valuation is a single-valuation-ledger approach: the legal valuation and the group valuation are stored in separate ledgers. From SAP S/4HANA 2023, profit center valuation is available, also in a separate ledger and based on transfer prices.

Asset Accounting

SAP describes the Asset Accounting that comes with the business function as one that fully supports parallel accounting. It differs from the previous Asset Accounting in several ways, each of which a newcomer should know about.

Area What changes with UPA

Data model

The data structures for asset master data and configuration were simplified and no longer repeat structures of General Ledger. All journal entries, even statistical postings, go to the Universal Journal, and all postings are ledger-specific.

Customizing

The Implementation Guide Asset Accounting (Parallel Accounting) replaces the previous guide. The accounting principle is now the main valuation entity. A valuation view is assigned to the accounting principle, and the chart of depreciation only bundles the depreciation keys.

Master data

The Manage Fixed Assets app (F3425) combines the asset worklist, master data maintenance and value display. Quantities are no longer stored in the asset master record; they are posted with journal entries, per ledger.

Posting and depreciation

The posting apps accept a ledger on the selection screen, journal entries are shown per ledger and depreciation area, and the depreciation run takes a ledger.

Currencies

All currencies defined in the ledger settings are supported, and one depreciation area manages the values in all of them. Parallel currency areas and foreign currency areas are no longer used.

Forecasting

Depreciation can be forecast for fiscal years that are not yet open, up to 5 years ahead; the forecast values are stored in table ACDOCP.

Group valuation

A specific ledger and a depreciation area are available for it.

Interfaces

SOAP-based services create and change asset master data (for example FAA_FIXED_ASSET_CREATE_MAIN_WEBI), and API_FIXEDASSETACQUISITION_G4BA, API_FIXEDASSETRETIREMENT_G4BA and API_FIXEDASSETREVALUATION_G4BA post transactions. The previous master data BAPIs are replaced.

The rule from the help page that matters most when you set it up is that every ledger created in General Ledger needs at least one depreciation area in Asset Accounting, so a ledger is never "just" a General Ledger setting once UPA is on.

Controlling

Overhead calculations, actual cost rate calculations and settlements run per ledger, and cost rates are maintained per ledger. A few consequences follow from the help page.

  • If an overhead calculation names no ledger, the overheads are calculated in the leading ledger only and the values are copied to all ledgers. To get ledger-specific results you run it once for each ledger.

  • A settlement run also needs a ledger. If you do not run it for a ledger, the values posted to that ledger stay on the settled object, which matters for archiving and for closing a project.

  • Cost rates are stored in table ACCOSTRATE, which replaced table COST. They are maintained with Manage Cost Rates - Plan (F3162), Manage Cost Rates - Services (F3161) and Manage Cost Rates - Actual (F3422), and shown with Display Cost Rates (F5150). When no ledger-specific rate exists, the ledger-independent rate is used. Intercompany cost rates are always ledger-independent, because they act as prices between legal entities.

  • The periodic actual cost rate (Periodic Price) pairs with the actual costing run type Single Period Run, and the cumulative one (Cumulative Price) with Year-to-Date Run. For each ledger and company code you choose one of the two.

  • Classic planning transactions are not supported with parallel accounting; SAP Analytics Cloud is the planning tool.

  • Only margin analysis is available in Profitability Analysis; costing-based and combined profitability analysis are not supported.

  • For Investment Management, only projects and internal orders can be investment measures, and for internal orders restrictions apply.

Material Ledger and product costing

For Product Cost Controlling, Product Cost Planning, Actual Costing, the Material Subledger and Inventory Balance Sheet Valuation, SAP lists what you can now do per ledger.

  • Maintain material prices per ledger, with Manage Material Valuations (F2680) or the material master apps, and change prices in bulk with Upload Material Inventory Prices (F4006).

  • Calculate the cost of goods manufactured per ledger. Ledger-specific costing variants (P00L, P02L and P03L for material cost estimates) and valuation variants (00L, 02L, 03L) are delivered for the ledgers 0L, 2L and 3L.

  • Run actual costing per ledger (a ledger run), after creating a run template per ledger in Edit Actual Costing Runs (transaction CKMLCP).

  • Determine inventory balance sheet values and lowest values per ledger with the apps Determine Inventory Balance Sheet Value (MRN9N), Determine Lowest Value - Movement Rate (MRN2N) and Determine Lowest Value - Range of Coverage (MRN1N), and post the result with MRND and MRNP. If you name no ledger, the leading ledger is used.

SAP spells out two cautions here. After the business function is active, you maintain material prices for each ledger individually, because a price change is not updated in the additional ledgers automatically; this applies to existing and to new materials. And FIFO valuation and lowest value determination based on market prices can currently be processed only for the leading ledger. Where Segmentation Valuation is activated, a valuation level between group and profit center valuation is available through segmentation views, which needs Profit Center Accounting in the Universal Journal and one Controlling area per group.

Reporting

Only the analytical apps listed by SAP are enabled for ledgers and up to ten currencies. They filter by ledger, but since the ledger filter takes a single value you cannot select and compare two ledgers in one run. Examples are Cost Centers - Actuals (F0963), Internal Orders - Actuals (F0941), Projects - Actuals (F0942), P&L - Actuals (F0958) and Allocation Flow (F4022).

Activating UPA

UPA is not a setting that you try and revert. SAP’s help states that the activation has major implications for multiple business processes, that the business function cannot be deactivated afterward, and that a team must plan well ahead. The business functions are only available to authorized users, so the first step is a request to SAP.

Question Start with an empty system Start with a system that has postings

Prerequisite

No postings in any company code, no asset master data or asset postings, and no material master data or material prices.

A migration project; the business function can only be activated together with it. A fully automated migration from feeder systems is not supported.

Business functions

FINS_PARALLEL_ACCOUNTING_BF (Universal Parallel Accounting).

FINS_UPA_MIGRATION (Universal Parallel Accounting Migration) for the prechecks, then FINS_PARALLEL_ACCOUNTING_BF; no separate permission is needed for the second.

Request

A case for component FI-GL-GL with the subject "Activation of Universal Parallel Accounting".

A case for component FI-GL-GL with the subject "Migration to Universal Parallel Accounting".

After approval

Implement the correction instruction of SAP Note 3207221, then activate with the Switch Framework (transaction SFW5).

Implement the correction instruction of SAP Note 3325920, then activate with SFW5.

Before activation, the scenario FIN_PARL_ACCTG_LEDGER has to be activated in the switchable authorization check framework (SACF), as described in SAP Note 1922808. Availability, scope and main restrictions are described in SAP Note 3191636. The notes are accessible only to authorized customers, so this page does not repeat their content.

The business function lists localization for the USA, Germany, France, Japan and Romania. Check that list and SAP Note 3191636 for your own country before planning a project; this page makes no statement about other countries.

Migrating an existing system

If Finance applications are already in use and master, transaction and Customizing data exist, you run a migration project first. SAP divides it into five phases.

  1. Information and preparation: learn the prerequisites, scope and process flow, then activate FINS_UPA_MIGRATION, which shows the IMG node with the precheck activities.

  2. Precheck: run the prechecks. When they finish without errors you can activate FINS_PARALLEL_ACCOUNTING_BF.

  3. Pre-migration: create the migration project in the system and migrate the Asset Accounting Customizing.

  4. Migration: the data is migrated automatically with a migration cockpit.

  5. Post-migration: run checks, make the Controlling Customizing settings and complete the project.

SAP gives four warnings. Only the current fiscal year, the migration year, may be open, so the previous year has to be closed at the latest before the project is created. No postings can be made during the migration phase. After the migration, the fiscal year before the migration year can no longer be reopened in Asset Accounting. The migration creates additional data volume. SAP recommends testing it first in a sandbox that is a copy of production, ideally on the same hardware.

Availability by release and edition

Release or edition What the SAP Help Portal states

SAP S/4HANA 2022

The business function FINS_PARALLEL_ACCOUNTING_BF is available from this release.

SAP S/4HANA 2023

Profit center valuation, in its own ledger, is available as of this release.

SAP S/4HANA 2025 FPS02, on premise and Private Edition

The business function is documented as above: requested from SAP, activated with SFW5, irreversible. Neither it nor the migration business function is generally available; you request activation.

SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition 2608

The Public Edition help does not document the business function. It documents the ledger combinations that you choose at scoping: 0L alone, 0L with 2L (IFRS by default) or 3L (US GAAP by default), three standard ledgers in exceptional cases, and optionally the group valuation ledger 4G (scope item 5W2), which provides a preconsolidated management perspective in which intercompany profits between trading partners are eliminated. Ledgers cannot be added or removed after scoping, and closing activities such as settlement, depreciation and allocation have to be performed for every ledger that is activated.

The last row matches the Controlling behavior above: where each ledger has its own closing run, the work is repeated per ledger. For a Public Edition project, rely on the Public Edition pages, not on the on-premise business function page.

Sources: Ledger Scenarios: Choosing the Right Combination for Your Business Needs (SAP Help Portal, Public Edition)

What older books show, and what has changed

Stoil Jotev’s Configuring SAP S/4HANA Finance (3rd edition, 2025), section 4.4, introduces UPA as new in SAP S/4HANA 2022, shows the activation of FINS_PARALLEL_ACCOUNTING_BF in SFW5 with its warning, and names four benefits: up to ten currencies posted in parallel in Financial Accounting and Controlling, parallel legal valuation per ledger, group valuation in a dedicated ledger with real-time elimination of intercompany profits, and a dedicated fiscal year variant for each ledger and process. The book describes the activation as possible only in a system without material or asset master data and transactions, or as part of a migration. It does not describe the request to SAP, the SAP Notes and the SACF scenario that the 2025 help page requires before activation, the per-ledger rules of overhead calculation and settlement, or the five-phase migration. Where the book and the help page differ, the help page wins.

Summary

  • UPA makes asset depreciation, Controlling distributions and Material Ledger valuation run per ledger, so each accounting principle receives its own result instead of a copy of the leading ledger.

  • The switch is the business function FINS_PARALLEL_ACCOUNTING_BF, available from SAP S/4HANA 2022, activated with SFW5 after SAP approves a request. It cannot be deactivated.

  • A system with no postings and no asset or material master data can activate it directly. A system with postings goes through a five-phase migration that needs FINS_UPA_MIGRATION and an open migration year only.

  • Run overhead, settlement, actual costing and balance sheet valuation for each ledger, and maintain material prices per ledger, because nothing is copied automatically.

  • Group valuation and profit center valuation get their own ledgers (4G, 5P), and every ledger needs a depreciation area in Asset Accounting.

  • The Public Edition help documents the ledger combinations and per-ledger closing, not the business function.
