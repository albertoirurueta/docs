Account Determination and Automatic Postings

This content was generated with the assistance of AI and should be verified against the official documentation before being relied on in production.

When a warehouse clerk books goods into stock, nobody types the stock account, the clearing account or the price difference account, yet the books receive all three lines. SAP calls these lines automatic postings, and the rules that pick the G/L account for each of them account determination. This page explains where the rules come from, how a goods movement in purchasing and inventory and a billing document in sales find their accounts, which other lines (tax, cash discount, exchange rate differences) the system adds by itself, and how to check a result. After reading it you can follow a posting from the business event to the G/L account and know which setting to look at when an account is wrong or missing. It is written against SAP S/4HANA 2025 FPS02 (on premise and Private Edition) and, where stated, SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition 2608. The inventory and invoice verification parts rest on SAP Learning and SAP support content, which are older than the product help pages, so check each setting in your own release.

Before you read

  • Journal Entries, Document Types and Posting Keys: what a balanced accounting document and a posting key are.

  • Ledgers, Currencies and Exchange Rates: company code currency, exchange rate types and the ledgers 0L and 2L of Example Trading Co.

  • Organizational Structures: plant, company code and chart of accounts.

Key terms

  • Automatic posting: a line the system adds to a document by itself, for example tax, a cash discount or an exchange rate difference.

  • Account determination: the rules that pick the G/L account for an automatic posting.

  • Transaction key: a three-character key such as BSX that names the kind of line in a goods movement or invoice, so that Customizing can assign an account to it.

  • Valuation class: a setting in the accounting data of a material that groups materials which post to the same accounts.

  • Movement type: the three-digit key of a goods movement, such as 261 for a material issue to a production order.

  • GR/IR clearing account: the account that holds the value of goods received but not yet invoiced, or invoiced but not yet received.

  • Account key: a key assigned to a pricing condition type so that its amount posts to its own revenue or deduction account.

  • Condition technique: the search method that tries condition tables from the most specific to the most general until it finds a matching record.

  • Chart of accounts: the list of G/L accounts a company code uses.

  • Exchange rate difference: the gain or loss that arises when an amount in a foreign currency is booked or valued at different rates.

The running example: Example Trading Co.

Example Trading Co. has company code EX01. It keeps its books in euro (EUR), reports to a parent that consolidates in British pounds (GBP), and posts to the leading ledger 0L and the non-leading ledger 2L. All amounts below are euro amounts in the company code currency unless a line says otherwise. Account names are descriptive, not account numbers, and quantities, prices, tax rates and exchange rates are illustrative values chosen for this site, not SAP defaults. Every automatic line is part of the accounting document, so it is stored for each ledger and currency type like a typed line (see the page on ledgers and currencies).

The idea: a key, not an account

A clerk who posts a goods receipt knows the material, the quantity and the plant. The accounting department knows which G/L accounts exist. Neither should have to meet at posting time. SAP solves this with an indirection. The business event carries keys (a movement type, a transaction key, an account key), and a table of Customizing connects those keys to G/L accounts once, for the whole company. The same event then always lands on the same accounts, and a change of account is a Customizing change, not a change to every posting.

Three families of automatic postings appear on this page:

  • Logistics postings from Materials Management (MM): goods movements in inventory management and invoices in invoice verification.

  • Sales postings from Sales and Distribution (SD): the lines of a billing document.

  • Financial Accounting (FI) postings that exist in any document: tax, cash discount and exchange rate differences.

SAP states for the last family that with certain transactions the system generates automatic postings, including input and output tax postings, exchange rate differences and cash discount gains and losses, and that each one is shown as a separate line item. For each you define the G/L account and the rules, for example a different tax account for each tax code. A line can be supplemented by hand if the Supplement auto. postings field is selected in the master record of the account it posts to, and the field status group of that account decides which fields are offered.

Source: Automatic Postings (SAP Help Portal, on premise)

Goods movements: from movement type to G/L account

What decides the account

SAP Learning describes the principle for inventory management and invoice verification: accounting-relevant transactions post to G/L accounts that the system determines without user input, from a special Customizing table. Because the meaning of an account depends on the chart of accounts, account determination is set up separately for each chart, and the chart is assigned to each company code in Financial Accounting. Four things steer the choice:

Influence What it contributes

Plant

Determines the company code, the chart of accounts, the valuation area and the valuation grouping code, a key for differentiated account assignment per valuation area.

Material

Determines the material type and the valuation class, which is used for differentiated account assignment.

Movement type

Decides the posting specifications and which stock and value fields are updated, for example 261 for a material issue to a production order and 551 for scrapping.

Transaction key

Names the kind of line in the posting, for example the stock line or the clearing line.

Different accounts can be assigned to different goods issue types, so production, scrapping and inventory differences can be reported separately in FI and in Controlling (CO).

Source: Describing Automatic Account Determination (SAP Learning)

Value strings and transaction keys

SAP S/4HANA predefines the posting for each accounting-relevant MM transaction as a value string, a general posting record that contains transaction keys and no G/L account numbers. This is what lets companies with different charts of accounts share the same logic. SAP Learning states that the value string assignments, and the keys inside a value string, cannot be changed. The keys named on the SAP Learning page are:

Key Use named by SAP Learning

BSX

Stock posting for a valuated goods receipt against a purchase order item without account assignment. The system always posts to a stock account.

WRX

GR/IR clearing account, the offsetting entry to the valuated goods receipt.

PRD

Price difference posting, used when a material is valued at standard price and the purchase order or invoice price differs from it.

KDM

Exchange rate differences on a foreign-currency purchase order, when the material account cannot be debited or credited because of standard price control or lack of stock.

EIN, EKG

Purchase account and purchase offsetting account, in company codes with purchase account management.

BSV, FRL, FRN

Subcontracting only: change in stock account, external services and incidental costs for external services.

The same page names GBB and UMB only as examples inside a value string and does not describe them. Stoil Jotev’s book describes GBB as the key for the offsetting entry of inventory postings, which is how a goods issue to a production order can debit a consumption account while the stock account is credited. A free-of-charge delivery (movement type 511) uses the value string WA14, and for a material with standard price control it produces a BSX stock line and a PRD price difference line.

Source: Setting Up Account Determination for Specific Transactions (SAP Learning)

From goods movement to G/L account

The figure follows one posting. The business event selects a value string, the value string lists transaction keys, and for each key the Customizing table is read with the chart of accounts, the valuation modifier, the general modifier and the valuation class. SAP support content names exactly these fields as the key of the account table T030.

flowchart TD GM["Goods movement in plant 1000: movement type and material"] --> VS["Value string of the movement type"] VS --> TK1["Transaction key BSX: stock"] VS --> TK2["Transaction key WRX: GR/IR clearing"] VS --> TK3["Transaction key PRD: price difference"] PL["Plant gives chart of accounts and valuation grouping code"] --> LK["Account table lookup"] MT["Material gives valuation class and, through the material type, the account modifier"] --> LK TK1 --> LK TK2 --> LK TK3 --> LK LK --> GL["G/L account for each line"] GL --> DOC["Accounting document, balanced, in every ledger"]

The figure shows that no step needs a typed account. The chart of accounts decides which table rows apply, the valuation class separates, for example, raw materials from trading goods, and the transaction key separates the stock line from the clearing line. Plant 1000 is an illustrative plant of EX01.

The tools that show and change the table

SAP support content for the error that reports a missing account (message M8147) names the places to look, in this order: the account assignment simulation in transaction OMWB, then OX14 (is the valuation area the plant), OMWM (is the valuation grouping code active), OMWD (valuation area linked to chart of accounts and grouping code), OMWN (material type linked to account modifiers), OMSK (is the valuation class assigned correctly) and OBYC (is each transaction key assigned to account numbers). It adds that a developer can compare the missing entry with table T030 through the function module MR_ACCOUNT_ASSIGNMENT. The SAP Learning page calls the same assignment activity Configure Automatic Postings and describes its simulation: you enter a material number or a valuation class and an inventory management or invoice verification transaction, and the simulation lists all influencing factors, checks that every assigned account exists in the chart of accounts, and shows all transaction keys of the value string, including those that are not relevant for the company code. Its Check Screen Layout button compares the field selection of a movement type with the assigned accounts, for inventory management only.

Sources: Error M8147 - Account Determination for entry not possible (SAP Help Portal, support content), Setting Up Account Determination for Specific Transactions (SAP Learning)

Sales: from billing document to revenue account

The search

In Sales, the lines of a billing document are posted to FI when the billing document is transferred, and the system finds the accounts for each amount by the condition technique, the same method that finds prices. The SAP Help Portal lists the inputs of the search:

  • the chart of accounts of the company code;

  • the sales organization;

  • the account assignment group of the customer (the Account Group field of the customer master);

  • the account assignment group of the material (the Account Assignment Group field on the sales screen of the material master);

  • the account key, which comes from the pricing procedure;

  • further fields of the billing document header and item, such as company code, item category or tax code.

All billing types use an account determination procedure. The procedure contains an account determination type, also called a condition type, for example KOFI, which is assigned to an access sequence of the same name. The access sequence tries condition tables one after another, from the most specific to the most general, until a condition record matches the billing document. SAP’s own example is a discounted item: the first access, which uses sales organization, customer account group, material account group and account key, finds no record, the second, which leaves out the material account group, finds one, and the discount posts to the sales deduction account in that record. Revenue account determination covers the revenue accounts and the sales deduction accounts for surcharges and discounts.

flowchart TD BD["Billing document: item, discount and freight"] --> PP["Pricing procedure gives each condition type an account key"] PP --> AP["Account determination procedure of the billing type"] AP --> KT["Determination type KOFI"] KT --> AS["Access sequence KOFI: condition tables from specific to general"] AS --> CR["Condition record found: account key, sales organization, account groups"] CR --> GL["G/L account: revenue or sales deduction"] GL --> DOC["Accounting document"]

The offsetting entry of a billing document goes to the customer account in the standard billing types. If you want it to go to a G/L account instead, for example a cash clearing account, SAP says to assign the account key for cash allocation EVV in the account determination procedure. Cash account determination and reconciliation account determination work by the same technique, and if the condition technique cannot find a reconciliation account, the customer master decides. If account determination fails, the billing data cannot be forwarded to FI, and the account determination analysis shows, per billing item, the accounts of each condition type, the accesses made and their results, and the keys used. On premise you start it in a billing document with Environment, Account determination analysis; in the Public Edition it is in the Display Billing Documents app under More, Environment, Acct. Determ. Analysis.

Sources: Account Determination (SAP Help Portal, on premise), Performing an Account Determination Analysis (SAP Help Portal, on premise), Revenue Account Determination (SAP Help Portal, Public Edition)

Account keys and VKOA

An account key is attached to a condition type in the pricing procedure, so that a freight surcharge, for example, can post to a freight revenue account and a packaging surcharge to a packaging revenue account. SAP support content for SAP ERP lists keys that are commonly delivered: ERL sales revenues, ERS sales deductions, ERF freight revenue, ERB rebate sales deductions, ERU accruals and MWS taxes on sales and purchases. That page is older, so confirm the keys in your own system. The same support content names transaction VKOA as the place that assigns G/L accounts to revenues (and, for revenue recognition, deferred revenues). SAP’s help pages name these on-premise Customizing activities instead of the transaction: under Sales and Distribution, Basic Functions, Account Assignment/Costing, Revenue Account Determination, activities for defining and assigning account determination procedures, and for Assign G/L Accounts. SAP also states that the condition records are Customizing content, so changes belong in the quality system first and are then transported to production.

Sources: ERP SD Account Determination (SAP Help Portal, support content), ERP SD Revenue Recognition (SAP Help Portal, support content), Account Determination (SAP Help Portal, on premise)

Valuation of a foreign currency balance

A realized difference arises when an item is cleared. A valuation difference arises on a key date, with nothing cleared. For foreign currency balance sheet accounts SAP states that the balance in each foreign currency is valuated, the result is posted to the valuated account or to an adjustment account, and the profit or loss is posted against a separate expense or revenue account for exchange rate differences. Because only postings in the account currency are allowed on such an account, the difference is posted with a foreign currency amount of zero and a local currency amount equal to the difference. The expense and revenue accounts are defined in Prepare Automatic Postings for Foreign Currency Valuation, and an exchange rate key in the account master groups accounts, for example USD accounts apart from the rest. Take a USD account with a balance of 1,000.00 USD, booked at 0.80 (EUR 800.00) and valuated at an illustrative key date rate of 0.85, which gives EUR 850.00:

The account is debited by EUR 50.00, with USD 0.00, and the revenue account for exchange rate differences is credited by EUR 50.00. The valuation run is the program FAGL_FCV (on the Easy Access menu under General Ledger, Periodic Processing, Closing, Valuate, Foreign Currency Valuation (New)), and it can be reset or reversed. This example requires an SAP system to reproduce; not run here.

Common errors and pitfalls

Symptom Where to look

Message M8147, account determination for an entry not possible

Run the simulation in OMWB, then check the valuation area, grouping code, modifiers, valuation class and the OBYC entry, as listed above.

A billing document cannot be forwarded to FI

Run the account determination analysis in the billing document and check the condition record for the combination of account key, sales organization and account groups.

Stock goes to a different account after a master data change

The valuation class is part of the key of the account table, so changing the valuation class of a material can lead to a different G/L account. Change it only when you are sure that the open goods receipts and invoice receipts are settled.

GR/IR items stay open

Postings are cleared only by automatic clearing; check purchase order number and item, and use MR11 for differences.

The same chart of accounts is assumed everywhere

Account determination is per chart of accounts. Two company codes with different charts need separate entries.

What older books show, and what has changed

Stoil Jotev’s Configuring SAP S/4HANA Finance (3rd edition, 2025), section 5.3, explains that every logistics flow ends in finance and that table ACDOCA collects all postings. It shows FBKP as the single entry to most automatic posting settings and OBYC for Materials Management, with the criteria valuation modifier, general modifier and valuation class, and treats automatic sales postings with the pricing procedure in a later chapter. Andrew Okungbowa’s SAP S/4HANA Financial Accounting Configuration (2nd edition, 2023), chapter 13, covers the integration of FI with MM using the valuation class, the BSX and WRX keys, and the integration of FI with SD and revenue account determination. Both teach the on-premise Implementation Guide screens. They are outdated for the Public Edition, where these settings are configuration activities of SAP Central Business Configuration and, for revenue account determination, a list of activities and an app instead of VKOA and the Implementation Guide. Where the books and the help pages differ, the help pages win.

Summary

  • An automatic posting is a line the system adds itself. Its account is found from keys, not typed: a movement type, a transaction key or an account key.

  • In Materials Management a movement type selects a value string of transaction keys; the account follows from the chart of accounts, the valuation grouping code, the account modifier and the valuation class of the material.

  • A purchase of 50 pieces at EUR 8.00 for a standard price of EUR 7.50 debits stock 375.00 and the price difference 25.00 and credits GR/IR 400.00; the invoice clears GR/IR and books the extra 10.00 as price difference.

  • In Sales the billing document finds revenue, deduction and freight accounts by the condition technique, with the account key of the pricing condition and the account groups of customer and material.

  • Tax, cash discount and exchange rate differences are automatic lines with accounts defined in Customizing, and a valuation difference is posted with a foreign currency amount of zero.

  • When an account is missing or wrong, simulate first: OMWB for goods movements, the account determination analysis for billing documents.
