Apps Media Optimization Cheat Sheet

This content was generated with the assistance of AI and should be verified against the official documentation before being relied on in production.

A single-page index of quick back-links to every page in the Apps Media Optimization sub-section, grouped the same way as the sub-section index.

The downloadable PDF is a separate, single A4 page meant for printing. It states the baseline date (2026-10-07) and covers:

  • choosing a variant by pixel size and platform format support (WebP, AVIF, HEIC);

  • Android: Coil 3 cache configuration and the network artifact, Glide cache strategies, BlurHash placeholders and Media3 (ExoPlayer, SimpleCache, Transformer);

  • iOS: the limits of AsyncImage, Kingfisher, Nuke and SDWebImage, plus AVFoundation for HLS, cover frames, export and background uploads;

  • React Native and Expo: expo-image props, react-native-blurhash, react-native-fast-image status and react-native-video;

  • Kotlin Multiplatform and Flutter equivalents; and

  • compression on the device and upload practice (background, resumable, retries).
